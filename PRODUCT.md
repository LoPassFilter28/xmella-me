# PRODUCT.md - US/DR Newspaper Timeline

Status: ADOPTED by PiK0, 2026-11-01 (approved with the adversarial
review's recommendations). Governs with PROJECT-RULES.md. This file is the product truth beside
DESIGN.MD's design truth (Impeccable split): DESIGN.MD says how it
looks, this file says what it is, who it serves, and what it may claim.

## What this is
A bilingual (EN/ES) research edition of United States newspaper
coverage of the Dominican Republic, built from the Library of
Congress Chronicling America archive. Every record is an article on
a dated page of a named newspaper, traceable to that page.

## Audience (in priority order)
1. Researchers and historians of the Dominican Republic, Hispaniola,
   and US-Caribbean relations - including high-scrutiny readers who
   will check citations (the Orlando Rees standard).
2. Dominican and Dominican-American readers tracing how the country
   was seen, including family-history material (the Mella clippings).
3. Students and educators who need citable primary-source coverage
   with the source one click away.
4. The general reader arriving from search with no prior knowledge.

## Purpose
Find, identify, select, obtain (Cutter): a reader who does not know
an item exists can discover it by era, newspaper, place, topic, or
search; identify it unambiguously (paper, place, date, page); and
obtain the original at the Library of Congress in one click.
Save the time of the reader (Ranganathan): no claim without its
evidence class, no summary that outruns its source, no count that
cannot be recomputed from the data on the page.

## Evidence standard (the product's core promise)
- Documented: substantiated by a cited primary source, labeled.
- Oral tradition / loosely verified: presented as such, never as fact.
- Extracted text: vision transcription of the page image, article
  only, faithful in substance, [illegible] where unreadable.
- Locator text: LOC OCR excerpts are finding aids, always labeled,
  never presented as the article and never silently "corrected"
  inside a summary.
- Provenance: every digest displayed must be reproducible from
  material the reader can reach (the page, the file, the text shown).
  A digest the reader cannot verify is decoration, not provenance.
- Counts on any page must be computable from the collection itself
  at build time. No hand-carried numbers.

## Voice
Plain, exact, bilingual. Names what a thing does, not how it was
built. No proof claims ("hand-verified") the process cannot back.
Uncertainty is stated in the open, in the same size type as the
claims it qualifies.

## Non-goals (until PiK0 rules otherwise)
- Not a general Hispaniola archive: Haiti-only material is context,
  admitted only under a stated rule, and labeled as context.
- Not a portfolio page: xmella.me's other branches stay separate.
- The 51,008-record corpus is a discovery layer, not a publication
  layer; nothing from it reaches a page without passing the same
  evidence standard as the 1,418.

## Success test
A hostile expert - a librarian, a Dominican historian, a peer
reviewer - samples any ten records, checks them against the Library
of Congress pages, and finds nothing to embarrass: every article
traceable, every claim labeled, every summary faithful, every
classification defensible, every number recomputed and matched.
