## Evidence standard (the Tito rule, project-wide)
- Every claim is labeled by its basis, spelled out for the
  high-scrutiny reader:
  - "Documented" = substantiated by a cited primary source.
  - "Oral tradition / loosely verified" = family or oral accounts that
    may or may not be accurate, presented as such, never as fact.
- Historians may discuss loosely verified material (the Orlando Rees
  example) as long as the basis is explicit. Named for the Grandpa Tito
  Mella material, applied to everything on the site.

## Extraction fidelity (from PiK0's 2026-10-31 rulings)
- Article only, very specific; the 500-char LOC OCR window is a locator
  hint, never authority. Faithful substance, not mechanical exactness.
  No invented titles, no neighbouring articles, unreadable = [illegible].
- Every record keeps its source_url and SHA-256 provenance digests.
