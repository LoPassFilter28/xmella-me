The Bridgeport Evening Farmer reported that a violent mob stormed the Dominican consulate in Port-au-Prince, dragging out Haitian General Oscar—who had sought refuge there following palace clashes and the execution of political prisoners—and shooting him dead. The crowd also besieged the French legation, where Haitian President Guillaume had fled after rebels damaged the executive mansion. In response to the breakdown of order, the U.S. cruiser Washington, carrying Rear Admiral Caperton and an expeditionary marine force, sailed to intervene.

The Bridgeport Evening Farmer informó que una violenta multitud asaltó el consulado dominicano en Puerto Príncipe, de donde sacaron a rastras al general haitiano Oscar —quien se había refugiado allí tras combates en el palacio y el fusilamiento de presos políticos— y lo acribillaron a tiros. La multitud sitió también la legación francesa, donde se había refugiado el presidente haitiano Guillaume tras los destrozos causados por los rebeldes en la mansión ejecutiva. Ante el quebranto del orden, el crucero de los EE. UU. Washington, con el contralmirante Caperton y una fuerza expedicionaria de infantería de marina a bordo, zarpó para intervenir.

Orestes Zamor, who were executed by order of General Oscar, the governor-general of Port Au Prince, when the rebels attacked the presidential palace and today the crowd threatened an attack on the French legation to seize the Haytian president. The president, together with members of his family, yesterday took refuge under the French flag after he had been driven from the presidential palace, by the revolutionists who, in an attack lasting several hours, partially destroyed the executive mansion. The attempt made yesterday to seize President Guillaume failed because of the energetic protestations of the French minister. The body of General Oscar today remained unburied before the consulate of the Dominican Republic where it had fallen after he had faced a firing squad. The governor had taken refuge at the Dominican consulate after his unsuccessful defense of the Presidential palace, but a mob stormed the building and, dragging him through the street, shot him to death. The arrival of the American cruiser Washington, with Rear Admiral Caperton on board, is expected at any minute. The Washington has been on the north coast of Hayti. M. Girard was counting on the coming of the cruiser, to afford protection to the legation. U. S. Cruiser on Way. Washington, July 28. The cruiser Washington, with Rear Admiral Caperton, 700 blue jackets and an expeditionary force of 100 marines, sailed from Cape Haytien last night for Port Au Prince.
