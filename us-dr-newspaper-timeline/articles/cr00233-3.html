A dispatch illustrates former president Heureaux's strong methods with the example of horsemen who shot at the glass insulators of the telegraph line between Puerto Plata and Santiago: Heureaux decreed that whoever indulged in the sport should be shot. It also reports that Ricardo Limardo resigned last week as Governor of Puerto Plata after finding the principal posts under him were to be filled at the dictation of a military officer with men he judged unfit, and his protests were ignored.

Un despacho ilustra los métodos enérgicos del expresidente Heureaux con el ejemplo de los jinetes que disparaban a los aisladores de cristal de la línea telegráfica entre Puerto Plata y Santiago: Heureaux decretó que quien se entregara a ese deporte sería fusilado. También informa que Ricardo Limardo renunció la semana pasada como gobernador de Puerto Plata tras descubrir que los principales cargos bajo su mando serían ocupados por imposición de un oficial militar con hombres que él juzgaba incapaces, y que sus protestas fueron ignoradas.

into execution. Heureaux's Strong Methods. Heureaux's methods may be judged by one or two examples. When the telegraph was strung between Puerto Plata and Santiago it used to amuse passing horsemen to shoot at the glass knobs to the detriment of the service. When Heureaux learned of the practice he decreed that whoever indulged in the sport should be shot. It is interesting to note that when Ricardo Limardo announced his resignation as Governor of Puerto Plata last week the two persons who had been very prominent in the revolution deemed it wise to expatriate themselves, on the theory that the pledges of immunity which Limardo would not violate might not hold good under a successor. Governor Limardo's resignation illustrates the danger of a partisan administration. He has been a consistent Horacist, believing that to be the party representing honesty in politics. After taking office he found that the principal positions under him were to be filled at the dictation of a military officer and in his judgment the men named were notoriously unfit for the duties assigned to them. As his protests were ignored he decided to resign. Few men have more personal friends than he and the administration can hardly afford to lose many such supporters. New President of Brazil. Rio Janeiro, Brazil, Thursday.—Dr. Affonso Moreira Penna, Vice President of the republic, was to-day elected President of Brazil.
