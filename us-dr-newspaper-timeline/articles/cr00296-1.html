The Washington Daily News outlined the itinerary for General Rafael Trujillo's visit to Washington, D.C., following an invitation from Marine Corps Commandant Major General Thomas Holcomb. The schedule included wreath-laying ceremonies at Mount Vernon and Arlington National Cemetery, official meetings with Secretary of State Cordell Hull and President Roosevelt, and a Senate luncheon. Trujillo was also scheduled to inspect military sites including the Navy Yard, Marine Barracks, and Fort Meade.

El Washington Daily News detalló el itinerario de la visita del general Rafael Trujillo a Washington D.C., tras una invitación cursada por el comandante del Cuerpo de Infantería de Marina, mayor general Thomas Holcomb. El programa incluía ofrendas florales en Mount Vernon y en el Cementerio Nacional de Arlington, reuniones oficiales con el secretario de Estado Cordell Hull y el presidente Roosevelt, así como un almuerzo en el Senado. Se tenía previsto además que Trujillo inspeccionara instalaciones castrenses como el Astillero Naval, el Cuartel de Marines y Fort Meade.

As a former member of the United States Marine Corps—during the Marine occupation of the Dominican Republic some years ago. Rafael Trujillo served with them —the general has been invited to Washington by the Maj. Gen. Commandant of the Marine Corps, Gen. Thomas Holcomb. And as such a guest many of his activities during his several days in Washington will be with the military. On Thursday the visitor will go to Mt. Vernon, and Arlington where he will lay wreathes on the tombs of George Washington and the Unknown Soldier. He. will then visit the CCC camp at Fort Hunt, following much the same schedule that the King and Queen followed on their recent visit. On Friday morning Gen. Trujillo will call on the Secretary of State, and later on President Roosevelt and will then be entertained at luncheon at the Senate by a group of senators, probably including Sen. Theodore Green of Rhode Island who was among the delegates who went to Trujillo City for the dedication of the Christopher Columbus Memorial site.
