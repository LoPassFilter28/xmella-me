Passage summaryResumen del pasaje

The New Hampshire Farmer and Weekly Union reported that Minister Sullivan successfully resolved the Dominican civil conflict, announcing a U.S. policy that rejected armed rebellion in favor of electoral remedies and constitutional governance. Following a battle on October 2, Commander Nulton of the Nashville and Receiver Walter W. Vick transported deadlocked peace envoys from Puerto Plata to Santo Domingo City. Negotiations at the capital concluded with a comprehensive peace accord accepted by all factions.

Vick trasladaron desde Puerto Plata a la ciudad de Santo Domingo a los delegados de paz cuyas gestiones se hallaban trabadas. Las negociaciones en la capital desembocaron en un amplio tratado de paz aceptado por todas las facciones.
