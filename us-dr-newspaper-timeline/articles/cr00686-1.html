President Alexjandro of the Republic of Santo Domingo has invitedEl presidente Alexjandro, de la República de Santo Domingo, ha invitado

President Alexjandro of the Republic of Santo Domingo has invited President Palma, advices from Havana to The Birmingham Age-Herald state, to take part in the inauguration of the president of the Dominican Republic. The report notes that President Palma will spend the majority of the present month touring eastern Cuba.El presidente Alexjandro, de la República de Santo Domingo, ha invitado al presidente Palma, indican noticias procedentes de La Habana para The Birmingham Age-Herald, a participar en la investidura del presidente de la República Dominicana. El informe señala que el presidente Palma pasará la mayor parte del presente mes recorriendo el oriente de Cuba.

The Birmingham Age-Herald. Birmingham, Ala., September 7, 1903.
