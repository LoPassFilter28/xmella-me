The Union prints a dispatch of June 10: William W. Handley, of New York, vice consul at Trinidad, has been transferred to Puerto Plata, Dominican Republic, as consul. Herbert Wright, of Des Moines, was originally appointed to Puerto Plata, but never qualified; he will be given another post, subject to examination. The announcement of Wright's selection recently precipitated a storm of protest to the department from the Rev. Frank W. Lomack, a minister of Des Moines, who forwarded to the department headquarters a series of legal documents showing that he had prosecuted Wright because of alleged intimacy between Wright and Mrs. Lomack. Wright is a lawyer.El Union publica un despacho del 10 de junio: William W. Handley, de Nueva York, vicecónsul en Trinidad, ha sido transferido a Puerto Plata, República Dominicana, como cónsul. Herbert Wright, de Des Moines, fue nombrado originalmente para Puerto Plata, pero nunca se calificó; se le dará otro puesto, sujeto a examen. El anuncio de la selección de Wright precipitó recientemente una tormenta de protestas al departamento del Rev. Frank W. Lomack, un ministro de Des Moines, quien remitió a la sede del departamento una serie de documentos legales que mostraban que había procesado a Wright por alegada intimidad entre Wright y la Sra. Lomack. Wright es abogado.

The Black Hills Union and Western Stock Review. Rapid City, S.D., June 16, 1905. Page 2.
