Late from St. Domingo—Peace Declared Between the Haytiens and Dominicans.

SourceFuente: The New York herald, New York [N.Y.], 1850-11-30
https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn83030313/1850-11-30/ed-1/?sp=1
diplomacy_treatiesculture_sports_tourism

Accounts from St. Domingo City, to the 2d instant, received by the brig George Otis, represent that, through the intervention of the English and American agents, a treaty of peace between the Dominicans and Haytiens had been concluded. Previous to this there had been some brisk fighting between the contending parties, but no serious loss had been sustained by either party. There was, at the time of the treaty, an English man-of-war in port.
