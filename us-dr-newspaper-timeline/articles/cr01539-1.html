1942-08-06 · Washington, D.C.

Evening star — 1942-08-06

SourceFuente: Evening star, Washington, D.C., 1942-08-06
https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn83045462/1942-08-06/ed-1/?sp=2
trujillo_regimediplomacy_treaties

The *Evening Star* reported that Representative Hamilton Fish denied being questioned by the Internal Revenue Bureau regarding a $25,000 credit he received from Dominican dictator Rafael L. Trujillo in 1939. Fish stated that he acted solely as an agent in oil speculations, explaining that $12,500 was lost and the remaining $12,500 was returned to Trujillo. Fish added that tax officials had advised him the agency transaction did not need to be declared on his income tax return.

El *Evening Star* informó que el legislador Hamilton Fish desmintió haber sido interrogado por la Oficina de Rentas Internas en torno a una partida de 25.000 dólares transferida por el dictador dominicano Rafael L. Trujillo en 1939. Fish alegó que se limitó a desempeñarse como comisionista intermediario en especulaciones petrolíferas, precisando que se esfumaron 12.500 dólares y se reembolsó a Trujillo el restante de 12.500. Fish arguyó asimismo que las autoridades impositivas le dictaminaron que dicha intermediación no requería ser asentada en su balance tributario anual.
