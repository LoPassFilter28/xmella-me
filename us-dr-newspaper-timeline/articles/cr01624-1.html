The Evening Star analyzed strategic vulnerabilities in Caribbean defenses, warning of potential submarine threats to the Panama Canal and regional bases. The analysis noted ongoing armed conflict in Costa Rica, where government forces reportedly received backing from Dominicans, Nicaraguans, and Hondurans against rebels supporting Otilio Ulate. The author cautioned that a communist victory would leave American defense lines bordered by hostile regimes, with the unsympathetic Dominican government positioned centrally in the region.

El Evening Star examinó las vulnerabilidades operativas en el dispositivo de seguridad caribeño, previniendo acerca de posibles amenazas submarinas contra el canal de Panamá y las bases tácticas comarcanas. La crónica reparó en las hostilidades fratricidas de Costa Rica, señalando que las tropas gobiernistas contaban presuntamente con el apoyo de efectivos dominicanos, nicaragüenses y hondureños para contrarrestar a los alzados que secundaban a Otilio Ulate. El redactor alertó de que una victoria de cuño comunista dejaría el cordón defensivo estadounidense flanqueado por regímenes antagonistas, con el desconfiado gobierno dominicano afianzado en el corazón del espacio antillano.

Given secret bases in South American or merely operating at long-range out of Russia's Arctic sea bases at Murmansk and Petsamo, Red U-boats today could easily mount devastating surprise attacks on the Panama Canal, Roosevelt Roads, St. Thomas or any other base we hold in the vital underbelly defense area of the Spanish Main. The situation also is brought into sharp focus by the current fight in leftist-run Costa Rica between the Communist government forces on one side and the rebelling Ulatistas on the other. Fighting for the Red Costa Rican government forces are Nicaraguans, Dominicans and Hondurans, all present with the blessing of their leftist-minded governments. Democrat Otilio Ulate is receiving aid from Guatemala and Panama. If the Communists win in Costa Rica, the United States must face the fact that left-wing dictator states will lie on our western flank in the Caribbean (Costa Rica, Nicaragua, Honduras) and at least one unsympathetic government (the Dominican Republic) is situated in the center of the entire defense area. The bloody April 9 revolution in Colombia—a short airplane flight from the Panama Canal—also should give pause to the Nation. A Red victory in Colombia would pose a definite threat to our Pacific lifeline—a threat the United States would be hard put to guard against with its present meager forces in the Caribbean area.
