The Buffalo bulletin reported that American Minister Russell announced U.S. marines would be distributed to strategic points in Santo Domingo to protect American sugar plantations and interests, while warships were stationed at key ports. The State Department declared it would not recognize the election of Federico H. Carvajal as provisional president following the impeachment of President Jimenes. Officials expressed confidence that Rear Admiral Caperton had the revolutionary movement under General Arias well under control.

UU. serían distribuidos en enclaves estratégicos de Santo Domingo para proteger los ingenios azucareros e intereses estadounidenses, a la vez que se apostaban buques de guerra en los puertos principales. Carvajal) como presidente provisional tras la destitución del presidente Jimenes. Los funcionarios manifestaron seguridad en que el contralmirante Caperton mantenía plenamente controlado el movimiento revolucionario acaudillado por el general Arias.

American Minister Russell, reporting today that the marines soon would be distributed to strategic points in the republic, said he had received many requests for such action in protection of American sugar plantations and other American Interests. American warships already are stationed at all the important ports. The arrangement for continued cooperation of the American forces in policing Santo Domingo probably will not be as far reaching as that in force in Haiti, officials say, but will be broad enough in scope to prevent any repetition of the present complicated situation. Under a treaty already in force, the United States administers the collection of customs for the Dominican government. It was made clear at the state department today that there was no intention of recognising the election of Frederick H. Carvajal, chosen provisional president of Santo Domingo last week by the Dominican congress, as valid. Minister Russell reported that Enrique Henriques, former American minister, was on his way to Washington to urge Carvajal's recognition. The department holds that the impeachment of President Jiminez was only a political coup and already has informed the Dominican congress that the proceedings cannot be recognised as legal. While Rear Admiral Caperton is not reporting details of his operations, officials here believe he now has the revolutionary movement under General Arias well controlled.
