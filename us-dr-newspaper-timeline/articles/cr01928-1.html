FOREIGN INTELLIGENCE.

A PARIS dispatch, on the 3d, stated that the expulsion of religious communities proceeded on that day in the provinces against the Capuchins, Dominicans and others. There were the usual protests against expulsion and door breakings. The Pope has protested against the present proceedings of the French Ministry.
