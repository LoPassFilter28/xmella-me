'Go over to enemy': at Dajabón, General Liriano's troops desert without firing a shot and join Pacheco; the frontier posts at Las Aguas are the revolutionists', and Jiménez's force augments every day.

«Se pasan al enemigo»: en Dajabón, las tropas del general Liriano desertan sin disparar un tiro y se unen a Pacheco; los puestos fronterizos de Las Aguas son de los revolucionarios y la fuerza de Jiménez aumenta cada día.

Cape Haitien, Hayti, Aug. 11. — General Francisco [Liriano?], sent from Monte Christi by the Dominican government to attack General Baron Pacheco at Dajabon, the headquarters of the revolutionists, was abandoned when he arrived in front of the enemy, by his troops, who, without firing a shot, deserted and entered the camp of General Pacheco. The advanced posts of the latter are at Las Aguas, and the revolutionists are masters of the fords across the river Yaque, thus cutting off communication between Monte Christi and the interior. The force of Don Juan Isidro Jiminez, leader of the revolution, is being augmented every day, and news from every part of the Dominican republic is favorable to him. WILL RETURN IN A BODY. Utah Volunteers to Arrive In Salt Lake City on August 19. San Francisco, Aug. 11. — The Utah volunteers have cast a unanimous vote to return home in a body. They will be brought home by special train, and will reach Salt Lake City on August 19. A stop of an hour or two will be made in Ogden, and at other towns on the line to give the people a chance to greet the returning heroes. Arriving in Salt Lake City, the boys will march through the principal streets to Liberty Park, where a program will be carried out. The Utah men will leave the Presidio for their homes on the afternoon of August 17, at 3 o'clock. Unless something unexpected happens this program will be carried out to the letter.
