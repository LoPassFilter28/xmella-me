The Salt Lake Herald reported on celebrations among Cuban expatriates in Florida following the outbreak of the Cuban War of Independence. To counter the uprising, a Spanish war vessel was dispatched to Santo Domingo to monitor the activities and movements of Cuban revolutionary leaders José Martí and Máximo Gómez.

The Salt Lake Herald informó sobre las celebraciones entre los expatriados cubanos en Florida tras el estallido de la Guerra de Independencia de Cuba. Para contrarrestar la sublevación, se despachó un buque de guerra español hacia Santo Domingo con el propósito de vigilar las actividades y desplazamientos de los líderes revolucionarios cubanos José Martí y Máximo Gómez.

depot. The Cubans here are having a big celebration in West Tampa. A hundred revolvers were fired at 1 o'clock. The children join in making the air resound with shouts of enthusiasm. The cigarmakers are to donate two days' work to the cause this week. The Spanish torpedo boat Nueva [illegible] went from here to San Domingo to watch the movements of Marti and Gomez. To Suppress [illegible]. Madrid, Feb. 26. At the conclusion of the special meeting of the cabinet last night, Minister of War General Dominguez announced that the government had authorized the issuing of a proclamation by the governor-general of Cuba to suppress the armed bands of brigands in the provinces of Matanzas and Puerto Principe. The minister denied that any serious disorder had occurred or that troops from Philippine Islands had been landed in Cuba. HAYWARD'S CASE. [illegible] Minneapolis, Feb. 26.
