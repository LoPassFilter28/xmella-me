The Washington times — 1926-10-08

The *Washington Times* reported that President Coolidge was scheduled to receive the new Spanish ambassador, Don Alejandro Padilla. In addition, the newly appointed ministers representing the Dominican Republic, Siam, and Albania were awaiting opportunities to present their credentials to the president.

El *Washington Times* informó que estaba previsto que el presidente Coolidge recibiera al nuevo embajador español, don Alejandro Padilla. Además, los recién nombrados ministros representantes de la República Dominicana, Siam y Albania aguardaban turno para presentar sus credenciales ante el mandatario.
