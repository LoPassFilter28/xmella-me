Armed with a document and an order from Secretary of the Navy Robeson, Babcock utilized U.S. warships to assist Baéz against his enemy Cabral to prevent annexation schemes from failing. Meanwhile, Davis Hatch acted as an agent for a New York salt company whose concessions were annulled by President Baéz, reconfirmed by Cabral, and affected by subsequent political shifts between the two leaders.

Munido de una credencial y una orden del secretario de Marina Robeson, Babcock recurrió a buques de guerra de los EE. UU. para apuntalar a Báez contra su adversario Cabral, resuelto a impedir el fracaso de los designios anexionistas. Por entonces, Davis Hatch fungía de apoderado de una compañía salinera neoyorquina cuyos privilegios fueron cancelados por Báez, revalidados por Cabral y sujetos a las continuas vicisitudes políticas entre ambos caudillos.
