Passage summaryResumen del pasaje

The *New York Herald* reported that the revolution in St. Domingo, which was initially confined to the province of Cibao, had spread so extensively that the entire country was now in rebellion. Evidence of the insurrection's growth included a proclamation by Captain General Don Felipe Rivero declaring a blockade of the entire coast due to multiple provinces declaring rebellion, as well as news that the town of Puerto Plata had been completely destroyed.

*The New York Herald* informó que la revolución en Santo Domingo, circunscrita inicialmente a la región del Cibao, se había propagado de tal forma que el país entero se hallaba en armas. Prueba del avance insurreccional era la proclama del capitán general don Felipe Rivero decretando el bloqueo de todas las costas debido a la adhesión de múltiples provincias a la revuelta, junto con noticias que daban cuenta de la completa destrucción de la ciudad de Puerto Plata.
