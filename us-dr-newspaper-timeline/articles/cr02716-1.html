A brief dispatch from Cape Haytien reports that a Dominican cruiser was expected to arrive there shortly with President Morales of Santo Domingo on board. The reason for the president's visit to the port was not known.

Un breve despacho desde Cabo Haitiano informa que se esperaba que un crucero dominicano llegara allí en breve con el presidente Morales de Santo Domingo a bordo. No se conocía el motivo de la visita del presidente a ese puerto.
