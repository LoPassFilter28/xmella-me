SummaryResumen

The *Press and daily Dakotaian* reported that negotiations for a treaty of commercial reciprocity with the Dominican Republic were successfully concluded and would soon be submitted to the Senate. The newspaper also noted that officers were instructed to prevent neutrality infractions near the Cuban coast, while reporting on unresolved matters with the Ottoman Empire and the lack of means for participating in international exhibitions.

El *Press and daily Dakotaian* comunicó la feliz conclusión de las negociaciones de un tratado de reciprocidad comercial con la República Dominicana, el cual sería elevado a la brevedad al Senado. Añadió que se giraron instrucciones para atajar infracciones a la neutralidad en aguas cubanas, dando cuenta además de asuntos no resueltos con el Imperio Otomano y de la insuficiencia de fondos para concurrir a certámenes internacionales.
