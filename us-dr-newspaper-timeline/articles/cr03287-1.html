1946-12-27 · East Hartford, Conn.

The East Hartford gazette — 1946-12-27

The East Hartford Gazette reviewed major news highlights of late 1946, noting that the Dominican Republic suffered severe losses from a violent earthquake and tidal wave in August. The retrospective also cited international incidents including the desertion of the steamship *American Farmer* and the Yugoslav shootdown of two American airplanes. Additional domestic events covered labor strikes, price controls, and the anniversary of the U.S. Army Air Forces.

El *East Hartford Gazette* presentó una recopilación de los sucesos más descollantes de finales de 1946, destacando que la República Dominicana sufrió desastrosas pérdidas a consecuencia del terremoto y maremoto registrados en agosto. La síntesis informativa rememoró también episodios internacionales como el abandono del vapor *American Farmer* y el derribo por fuerzas yugoslavas de dos aeronaves militares estadounidenses. En el ámbito interno, recapituló jornadas de huelga obrera, controversias por los controles de precios y el aniversario de las Fuerzas Aéreas del Ejército norteamericano.

AUGUST. August 2nd was the 39th birthday of the United States Army Air Forces. On August 4th Protestant leaders from eight nations, gathered in England, favored joint action with the Catholics on world problems. Athens, Tennessee, saw ex-Gls drive the sheriff elect out of town and seize control of the government. The Dominican Republic suf fered great loss in a tidal wave and violent earthquake. The steamship American Farmer was deserted at sea and became a problem for international liti gation.
