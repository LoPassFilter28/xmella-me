The Atlanta Daily World reported that Haitian Ambassador Joseph D. Charles accused Dominican President Rafael Trujillo of conspiring with Col. Astrel Roland to incite insurrection against Haiti via broadcasts from radio station La Voz Dominicana. Charles delivered a formal protest to Dominican Ambassador Luis Francisco Thomen, charging that the incendiary radio appeals violated the June 9 peace accord between the two countries. Thomen countered that Haiti was conducting a malicious propaganda campaign, while Charles affirmed that calm prevailed in Haiti under a state of siege.

Thomen replicó que Haití llevaba a cabo una maliciosa campaña de propaganda, mientras que Charles afirmó que reinaba la calma en Haití bajo un estado de sitio.
