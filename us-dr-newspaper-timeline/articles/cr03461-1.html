The Omaha Daily Bee reported that departing United States Minister Powell assured the Dominican president that the United States had no intention of annexing the republic and strongly supported its sovereignty under the Monroe Doctrine. Powell urged the government to prevent foreign intervention by honoring debts to foreign creditors, protecting foreign commercial concessions, and governing transparently. The article added that the Dominican gunboat Independencia transported government troops to assault Monte Cristi, which remained held by insurgents.

El Omaha Daily Bee informó que el saliente ministro de los Estados Unidos Powell aseguró al presidente dominicano que los Estados Unidos no tenían intención de anexar la república y respaldaban firmemente su soberanía bajo la Doctrina Monroe. Powell instó al gobierno a prevenir intervenciones extranjeras honrando las deudas con los acreedores externos, resguardando las concesiones comerciales foráneas y gobernando con transparencia. El artículo añadió que el cañonero dominicano Independencia transportó tropas gubernamentales para asaltar Monte Cristi, ciudad que continuaba en manos de los insurgentes.

the future of Santo Domingo. The president expressed hope that the revolutions would cease and that the Dominican family would become united. Neither the government nor the people of the United States had tha least idea of annexation. They desired that San Domingo should remain a sovereign Independent power, and in this the republic would have the support of the United States government. Mr. Powell said his government would not consent to any portion of Domingo passing under the control of any foreign power. The future of the republic rested largely On the keeping of all obligations toward foreign creditors, on respecting the concessions the government made to foreigners, on developing the country's resources and on passage of such laws as would bring prosperity. These considerations and the taking of the whole people into the confidence of the government would be the greatest guard against revolution and anarchy. The minister assured the president that the American people would be at his side If he remained steadfast to the principles of a good republic. Mr. Powell will leave tomorrow. His departure is regretted by all classes. The United States cruiser Detroit returned last night from visiting northern ports. The Dominican warship Independencia has left her with troops for Monte Cristi, which Is still In the hands of the revolutionists. THINK TOBACCONIST [illegible]. LONDON, May [illegible].
