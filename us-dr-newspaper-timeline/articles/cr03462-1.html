A late arrival from St. Domingo announced that the Dominican government was making great preparations to repel the expected invasion by Haytiens under the black Emperor Faustin I. The Dominican fleet, consisting of one ship, one bark, three brigs and a large number of schooners, was being put in order; the fortifications were undergoing repairs, and every male inhabitant capable of bearing arms had been enrolled. Santa Ana has been placed in chief command of the army. The ratification of a treaty with England, in which that nation acknowledges the independence from Hayti of the Republic of St. Domingo, was celebrated by a public dinner, at which the President of the Republic presided, and the British Consul was present. The latter made a speech, in which he said that the English would not interfere in the coming contest between them and Hayti. Neither the American Consul nor any of the American shipmasters were present at the dinner.
