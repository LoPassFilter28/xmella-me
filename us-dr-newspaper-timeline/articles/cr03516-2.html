The Secretary of State has received a dispatch from the United States Consul at Puerto Plata, dated December 13, saying that the revolution in the northern part of San Domingo had been suppressed, and that many of the participants had been arrested. A report in circulation in Berlin, on the 2d, that the Emperor had conferred the order of the Black Eagle upon Herr Von Puttkamer, created much excitement among the Liberals. Eleven vessels bound either to or from Philadelphia, or owned in that city, went to the bottom of the ocean, with a loss of 115 lives, during the year [illegible], while nine more were wrecked, but fortunately without loss of life. The property swallowed up with these twenty vessels was valued at $350,000. The Americans awarded Legion of Honor decorations at Paris are Messrs. Scribner, of the Agricultural Department at Washington; [Manson?], a vine-grower of Texas, and [Jaeger?], of Missouri. The Australian system of voting went into effect in Massachusetts with the opening of the year, and the authorities of that State have requests from all over the country for copies of the law. It is reported that Lady Salisbury, wife of the British Premier, will present Mrs. Joseph Chamberlain to the Queen at the first drawing-room of the season.
