From: A Republic Looking for RecognitionDe: Una república en busca de reconocimiento · 1844–1861

News from Port-au-Prince reports that troops were dispatched against an insurrection, some forty leaders were capturedNoticias de Port-au-Prince informan que se enviaron tropas contra una insurrección, unos cuarenta líderes fueron capturados

News from Port-au-Prince reports that troops were dispatched against an insurrection, some forty leaders were captured, twenty were summarily shot, and nine of twelve who were tried were condemned to the same fate but awaited the President's orders. The item also says the citizens had been ordered on duty, business had halted, and a strong Haitian force by sea and land had been collected for the purpose of attacking the Dominicans.Noticias de Puerto Príncipe informan que se enviaron tropas contra una insurrección, que unos cuarenta líderes fueron capturados, veinte fueron fusilados sumariamente y nueve de doce que fueron juzgados fueron condenados a la misma suerte pero esperaban las órdenes del presidente. La nota también dice que los ciudadanos habían sido llamados a servicio, el comercio se había detenido y una fuerte fuerza haitiana por mar y tierra había sido reunida con el propósito de atacar a los dominicanos.

The Daily Union. Washington, October 21, 1845.
