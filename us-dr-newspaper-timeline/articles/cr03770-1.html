SummaryResumen

Based on advices from Turk's Island to May 10th, the newspaper reported that a strong body of Dominican and Haytian revolutionists were well supplied with Remington rifles and ammunition, with the supposition that they intended hostilities. Additionally, advices from Porto Rico stated that the *Union Mercantil* professed to have information that the United States and Denmark had agreed to alienate the Danish Islands for 30,000,000 francs.

Conforme a noticias remitidas desde las islas Turcas hasta el 10 de mayo, el periódico dio cuenta de que una nutrida partida de insurrectos dominicanos y haitianos se hallaba bien pertrechada de fusiles Remington y parque, conjeturándose que fraguaban hostilidades inmediatas. Comunicaciones de Puerto Rico aseguraban paralelamente que *La Unión Mercantil* aseveraba conocer un concierto secreto entre los Estados Unidos y Dinamarca para ceder las Antillas Danesas por 30.000.000 de francos.
