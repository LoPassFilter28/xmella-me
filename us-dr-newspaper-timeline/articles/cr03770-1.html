West Indies.

HOSTILITIES IMMINENT--DANISH ISLANDS--VENEZUELA RISING.

HAVANA, May 23d.--Advices from Turk's Island to the 10th inst. represent that a strong body of Dominican and Haytian revolutionists there are well supplied with Remington rifles and all kinds of ammunition. It is supposed that they intend hostilities against San Domingo.

Advices from Porto Rico state that the Boletino Mercantil professes to have information that the United States and Denmark have agreed to the alienation of the Danish Islands for the sum of 30,000,000 francs.
