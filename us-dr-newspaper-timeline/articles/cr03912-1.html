A short commercial note reports that several prosperous townsUna breve nota comercial informa que varios pueblos prósperos

A short commercial note reports that several prosperous towns in the Puerto Plata consular district, covering the northern half of the Dominican Republic, lack banking institutions. Ranked in order of importance, those towns are La Vega with 5,500 inhabitants, San Francisco de Macoris with 5,000, Moca with 4,000, Monte Cristi with 3,000, and Salcedo and Samana with about 2,500 each. The passage breaks off mid-sentence while noting that Samana and Monte Cristi are seaports, whereas Moca, La Vega, Salcedo, and San Francisco de Macoris lie inland.Una breve nota comercial informa que varias poblaciones prósperas del distrito consular de Puerto Plata, que comprende la mitad norte de la República Dominicana, carecen de instituciones bancarias. En orden de importancia, dichas localidades son La Vega con 5,500 habitantes, San Francisco de Macorís con 5,000, Moca con 4,000, Monte Cristi con 3,000, y Salcedo y Samaná con unos 2,500 cada una. El pasaje se interrumpe a mitad de frase al señalar que Samaná y Monte Cristi son puertos marítimos, mientras que Moca, La Vega, Salcedo y San Francisco de Macorís son poblaciones del interior.

New-York Tribune. New York [N.Y.], June 30, 1918. Page 12.
