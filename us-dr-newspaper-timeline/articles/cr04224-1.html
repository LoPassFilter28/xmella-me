Long in Government The late Dominican dictator, Generalissimo Rafael Trujillo, exerted various forms of economic pressure to bring the country’s influential citizens into his service. For the first 10 years of the Trujillo regime, Dr. Bonnelly stayed clear of any association with the govern ment, but in the 1940s he joined the official family. Among the high posts he held were Secretary of the Interior, Ambassador to Spain and to Venezuela. He was also rector of Santo Domingo University, a job in which he still takes pride since it gave him a chance to teach and direct what Dominicans consider the hemisphere’s oldest institution of learning. During this career, Dr. Bonnelly says he was able to avoid identification with the regime except in a purely professional capacity.
