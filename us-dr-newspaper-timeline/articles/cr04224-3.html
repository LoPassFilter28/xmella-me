Evening star — 1962-01-20

SourceFuente: Evening star, Washington, D.C., 1962-01-20

The *Evening Star* reported that ousted military leader General Pedro Rodríguez Echavarría was expected to be sent abroad rather than harshly punished, following the counter-coup in Santo Domingo. Former President Joaquín Balaguer remained under guard at his residence, asserting that he had previously resisted resigning precisely to avoid provoking a military seizure of power.

El *Evening Star* informó que se esperaba que el derrocado líder militar, general Pedro Rodríguez Echavarría, fuera enviado al extranjero en lugar de recibir un castigo severo, tras el contragolpe en Santo Domingo. El expresidente Joaquín Balaguer permanecía bajo custodia en su residencia, sosteniendo que se había resistido previamente a renunciar precisamente para evitar provocar una usurpación militar del poder.
