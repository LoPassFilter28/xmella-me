The Washington Daily News reported on Fourth of July congratulatory messages transmitted to the United States from foreign rulers and diplomats. President Rafael Trujillo of the Dominican Republic praised the occasion as a glorious day of American liberty, alongside greetings sent by monarchs and leaders from Europe, Asia, and Latin America.

El Washington Daily News informó sobre los mensajes de felicitación con motivo del Cuatro de Julio transmitidos a Estados Unidos por mandatarios y diplomáticos extranjeros. El presidente Rafael Trujillo, de la República Dominicana, encomió la efeméride como un día glorioso para la libertad estadounidense, sumándose a los saludos enviados por monarcas y estadistas de Europa, Asia y América Latina.

KING FUAD of Egypt sent a long, flowery wire of congratulations [illegible] and President-Dictator Gomez of Venezuela, who is not reputed a great admirer of independence, dispatched a flamboyant cable about "the day of glory of your friendly nation.' President-Dictator Trujillo of the Dominican Republic referred to "this glorious day of American liberty.-' [illegible] King Alexander of Yugoslavia addressed a brief, personal message to The President of the Republic.'' Mr. Charalambos Simopoulos who only had to pay the charges from his legation in Washington to the State Department). to Secretary of State Hull. The shortest wire was sent by Don Miguel Cruchaga. who used to be Chilean Ambassador here and is now Minister of Foreign Relations. It read: "Accept, Excellency, sincere congratulations." President Masaryk of Czechoslovakia addressed his wire to "President of U. S. A." His Imperial Majesty, Emperor Hirohito of Japan, forwarded his message to "The president. The White House." Ambassador Troyanovsky of Soviet Russia wrote an informal letter to Secretary Hull and President Kalinin sent "best greetings" to President Roosevelt. King Carol of Rumania sent "from all my heart" best wishes for "the Great Nation of the United States." Czar Boris of Bulgaria wired: "I send you Mister President the most heartiest congratulations."
