Execution of Revolutionists — Baez Snubbed by a Spanish Man-of-War — Cabral Inactive. TELEGRAM TO THE NEW YORK HERALD. Puerto Plata, March 1, 1872. President Baez on the 22d ult. retook the towns of [Savaneta?] and [Guayubin?] and executed seventeen revolutionists. Baez is again powerful. The Dominican authorities seized the Spanish schooner Julia on suspicion of smuggling and unrigged her. They subsequently restored her sails and rudder at the demand of the commander of the Spanish ship-of-war Tornado; but Baez rejected his additional demand for $10,003 damages, and the Tornado left without her usual salute. Baez is infuriated at the slight. Cabral has 2,000 men, but remains inactive for want of funds and ammunition. Baez, with 800 men, intends to attack him
