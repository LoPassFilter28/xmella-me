It was believed at St. Domingo that the U. S. Government had recognized the Dominican republic, and that a U. S. ship of war, with a consul on board, would soon arrive at St. Domingo.

The story prevailed, also, that a Spanish squadron from Cuba was coming to protect the Dominicans against the Haytiens.
