Ambassador of Cuba and Senora de Ferrara, the Minister of Honduras, Senor Bogran; the Minister of Venezuela and Senora de Grisanti, the Minister of Ecuador and Senora de Zaldumbide, the charge d'affaires of Paraguay and Mme. Ramirez, the charge d'affaires of Mexico, Senor Luis Padilla-Nervo; Dr. Leo S. Rowe, director general of the Pan-American Union; the chief of naval operations and Mrs. Charles F. Hughes, the secretary to the President and Mrs. Everett Sanders, the chief of the Latin American division of the State Department and Mrs. Stokeley Morgan, the president of the Dominican Senate, Dr. Gustavo A. Diaz, and Dominican delegate to the conference; the newly appointed Dominican Minister to Paris, Senor M. L. Vasquez, and Senora de Vasquez; Senor Felix Cordova Davila, the secretary of the Dominican Republic legation, Senor Francisco de Moya; Senorita Blanca Diaz, Senorita Grecia Diaz, Mr. and Mrs. B. Gwynn Dent, Mr. and Mrs. John T. Vance, Jr.; Senora de Sanchez Latour, Mme. Louisa Soyez, Mrs. Claude B. Mayo, Mr. and Mrs. H. S. Reeside and Senor Benjamin Cohen of Chile. The Minister of the Netherlands and Mme. van Royen have with them for the holidays their younger son, Mr. Robert van Royen. Their elder son, Mr. Herman van Royen, will arrive tomorrow or Sunday to be with them for the holiday season. [illegible]
