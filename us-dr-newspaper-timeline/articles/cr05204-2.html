Passage summaryResumen del pasaje

*The New York Freeman* published correspondence from H. C. C. Astwood, the United States Consul in Santo Domingo, who reflected on American national politics and party dynamics. Astwood discussed his recovery from illness and provided commentary on recent Dominican elections and the revolutionary unrest that followed.
