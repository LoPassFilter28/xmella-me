The Evening Star reported that Dr. Sevilla of Nicaragua was scheduled to fly to the Dominican Republic to present his credentials to President Rafael Trujillo as the Nicaraguan Minister in Ciudad Trujillo. Sevilla was staying at the Roosevelt Hotel in Washington alongside legal adviser Leopoldo Arguello Gil before departing. The report also covered social and diplomatic activities involving Cuban and Venezuelan embassy officials.

El Evening Star comunicó que el Dr. Sevilla, representante de Nicaragua, tenía proyectado viajar por vía aérea a la República Dominicana a fin de hacer entrega de sus credenciales al presidente Rafael Trujillo como ministro nicaragüense en Ciudad Trujillo. Previo a su despegue, Sevilla se alojaba en el Roosevelt Hotel de Washington junto a su consultor jurídico Leopoldo Argüello Gil. La nota también pasó revista a reuniones de carácter social protagonizadas por diplomáticos adscritos a las embajadas de Cuba y Venezuela.

tary of the Presidency of Nicaragua, and Senor Leopoldo Arguello Gil, legal adviser to the Nicaraguan government, are accompanied by their wives and are staying at the Roosevelt Hotel until their departure Thursday. Dr. Sevilla will leave by plane Tuesday for the Dominican Republic, to present his credentials to President Trujillo as Nicaraguan Minister in Ciudad Trujillo. He will return April 26. The Cuban Ambassador and Senora de Belt have returned from the Hyde Park ceremonies, and while in New York were guests of honor at a luncheon given at the Waldorf-Astoria by the Pan-American Society and the Cuban Chamber of Commerce. The Venezuelan Ambassador, Dr. Alfredo Machado-Hernandez, and Senora de Machado-Hernandez have with them at the Embassy their son and daughter-in-law, Dr. Alfredo Machado-Gomez and Senora de Machado-Gomez. Dr. Machado-Gomez has been appointed Second Secretary of the Embassy and he and his bride of eight months will be an attractive addition to the younger married set in the Capital. A native of Caracas, Dr. Machado-Gomez attended school in Paris and received the degrees of bachelor of arts and doctor of political science at the University of Venezuela.
