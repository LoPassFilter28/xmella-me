The New-York Tribune reported on escalating authoritarian measures by President Heureaux, who restricted foreign travel and arrested suspected political opponents across the country. Following Heureaux's armed seizure of $60,000 from a French bank, the bank’s Dominican attorney, Jacinto de Castro Peynado, was imprisoned for his arguments during legal proceedings against the government. Additionally, Dominican troops blocked the son of a former Spanish consul from landing in Santo Domingo, threatening him with rifles until he departed on the steamer.

El New-York Tribune informó sobre la escalada de medidas autoritarias adoptadas por el presidente Heureaux, quien restringió los viajes al extranjero y arrestó a presuntos opositores políticos en todo el país. Tras la incautación armada por parte de Heureaux de 60.000 dólares de un banco francés, el abogado dominicano de la entidad bancaria, Jacinto de Castro Peynado, fue encarcelado a causa de sus alegatos en los procedimientos judiciales contra el gobierno. Además, tropas dominicanas impidieron el desembarco en Santo Domingo del hijo de un excónsul español, amenazándolo con fusiles hasta que abandonó el lugar a bordo del vapor.

It will be remembered that in last March Heureaux took possession by force of the French bank in San Domingo, and took therefrom $60,000, which he claimed was his. An account of the affair was published in the New-York papers at the time. The bank brought suit against the Government and engaged a Dominican lawyer named Pinado as counsel. It is now said that Pinado has been imprisoned for what he said in the course of the trial, and will probably be shot. On January 15 Gomez Pintado, the son of a former Consul of Spain at San Domingo City, arrived at that port on board a French steamer. When his father was consul Gomez was an officer [illegible], told that he would be in danger if he persisted, and a body of twenty soldiers marched down on the pier with loaded rifles to resist his landing. There is little doubt but that he would have been shot if he had attempted to go ashore. He remained on the steamer, therefore, until she sailed. It is supposed that the Spanish Government will take official notice of the affair. TRICKY SUBURBAN COMMUTERS CAUGHT.
