The Atlanta Daily World reported that Haitian Ambassador Joseph D. Charles exposed an alleged conspiracy involving Col. Astrel Roland, Belgian national Johanna Verbracken, and high Dominican officials including President Rafael Trujillo. Charles declared that Dominican support for Roland was considered an act of hostility and condemned Roland's subversive radio addresses broadcast from a Dominican station owned by Trujillo's brother, Col. Arismendi Trujillo. The broadcasts vowed to liberate Haiti, precipitating official diplomatic protests.
