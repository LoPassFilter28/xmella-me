Passage summaryResumen del pasaje

According to a June 3 dispatch from New York via the steamer *Prince Albert*, the Queen of Spain has signed the decree for the annexation of San Domingo. The report also notes general European and market updates, including Liverpool dates to May 21 and statements from American Minister to Russia, Mr. Clay, regarding the American struggle.
