Dispatches from Port-au-Prince indicate, in the paper's phrase, a war of extermination between the Haitians and Dominicans. It says the armistice between Soulouque and the Dominicans was to expire on the 3d, and Soulouque was collecting an army to renew the war, which is to commence after one month's notice from the expiration of the armistice.

Despachos de Puerto Príncipe indican, en palabras del periódico, una guerra de exterminio entre haitianos y dominicanos. Dice que el armisticio entre Soulouque y los dominicanos expiraba el día 3, y que Soulouque reunía un ejército para reanudar la guerra, que comenzaría un mes después del aviso de expiración del armisticio.
