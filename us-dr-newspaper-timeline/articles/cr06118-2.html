Independence, Recognition, and Samaná SchemesIndependencia, Reconocimiento y Proyectos de Samaná · 1844–1861

On February 27, 1844, Los Trinitarios proclaimed Dominican independence. American press coverage surged with reports on the new republic, repeated Haitian invasion threats, diplomatic recognition appeals, and southern schemes to secure Samaná Bay as a naval base.El 27 de febrero de 1844, Los Trinitarios proclamaron la independencia. La prensa estadounidense cubrió la nueva república, las invasiones haitianas, llamamientos de reconocimiento diplomático y proyectos para adquirir la Bahía de Samaná.

SANTO DOMINGO. Historical and Descriptive Sketch of the Dominican Republic. The cultivation of tobacco, introduced some years since into this part of the island, has given occasion to a certain degree of industry and activity in Santiago and its neighborhood. Santiago, which by virtue of its elevation possesses a fine climate, can boast quite a number of handsome new buildings. Its population is greater than that of its southern rival, and it is the policy of the prevailing party in the late civil war to make it the seat of government. The article contains many facts not generally known, with several original engravings.
