1939-07-15 · Washington, D.C.

The Washington daily news — 1939-07-15

SourceFuente: The Washington daily news, Washington, D.C., 1939-07-15

Columnist Raymond Clapper in the *Washington Daily News* criticized Representative Hamilton Fish for praising General Trujillo's regime as a "golden age" during a New York dinner. Clapper cited first-hand reporting by Quentin Reynolds of *Collier's*, who investigated the border atrocities and observed victims mutilated by Dominican forces. Clapper maintained that any prosperity Trujillo claimed was purchased through the murder of thousands of obscure Haitian peasants.

El columnista Raymond Clapper, en *The Washington Daily News*, censuró al congresista Hamilton Fish por alabar el régimen del general Trujillo calificándolo de «era dorada» durante un banquete en Nueva York. Clapper citó testimonios directos de Quentin Reynolds de *Collier's*, quien investigó las atrocidades en la frontera y constató las mutilaciones de víctimas infligidas por tropas dominicanas. Clapper aseveró que cualquier prosperidad exhibida por Trujillo se pagó con la sangre y el exterminio de miles de humildes campesinos haitianos.
