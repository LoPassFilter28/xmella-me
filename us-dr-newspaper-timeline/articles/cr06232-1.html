The paper reports rejoicings at Port-au-Prince a few days before September 14 for a victory the Haitian navy had gained over the Dominicans, taking two small schooners from them.

El periódico informa de celebraciones en Puerto Príncipe unos días antes del 14 de septiembre por una victoria que la marina haitiana había obtenido sobre los dominicanos, capturándoles dos pequeñas goletas.

Tuae Mormox War.—The lilinois Siate Reg

For Texas.—A company of U. 8. troops, from

Fish River, under command of Lieut. H. D.

Grafion,arrived yesterday morning, in the steamer

Penobscot, and are to embark in the brig Coche

co, from this port for Galveston Boston Post.

The cars on the Springfield and Hartford line

The engine was smashed to pieces; but beyond
