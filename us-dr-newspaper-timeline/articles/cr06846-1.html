New-York tribune — 1881-05-24

SourceFuente: New-York tribune, New York [N.Y.], 1881-05-24
https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn83030214/1881-05-24/ed-1/?sp=1
revolution_unrest

The *New-York Tribune* reported on May 24, 1881, that certain Dominican and Haitian revolutionists had assembled on Turk’s Island with the intention of opening hostilities against Santo Domingo. Additionally, the newspaper noted a revolutionary rising reported from Venezuela.

El *New-York Tribune* informó el 24 de mayo de 1881 de que varios sediciosos dominicanos y haitianos se habían concentrado en las islas Turcas con el propósito de romper hostilidades contra Santo Domingo. Consignó además el diario un movimiento insurreccional denunciado desde Venezuela.
