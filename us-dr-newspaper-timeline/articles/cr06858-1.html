The Washington Times reported that the Dominican Legation in Washington would not hold a celebration for the forty-third birthday of Dominican President Rafael Leonidas Trujillo due to the absence of the Dominican Minister and his wife. The Minister and Señora de Brache were scheduled to return to the capital on the following Saturday.

The Washington Times informó que la Legación dominicana en Washington no celebraría el cuadragésimo tercer cumpleaños del presidente dominicano Rafael Leónidas Trujillo debido a la ausencia del ministro dominicano y su esposa. Estaba previsto que el ministro y la señora de Brache regresaran a la capital el sábado siguiente.

at 222 West Seventy-seventh St. Due to the absence from the city of the Minister and his wife, there will be no celebration at the Legation in honor of the forty-third anniversary of the birth of the President of the Dominican Republic, Senor Rafael Leonidas Trujillo. The Minister and Senora de Brache will return to the Capital on Saturday. At the White House President Roosevelt arrived in the Capital this morning after a month's absence during which he travelled across country to the San Diego Exposition and returned aboard the U. S. S. Houston. He was met at the station by Mrs. Roosevelt and members of the Cabinet. Mrs. Roosevelt attended the chrysanthemum exhibit this morning at the Agriculture Department where she was received by Mrs. Henry A. Wallace, wife of the Secretary of Agriculture. Envoy Returns Tomorrow The Ambassador of Argentina and Mme. Espil are expected to return to the Embassy sometime tomorrow from Atlantic City, where they have been for the past several days. They have been stopping at the Ritz-Carlton Hotel. Mrs. George A. Gorden, wife of the American Minister to Haiti, is at the Ambassador Hotel in New York city. Mr. Gordon expects to join her in about 10 days. To Wed Nov. 28 Mr. and Mrs. William Thomas Edgar have announced the engagement of their niece. Miss Evelyn D. Taylor, to Mr. Carl E.
