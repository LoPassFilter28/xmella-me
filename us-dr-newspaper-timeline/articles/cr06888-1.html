NEWS FROM HAVANA. DEATH OF THE BRITISH CONSUL GENERAL. News from St. Domingo and Venezuela, &c., &c., &c.

The steamship Roanoke, Captain Drew, from Havana on the 29th ult., arrived at this port yesterday morning. We are indebted to Mr. J. S. de Agostini, of the Roanoke, for Havana papers to the date of her departure.

The British Consul General at Havana, Mr. Joseph T. Crawford, died on the 27th ult. He had been in the service of the British government for many years, and was highly respected by all classes in Havana.

The news from St. Domingo is to the 18th ult. The Spanish troops had been successful in several small encounters with the Dominicans.

The news from Venezuela is to the 14th ult. The country was quiet.

The markets at Havana were dull. Exchange on London, 14 a 15 per cent premium; on New York, 1 a 2 per cent discount.
