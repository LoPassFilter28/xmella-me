THE HAYTIENS.—The schooner Howard, from St. Domingo 27th ult., arrived at this port this morning. The captain, Wright, reports that the Dominicians are actively preparing to meet the Haytiens. Santanna has been placed in chief command, and is busily engaged in forming an army. They are plentifully supplied with arms and ammunition.
