By LOUIS JAY HEATH

United Press Staff Correspondent

Haitian and Dominican politicians and revolutionists were charged with malicious distortion of a “small incident” which took place early in October near the Dominican-Haitian border, in a formal communique issued by Dominican Minister Andres Pastoriza here today.

Haitian officials have charged that the so-called “small incident” referred to by Pastoriza consisted of “brutal killings of from 3000 to 5000 Haitian nationals by Dominican soldiers and police.” At the request of the Haitian government the United States, Mexico and Cuba have tendered their good offices to mediate the incident. To date the Dominican government has not accepted the tender and has sent special envoys to all three capitols to present the Dominican case.

The Pastoriza communique, addressed to editors, stated that the Dominican government already considered the “incident closed.”

USED BY ENEMIES

“The small incident . . . between Dominican citizens and Haitian citizens who were maintaining illegal residence in certain zones of the Dominican Republic, or who were violently entering the Dominican Republic,” the communique said, “is being used by political enemies of President Trujillo (of the Dominican Republic) and by Haitian elements interested in covering up the political unrest existing for some time in the Republic of Haiti. . . .”
