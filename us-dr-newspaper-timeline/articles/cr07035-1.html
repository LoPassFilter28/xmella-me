'Rebels killed in Santo Domingo': Nené Cepín, one-armed revolutionary general, and six followers were killed at Guayubín, where he had gone to confer with Governor Camacho of Monte Cristi after taking the field in the Dajabón smuggling district.

«Rebeldes muertos en Santo Domingo»: Nené Cepín, general revolucionario manco, y seis seguidores fueron muertos en Guayubín, adonde había ido a conferenciar con el gobernador Camacho de Monte Cristi tras alzarse en la zona de contrabando de Dajabón.

Cepin was one of the most noted revolutionists in the island. He took sides with Morales in the last outbreak. After the latter's defeat he received guaranty of safety, but with Navarro, took the field in the Monte Christi district with a small party, presumably to assist smugglers at Dajabon, on the Haitian frontier. No duties ever had been paid at Dajabon, tho large quantities of merchandise are received by the customs officers. Cepin visited the town and declared all goods there contraband. While Cepin and his party were fighting many goods were removed inland. After negotiations lasting some days Cepin and Navarro agreed to lay down arms. Cepin was promised the office of military commandant at Dajabon. He went to Guanyubin on a friendly visit to arrange terms. 24th Annual Reduced-Price Sale. The Great Plymouth Clothing House. MILLIONAIRE'S SON TO BECOME A CLERK. Journal Special Service. Chicago, March 10. — Stuyvesant Fish, president of the Illinois Central railroad, and his son, Stuyvesant Fish, Jr., are in Chicago. The son has announced his intention of taking a clerkship in the Illinois Central offices at $60 a month. He purposes to learn the railroad business from the ground up. Young Mr. Fish arrived from New York with his father in a palatial private car. He put up in one of the finest suites on the parlor floor of the Annex and took breakfast yesterday at the Chicago club. This is not the usual program of a working man.
