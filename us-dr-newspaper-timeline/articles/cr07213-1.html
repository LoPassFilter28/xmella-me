'Join Jiménez': five generals - Pablo Reyes, Ramón Pacheco and others - have taken the field in Santo Domingo for Juan Isidro Jiménez, occupying the plains and towns toward Monte Cristi, while General Guerrier, the government commander there, loses half his troops to the enemy and faces capitulation.

«Únanse a Jiménez»: cinco generales - Pablo Reyes, Ramón Pacheco y otros - se han alzado en Santo Domingo por Juan Isidro Jiménez, ocupando las llanuras y los pueblos hacia Monte Cristi, mientras el general Guerrier, comandante gubernamental allí, pierde la mitad de sus tropas a manos del enemigo y enfrenta la capitulación.

AS WASHINGTON VIEWS IT. WASHINGTON, [illegible] — Notwithstanding the growing turbulence in San Domingo, as disclosed in Press dispatches, neither the State nor Navy Departments had received anything up to the close of office hours today to show that our officials there were apprehensive over the developments. The authorities here are proceeding on the theory that the existing order of affairs is regular, and this will continue to be the course observed. Only in the event that the insurrection becomes strong enough to establish a de facto form of government is there any likelihood that its claim could be taken into consideration as against the de jure government.
