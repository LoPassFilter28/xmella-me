News briefs include the Dajabón dispatch: Liriano's troops have deserted to Pacheco, the revolutionists hold the Yaque fords, and a Philadelphia report that San Domingo insurgents are seeking vessels to carry munitions.

Las notas breves incluyen el despacho de Dajabón: las tropas de Liriano han desertado hacia Pacheco, los revolucionarios dominan los vados del Yaque, y un informe de Filadelfia de que insurgentes de Santo Domingo buscan buques para transportar municiones.

The gunboat Yorktown subsequently bombarded San Fernando. General Francisco Liriano was sent from Monte Christi by the Dominican government of Hayti to attack General Ramon Pacheco at Dajabon, headquarters of the revolutionists. When he arrived in front of the enemy he was abandoned by his troops, who, without firing a shot, deserted and entered Pacheco's camp. The advance posts of the latter are at Las Aguas. The revolutionists are master of the ford in the river Yaque, thus cutting off communication between Monte Christi and the interior. The forces of Don Juan Isidro Jiminez, leader of the revolution, are being augmented every day. News from all parts of the republic are favorable to him. FIREMEN ELECTROCUTED. Their Truck Comes in Contact With A Live Wire. Omaha, Neb., August 10. — Four firemen lost their lives last night in a blaze on the upper floor of the Mercer Chemical company's building at Eleventh and Henry streets. The fire was small, the fatalities resulting from contact with a live wire. The dead are: Joseph Adams, lieutenant of No. 3 engine company; Otto Geiske, tillerman of big truck; George Benson, pipeman of No. 3 engine company; Charles Hopper, relief driver of No. 3 engine company.
