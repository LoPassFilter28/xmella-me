A morning news summary reports that Carlos F. Morales, the fugitive President of Santo Domingo, has been deposed by Congress and that Caceres is in control of the government. It is also reported that Jimenez has gone to Monte Cristi and will enter the fight for the presidency.

Un resumen de noticias de la mañana informa que Carlos F. Morales, el presidente fugitivo de Santo Domingo, ha sido depuesto por el Congreso y que Cáceres controla el gobierno. También se informa que Jiménez ha ido a Monte Cristi y que entrará en la lucha por la presidencia.

George at Croydon quoted from unpublished Board of Trade returns to show the fallacy of Mr. Chamberlain's proposals. Carlos F. Morales, the fugitive President of Santo Domingo, has been [illegible] by Congress, and Caceres is in control of the government; it is reported that Jiménez has gone to Monte Cristi and that he will enter the fight for the Presidency. Sir Edward Grey, according to «The Standard,” has announced that he will carry out the Moroccan policy of Lord Lansdowne. The steamer Carlisle, which met many misfortunes, sank after a fire on board at Saigon. Considerable military activity prevails at Manila, owing, it is said, to fears of possible complications with China. DOMESTIC—Eleven medals were awarded in Washington to survivors of the Bennington disaster. It was reported that the American representatives at the Moroccan conference had been instructed to insist on an open door and work for peace. It was believed at Albany that Speaker Wadsworth had nearly completed his slate; Assemblyman Moreland will be majority leader, it was said. Governor Higgins at Albany expressed the utmost confidence in John J. Merrill, chief corporation tax clerk in the Controller's office, against whom charges were made by Frank White, a lawyer. Governor Higgins at Albany said that he believed legislation would be passed this session giving cheaper gas to this city.
