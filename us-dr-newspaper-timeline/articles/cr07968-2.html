HAYTI.

By the arrival of the brig H. D. Brookman, at this port, from Port-au-Prince, we have later accounts from that island. The colored people who had fled from Port-au-Prince, had taken a stand at a place called 'Croix de Bouquet,' four leagues from the former place, and were secure for the present, in the hope that the army on the borders of the Dominican side of the island would come to their assistance, and enable them to remove from the Presidency the tyrant who has been the cause of such bloodshed. The Fort at Croix de Bouquet was immediately delivered up to the colored men that had retreated from Port-au-Prince, by the officer in command, who was a colored man. Soulouque, the President, was still at Port-au-Prince, and had not attempted to leave. Business was entirely at a stand, and the greatest distress prevailed among all classes. The H. D. Brookman was the only vessel in port when she left.
