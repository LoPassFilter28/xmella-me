Passage summaryResumen del pasaje

The *Turner County Herald* reported on December 11, 1884, that negotiations for a commercial reciprocity treaty with the Dominican Republic had been successfully concluded and would soon be submitted to the Senate. Additionally, the newspaper noted that certain citizen claims outside the Spanish-American claims commission's jurisdiction were remitted to diplomatic channels for urgent settlement, while ongoing diplomatic and commercial matters involving the Ottoman Empire, Venezuela, and Mexico were also addressed.

El *Turner County Herald* informó el 11 de diciembre de 1884 de la venturosa conclusión de las negociaciones de un tratado de reciprocidad comercial con la República Dominicana, pendiente de envío al Senado. Detalló igualmente el rotativo que ciertas demandas particulares ajenas a la comisión de reclamaciones hispano-estadounidense pasaron al fuero diplomático para su inaplazable resolución, abordándose a la par gestiones mercantiles y diplomáticas con el Imperio Otomano, Venezuela y México.
