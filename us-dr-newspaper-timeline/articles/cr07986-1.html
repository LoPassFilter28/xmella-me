The latest news from Hayti is to the 20th ultimo. The Emperor Soulouque still refused to acknowledge the independence of the Dominican republic, and would not agree to an exchange of prisoners. He had threatened the messengers who went to Port-au-Prince to effect the exchange that unless the Haytien flag was hoisted he would march against them in October next. Arrangements were making to meet him on the frontier, under General Santana, with a force of 6,000 men. The Dominicans are also busily employed repairing their fortifications.
