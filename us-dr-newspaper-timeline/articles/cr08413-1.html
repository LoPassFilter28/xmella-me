The Times published the diplomatic exchange between President McKinley and the newly accredited Dominican Minister Plenipotentiary during the presentation of credentials. The Dominican representative conveyed warm regards from his government and proposed celebrating a commercial treaty to strengthen economic ties. President McKinley warmly reciprocated, expressing American commitment to cordial friendship and expanded bilateral trade.

El Times publicó el intercambio diplomático entre el presidente McKinley y el recién acreditado ministro plenipotenciario dominicano durante la presentación de credenciales. El representante dominicano transmitió los cordiales saludos de su gobierno y propuso concertar un tratado comercial para fortalecer los vínculos económicos. El presidente McKinley correspondió con calidez, expresando el compromiso estadounidense con una amistad cordial y la ampliación del comercio bilateral.

Mr President I have the honor to place in your excellencys hands the letters credential by which the President of the Dominican Republic accredits me as Envoy Extraordinary and Minister Plenipotentiary near the Government of the United States The especial mission with which my Government has honored me Is a new proof of the sincere friendship which It cherishes toward the American people and of the desire which animates it to draw still more closely upon solid commercial foundations the fraternal bonds which happily have thus far united them The different productions of both countries as well as their neighborhood have made my Government believe that it would be of mutual advantage for both countries to celebrate a commercial treaty which might Increase the good and cordial relation which have united them I am especially charged to present to your excellency In the name of the President of the Dominican Republic his most sincere wishes as well as my own for your personal happiness and the expression of his enthusiastic admiration for the great Republic of the North The President in reply said Mr Minister It gives me pleasure to receive at your hands the letters of credence by which the President of the Dominican Republic accredits you as Envoy Extraordinary and Minister Plenipotentiary near this Government I am glad to recognize in this special mission a new proof of the friendship which unites the two Republics I cordially reciprocate the wishes of your Government for closer and still more advantageous commercial relations
