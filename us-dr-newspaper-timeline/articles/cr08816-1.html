Jimenez Confers With Richard—Settlement Expected To-Day.

SPECIAL BY CABLE.

Santo Domingo, Jan. 9.—(Copyright, 1900, by James Gordon Bennett.)—Admiral Richard of the French navy, with his staff, formally called upon President Jimenez this morning. No business was transacted. The French Consul was present. It is rumored on good authority that the French Consul has not been sustained by his Government, and that he has been asked for his resignation.

It is expected that official action will be taken to-morrow. Americans of this place have asked the United States not to allow France to dictate terms to the Dominican Government. An American cruiser is expected to arrive to-day. One is reported to have left Sanchez yesterday.

ORDERS TO THE MACHIAS.

REPUBLIC SPECIAL.

Washington, Jan. 9.—Instructions were to-day cabled to Commander L. C. Logan, commanding the Machias, directing him to promptly return to Santo Domingo.

In compliance with this instruction, it is presumed the Machias has left San Juan bound for the capital of Santo Domingo. Upon his arrival Commander Logan will consult with the American Minister relative to the protection of American interests. It was said at the State Department this evening that no appeal for the intervention of the United States had been received from Santo Domingo and it is hardly likely that this Government will interfere, in view of the fact that the Jimenez Government has collected by popular subscription the money necessary to pay the French claims. It is possible that Minister Powell may act as a mediator between the French fleet and President Jimenez, looking to compliance with the French demand for an apology, but now that the money has been collected [illegible] [illegible] believed that the worst is about over.
