The *Arizona Sun* published an official itinerary for Haitian President Paul Magloire's tour across the United States. His schedule included receptions and visits in New York, Fisk University in Nashville, Chicago, and Boston.

El *Arizona Sun* publicó un itinerario oficial de la gira del presidente haitiano Paul Magloire por Estados Unidos. Su agenda incluía recepciones y visitas en Nueva York, la Universidad Fisk en Nashville, Chicago y Boston.
