FROM HAVANA.

Late from St. Domingo—Baez Probably President.

HAVANA, Dec. 6.—Evening.—Senor Pujol is not at all reserved as to the commission which he bears. It is to close negotiations in relation to Samana. He will leave here to-morrow by the steamer Moro Castle for New York.

Gen. Cabral's position is thought to be desperate through the influence of the clergy and the fact that the Dominican troops have not been paid. Baez will probably be President within a few weeks.
