News from San Domingo reports that Señor Pujol will leave for New York by the steamer Moro Castle to close negotiations in relation to Samaná, and that part of President Cabral's cabinet has resigned, with Valverde, Curiel and Perdomo appointed to fill the vacancies. Congress has sanctioned a new civil code and a reorganization of the judiciary, the French loan has been disapproved, and extraordinary powers have been conferred on Cabral, while in the north the Baezist revolution under General Hungría is still making progress and the provinces named are tranquil.
