The Montgomery Advertiser reported that the Dominican gunboat Independencia captured two armed insurgent schooners and shelled suburban districts of the capital to dislodge revolutionary fighters. Government forces successfully retook Baní and San Cristóbal and repelled a major assault on Santo Domingo following an engagement at Haina. To isolate the insurrection, the government issued a decree closing six major coastal ports to foreign shipping, supported by naval patrols.

El Montgomery Advertiser informó que el cañonero dominicano Independencia capturó dos goletas insurgentes armadas y cañoneó distritos suburbanos de la capital para desalojar a los combatientes revolucionarios. Las fuerzas del gobierno recuperaron con éxito Baní y San Cristóbal y repelieron un asalto de envergadura contra Santo Domingo tras un combate en Haina. Para aislar la insurrección, el gobierno emitió un decreto clausurando seis puertos costeros principales al comercio marítimo exterior, apoyado por patrullajes navales.

San Domingo, Sunday, Jan. 3.—The gun boat Independence arrived here last night with two captured Dominican schooners In tow. The schooners carried arms and munitions of war Intended for coast defense. The Independence bombarded the suburbs of the city today with the object driving the revolutionists out. It is officially reported that [Bani?] and San Cristobal have been retaken by the government troops. There was a severe encounter between the government troops and the revolutionists at [Haina?], [with?] result favorable to the government. The government has issued a decree closing the ports of Asua de Compostella, San Pedro de Macoris, Samana, Sanches, Puerto Plata and Monte Cristo to take effect within twenty days. In the case of vessels coming from the Antilles within thirty days for vessels from American ports and within forty-five days for vessels from European ports. Armed schooners and gunboats will guard the closed ports. There was a severe attack on the city this evening but the troops managed to repulse the revolutionists. Commerce Is completely at a standstill. News from the interior cannot be obtained. Moody Changes Estimates. Washington, Jan. 6.—Secretary Moody with the view to asking Congress only for such sums as are actually necessary for the needs of the service, has reduced the estimates for Increase in the navy for the coming year for construction and machinery by $1,000,000 and also has eliminated the item of $400,000 for equipment of vessels under the same heading.
