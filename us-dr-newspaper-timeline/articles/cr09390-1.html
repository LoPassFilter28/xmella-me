The New York herald — 1858-12-24

Pork was in moderately active demand, and about 1,100 barrels were sold, at $17 87 1/2 a $18 for old mess, and $18 25 for new mess. Tallow was steady, with sales of 30,000 lbs. at 10 1/4c. Lard was unchanged, with sales of 250 barrels at 11 1/4c. Cut meats were firm, and prices in a general way were without any marked change. Wheat was steady, and sales were made to a fair extent, including 11,000 bushels of red Western at $1 25. Flour was unchanged, with sales of 6,500 barrels at $5 05 a $5 15 for super, and $5 22 1/2 a $5 30 for extra. Provisions were less active, while prices were pretty well maintained. Small sales of new mess at $18 12 1/2; old do. at $17 25 a $17 50, and prime at $13 75. Sugars were firm, with sales of about 1,500 hhds. at rates given in another place, and 1,400 boxes, for export, on private terms. Coffee was steady, with sales of St. Domingo and a small lot of Maracaibo at rates given in another place. Freights were inactive and engagements light.
