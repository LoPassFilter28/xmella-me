Pork was in moderately active demand, and about 1,100 barrels were sold, at $17 87 1/2 a $18 for old mess, and $18 25 for new mess. Tallow was steady, with sales of 30,000 lbs. at 10 1/4c. Lard was unchanged, with sales of 250 barrels at 11 1/4c. Cut meats were firm, and prices in a general way were without any marked change. Wheat was steady, and sales were made to a fair extent, including 11,000 bushels of red Western at $1 25. Flour was unchanged, with sales of 6,500 barrels at $5 05 a $5 15 for super, and $5 22 1/2 a $5 30 for extra. Provisions were less active, while prices were pretty well maintained. Small sales of new mess at $18 12 1/2; old do. at $17 25 a $17 50, and prime at $13 75. Sugars were firm, with sales of about 1,500 hhds. at rates given in another place, and 1,400 boxes, for export, on private terms. Coffee was steady, with sales of St. Domingo and a small lot of Maracaibo at rates given in another place. Freights were inactive and engagements light.El cerdo estaba en demanda moderadamente activa, y unos 1,100 barriles fueron vendidos, a $17 87 1/2 a $18 por mess viejo, y $18 25 por mess nuevo. El sebo estaba firme, con ventas de 30,000 lbs. a 10 1/4c. La manteca estaba sin cambio, con ventas de 250 barriles a 11 1/4c. Las carnes cortadas estaban firmes, y los precios en general sin cambio marcado alguno. El trigo estaba firme, y se hicieron ventas en buena medida, incluyendo 11,000 bushels de rojo Occidental a $1 25. La harina estaba sin cambio, con ventas de 6,500 barriles a $5 05 a $5 15 por super, y $5 22 1/2 a $5 30 por extra. Las provisiones estaban menos activas, mientras los precios se mantenían bastante bien. Pequeñas ventas de mess nuevo a $18 12 1/2; viejo íd. a $17 25 a $17 50, y prime a $13 75. Los azúcares estaban firmes, con ventas de unos 1,500 bocoyes a precios dados en otro lugar, y 1,400 cajas, para exportación, en términos privados. El café estaba firme, con ventas de Santo Domingo y un pequeño lote de Maracaibo a precios dados en otro lugar. Los fletes estaban inactivos y los compromisos ligeros.

The New York Herald. New York, December 24, 1858. Page 4.
