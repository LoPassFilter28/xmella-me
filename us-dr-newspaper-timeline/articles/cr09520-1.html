The release of the Contoy prisoners. What is the next scene in the bill of the play? Some say that Hayti is next to become the subject of some peremptory demands, enforced at the cannon's mouth. English abolitionists, it is said, are intriguing for the purpose of aiding the blacks to conquer the Spanish Dominicans, and that they will then buy Cuba from Spain and make a black empire, by abolishing slavery. Another idea is, that Spain will take part with the Dominicans, and that the United States will be called upon to interfere. We shall see.
