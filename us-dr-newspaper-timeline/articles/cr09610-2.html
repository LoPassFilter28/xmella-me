TURKS ISLAND.—The quantity of Salt stated in our last Gazette as having been raked within the then last fortnight, say 100,000 bushels, we are informed was under the true quantity, and that we may safely say another 100,000 bushels have been gathered on the two Cays during the last week. Price, 10 cents on board ship.

The price of Flour may be stated at $9. Corn at $1 25 cents per bushel. Lumber and Shingles are not to be had at any price. Butter, lard, and salt provisions are much wanted.

We understand from undoubted authority that F. C. Harrison, Esq., has been appointed United States Commercial Agent for the Dominican Republic.

We believe it to be the first official appointment from abroad to that Republic.

Their present crop of Tobacco is likely to exceed both in quantity and quality any previous crop.
