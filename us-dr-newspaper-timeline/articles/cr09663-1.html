The ceremony took place in the picture gallery. —News have been received from the city of St. Domingo, stating that the Emperor Soulouqne has raised an army of 40,000 men, to march against the Dominicans, who are making active preparations to receive him. —James Montgomery, the poet, is dead. He died at Sheffield, England, in the eighty third year of his age. He was considered to be the first religious poet of his time. He was educated by his father, a Moravian clergyman, for the ministry, but ultimately became editor of the Register a weekly paper published in Sheffield, the name of which he changed to the Iris, where he composed many of his poems.
