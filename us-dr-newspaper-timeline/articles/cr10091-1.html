1844-10-18 · Bloomington, I. T. [Iowa]

IMPORTANT FROM ST. DOMINGO.

SourceFuente: Bloomington herald, Bloomington, I. T. [Iowa], 1844-10-18
https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn85050801/1844-10-18/ed-1/?sp=2
unclassified

IMPORTANT FROM ST. DOMINGO.—The Island continues in a very unsettled state, in consequence of an expected attack by the Haytiens. Gen. Boewavo had been recalled to the capital, and was succeeded by Gen. St. Jago. The Spaniards are being appointed to office in place of the Dominicans, and every possible effort was made to defend the town.

It was reported that President Guerrier, had arrived at Cape Haytien, and visited all the eastern ports as far as Lake Enery; and from these movements it was thought there would be an immediate attack on the Spanish portion.
