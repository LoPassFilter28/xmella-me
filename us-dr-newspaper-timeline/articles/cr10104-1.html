The Eagle River review reported that the Dominican Congress ratified the treaty with the United States establishing terms for the evacuation of American military forces. The pact had previously been signed by American and Dominican representatives on June 13.

El Eagle River review informó que el Congreso dominicano ratificó el tratado con Estados Unidos que fijaba los términos para la evacuación de las fuerzas militares estadounidenses. El acuerdo había sido suscrito previamente por representantes estadounidenses y dominicanos el 13 de junio.

Dominican Republic to Have Home Rule Santo Domingo. — Congress approved the treaty with the United States providing for the evacuation of the Dominican republic by American military forces. The treaty was signed June 13. Serious Russian Floods Leningrad, Russia. — Floods in the Lake Ladoga region have engulfed 61 villages. The water rose 14 feet, causing enormous damage. Many lives are reported to have been lost in the flood. Mellon Going to Europe Washington. Secretary of the Treasury Mellon will sail for London July 5, it was announced at the treasury.
