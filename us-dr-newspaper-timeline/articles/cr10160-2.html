WASHINGTON, Oct. 8 (JTA). —The Dominican Legation today stated that no reply has been received as yet from the French Government in Vichy to the offer made by President Rafael Leonidas Trujillo of the Dominican Republic to admit Jewish refugee children whose parents have deported from France to Nazi held territory. The offer was hailed as "humanitarian and munificent" in a message of appreciation telegraphed yesterday by the executive board of the Central Conference of American Rabbis to President Trujillo, in behalf of 430 rabbis who are members of the C.C.A.R. NEW YORK, Oct, 8 (JTA),— President Roosevelt has addressed a special message to the conventions of the Zionist Organization of America and the Hadassah, which are to be held concurrently this year, opening on October 14, It was announced here today. More than 2,000 delegates are expected to attend the sessions of the two Zionist conventions here. The Zionist Organization convention will be held at the Hotel New Yorker, and the Hadassah will gather at the Hotel Pennsylvania, The two conventions will have a joint "political session" with Judge Louis E.
