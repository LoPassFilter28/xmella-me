The Sunday Telegram prints a dispatch: Washington, Jan. 23. The reported disappearance of some thousands of dollars of public works funds in Santo Domingo occurred before any of the happenings which led up to the present investigation in New York of James M. Sullivan, American minister to Santo Domingo, according to Secretary Bryan's understanding of the situation. He said today he probably would have a statement to make later in regard to the matter and did not care to discuss it until he could present all the facts in the case. It became known today that Enrique Jimenez, nephew of the new president of the Dominican republic and who recently arrived here as minister, had discussed the alleged shortage with State Department officials. It was understood, however, that the amount which is charged as having been taken from the public fund approximated $8,000.The Sunday Telegram publica un despacho: Washington, 23 de enero. La desaparición reportada de algunos miles de dólares de fondos de obras públicas en Santo Domingo ocurrió antes que cualquiera de los sucesos que condujeron a la presente investigación en Nueva York de James M. Sullivan, ministro americano en Santo Domingo, según el entendimiento de la situación del secretario Bryan. Dijo hoy que probablemente haría una declaración más tarde con respecto al asunto y que no deseaba discutirlo hasta que pudiera presentar todos los hechos del caso. Se supo hoy que Enrique Jimenez, sobrino del nuevo presidente de la República Dominicana y que recientemente llegó aquí como ministro, había discutido el presunto faltante con funcionarios del Departamento de Estado. Se entendía, sin embargo, que la cantidad que se acusa haber sido tomada del fondo público se aproximaba a $8,000.

The Sunday Telegram. Clarksburg, W. Va., January 24, 1915.
