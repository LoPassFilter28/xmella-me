The Herald reported that plans for a Dominican revolution were nearing completion in New York under the leadership of General Moscoso, an opponent of President Heureaux. Moscoso had fled to Puerto Rico to avoid arrest, narrowly survived an assassination attempt there, and arrived in New York in July to organize support. Critics accused Heureaux of using bribery and intimidation during his three presidential terms to establish a lifetime dictatorship.

The Herald informó que los planes para una revolución dominicana estaban a punto de completarse en Nueva York bajo la dirección del general Moscoso, opositor al presidente Heureaux. Moscoso había huido a Puerto Rico para evitar su captura, sobrevivió milagrosamente a un atentado allí y llegó a Nueva York en julio para organizar apoyos. Los críticos acusaban a Heureaux de valerse de sobornos e intimidaciones durante sus tres períodos presidenciales para instaurar una dictadura vitalicia.

Moscoso, who arrived here last July, and is said to have been constantly busy ever since with secret meetings with that purpose in view. He is one of Hereaux's implacable foes, and his friends here declare that he is the most available man to lead a revolt against the Dominican rulers. Moscoso was long ago recognized by Hereaux as a dangerous man, and Hereaux was scheming to put him in prison and thus get him where he could not endanger the administration, when Moscoso got wind of his intentions and promptly skipped out of the country. He went to the neighboring island of Porto Rico, the usual refuge of Dominican exiles, and was nearly assassinated there shortly after. Moscoso is barely 31 years of age. He was educated in this country, and speaks several languages with fluency. His personal bravery is extolled as highly as his mental accomplishments, and his following at home is declared to be large and enthusiastic. He refuses to discuss his plans. Hereaux has been President of Santo Domingo three terms, a much longer period than any of his predecessors. His enemies declare that he has been unscrupulous in carrying out his secret intention of making himself dictator for life by stifling opposition by means of bribery and intimidation.
