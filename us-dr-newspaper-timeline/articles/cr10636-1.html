The New York Herald reported that France allegedly intended to take control of the Dominican Republic to subsequently conquer the western part under the black regime and restore French dominion over the island. According to the excerpt, these secret machinations—initially pursued under Louis Philippe and later renewed under Louis Napoleon—found a warm supporter in Dominican President Buenaventura Baez, who was educated in France and held French notions.El New York Herald refirió que Francia pretendía supuestamente asumir el señorío de la República Dominicana para sojuzgar ulteriormente la sección occidental gobernada por el régimen negro y restablecer el dominio imperial sobre la isla. A juicio del extracto, semejantes maquinaciones secretas —fraguadas inicialmente bajo Luis Felipe y reactivadas luego con Luis Napoleón— hallaron un decidido valedor en el presidente dominicano Buenaventura Báez, instruido en Francia e imbuido de ideas francesas.

I am now resident here for some years, during which time I have studied the character of the Haytians, and remarkably; and I must confess that the steady exercise of French diplomatic agents in Hayti, for some years past, inspires me with a suspicion that those agents have only had one aim—namely, to get hold under some plausible appearance of the Dominican republic and to conquer afterwards the western part, under the black ribbon, with comparative facility, thereby restoring the 'Queen of the Antilles' to the dominion of France; or, if you will, since the Dictator has restored 'the eagles' of Napoleon to the French army, to the claws of the rapacious bird, which, although it may resemble ours in form, has no relationship with it in character.Ya resido aquí por algunos años, durante cuyo tiempo he estudiado el carácter de los haitianos, y notablemente; y debo confesar que el ejercicio constante de los agentes diplomáticos franceses en Haití, por algunos años pasados, me inspira con una sospecha de que esos agentes solo han tenido un objetivo —a saber, apoderarse bajo alguna apariencia plausible de la república dominicana y conquistar después la parte occidental, bajo la cinta negra, con facilidad comparativa, restaurando así la «Reina de las Antillas» al dominio de Francia; o, si quieren, ya que el Dictador ha restaurado «las águilas» de Napoleón al ejército francés, a las garras del ave rapaz, que, aunque pueda parecerse a la nuestra en forma, no tiene relación con ella en carácter.

The children of Louis Philippe, the late King of the French, came to Hayti under the pretext of paying the Montalais their legacy, which was left by the late King of Hayti to the widow of Prince de Joinville's brother, the late Prince of Joinville, and to her sister-in-law, the Duchess of Orleans; but the real object of their visit was to prepare the way for the French fleet to come and occupy Cuba and Porto Rico to her sister, bidding the young princes a very cool adieu, and telling them they had better turn back, as they would find nothing there to do, and to render it in time the stronghold of these seas, and to conquer the rest of Hayti.La suposición de un protectorado intencionado en Santo Domingo se fortalece aún más por la llegada del Príncipe de Joinville y el Duque d'Aumale a Haití, durante la presidencia del General Guerrier.
