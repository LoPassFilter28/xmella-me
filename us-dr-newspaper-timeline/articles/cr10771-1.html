The Evening Journal reported that Germany was actively working to undermine the reciprocity treaty between the Dominican Republic and the United States. Relying on an 1884 most-favored-nation treaty and its major market for Dominican tobacco, a German envoy reportedly threatened to close German ports and offered President Ulises Heureaux a personal annual bribe of $50,000 to abrogate the American agreement.

El Evening Journal informó que Alemania trabajaba activamente para socavar el tratado de reciprocidad entre la República Dominicana y los Estados Unidos. Amparándose en un tratado de nación más favorecida de 1884 y en su papel de principal mercado para el tabaco dominicano, según trascendió, un enviado alemán amenazó con cerrar los puertos alemanes y ofreció al presidente Ulises Heureaux un soborno anual personal de 50.000 dólares para que revocara el acuerdo estadounidense.

As soon as it was found that bullying did not succeed another tack was taken. Germany was chosen as the moving power of the conspiracy, because her relations with the little republic are peculiar. In [illegible] a treaty was signed between the two, in which Germany was named as the "most favored of nations." Her goods were permitted to enter Dominican ports at extremely low imposts, and her citizens were to receive special favors and exemptions. On the other hand, many Dominican products were given precedence in Germany, and under this treaty a large and valuable trade grew up between the two nations. Dominican tobacco especially found favor in Germany and soon crowded out the Sumatra product, and at present almost the entire product of Puerto Plata is used in Germany. The loss of this market for its staple product would be a serious blow to the San Domingan people, and it was on this line that the conspirators went to work. A special envoy was sent from Germany to San Domingo, and about two weeks ago he had an interview with President Ulises Heureaux, in which the harm which could be done his country should Germany close her ports to Dominican products was fully set forth.
