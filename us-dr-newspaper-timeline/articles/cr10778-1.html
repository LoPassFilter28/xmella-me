FOREIGN NEWS.

SOUTH AMERICA.

HAVANA, July 3.--President Suzman Blanco, of Venezuela, proposes that Holland sell the island of Curacoa to Venezuela.

HAYTI.

HAVANA, July 3.--A steamer has arrived from the Thames with the following news from Cape Haytien, June 24: It is rumored that the National Assembly at Port-au-Prince has been closed, owing to dissentions between the Senators and deputies. On the 20th Gen. Teofill Parisen, with his followers, attacked and occupied the government house in Cape Haytien City. There was fighting all night. The government troops finally drove the rebels out of the city. Thirty men were killed and wounded and a large number of arrests made. Gen. Parisen fled, it is said, to Monte Christo, Dominican territory.

There is general uneasiness throughout the republic. Business is paralyzed and the harbor crowded with vessels waiting cargoes. Produce comes in slowly, owing to the heavy rain.
