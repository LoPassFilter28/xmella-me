The Washington times — 1933-08-31

The *Washington Times* reported that Mrs. H. F. Arthur Schoenfeld, wife of the U.S. Minister to the Dominican Republic, was visiting Washington and staying at the Wardman Park Hotel. Her visit was scheduled to last a few weeks before she traveled to join the legation in Santo Domingo.

El *Washington Times* informó que la señora de H. F. Arthur Schoenfeld, esposa del ministro de Estados Unidos ante la República Dominicana, se encontraba de visita en Washington y hospedada en el Hotel Wardman Park. Su estancia estaba prevista para unas pocas semanas antes de viajar para incorporarse a la legación en Santo Domingo.
