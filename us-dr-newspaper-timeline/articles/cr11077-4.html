Diario Las Américas reported that General Paul E. Magloire resigned as head of Haiti's provisional military government and prepared to leave the country. Magloire transferred executive authority to Supreme Court President Joseph Pierre-Louis following a general strike in Port-au-Prince, seeking to prevent civil violence ahead of scheduled elections.

El Diario Las Américas informó que el general Paul E. Magloire renunció como jefe del gobierno militar provisional de Haití y se dispuso a abandonar el país. Magloire transfirió el poder ejecutivo al presidente de la Corte Suprema, Joseph Pierre-Louis, tras una huelga general en Puerto Príncipe, en un intento por evitar la violencia civil de cara a las elecciones programadas.

Magloire resigned today the Presidency of the Haitian Provisional Military Government and prepared to leave the country by plane, according to a creditable information received in this capital. Dispatchs from Port-au-Prince reveal that Magloire handed over the Presidency, at 11 a.m., to Jo seph Pierre Louis, President of the Supreme Court and constitutional successor to the President. It is expected that Magloire will announce his resignation publicly, making a plea to the Haitian people for the keeping of peace and order during the transition period until celebration of new presidential elections next April. Magloire was said to have decid ed to leave Haiti to avert the pos sibility of an outbreak of violence over his recent unpopular actions. The people of Port-au-Prince ex pressed their displeasure by stag ing a general strike Monday.
