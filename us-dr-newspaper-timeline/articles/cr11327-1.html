The Evening Star reported that American naval assistance was dispatched to Belize following a hurricane that killed between 150 and 200 people. Meanwhile, officials monitored a second storm advancing across Puerto Rico toward Haiti and the Dominican Republic. That cyclone had already caused one death and torn roofs from 200 homes in San Juan while tracking westward across the Caribbean.

El Evening Star informó del envío de socorro naval estadounidense a Belice tras un huracán que causó entre 150 y 200 muertes. Paralelamente, las autoridades monitoreaban una segunda tormenta que cruzaba Puerto Rico hacia Haití y la República Dominicana. Dicho ciclón ya había dejado una víctima mortal y desprendido los techos de 200 viviendas en San Juan en su avance hacia el oeste a través del Caribe.

While relief was being mustered to care for those stricken in Belize, the path of a second hurricane across Porto Rico toward Haiti and the Dominican Republic was being anxiously watched. This storm had killed 1 person, unroofed 200 houses and temporarily disrupted communication and transportation facilities at San Juan and was sweeping westward. Haiti and Santo Domingo were reported directly in its path. Orders were issued at the Navy Department early this afternoon for a Marine Corps plane to leave Managua immediately for Belize, nearly [illegible] miles away, carrying $2,500 worth of medical supplies.
