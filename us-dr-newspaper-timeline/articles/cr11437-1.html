With an opening salute of 21 guns from the batteries of Ozama Fortress, the Dominican Republic today Inaugurated the most extensive and elaborate celebration of Washington's birthday ever held outside the United States. In a gesture of friendship and admiration for the United States and its first President, George Washington, Pres. Gen. Rafael L. Trujillo recently suggested that the name of Trujillo City's most beautiful boulevard, until today called Avenida Presidente Trujillo, be changed to Avenida George Washington. Acting on this suggestion the national congress approved an ordinance to that effect and named February 22, 1936, as the date for the official inaugural ceremonies. Solemn dedication ceremonies were held here, including an address by President Trujillo, and another by the American Minister Hans F. Arthur Schoenfeld. A commemorative bronze plaque of large dimensions, suitably inscribed, was unveiled. Band concerts and other festivities were attended by many thousands cele[illegible] this great event.
