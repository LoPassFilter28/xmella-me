Passage summaryResumen del pasaje

The *Evening Star* listed several diplomatic nominations submitted for diplomatic missions across Latin America. Among the assignments, Avra M. Warren of Maryland was designated Minister to the Dominican Republic, while Robert M. Scotten was named Minister to Costa Rica. Other appointments included Arthur Bliss Lane to Colombia and Boaz Long to Ecuador.

El *Evening Star* consignó las postulaciones diplomáticas elevadas para distintas misiones en Latinoamérica. Entre los nombramientos constaba la designación de Avra M. Warren, de Maryland, como ministro ante la República Dominicana, en tanto que Robert M. Scotten quedaba acreditado ante Costa Rica. Las demás designaciones incluyeron a Arthur Bliss Lane en Colombia y a Boaz Long en Ecuador.

6-Million-Dollar Sub

Amberjack Launched

By the Associated Press.

GROTON. Conn.. March 6.—The

$6,000,000 submarine Amberjack,

latest of such craft built for the

United States Navy, slipped from the

ways at the Electric Boat Co. ship

yard into the fog-shrouded waters

of the Thames River today less than

10 months after her keel had been

laid.

The launching took place as wet

snow fell and first-shift workers

stood about during their brief lunch

period watching the wife of Rear

Admiral Randall Jacobs, chief of the

Navy's Bureau of Navigation, smash

a bottle of champagne over the bow.
