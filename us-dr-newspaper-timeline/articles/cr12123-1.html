The editorial reports that the steamer Tennessee, after much talk of her loss, has reached the Bay of Samana safely, and that some of her passengers, including members of the San Domingo Commission, crossed the island to the city of Santo Domingo on the south coast. Quoting a Republican writer's account of the country and its people, the editorial argues against President Grant's annexation of St. Domingo and warns that he and his friends will likely find the Dominicans a burden before many months are over.

El editorial informa que el vapor Tennessee, tras mucho hablarse de su pérdida, ha llegado sano y salvo a la bahía de Samaná, y que algunos de sus pasajeros, incluidos miembros de la Comisión de Santo Domingo, cruzaron la isla hasta la ciudad de Santo Domingo, en la costa sur. Citando el relato de un escritor republicano sobre el país y su gente, el editorial argumenta contra la anexión de Santo Domingo por el presidente Grant y advierte que él y sus amigos probablemente encontrarán a los dominicanos una carga antes de que pasen muchos meses.

Were ancient Hispaniola even a blessing to us, modern Dominicans would be a curse, as Gen. Grant and his friends will probably find before they are many months older. As to its inhabitants, we have the high Republican authority of Mr. Fulton for this: "The majority of the people of the country are of pure African descent. Of this there cannot be the slightest doubt, and we could trace but little evidence of what is called a mixture of races among the country people, though the population of Santo Domingo is mixed and motley. Among the elderly men and women especially there is a grace of carriage and a dignity of manner that is peculiarly attractive, and reminds one of the high-toned Spanish character. They are cleanly but very slightly appareled. The men wear a white mantle when out at night or to protect them from the rain, and assume quite a picturesque aspect. They all carry swords at their side, and I saw some boys not over fifteen years of age with the inevitable sword.
