The Evening Star listed foreign diplomats who attended an international gathering in Washington, including Dominican Minister Dr. Jesús María Troncoso. Other envoys in attendance represented nations such as Panama, China, Mexico, Canada, Yugoslavia, Australia, and the Netherlands.

El Evening Star enumeró al cuerpo diplomático acreditado que concurrió a una recepción internacional en Washington, contándose entre los presentes al ministro de la República Dominicana, Dr. Jesús María Troncoso. Otros dignatarios asistentes integraban legaciones de Panamá, China, México, Canadá, Yugoslavia, Australia y los Países Bajos.

WHAT A U-BOAT CRASH-DIVE LOOKS LIKE FROM A PLANE—Caught by the camera in what are believed to have been Its death throes was this Nazi U-boat, pictured from an attacking Royal Canadian Air Force bomber off the east coast of Canada. This rare picture of a submarine crash-diving for its life was taken as a big Bolingbroke bomber skippered by Warrant Officer C. S. Buchanan of London, Ontario, roared down to the attack. The U-boat, surprised on the surface, is suddenly plunging beneath the water, only its "jumping wires"—designed to ward off mines and nets—appearing above the surface as waves close over it. [illegible] A few minutes later this picture was made as the U-boat was blown to the surface at a crazy angle by heavy depth charges dropped by the bomber. One more depth charge then was dropped, the sub sank, and the only remaining signs were bubbles of air, then a big patch of oil.
