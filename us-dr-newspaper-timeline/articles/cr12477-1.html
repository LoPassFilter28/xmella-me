Passage summaryResumen del pasaje

A St. Domingo correspondent for *The New York Herald* reported that Spanish military forces committed numerous outrages and spoliation against United States citizens and their agricultural, manufacturing, and mining enterprises during the Spanish invasion of the Dominican Republic. Additionally, the Dominican Minister who visited Washington returned home disheartened after being unable to obtain an interview with Mr. Seward to present a statement regarding these Spanish outrages.

Un corresponsal de *The New York Herald* en Santo Domingo informó que las tropas españolas perpetraron numerosos atropellos y despojos contra ciudadanos estadounidenses y sus establecimientos agrícolas, manufactureros y mineros durante la invasión española a la República Dominicana. Además, el ministro dominicano comisionado a Washington regresó a su patria desalentado tras no lograr concertar una entrevista con el secretario Seward para presentarle un memorial relativo a tales desafueros españoles.
