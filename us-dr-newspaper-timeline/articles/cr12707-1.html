The Washington daily news — 1938-07-23

SourceFuente: The Washington daily news, Washington, D.C., 1938-07-23

The *Washington Daily News* reported on the enduring friendship between U.S. Ambassador Joseph E. Davies and Dominican President Rafael Trujillo, noting that Davies's daughter had recently stayed at the Presidential Palace in Trujillo City. Dominican Minister Andrés Pastoriza was returning to Washington after sending his family home, preparing for an upcoming coast-to-coast American tour by Trujillo following his pending retirement from the presidency.

El *Washington Daily News* informó sobre la entrañable amistad entre el embajador estadounidense Joseph E. Davies y el mandatario dominicano Rafael Trujillo, destacando que la hija de Davies se había alojado recientemente en el Palacio Presidencial de Ciudad Trujillo. El ministro dominicano Andrés Pastoriza regresaba a Washington tras despedir a su familia de vuelta a su país, a fin de preparar una gira por Estados Unidos de costa a costa que Trujillo realizaría tras su inminente retiro de la presidencia.
