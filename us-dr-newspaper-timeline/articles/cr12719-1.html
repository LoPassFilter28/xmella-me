A brief news item reports that President Stockwell of the Samana Bay Company has resigned and William G. Fargo takes his place. The company has also resolved to have nothing to do with the so-called Hartmont bonds, held in England and declared null and void by the Dominican government.

Una breve noticia informa que el presidente Stockwell, de la Compañía de la Bahía de Samaná, ha renunciado y que William G. Fargo ocupa su lugar. La compañía también ha resuelto no tener nada que ver con los llamados bonos Hartmont, en poder de tenedores en Inglaterra y declarados nulos por el gobierno dominicano.

The consecration of Bishop Paddock in Brooklyn, as bishop of Massachusetts, takes place, next week. President Stockwell of the Samana Bay company has resigned, and William G. Fargo takes his place. The company has resolved to have nothing to do with the so-called Hartmont bonds, held in England, and declared null and void by the Dominican government. MISCELLANEOUS. It is now said to be the quarreling of the two French ports of Calais and Boulogne which precludes the abatement of the "channel nuisance." Neither of these ports will admit large steamers, and neither will submit to have the other improved. It is now proposed to make a new port at Andracelles, which is nearer Paris than either of the others and has greater natural advantages. The Southeastern railway of England and the Northern railway of France propose to lay out $1,000,000 apiece upon the new harbor and then sell it to France for a naval station. Anything to introduce a civilized method of "getting across." The Havana fire of Saturday night proves to have been a most disastrous one. An entire square was destroyed in less than three hours, the flames spreading with almost unexampled rapidity. The loss is variously estimated at from three to eight millions, on which there is an insurance of only a trifle over half a million. From the silence of later dispatches on the subject, it is, we hope, fair to presume that the first reports of the loss of life—which put the number burned to death at twenty —were either unfounded or greatly exaggerated.
