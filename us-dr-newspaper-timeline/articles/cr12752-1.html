Passage summaryResumen del pasaje

The Birmingham Age-Herald reported that the U.S. Cabinet discussed the escalating crisis in Haiti, noting that unlike Santo Domingo, no treaty existed authorizing an American customs receivership. While four American warships patrolled Haitian ports to protect foreign nationals, the German cruiser Strassburg was stationed nearby in Dominican waters. Haitian Minister Ulrich Duvivier denied that his country was defaulting on legitimate debts and condemned foreign intervention rumors.
