The Bisbee Daily Review reports, in a March 31 San Domingo cable, that Archbishop Nouel (printed as "Mouel") presented his resignation as provisional president of the Dominican Republic at that day's session of the national congress. The report adds that congress will appoint his successor. Ill health was assigned as the reason for the archbishop's action.El Bisbee Daily Review informa, en un cable del 31 de marzo desde Santo Domingo, que el arzobispo Nouel (impreso como "Mouel") presentó su renuncia como presidente provisional de la República Dominicana en la sesión celebrada ese día por el congreso nacional. La nota añade que el congreso designará a su sucesor. Se indicaron problemas de salud como motivo de la decisión del arzobispo.

Bisbee Daily Review. Bisbee, Ariz., April 1, 1913.
