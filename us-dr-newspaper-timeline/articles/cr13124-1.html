The Spanish troops in San Domingo were harrassed terribly by Dominican guerrillas; and on reaching a defile at the river Jomera, were driven back helter-skelter to the city, losing some 200 killed and wounded. The Dominicans still hold St. Christobal, thus preventing all land communications with the places occupied by the Spaniards. Some 200 Spaniards, 300 rifles, 40,000 rounds of cartridges, and a field piece, had been captured by the Dominicans. Santanna's position is critical, and it is thought he will be compelled to leave the island.
