1917-01-25 · Springfield, Mass.

Springfield weekly Republican — 1917-01-25

SourceFuente: Springfield weekly Republican, Springfield, Mass., 1917-01-25

The *Springfield weekly Republican* reported that Private J. R. Olson was killed and Corporal George Wilson was severely wounded during a night battle between marines and bandits near Macoris. The clash occurred near the Porvenir sugar plantation, representing the third encounter in that locality as troops worked to disarm the population. The paper noted that Dominican casualties were not disclosed due to strict military censorship imposed by naval authorities.

El *Springfield weekly Republican* informó que el soldado raso J. R. Olson perdió la vida y el cabo George Wilson resultó gravemente herido durante un combate nocturno entre infantes de marina y bandoleros en cercanías de Macorís. El choque se libró junto al ingenio Porvenir, constituyendo la tercera contienda en ese paraje mientras las tropas operaban en el desarme de la población. El rotativo señaló que las bajas dominicanas no fueron reveladas a causa de la rigurosa censura militar dispuesta por los mandos navales.
