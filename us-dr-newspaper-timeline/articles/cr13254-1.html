The Richmond Dispatch reported that an attempted revolution against Dominican President Heureaux met swift defeat at Monte Cristi. According to an official dispatch from Heureaux, insurgent leader General Jimenes fled aboard the steamer Fanita under fire, while Haitian General Morales and several others were killed. Dominican authorities captured several prisoners, including General Villanueva's son, who were slated for execution.

El Richmond Dispatch informó que un intento de revolución contra el presidente dominicano Heureaux fue rápidamente derrotado en Monte Cristi. Según un parte oficial de Heureaux, el líder insurgente general Jimenes huyó bajo fuego a bordo del vapor Fanita, mientras que el general haitiano Morales y varios otros perecieron. Las autoridades dominicanas capturaron a varios prisioneros, entre ellos el hijo del general Villanueva, quienes estaban destinados a ser ejecutados.

General Garcia, another important Dominican leader, is said to be taking part in the revolution, and there is said to be a rising at Santo Domingo, the capital of the Dominican republic. HEUREUX'S BLOODTHIRSTNESS. President Heureux, according to a common saying among the Dominicans, has killed off all the prominent men on whom he could lay hands, and who might be rivals. President Heureux is said to have recently resumed the arbitrary execution of his opponents, sometimes openly, sometimes stealthily. The tragic disappearances of men of political note have been frequent of late in Dominica. General Pablo Reyes is said to be among the officers who have risen. THE REVOLT PUT DOWN. LATER. — This afternoon the Dominican Consul here received a dispatch from President Heureux, saying that an attempted revolution at Monte Cristo had been defeated; that General Jiminez fled at the first fire of the government troops, taking refuge on board the Fanita, and that all others who landed, with the exception of three, had been killed. Two of the prisoners are Pablo Villanueva, son of General Villanueva, and a laborer. They will be executed to-day, with other prisoners. General Morales, the Haitian general, and several Dominicans who shipped here, are among the killed. Another person was killed on board the steamer Fanita, which put to sea immediately and escaped.
