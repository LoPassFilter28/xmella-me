The Democrat prints a dispatch reporting that General Salnave, a Haytian refugee, has been warned to leave Dominican territory immediately: under the auspices of General Jiminez, former president of Santo Domingo, it was asserted, General Salnave was preparing an expedition, the object of which was said to have been the overthrow of the Haytian government.El Democrat publica un despacho que informa que al general Salnave, refugiado haitiano, se le ha advertido que abandone inmediatamente el territorio dominicano: bajo los auspicios del general Jiménez, expresidente de Santo Domingo, se afirmó que el general Salnave estaba preparando una expedición, cuyo objeto se decía que era el derrocamiento del gobierno haitiano.

Waterbury Evening Democrat. Waterbury [Connecticut], May 11, 1904. Page 2.
