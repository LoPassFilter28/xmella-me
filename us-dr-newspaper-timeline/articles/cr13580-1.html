The Turk's Island Standard of the 2d inst. has intelligence from the city of St. Domingo of Dec. 14th and Puerto Plata of the 24th. The capture of Azua by the Spaniards under Gualier is confirmed, but the Dominicans claim that although it has cost two months' fighting and many lives, its possession is of little real value to the Spaniards.

A train of eighty mules laden with ammunition for Santana's army was captured Dec. 9th by the Dominicans.

Twelve hundred Spaniards left St. Domingo city Dec. 6th to capture St. Christobal, but were harassed terribly by the Dominican guerrillas, and on reaching a defile at the river Jaina were driven back hilter skelter to the city, losing some 300 in killed and wounded. The Dominicans still hold St. Christobal, thus preventing all land communication with places occupied by the Spaniards.

Some 20 Spaniards, 300 rifles, 4000 rounds of cartridges and a field piece had been captured by the Dominicans at Yabacoa, Santana's army supply depot. The Spaniards are strengthening the fortifications of St. Domingo by two redoubts. Santana is near San Juan, and has been lately reinforced by 1000 men, but there were more than 700 sick in his camp. He is unable to move.
