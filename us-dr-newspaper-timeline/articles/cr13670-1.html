Passage summaryResumen del pasaje

On July 5, Madrid reported that rumors of the Spanish frigate *Almanza* and war steamer *Jarge Juan* being ordered from Havana to blockade the coast of San Domingo were denied. The Spanish government stated it would instead await a reply from San Domingo before taking active steps.

El 5 de julio se desmintieron en Madrid las versiones de que la fragata española *Almanza* y el vapor de guerra *Jorge Juan* hubieran recibido órdenes desde La Habana para bloquear las costas de Santo Domingo. El gabinete peninsular manifestó que aguardaría la respuesta de Santo Domingo antes de emprender acciones bélicas.

Spain and San Domingo.

Madrid, July 5.—The statement that the

Spanish frigate Almanza and war steamer

Jarge Juan have been ordered to leave Havana

and proceed to San Domingo to establish a

blockade of the coast if the Dominican gov

ernment refuses to give satisfaction to Spain

is denied. The government will await a reply

from San Domingo before taking active steps.

Germany’s Tariff Bill.

Berlin, July 5.—Parliament today voted the

remaining clauses of tho new tariff bill, with

the exception of that relating to tobacco, which

will be discussed on Monday. A duty of 40

marks on coffee was voted—174 to 97, and one

of six marks on petroloum—171 to 92. These

votes were tho result of an arrangement be

tween tho Centre and Conservative parties.
