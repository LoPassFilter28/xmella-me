W. Peal-hostess. A delicious dinner was served the members upon their arrival at 7:30 o'clock. During the business meeting 25 new books for the children's shelf of the town library were on display. Concluding the year's study of Latin America, Mrs. T. O. Goodman ably presented the countries of Puerto Rico, Haiti and the Dominican Republic. TREATY RATIFIED MOSCOW, April 25.—(UP) The Presidium of the Supreme Soviet and the Emperor of Japan have ratified the Russian-Japanese neutrality treaty signed April 13, it was announced today in a Tass Soviet news agency dispatch. Thus, in accordance with the third article of the treaty, it is in force as of today, Tass said.
