A feature on Santo Domingo describes its minerals, including rock salt, petroleum found near Azua, abundant copper and great masses of iron ore in the interior, and explains that the president is chosen by an electoral college for four years with a cabinet of six. It says sugar, tobacco, cacao and coffee are the leading exports, that the United States led all other countries in trade with the republic, that most sugar estates are owned by Americans with the crop estimated at 65,000 tons, and that steamers of the Samana Bay Fruit Company carry fifteen to eighteen thousand bunches of bananas on each trip to the United States.

Un artículo sobre Santo Domingo describe sus minerales, incluida la sal de roca, el petróleo encontrado cerca de Azua, el cobre abundante y grandes masas de mineral de hierro en el interior, y explica que el presidente es elegido por un colegio electoral por cuatro años con un gabinete de seis miembros. Dice que el azúcar, el tabaco, el cacao y el café son las principales exportaciones, que los Estados Unidos encabezaban a todos los demás países en el comercio con la república, que la mayoría de las fincas azucareras son propiedad de americanos con la cosecha estimada en 65.000 toneladas, y que los vapores de la Samana Bay Fruit Company llevan de quince a dieciocho mil racimos de bananas en cada viaje a los Estados Unidos.

One price— Only to be had of us [illegible] and the natives still wash out small amounts of gold. In the Neyba valley there are two hills composed entirely of rock salt, which chemical analysis has proved to be of sufficient purity for commercial purposes. Petroleum has been found in the vicinity of Azua, and copper is said by geologists and prospectors to be abundant. There are said also to be great masses of iron ore in the interior, but at such a great distance from any port that they cannot be made available until railroads shall have been built. Other minerals in the Island are silver, platinum, manganese, tin, antimony, marble, coal, and chalcedony. Mineral springs abound. The executive authority is vested in the President, who is chosen by an electoral college, and serves for four years. He is assisted by a Cabinet of six members. There is also a Vice President. The republic is divided into eleven districts for purposes of administration. Six of these districts are called provinces. The Island's Trade. The four leading articles of export are sugar, tobacco, cacao, and coffee. Last year the United States led all Europe in the amount of exports to the republic by $636,403.05, and all the world, exclusive of the United States, by more than one-half million dollars. As might be supposed, large quantities of drills and cotton goods are used, these coming from England and the United States, but the greater part from England, mainly because of cheapness.
