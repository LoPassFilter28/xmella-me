Col. Charles A. Lindbergh took off from Santo Domingo at 11 a.m. on February 6 for Port-au-Prince, Haiti, on a 160-mile route flown entirely across land, as he neared the end of his goodwill flight to the Pan American conference at Havana. The night before, he had been guest of honor at a brilliant ball at the presidential palace, attended by the diplomatic body, members of the government and prominent society figures.
