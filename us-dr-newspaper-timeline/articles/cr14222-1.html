The Key West Citizen reported that Cuba and the Dominican Republic signed a bilateral postal treaty on December 20. The accord was concluded by Cuban Secretary of State Dr. Orestes Ferrara and Dominican Minister to Cuba Osvaldo Bazil representing their respective governments.

El Key West Citizen informó que Cuba y la República Dominicana firmaron un tratado postal bilateral el 20 de diciembre. El acuerdo fue concertado por el secretario de Estado cubano, Dr. Orestes Ferrara, y el ministro dominicano en Cuba, Osvaldo Bazil, en representación de sus respectivos gobiernos.

According to news received, Cuban avocado pears have been declared duty free in the United States. The sugar crop will not begin until February 15 of the present year. The postal treaty between Cuba and the Dominican Republic was signed on the 20th day of December, Dr. Orestes Ferrara, secretary of state, representing Cuba, and Mr. Osvaldo Bazil, Dominican minister to Cuba, representing the Dominican republic. It has been announced that the racing season on the Marianao race tracks [illegible] will begin on January 21 under the auspices of the Cuban-American Jockey Club which has taken the place of the Havana-American Jockey Club. The president of the republic, General Gerardo Machado, has denied the news published in the United States regarding an economic project calling for the emission of $70,000,000 and a loan. The department of agriculture is organizing expositions of livestock, aviculture and agriculture, to be celebrated in the Exposition Park, in the "Lutgardita" subdivision, from February 20 to [illegible]. According to estimates which have been published, exportation of coffee in 1932 amounted to 3,600,000 "kilos," [illegible] of which went to the United States. The government has acquired four new cruising planes to be used for military aviation purposes and already four lieutenants of the aviation corps have left for the United States with the sole object of bringing said planes to Cuba.
