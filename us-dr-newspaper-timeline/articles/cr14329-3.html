SummaryResumen

A report on the Dominican revolt states that one of the contributory causes of the present revolt is the attempt of the central government to lease the Dominican Central Railroad, which runs through Puerto Plata Province.

Un informe sobre la revuelta dominicana afirma que una de las causas que contribuyeron a la revuelta actual es el intento del gobierno central de arrendar el Ferrocarril Central Dominicano, que atraviesa la provincia de Puerto Plata.
