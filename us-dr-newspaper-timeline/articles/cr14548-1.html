The Cunard steamship Abyssinia, which arrived in New York on Thursday last brought $12,000,000 of United States six per cent. called bonds from Europe, on the way to the Treasury Department at Washington for cancellation. With his annual message the President laid before Congress yesterday the application of Baez, President of the Dominican Republic, and his Cabinet, for a protectorate of the United States over their country. B. H. Bristow, of Kentucky, who has been appointed Attorney General, was the first Solicitor General under the law establishing a department of Justice, and he resigned that office about a year ago. There is trouble in Providence, R. I., in reference to the Sprague trust mortgage. One trustee has been substituted for another, without the consent of the creditors. Bishop Cummins, of the Episcopal Church, has made an important secession from the church, and yesterday organized a new body to be known as the "Reformed Episcopal Church." On the first day of January the Virginia Legislature will assemble. FOREIGN NEWS. Count Segur, a member of the Right Centre, was yesterday elected Secretary of the French Assembly by three hundred and nine votes. The Journal de Paris says that M. Bartholdi, now First Secretary of the Embassy at St. Petersburg, is mentioned as the probable successor of the Marquis de Noailles at Washington.
