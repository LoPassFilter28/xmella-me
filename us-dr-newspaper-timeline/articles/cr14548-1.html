With his annual message, the President laid before Congress yesterday the application of Baez, President of the Dominican Republic, and his Cabinet, for a protectorate of the United States over their country.

Con su mensaje anual, el presidente presentó ayer ante el Congreso la solicitud de Baez, presidente de la República Dominicana, y su gabinete, de un protectorado de los Estados Unidos sobre su país.
