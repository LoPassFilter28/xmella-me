OUR FLAG INSULTED

The Steamship Tybee Boarded by Dominican Troops—Forcible Removal of One of Her Passengers.

An insult to the American flag that calls for prompt redress was recently committed in St. Domingo. The following are the details of the occurrence furnished by several of the crew of the steamship Tybee, now lying at the foot of Congress street, Brooklyn, in the absence of Captain Kuehl:—

On the 20th of June we arrived at Cape Haytien, in Hayti, where we took on board a passenger named Pablo Villanueva, ex-Minister of War and Marine under President Gonzales of the Republic of St. Domingo. He was provided with a Dominican passport, duly visited by the Haytien authorities, and was bound for St. Domingo City, the capital of St. Domingo. In due course we arrived there and came to anchor in the port, where we were boarded by the Captain of the Port and other officials, several of whom spoke and recognized Villanueva. A few hours afterwards word was sent to Villanueva from the recently elected President of the Republic that if he should come ashore he would be arrested. Villanueva, feeling safe under the protection, sent for the American Consul, Mr. Paul Jones, and told him in English, which he speaks very fluently, that he came to St. Domingo City to see his wife and child, as all the letters he wrote to them had been intercepted, and that he intended to take them away to New York, where he was going to reside for the future. He also said he came to claim some $5,000 due to him from different people, and, in conclusion, that he had no desire to cause any new political strife, his errand being simply as above stated.—After learning this the Consul left the Tybee and was rowed ashore.
