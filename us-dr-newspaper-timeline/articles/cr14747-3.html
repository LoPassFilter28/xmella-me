The New-York Tribune reported on the background of revolutionary operative Giordani, who had previously created confusion among shippers by setting up an unauthorized rival Dominican consulate in New York until the State Department intervened. Giordani had also operated as an unsuccessful lobbyist opposing the Dominican treaty and was a prominent supporter of former Dominican President Juan Isidro Jimenez. His activities and arrest were compared to the earlier case of Captain Boynton involving revolutionary currency schemes.

The New-York Tribune informó sobre los antecedentes del conspirador revolucionario Giordani, quien anteriormente había provocado desorden entre las navieras al instalar en Nueva York un consulado dominicano rival y no autorizado hasta que intervino el Departamento de Estado. Giordani también había actuado sin éxito como cabildero opositor al tratado dominicano y era un destacado partidario del expresidente dominicano Juan Isidro Jimenes. Sus actividades y arresto fueron comparados con el caso anterior del capitán Boynton relativo a planes monetarios revolucionarios.
