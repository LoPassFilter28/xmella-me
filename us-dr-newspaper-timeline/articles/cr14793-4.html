A dispatch from Samana reports that small parties of the scientific corps will be sent into the country in various directions, and that those who examined the place as a naval station agreed the harbor where the coaling station stands should be obtained by the United States Government by lease or purchase. It says the commission hopes to start home soon after March 1, with no case of illness in the party.

Un despacho de Samaná informa que pequeños grupos del cuerpo científico serán enviados al interior en varias direcciones, y que quienes examinaron el lugar como estación naval coincidieron en que el puerto donde está la estación carbonera debería ser obtenido por el Gobierno de los Estados Unidos mediante arrendamiento o compra. Dice que la comisión espera partir de regreso poco después del 1 de marzo, sin ningún caso de enfermedad en el grupo.

Small parties of the scientific corps will be sent into the country from here in various directions. VALUE AS A NAVAL STATION. What was seen of the people around Samana was of little consequence compared with the investigation into the value of the place as a naval station. All seemed to agree that if the present condition of the finances would justify it, that the harbor where the coaling station is should be obtained by our government, by lease or purchase. SCENERY AND [illegible]. The magnificence of the foliage, beauty and grandeur of the mountains and [illegible] of the country cannot be overrated. There is, however, neither energy nor thrift. STARTING FOR HOME. The commission hopes to start home soon after the first of March. HEALTH OF THE PARTY. There has been no case of illness in the party, and the season is healthy everywhere in the island, so far as heard from. All United States officers upon the island are well.
