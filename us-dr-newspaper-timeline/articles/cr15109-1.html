Peronist chieftain John William Cooke and his wife, who had been living with ex-dictator Juan D. Perón in Ciudad Trujillo, left the Dominican Republic and came by way of Miami, spending two days at a main hotel before moving to a private house, while Perón left the Dominican capital to vacation at Jarabacoa. A separate item said weapons from the Dominican Republic were intended to strengthen the Cuban government against the swelling revolt in Oriente Province, replacing 1,950 Garand rifles whose shipment from the United States had been blocked by the State Department on March 14.
