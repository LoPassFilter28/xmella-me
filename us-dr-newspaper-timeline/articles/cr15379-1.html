The New-York tribune reported that the State Department issued a formal denial that the National City Bank of New York received improper government favors in Haiti. The department stated it only provided routine support and had actively intervened to remove monopolistic provisions from the bank's charter when it increased its holdings in the National Bank of Haiti. Furthermore, the statement emphasized that the bank exercised no control over customs revenues, foreign loans, or exchange rates.

El New-York tribune informó que el Departamento de Estado desmintió formalmente que el National City Bank of New York hubiera recibido favores gubernamentales indebidos en Haití. El departamento afirmó que únicamente prestó apoyo rutinario y que había intervenido activamente para eliminar disposiciones monopolísticas de los estatutos del banco cuando este amplió su participación en el Banco Nacional de Haití. Asimismo, la declaración enfatizó que la entidad bancaria no ejercía control alguno sobre las rentas aduaneras, los préstamos externos ni los tipos de cambio.

in the press recently to the effect that the National City Bank of New York has received improper support from this government in the matter of its Haytian branch, the following are the facts: "1. No assistance or support has been given the National City Bank in Hayti other than that which would be extended to any first-class American bank in any foreign country. "2. Far from encouraging the National City Bank in obtaining monopolistic privileges in connection with its interests in Hayti, the department has, in so far as it has had an opportunity to exert an influence, used it toward preventing the National City Bank from enjoying monopolistic privileges. The National City Bank owned 50 per cent of the stock of the Banque Nationale de la Republique d'Haiti, and last year, when the approval of the department was sought to the purchase by the National City Bank of a still larger interest in the French bank, the approval of this government was only given after the National City Bank had agreed to certain important modifications in the charter of the Banque Nationale de la Republique d'Haiti insisted upon by the Department of State, and all of which were designed in the interests of the Haytian people to eliminate the monopolistic features of the original concession granted by Hayti.
