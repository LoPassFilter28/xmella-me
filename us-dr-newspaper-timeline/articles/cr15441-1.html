The paper reports that Dominican revolutionary troops assembled at Ouanaminthe crossed the frontier and took Dajabón, its garrison retiring to Fort Belair. It says leader Jiménez was expected to land to join them, and the Haitian government was taking measures to prevent him landing in Haiti.

El periódico informa que tropas revolucionarias dominicanas reunidas en Ouanaminthe cruzaron la frontera y tomaron Dajabón, y su guarnición se retiró a Fort Belair. Dice que se esperaba que el líder Jiménez desembarcara para unirse a ellos, y que el gobierno haitiano tomaba medidas para impedir su desembarco en Haití.
