Cabral has sent a communication to the frontier for the Commission in answer to a letter from Mr. Howe written at Azua, but the letter is delayed until the Haitian authorities can correspond with the American Minister about receiving it, and Mr. Wade is anxious not to complicate matters while in Haiti by appearing to prosecute investigations looking to annexation. A Kingston dispatch of March 10 reports that the Tennessee, with the San Domingo Commission, sailed that morning for Key West while the Nantasket returns to Samana, all on board both ships being well.

Un despacho de Kingston del 10 de marzo informa que la Tennessee, con la Comisión de Santo Domingo, zarpó esa mañana para Cayo Hueso mientras la Nantasket regresa a Samaná, estando bien todos los que van a bordo de ambos buques.
