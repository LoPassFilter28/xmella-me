Upon his return, General Babcock was minutely questioned regarding his mission and made a verbal report upon it to a Senate committee. His report was highly favorable, confirming the information previously in possession of the department. The results of General Babcock's examination were deemed favorable to the maintenance of the American policy, and he was instructed to return to San Domingo to aid Raymond H. Perry, the commercial agent of the United States, in negotiating for the annexation of the whole territory of the Island republic to the United States and, as an alternative proposition, for a lease of the peninsula and bay of Samana. These negotiations were successful and a treaty was drawn up agreeable to the Dominican Government. A popular vote of the island was taken on the question of annexation, which was almost unanimous in favor of that plan.
