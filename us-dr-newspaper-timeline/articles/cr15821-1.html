The Evening Star reported that prominent hotels in the Dominican Republic were expanding recreational amenities to attract American tourists. Features highlighted included swimming pools, tennis courts, open-air dancing, nightly entertainment, and a gambling casino. The edition also covered congressional approval of emergency housing funds to assist victims of Midwest flooding.

El Evening Star informó que hoteles destacados de la República Dominicana ampliaban sus instalaciones recreativas para atraer turistas estadounidenses. Entre las comodidades resaltadas se contaban piscinas, canchas de tenis, baile al aire libre, espectáculos nocturnos y un casino de juegos. La edición también cubrió la aprobación por parte del Congreso de fondos de emergencia para viviendas destinados a socorrer a las víctimas de las inundaciones en el Medio Oeste.

Congress Votes Fund For Flood Housing By the Associated Press Congress yesterday approved spending Federal money for emer gency housing for Midwest flood victims and received proposals for financial aid in rebuilding flood-destroyed farms, homes, business and public property. The Senate gave the final nod to the emergency aid plan, pre viously approved by the House. It would authorize use of part of a $25 million emergency flood re lief fund voted by Congress to supply trailers and other tempo rary shelters. The measure, which now goes to the White House for approval, also would permit the Federal Housing Administration to insure low-cost homes—built to replace flood-destroyed houses—up to 100 per cent of their value. Present law limits such insurance to 95 per cent of value. Meantime, Senator Hennings and Representative Bolling, Mis souri Democrats, introduced bills to provide Federal aid for repair ing or rebuilding property dam aged or destroyed by the floods.
