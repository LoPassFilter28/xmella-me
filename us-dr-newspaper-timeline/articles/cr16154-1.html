A dispatch from Santo Domingo dated September 9 reports fresh trouble for the republic: owing to the Dominican government's alleged violation of the law governing foreign landholders, the French government has decided to postpone delivery of an exequatur to the new Dominican consul at Havre. Other very strong measures, it says, will be adopted by France if the Dominican government maintains its present attitude.

Un despacho de Santo Domingo fechado el 9 de septiembre informa de nuevos problemas para la república: a causa de la supuesta violación por el gobierno dominicano de la ley que rige a los propietarios extranjeros de tierras, el gobierno francés ha decidido posponer la entrega de un exequátur al nuevo cónsul dominicano en El Havre. Otras medidas muy enérgicas, dice, serán adoptadas por Francia si el gobierno dominicano mantiene su actitud actual.

Retailers Serial Number Scheme for Selling Proprietory Goods May Be Given a Trial. BOSTON, Mass., Sept. 9. — At to-day's session of the National Wholesale Druggists' Association Thomas V. Woolen, of Chicago, secretary of the National Association of Retail Druggists, presented the views of retail druggists of the country to the jobbers. The condition of the retail drug trade, he said, was unsatisfactory. There were 40,000 and more retail druggists in the United States. Thousands of them were prosperous, but all realized that at any moment they were likely to be reduced to the precarious living which is the lot of other thousands through the operations of the rate cutters.
