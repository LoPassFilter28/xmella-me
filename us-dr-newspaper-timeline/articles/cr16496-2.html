The Pittsburg Dispatch reported that reports confirming the alleged cession of Samaná Bay to the United States caused widespread excitement in Santo Domingo and alarm in Haiti. In response to United States Minister John S. Durham residing in Santo Domingo, the German government instructed its consul in Port-au-Prince to coordinate with British and French representatives to protect Dominican sovereignty.

El Pittsburg Dispatch informó que las noticias que confirmaban la supuesta cesión de la bahía de Samaná a los Estados Unidos provocaron una agitación generalizada en Santo Domingo y alarma en Haití. En respuesta a la presencia del ministro de los Estados Unidos, John S. Durham, residiendo en Santo Domingo, el gobierno alemán instruyó a su cónsul en Puerto Príncipe para que se coordinara con los representantes británico y francés con el fin de proteger la soberanía dominicana.

city from Kingston, Jamaica, states that great excitement has been caused in San Domingo by the announcement that the report that Samana Bay, in the western part of [illegible] the Sanchez Lavega Railway, Dominican Republic, had been ceded to the United States had been confirmed. The Haitian Minister, the dispatch adds, has become alarmed, owing to the fact that Mr. John S. Durham, the American Minister to Haiti and Charge d'Affaires to San Domingo, has taken a residence in the city of San Domingo. The German Consul at Port-au-Prince, the dispatch further says, has received instructions from his Government to come to an understanding with the representatives of France and England regarding the steps necessary to be taken to safeguard the autonomy of San Domingo. The news has caused great uneasiness in Haiti. CONTENTION TALK OF THE PRESS. The outlook at Minneapolis is still uncertain. No man can tell the outcome. New York World.
