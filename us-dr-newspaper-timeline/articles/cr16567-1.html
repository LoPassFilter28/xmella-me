Passage summaryResumen del pasaje

The *Evening Star* published a photograph and caption showing Latin American diplomats whose ranks were raised from minister to ambassador presenting their credentials to President Roosevelt at the White House. The newly accredited ambassadors included J. M. Troncoso of the Dominican Republic, André Liautaud of Haiti, Adrián Recinos of Guatemala, Héctor David Castro of El Salvador, León De Bayle of Nicaragua, and Julián R. Cáceres of Honduras.

El *Evening Star* insertó un grabado fotográfico con su respectivo texto explicativo mostrando a los jefes de misión latinoamericanos ascendidos de ministros a embajadores al momento de presentar cartas credenciales ante el mandatario Roosevelt en la Casa Blanca. Los diplomáticos recién reconocidos comprendían a J. M. Troncoso por la República Dominicana, André Liautaud por Haití, Adrián Recinos por Guatemala, Héctor David Castro por El Salvador, León De Bayle por Nicaragua y Julián R. Cáceres por Honduras.

D. C. Skipper Dies

In Schooner Wreck

Coast Guard Recovers

Body of Capt. Wrightson

The sinking of a schooner and

the death of its captain—Walter

Wrightson, 70, of 523 Eleventh street

NW.—were reported today from

Cape May, N. J., where two survivors

from the vessel and the body of the

captain were brought to shore by

Coast Guards.
