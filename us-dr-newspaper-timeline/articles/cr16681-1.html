The Star prints part of the profile of General Deschamps, an official of the Dominican republicEl Star publica parte del perfil del general Deschamps, un funcionario de la república dominicana

The Star prints part of the profile of General Deschamps, an official of the Dominican republic in the government of President Alejandro Woss y Gil up to a few days ago, when he resigned his position to join his former chief, ex-President Juan Isidro Jiminez: General Deschamps is one of the most popular men in the Dominican republic; his popularity is not, however, due to military prowess, but to the fact that he has always been the people's friend, is a great lover of progress, has always had the tact to espouse the right cause, and above all because he has been the victim of the most terrible persecution; due to his bold opposition to President Heureaux's sanguinary and corrupted policy he was forced to leave Santo Domingo and live abroad, where he had to suffer many hardships; although living in exile he did not slacken in his attacks upon President Heureaux's policy, and about seven years ago, while residing in Ponce, Porto Rico, an assassin paid by President Heureaux to assassinate him drove a dagger into his back, very nearly killing him; the assassin escaped and Deschamps slowly recovered, but he has never succeeded in making the wound heal thoroughly, and it is a constant source of annoyance to him; at another time his wife, who had accompanied him in exile, embarked in a small schooner at the British island of Grand Turk for Puerto Plata, but on her arrival at that port she was not permitted to disembark, and though she was accompanied by her children and the eldest boy took ill on board, still she was not permitted to disembark.El Star publica parte del perfil del general Deschamps, un funcionario de la república dominicana en el gobierno del presidente Alejandro Woss y Gil hasta hace pocos días, cuando renunció a su cargo para unirse a su antiguo jefe, el expresidente Juan Isidro Jiménez: el general Deschamps es uno de los hombres más populares de la república dominicana; su popularidad no se debe, sin embargo, a sus proezas militares, sino al hecho de que siempre ha sido amigo del pueblo, es un gran amante del progreso, siempre ha tenido el tacto de abrazar la causa correcta y, sobre todo, porque ha sido víctima de la más terrible persecución; debido a su audaz oposición a la política sanguinaria y corrupta del presidente Heureaux se vio obligado a salir de Santo Domingo y vivir en el extranjero, donde tuvo que sufrir muchas penurias; aunque vivía en el exilio no aflojó en sus ataques contra la política del presidente Heureaux, y hace unos siete años, mientras residía en Ponce, Puerto Rico, un asesino pagado por el presidente Heureaux para asesinarlo le clavó un puñal en la espalda, casi matándolo; el asesino escapó y Deschamps se recuperó lentamente, pero nunca ha logrado que la herida sane por completo, y es para él una fuente constante de molestia; en otra ocasión su esposa, que lo había acompañado en el exilio, se embarcó en una pequeña goleta en la isla británica de Gran Turca rumbo a Puerto Plata, pero a su llegada a ese puerto no se le permitió desembarcar, y aunque la acompañaban sus hijos y el niño mayor enfermó a bordo, aun así no se le permitió desembarcar.

Evening Star. Washington, D.C., November 25, 1903.
