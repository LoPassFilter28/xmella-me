The Evening Star reported on U.S. wartime cooperation and mutual aid programs established across Latin America. Cooperative health programs to control malaria were launched in eighteen nations, including the Dominican Republic and Haiti, to sustain the production of vital war materials. The article emphasized that these initiatives, along with agricultural and technical assistance, were mutually beneficial cooperative efforts rather than unilateral handouts.

El Evening Star abordó los planes cooperativos y de auxilio mutuo desplegados por Estados Unidos en Hispanoamérica en el contexto bélico. Se estructuraron programas sanitarios mixtos para el control del paludismo en dieciocho Estados, incluidas la República Dominicana y Haití, con el fin de asegurar el rendimiento continuo en la extracción de insumos estratégicos para la contienda. El informe recalcó que estos planes, junto al respaldo técnico y agrícola, obedecían a esquemas bilaterales equitativos de beneficio común y no a dádivas discrecionales unilaterales.

Health programs were established in 18 countries because, without controlling malaria, workers in Paraguay and Uruguay, the Dominican Republic and Haiti could never have been able to produce goods and materials needed for the war. Our men helped establish food-supply programs in Costa Rica, Panama, Honduras and elsewhere for the same reason, and to help supply our own troops and civilian workers at air and naval bases. Vocational education, to cite only one example, helped provide a pool of skilled Latin American manpower for the emergency. When we couldn't send rolling stock needed by Mexican railroads, we did find Spanish speaking experts who showed how twice as much freight could be carried with the same equipment and got Mexican workers to help pick crops in manpower-short areas. We've helped develop rubber plantations in the Amazon, fisheries in the Caribbean, GLAMOUR: Colorful personalities enjoy Rio's fashionable night clubs vegetable oil farms in Brazil. Our Import Export Bank has loaned and taken back, right on time, many millions more dollars than anybody thought could be handled.
