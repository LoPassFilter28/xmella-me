was Jimenez, son of an island hidalgo and a former President. Mr. Jimenez began business in Monte Cristi, accumulating a fortune and becoming popular with his countrymen. But with the increasing popularity of any of the islanders a corresponding distrust arose in the mind of the President. Jimenez went to Hayti, from which through the influence of Heureaux again he was forced to take refuge, this time in Europe. However, Jimenez kept secretly in touch with the islanders who regarded him as their future ruler, communications being smuggled in and out, made up [illegible] in the false bottoms of samples of coffee, and he bided his time till events shaped themselves to his ends. HEUREAUX'S DOWNFALL. It was not until 1899 that President Heureaux reaped as he had sown and was assassinated at Moca in August by a youth of one of the most influential families, an adherent of Jimenez, and now occupying high office under the new Government.
