Passage summaryResumen del pasaje

The *New-York Tribune* reported from San Juan that the newly installed administration of Dominican President Horacio Vásquez was facing severe internal collapse. Private correspondence from Santo Domingo alleged that the president suffered from mental instability and that the national treasury was so exhausted that government employees received only half pay. Dispatches indicated widespread loss of public confidence and increasing emigration, with observers predicting the regime could fall within months.

El *New-York Tribune* informó desde San Juan que la recién instaurada administración del presidente dominicano Horacio Vásquez afrontaba un grave colapso interno.
