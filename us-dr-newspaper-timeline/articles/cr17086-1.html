1851-05-10 · [New Orleans, La.]

ONE DAY LATER FROM HAYTI.

SourceFuente: The daily crescent, [New Orleans, La.], 1851-05-10
https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn82015378/1851-05-10/ed-1/?sp=1
shipping_vessels_navaldiplomacy_treatiessugar_trade_economy

ONE DAY LATER FROM HAYTI.—A letter from Porte-au-Prince, dated April 10, published in the New York Journal of Commerce, says that the Haytien Congress have not yet made a decision on the offer of mediation between the Haytiens and Dominicans, by the three commercial powers. This is one day later than the report that the offered mediation had been rejected. A Boston dispatch of the 1st inst. has the following:

The schooner October, from Porte-au-Prince, with advices to April 13, arrived here to-day. It is currently reported at St. Domingo that the Chambers had refused to comply with the demands of the American Commissioner, or to make peace. Emperor Soulouque was preparing to march on St. Domingo with all his forces. He had dismissed his Cabinet, and replaced them with men who were in favor of war.

The steamboat General Taylor arrived at Port-au-Prince on the 10th April, and was offered for sale.
