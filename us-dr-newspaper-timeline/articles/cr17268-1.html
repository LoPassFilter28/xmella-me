Certain claims of our citizens which were declared to be without

The revolution in Hayti against the established government has terminated. While it was in progress it became necessary to enforce our neutrality laws by instituting proceedings against individuals and vessels charged with their infringements. These precautions were, in all cases, successful.

The government has more than once been called upon of late to take action in the fulfillment of its international obligation toward Spain, the agitation of the island of Cuba hostile to the Spanish crown having been fermented by persons abusing the sacred rights of hospitality which our territory affords. The officers of the government have been instructed to exercise vigilance to prevent an infraction of our neutrality laws at Key West and at other points near the Cuban coast. I am happy to say that in the only instance where these precautionary measures were successfully eluded, the offenders when found in our territory were subsequently tried and convicted. The growing need of close relationship of intercourse and traffic between the Spanish Antilles and their natural market in the United States, led to the adoption in January last of a commercial agreement looking to that end. The agreement has since been superseded by a more carefully framed and comprehensive covenant, which I shall submit to the senate for approval. It has been the aim of this negotiation to open such of the reciprocal exchange of productions carried under the flag of either country as to make the intercourse between Cuba and Porto Rico and ourselves scarcely less intimate than the commercial movement between our domestic ships, and to insure a removal of the burdens on shipping in the Spanish Indies of which, in the past, our ship owners and ship masters have so often had cause to complain.

The negotiation of this convention has for a time postponed the prosecution of certain claims of our citizens which were declared to be without the jurisdiction of the Spanish American commission, to and which we therefore remitted to diplomatic channels for adjustment, The speedy settlement of these claims will not be urged by this government.

Negotiations for a treaty of commerce reciprocity with the Dominican republic have been successfully concluded and the result will shortly be laid before the senate.
