HOSTILITIES BETWEEN DENMARK AND DOMINICA.—New York, April 6.—Letters from St. Domingo of the 15th of March announce hostilities between Dominica and Denmark. A Danish Envoy, who recently arrived in a war vessel, peremptorily demanded $150,000 as indemnity for the seizure of two Danish vessels by President Baez during the recent revolution in Dominica. President Santana offered to refer the matter to arbitrators, but the Danish functionary declined, giving the Dominicans only twenty-four hours to comply with his demands, with the understanding that a refusal would be followed by a blockade of the port.
