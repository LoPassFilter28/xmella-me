NEW YORK, August 22.—The August 2d St. Thomas advice gives further particulars of the matter heretofore mentioned, regarding the new issue raised by General Gandara, the commander of the Spanish troops, with the Dominican Government, apparently only for the purpose of having a pretext to maintain a foothold with his forces in the republic. The Spanish Cortes, in May last, decreed that the attempt by Spain to subjugate St. Domingo should be abandoned, and that the island should be immediately evacuated by their military forces. The evacuation soon after commenced, but has since dragged along so slowly that it is not yet nearly completed. It appears that Gen. Gandara does not intend it shall be, since he insists on an insertion in the treaty of peace of a clause securing protection to Spanish sympathizers in the Dominican Republic. This the Dominicans naturally refused to grant, and Gen. Gandara now threatens to attack them if they do not agree to the proposed terms.
