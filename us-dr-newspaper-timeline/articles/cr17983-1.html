Based on the provided newspaper excerpt, the Dominican Government was making extensive preparations at St. Domingo City to repel an anticipated Haytian invasion by fitting out a fleet, enrolling every male inhabitant, and repairing fortifications. Additionally, the British Royal Mail Steamship *Teviot* arrived from Port-au-Prince carrying the ratification of a treaty between the British and Dominican governments.

A partir del fragmento publicado, el gobierno dominicano adelantaba enérgicos aprestos en la ciudad de Santo Domingo a fin de rechazar una inminente invasión haitiana, armando una escuadrilla, alistando a todos los varones y rehabilitando fortines. Asimismo, el vapor del Real Correo Británico *Teviot* fondeó procedente de Puerto Príncipe portando la ratificación del tratado ajustado entre los gabinetes británico y dominicano.
