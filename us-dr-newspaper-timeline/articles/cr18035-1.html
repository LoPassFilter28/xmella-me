The Sun highlighted the strategic value of Samana Bay as a coaling station along the Mona Passage between Santo Domingo and Puerto Rico, citing its sheltered anchorage and defensible geography. The article reviewed the rejection of President Grant's annexation project 28 years prior, which led the Dominican Republic to refuse any sale, cession, or lease of its territory. It emphasized that current American interest did not contemplate territorial annexation or permanent fortifications.

El artículo repasó el rechazo al proyecto de anexión del presidente Grant ocurrido 28 años atrás, lo que llevó a la República Dominicana a negarse a cualquier venta, cesión o arrendamiento de su territorio. Subrayó que el interés estadounidense en ese momento no contemplaba anexiones territoriales ni fortificaciones permanentes.

An American company held land in the neighborhood many years ago, and a coal pile was established on Carenero Island. Samana Bay not only gives anchorage enough for the fleets of the world, but it is exceedingly well situated for our purpose. It is true that Mole St. Nicolas, on the Windward Passage, between Hayti and Cuba, has strategic advantages, that route being shorter from our ports to the Isthmus, but Samana Bay lies on the Mona Passage, a route which is taken by the merchant vessels that go to South America between San Domingo and Porto Rico. It is healthful, under the trade winds which blow into it, and it is capable of defence, its narrow entrance being commanded by Levantada Island, while the bold headlands on the north shore give a plunging fire. But we are to assume that there is no question whatever of fortifications now, since no annexation of territory is undertaken, nor even any lease from the Dominican Government. It will be remembered that the old project of acquiring Samana Bay as a coaling station, under President Grant's Administration, about twenty-eight years ago, developed into the form of annexing all San Domingo, and this the Senate did not approve. But afterward the little republic took the ground not only that she would not be annexed, but that she would not sell, cede, or even lease us any territory at all.
