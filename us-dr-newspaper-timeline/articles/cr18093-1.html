It is said that President Fillmore has expressed himself in favor of recognizing the independence both of Hayti and the Dominican republic; but Congress will never consent to the formal recognition of the Haytien government. But although diplomatic relations may not be established with Hayti, it will make a serious question with the United States if France should reclaim possession of the Island of St. Domingo.
