The Evening Star reported that President Rafael Trujillo directed relief operations across the Dominican Republic following a severe earthquake, deploying the military, police, and Red Cross to stricken communities including Matanzas, Nagua, Moca, and San Francisco de Macoris. Santiago suffered extensive building damage and one death, while another casualty occurred in San Francisco de Macoris. Neighboring Haiti reported minor effects in Port-au-Prince but significant destruction across northern border towns.

El Evening Star informó que el mandatario Rafael Trujillo encabezó personalmente las labores de rescate en toda la República Dominicana a raíz del feroz terremoto, comisionando tropas del ejército, agentes policiales y brigadas de la Cruz Roja a las poblaciones más flageladas, entre ellas Matanzas, Nagua, Moca y San Francisco de Macorís. En Santiago se verificaron serios derrumbes y una víctima fatal, registrándose otro muerto en San Francisco de Macorís. En el colindante territorio de Haití se sintieron efectos menores en Puerto Príncipe, pero se reportaron considerables averías en los distritos de la frontera norte.

Chambers of Commerce of the Spanish and Dominican colonies and the Association of Architects and Engineers have offered their co-operation to the government in saving and reconstructing damaged buildings. Advices from Haiti, where communications have been reestablished, said Sunday's quake did little damage to Port au Prince, but caused considerable damage in the smaller towns. In the northern section of Haiti nearest the Dominican Republic more than 50 houses were reported badly damaged and several churches were devastated.
