GARY, Ind., May 2. — (United Press). — Gary police tonight virtually gave up the theory that the Chesterton murder victim was Mrs. Josephine Desiderio, 23, of Gary, although they have not been able to locate her. Miss Florence Kish, of South Bend, Indiana, a chum of Mrs. Desiderio, told police she believed the woman and her husband are in Detroit. A dentist also told police that he had a chart of Mrs. Desiderio's teeth, and this chart does not correspond to the teeth of the victim.
