Passage summaryResumen del pasaje

The *Casper daily tribune* reported that the U.S. State Department concluded diplomatic exchanges of notes to establish commercial modus vivendi arrangements with several nations. The Dominican Republic was listed among the participating countries, alongside Brazil, Czechoslovakia, Estonia, Greece, Guatemala, Nicaragua, and Poland.

El *Casper daily tribune* informó que el Departamento de Estado de Estados Unidos concertó intercambios de notas diplomáticas para establecer acuerdos comerciales de *modus vivendi* con diversas naciones. La República Dominicana figuraba entre los países participantes, junto con Brasil, Checoslovaquia, Estonia, Grecia, Guatemala, Nicaragua y Polonia.
