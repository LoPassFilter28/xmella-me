The Evening Star reported that Secretary of State Cordell Hull commended diplomat Hugh R. Wilson for completing confidential assignments, which included negotiating a new customs treaty with the Dominican Republic. The treaty was understood to be ready for submission to the U.S. Senate for ratification. Wilson had previously served as Ambassador to Germany and held various diplomatic posts before focusing on wartime issues in Europe and Latin America.

El Evening Star informó que el secretario de Estado Cordell Hull felicitó al diplomático Hugh R. Wilson por la culminación de misiones reservadas, entre las cuales figuró la negociación de un nuevo tratado de aduanas con la República Dominicana. Se daba por hecho que el instrumento internacional estaba listo para ser sometido al Senado de EE. UU. para su debida ratificación. Wilson se había desempeñado previamente como embajador en Alemania y ocupado diversos cargos diplomáticos antes de volcarse a asuntos bélicos en Europa y América Latina.

Hull praising Mr. Wilson's services and saying "certain important and confidential work" which he had undertaken "has now been satisfactorily completed." Mr. Hull's letter did not indicate the nature of the work to which he referred. Mr. Wilson recently negotiated a new customs treaty with the Dominican Republic which is understood now to be ready for submission to the Senate for ratification. His work at the State Department also has been concerned with special problems arising from the war in Europe. Mr. Wilson, who is 55 years old. entered the foreign service in 1911 and has held posts in Lisbon, Guatemala. Buenos Aires, Vienna. Berne and Tokio, as well as Berlin. He also served as chief of the Division of Current Information and as an Assistant Secretary at the State Department. As a protest against Nazi anti-Semitic and other policies, Mr. Wilson was called home from Berlin "for report and consultation" in November, 1938. The German Ambassador was recalled to Berlin soon thereafter. There has been no indication that a new Ambassador to Germany would be appointed.
