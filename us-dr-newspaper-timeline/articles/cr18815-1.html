Puerto Plata cables confirm the repulse of the revolutionary landing at Monte Cristi: the leaders killed or escaped aboard the steamer Fanita, prisoners taken to be shot, and Heureaux on his way from the capital with three men-of-war.

Cables de Puerto Plata confirman el rechazo del desembarco revolucionario en Monte Cristi: los jefes muertos o escapados a bordo del vapor Fanita, prisioneros tomados para ser fusilados, y Heureaux en camino desde la capital con tres buques de guerra.

[illegible] of the reports of the [illegible] of the revolutionary party at Monte Christi. General Augustin Morales and Romulo [illegible] were killed. Five of the insurgents were taken prisoner and shot this morning. Three others escaped in the steamer Fanita. President Heureaux left the capital this morning with three men-of-war. The correspondent, in concluding his dispatch, says the revolutionary movement is regarded at Puerto Plata as of no consequence. The Dominican consul here at Cape Haytien reasserts that Jiminez escaped on the Fanita. He says that had Jiminez been captured President Heureaux would have informed him. This afternoon the Dominican consul here received a dispatch from President Heureaux saying that an attempted revolution at Monte Christi had been defeated, that General Jiminez fled at the first fire of the government troops, taking refuge on board the Fanita, and that all the others who landed, with the exception of three, had been killed. Two of the prisoners are Pablo Villanueva, son of General Villanueva, and a laborer named Calderon. They will be executed today with the other prisoners. The Haytien general Morales and seven Dominicans who shipped here are among the killed. Another person was killed on board the steamer Fanita, which put to sea immediately and escaped.
