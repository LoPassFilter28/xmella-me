The Abbeville Press and Banner reported that the Dominican government reported general tranquillity despite the assassins of President Heureaux remaining at large, though commerce struggled under an unresolved paper currency crisis. The paper also noted catastrophic hurricane damage and river flooding in San Pedro de Macoris, Samana, and Puerto Plata. Meanwhile, American customs authorities at Baracoa, Cuba, intercepted 25 men and seized 143 rifles with ammunition destined for Dominican insurgents.

The Abbeville Press and Banner informó que el gobierno dominicano reportaba tranquilidad general a pesar de que los asesinos del presidente Heureaux continuaban prófugos, mientras el comercio batallaba contra una crisis monetaria de papel no resuelta. El periódico también dio cuenta de pérdidas catastróficas por huracanes e inundaciones de ríos en San Pedro de Macorís, Samaná y Puerto Plata. Mientras tanto, las autoridades aduaneras estadounidenses en Baracoa, Cuba, interceptaron a 25 hombres e incautaron 143 fusiles con municiones destinados a los insurgentes dominicanos.

Government officials, tranquillity reigns throughout the country. The assassins of President Heureaux are still at large, but measures have been taken for their capture. It is said the band numbers sixteen. Although the rate for drafts is falling, and there are some signs of returning confidence, business continues dull, and imports are limited. The financial situation is bad. The problem of taking up the paper currency has not yet been solved, as the people in general do not wish it taken up. This is the real cause of dullness in business. Once the matter has been arranged, affairs should take on a different aspect. The latest news from Macoris regarding the effects of the hurricane shows that the damage was more serious than was at first supposed. The river overflowed its banks and inundated the ground. Many families had abandoned their houses for the time and boats were used in the streets to carry people from one point to another. The storm did severe damage in the country about Samana and Puerto Plata. The United States cruiser New Orleans has left San Domingo Harbor for Porto Rico. The United States gunboat Machias is still here. Watch on Filibusters. HAVANA (By Cable). — Lieutenant Schaum, Collector of Customs at Baracoa, in the Province of Santiago de Cuba, has seized 143 carbines and 18,000 rounds of ammunition that were evidently intended for San Domingo.
