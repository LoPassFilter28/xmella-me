The southern press — 1851-07-19

By an arrival here to-day, we have dates from St. Domingo to the 30th. The markets were glutted with American provisions. Coffee was much depressed. The excessive issue of government paper money, has had a disastrous effect on business.

The political affairs are rather tranquil.

The emperor's address to the Dominicans has had a good effect.

The coronation of the emperor is soon to be solemnized, when a general amnesty is to be granted, and the exiles invited back.
