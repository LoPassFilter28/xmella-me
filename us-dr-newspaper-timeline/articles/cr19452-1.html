SummaryResumen

General Juan Isidro Jimenez, aspirant to the presidency of the republic of Dominica, says several Dominican generals have already declared in his favor, in spite of his advice to them to delay definite action. He denies the Dominican government has 3,700 troops under arms, and asserts the present executive will offer no resistance, owing to the scarcity of resources and sympathizers.

El general Juan Isidro Jiménez, aspirante a la presidencia de la república de Dominica, dice que varios generales dominicanos ya se han declarado a su favor, a pesar de su consejo de que retrasaran una acción definitiva. Niega que el gobierno dominicano tenga 3.700 soldados en armas, y afirma que el actual ejecutivo no ofrecerá resistencia, debido a la escasez de recursos y de simpatizantes.
