—The steamer Black Warrior, with Havana dates to the 16th instant, arrived at New York on Monday night. It was reported in Havana that a Spanish brig-of-war has 6,000 stand of arms on board, destined for the use of the Spanish faction against the Dominican republic, to be taken down in a few days by an officer of the Spanish army, and that the annexation of the west end will be insisted upon by the Spanish as another gem to the Spanish crown. Santa Anna is expected to arrive at Havana on his way to Spain. Gen. Concha issued an order suspending all action of Spain as soon as the expected orders to that effect arrive from Madrid.
