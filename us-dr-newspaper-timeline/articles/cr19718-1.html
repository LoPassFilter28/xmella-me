The Evening Star reported the inauguration of a transitional Council of State in Santo Domingo, with opposition leader Rafael Bonnelly sworn in as vice president to eventually succeed President Joaquín Balaguer. The seven-member council included Antonio Imbert and Luis Amiama Tió, the sole surviving conspirators of the plot that assassinated Rafael Trujillo.

El Evening Star informó sobre la juramentación de un Consejo de Estado de transición en Santo Domingo, en el cual el líder opositor Rafael Bonnelly prestó juramento como vicepresidente con el cometido de suceder eventualmente al presidente Joaquín Balaguer. El consejo de siete miembros incluía a Antonio Imbert y a Luis Amiama Tió, los únicos conspiradores sobrevivientes de la trama que dio muerte a Rafael Trujillo.

May Take Over Within Week Sworn in as Vice President of the council, Mr. Bonnelly will take over from Mr. Balaguer when the latter resigns, a possibility within a week. Mr. Balaguer drew up the plan for an interim government and promised to quit when the Organization of American States lifts its economic and diplomatic sanctions against the Dominican Republic. Following lukewarm approval of the council plan by opposition factions, President Kennedy announced that the United States favored lifting the sanctions imposed against the old Trujillo regime for plotting to assassinate President Romulo Betancourt of Venezuela. Mr. Bonnelly represents the National Civic Union, major opposition faction which agreed to give the Balaguer plan a chance after weeks of off-and-on negotiations. Rioting Recalled Mr. Balaguer is a holdover from the Trujillo reign whose efforts to form a transitional government were marked by weeks of rioting and a nationwide general strike. He appealed in a radio address “to all men of good will and patriotism” to help the council install democracy.
