'Dominican rebels have cut the cables': Washington got no news from the republic yesterday; preparations rush for the Prairie's sailing with 750 marines, and Minister Peynado expects the rebels to reopen the custom houses at the mere news of their coming.

«Los rebeldes dominicanos han cortado los cables»: Washington no recibió ayer noticias de la república; se apresuran los preparativos para la salida del Prairie con 750 marines, y el ministro Peynado espera que los rebeldes reabran las aduanas con la sola noticia de su llegada.

Washington. Sept 35. Washington

rations went forward actively for the

sailing of the transport Prsirie from

Dominican customs houses along the

border of Haiti. Practically all of the

men who will make up the expedition
