What About the Past In the minds of foreigners comparing the Dominican Republic with its neighbors the question often arises: could the country have progressed as much under democratic rule in the past 31 years? Trujillo’s defenders—and there are still many here—point to material gains, especially in construction works, and the cleanliness that seem to give his country a more progressive air than other small nations in the area. His critics allege this progress was made at the cost of many lives, with the loss of dignity and liberty that bred cultural, social and political backwardness. In any case the dynasty is gone.
