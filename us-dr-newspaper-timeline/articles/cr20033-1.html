The Morning Astorian reported on the breakdown of the joint boundary commission that convened at Anses-à-Pitres to define the Haitian-Dominican border based on 1874 territorial positions. Negotiations collapsed after Dominican delegates insisted on conditional compliance and Dominican troops moved into vacated frontier points, prompting Haitian accusations of bad faith. Tensions escalated sharply following armed confrontations at Philobert near the Massacre River, where Haitian border outposts repelled attacks from Dominican territory.

El Morning Astorian informó sobre la ruptura de la comisión limítrofe conjunta que se reunió en Anses-à-Pitres para definir la frontera domínico-haitiana tomando como base las posiciones territoriales de 1874. Las negociaciones fracasaron luego de que los delegados dominicanos insistieran en un cumplimiento condicional y las tropas dominicanas avanzaran hacia puntos fronterizos desocupados, lo que motivó acusaciones haitianas de mala fe. Las tensiones se intensificaron bruscamente tras choques armados en Philobert, cerca del río Masacre, donde los puestos fronterizos haitianos repelieron ataques procedentes de territorio dominicano.

Dominican commission with full powers to define and settle the boundaries between the two republics, such boundaries to be based on the respective positions in [illegible]. This commission met at Anses-a-Pitres In January [illegible] and Immediately set to work. In spite of explicit terms of the Stm-Heureux convention, which stipulated that the uti possidetis of [illegible] should be taken as the base for the delimitation, the Dominican commissioners openly declared that they would only abide by the provisions of this clause so long as it would be favorable to their Interests. In order to explain this stand on the part of the Dominican representatives it might be stated that. Immediately after the signing of the convention, the Dominican government had caused to be occupied several points along the disputed frontier, which occupation the Haytiens regarded as an encroachment on their territory at points from which Haytien troops had been withdrawn in order to facilitate an amicable settlement of the dispute. In the end this mixed commission separated without having In any way improved the situation. The incident that has recently occurred at Philobert on the northern frontier, and which greatly endangers the friendly relations of the sister republics is considered by the Haytiens to be premeditated provocation on the part of the Dominicans. The Haytien posts established In that district to put down smuggling and brigandage from over the border have had to defend themselves against repeated attacks. [illegible]
