- The Spanish brigantine of war, the 'Habanero,' under the command of Don Carlos Valcarcel, had arrived at St. Domingo from Port Rico, for the purpose of felicitating the President of the Republic, in the name of her Catholic Majesty. The Progreso says 'It is very agreeable for the Dominicans to see floating on their shores the flag of a friendly nation, and to see pass through their streets the sons of a fraternal people, of whom our fathers have told us so much.' The brigantine was soon to sail for Port Rico.
