The career of President Caceres of Santo Domingo is reviewedLa trayectoria del presidente Cáceres de Santo Domingo es examinada

The career of President Caceres of Santo Domingo is reviewed in a feature in The Shoshone Journal, following it through a series of political tragedies, beginning in 1884 when his father was executed by order of President Heureaux. After studying and graduating from the Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute in Troy, New York, and marrying Lillian Hakes (printed earlier as 'U?kes'), Caceres returned to Santo Domingo. His political career began in earnest when he avenged his father by assassinating Heureaux on July 28, 1899, facilitating General Jimenez's rise to the presidency. After serving as vice president under President Morales, Caceres was installed in the presidency following Morales's flight from the country in 1905.La trayectoria del presidente Cáceres de Santo Domingo es examinada en un artículo en el Shoshone Journal, siguiéndola a través de una serie de tragedias políticas, comenzando en 1884 cuando su padre fue ejecutado por orden del presidente Heureaux. Tras estudiar y graduarse en el Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute en Troy, Nueva York, y contraer matrimonio con Lillian Hakes (impreso antes como «U?kes»), Cáceres regresó a Santo Domingo. Su carrera política comenzó formalmente cuando vengó a su padre asesinando a Heureaux el 28 de julio de 1899, lo que facilitó la llegada del general Jiménez a la presidencia. Tras desempeñarse como vicepresidente bajo el presidente Morales, Cáceres asumió la presidencia tras la huida de Morales del país en 1905.

Shoshone Journal. Shoshone, Idaho, January 6, 1911.
