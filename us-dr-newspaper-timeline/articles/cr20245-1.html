Annexation of foreign territory to another nation ought never to take place except by a common agreement between the annexer and the annexed. The annexations to the United States have not been conducted on such principles, unfortunately for our principles. The monarchs of Spain and France sold us their possessions at the South without consulting their inhabitants, because they wanted to get rid of them, as they cost them more than they were worth. The people of Texas generally did desire annexation, but it would not have been accomplished when it was but for the determination of the Democratic party to procure more territory in which to extend slavery and create larger representation in the senate and house for the slave holding aristocracy. A bloody war had to be fought to secure this end, which might, at a later period, have been secured without war and with the deliberate and formal consent of Mexico as well as that of the people of Texas. When Alaska was annexed, there were no people to consult worth speaking of, and the advantage or disadvantage to the United States or the Alaskans is a matter of such minute consideration, that there is no need of trying to exhibit the moral or political principle of the transaction except under a microscope. The price we had to pay for it is big enough to see without common spectacles. If the authors of the project for annexing St. Domingo had been able to show that the Dominicans were largely in favor of it, they would have been able to make a much better show in congress and among the people of the United States. The other project so often broached, of annexing Canada to the Union--a most undesirable thing, as we have always thought--can never be accomplished except on the earnest petition of the Canadians, and then only with guarantees that the Federal government shall not be bound by the restrictions of the old treaty between Great Britain and France, which maintains a wealthy and powerful hierarchy inside of a political authority, and leads to the chief disorders that are continually occurring in the Dominion.
