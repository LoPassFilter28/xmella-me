THE DOMINICAN REPUBLIC. —We learn from the

Gaeeta Ofieial, of San Domingo city, that the

Dominican government has published a protest in

regard to the recent ceremony of saluting the

French and English Consulates, declaring it to

have been done under compulsion.

The military force of Louisiana, according to the

Adjutant General's report, consists of 5 divisions,

11 brigades, 68 regimtnts, 169 companies, 88,137

non-commissioned officers, musicians and privates,

and a total rank and file of 91,179.

It is stated that Governor Stewart, of Missouri,

has refused to sign the bill passed by the Legisla

ture for the exclusion of the free colored population

from that State. It is also said Gov. Perry, of

Florida, has declined signing a bill of similar pro

visions.

A special commission appointed by the Legisla

lature of Virginia to make out an estimate of the

cost of arming and equipping a brigade of five

thousand men, made their report on Saturday last,

in which they state the amount necessary at $148,-

342.

In a petition recently presented to the Ohio Legis

lature, praying for relief against the ravages of

dogs among sheep, it is asserted that not less than

$150,000 worth of sheep is annually destroyed by

these worse than useless brutes.
