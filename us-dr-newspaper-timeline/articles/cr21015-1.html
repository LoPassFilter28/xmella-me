The Springfield Weekly Republican reported that while the Dominican modus vivendi failed to protect President Morales from deposition, it brought financial stability and generated $3,000,000 on deposit in New York. The fiscal agreement enabled improved administration, suppressed smuggling, and provided four U.S.-built coast guard cutters to guard customs. Secretary Root subsequently restructured the treaty, supported by Finance Minister Federico Velasquez's agreement with creditors for a 50 percent debt reduction in cash.

The Springfield Weekly Republican informó que, aunque el modus vivendi dominicano no logró proteger al presidente Morales de la destitución, aportó estabilidad financiera y generó 3.000.000 de dólares en depósitos en Nueva York. El acuerdo fiscal facilitó una mejor administración, reprimió el contrabando y suministró cuatro guardacostas construidos en los Estados Unidos para vigilar las aduanas. Posteriormente, el secretario Root reestructuró el tratado, apoyado en el acuerdo del ministro de Hacienda Federico Velásquez con los acreedores para una reducción de deuda del 50 por ciento en efectivo.

Dominican creditors. The modus vivendi did not save Morales from political destruction, for he was overcome and deported by the revolutionists, and his vice president, Gen Caceres, installed as president. But the modus vivendi did result very beneficially so far as the little republic's finances were concerned. For not only did it lead to the deposit in New York of $3,000,090, which will now be placed to the credit of the Dominican government, but that government has had more available money during the period of operation of the modus vivendi than at any other time in its history. This state of affairs resulted from a more economical administration, the suppression of smuggling and the encouragement of business. The Dominican government also finds itself in possession of four fine coast guard cutters built in the United States, and forming an effective force for the protection of the customs revenues and available for all public uses. The substance of the modus vivendi in the shape of a treaty was pending before the United States Senate when Secretary Root assumed office. Recognizing the force of the objections that developed to its ratification, he set about recasting the document, and February 19 last the treaty which has just been ratified was submitted to the Senate.
