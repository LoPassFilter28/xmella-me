This travel feature describes the Dominican capital as a place where automobiles are plentiful and the ride to San Cristobal along the new macadam boulevard is most attractive, with cars to be had for four dollars an hour. It notes several hotels, of which the Hotel Francis is the best known, a large foreign colony at the capital and in Macoris and Puerto Plata, a trip from New York of about eight or nine days stopping at all ports, and a climate it calls wrongly thought unhealthy, with no serious epidemic since the cholera outbreak of the early sixties.

Señala varios hoteles, de los cuales el Hotel Francis es el más conocido, una numerosa colonia extranjera en la capital y en Macorís y Puerto Plata, un viaje desde Nueva York de unos ocho o nueve días con escala en todos los puertos, y un clima que califica de erróneamente tenido por insalubre, sin ninguna epidemia grave desde el brote de cólera de principios de los años sesenta.

The whole trip from New York, including a stop at all ports, is about eight or nine days. A very erroneous impression prevails that the climate of Santo Domingo is unhealthy. From its geographical position it cannot be unhealthy.
