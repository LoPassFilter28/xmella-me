The Evening Star reported that Dominican President Heureaux issued a monetary decree requiring import duties to be paid fifty percent in gold. The decree also fixed the legal tender valuation of the Mexican silver peso at 55 cents to stabilize revenues against falling global silver prices. Dominican newspapers anticipated further increases in customs tariffs during the upcoming legislative session.

El Evening Star informó que el presidente dominicano Heureaux emitió un decreto monetario que exigía que los derechos de importación se pagaran en un cincuenta por ciento en oro. El decreto también fijó el valor de curso legal del peso de plata mexicano en 55 centavos para estabilizar los ingresos fiscales frente a la caída de los precios mundiales de la plata. Los periódicos dominicanos preveían mayores aumentos en los aranceles aduaneros durante el siguiente período de sesiones legislativas.

Santo Domingo advices are to the effect that President Hereaux has decreed that hereafter import duties are to be collected half in gold; the value of the Mexican peso, or dollar, is fixed at 55 cents, and it is declared legal tender at that figure. Semi-official newspapers of the Dominican capital also forecast important changes in the customs tariff at the next session of congress, an increase of duties being considered unavoidable, should silver continue at its present low price. An immense petition, signed by the coffee and cacao exporting houses of Guayaquil, has been presented to the Ecuadorian congress, in opposition to the export tax upon these products proposed by the executive in his late message. Gold Mining Interests. Rich gold discoveries have lately been made in Counani, the disputed territory situated between French Guayana and Brazil. By employing but the most primitive methods, single prospectors are known to have secured 200 to 250 ounces of dust per week. Regardless of Brazil's rights in the premises, hundreds of French gold hunters have already entered the territory, and are rapidly taking up or squatting upon all the best claims.
