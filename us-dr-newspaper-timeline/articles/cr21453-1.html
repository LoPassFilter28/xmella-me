Senate Agrees to Pass on Bill Tomorrow. The Senate is to vote on the so-called judges bill, providing for additional federal district judges, tomorrow, under agreement reached yesterday afternoon. On a tie vote, an amendment offered by Senator Fletcher of Florida for an additional judge for the northern district of Florida was lost. Without a record vote the Senate then adopted an amendment by Senator Reed, democrat, Missouri, to authorize an additional judge for the western district of Missouri. Senator Reed, in urging his amendment, presented statements by the present Missouri federal judges as to the congestion of business there and as to the necessity for an extra judge.
