The paper reports that several boats filled with marines at once put off from both the French and English ships of war in the harbor, on seeing which Baez gave orders to an officer and the insulting assemblages around the American Consulate were stopped. It hopes the sloop-of-war Cyane, on her way to Haiti to settle difficulties there, had orders to proceed at once to Santo Domingo to protect Mr. Elliott and look into the case of the captain of the Charles Hill.

El periódico informa que varias lanchas llenas de marines partieron de inmediato de los buques de guerra francés e inglés en el puerto, ante lo cual Báez dio órdenes a un oficial y se detuvieron las reuniones insultantes en torno al consulado estadounidense. Espera que la balandra de guerra Cyane, en camino a Haití para resolver dificultades allí, tuviera órdenes de dirigirse de inmediato a Santo Domingo para proteger al Sr. Elliott e investigar el caso del capitán del Charles Hill.
