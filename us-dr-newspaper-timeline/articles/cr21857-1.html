The Houston Daily Post reported that the Dominican rebellion against the government was expanding, with the province of La Vega in open revolt. Haitian authorities arrested several Dominicans and seized weapons destined for the insurgents, with President Sam declaring Haiti would not allow its territory to serve as an insurgent base. Meanwhile, Dominican government troops sent to confront rebel leader General Ramon Pacheco at Dajabon deserted to the insurgent side, cutting communications with Monte Cristi.

El Houston Daily Post informó que la rebelión dominicana contra el gobierno continuaba expandiéndose, hallándose la provincia de La Vega en abierta insurrección. Autoridades haitianas arrestaron a varios dominicanos e incautaron armas destinadas a los insurgentes, declarando el presidente Sam que Haití no permitiría que su territorio sirviera de base para los sublevados. Mientras tanto, las tropas del gobierno dominicano enviadas para enfrentar al líder rebelde general Ramón Pacheco en Dajabón desertaron al bando insurgente, cortando las comunicaciones con Monte Cristi.

latest news from Santo Domingo says the revolutionary movement is extending and the province of Vega is in arms. It is impossible to obtain any details here. This city is calm. The relations between the United States and Hayti are cordial. The Haytian government has authorized the erection of an American meteorological station at Cape Haytien. Permission to establish such a station had previously been refused, and the fact that permission has now been granted demonstrates the extension of American influence in Hayti. Puerto Plata, August 9. — The Haytian government has seized at Fort Liberte arms and ammunition destined for Dominican insurgents. Several Dominicans in Hayti have been arrested for violating the neutrality laws. Among them is Francisco de [illegible], in whose keeping arms were found. President Sam of Hayti has wired the Dominican government that insurgents will not be permitted to use Haytian territory as a base for operations. The forces of the government under the command of Minister Cordoro have stopped insurgent forces landing here. Cape Haytien, August 9. — General Francisco Tiriano, sent from Monte Cristo by the Dominican government to attack General Ramon Pacheco at Dajabon, the leader of the revolutionists, was abandoned when he arrived in front of the enemy by his troops, who, without firing a shot, deserted and entered the camp of General Pacheco.
