The Portland Daily Press reported that an incursion of around 800 Haitian troops into Dominican territory on August 4 was repulsed by Dominican forces, resulting in twenty-three Haitian fatalities. Dominican President Heureaux declared that the underlying conflict arose from Haiti's efforts to dissolve the commercial treaty of 1875.

The Portland Daily Press informó que una incursión de unos 800 soldados haitianos en territorio dominicano el 4 de agosto fue repelida por fuerzas dominicanas, con un saldo de veintitrés bajas mortales haitianas. El presidente dominicano Heureaux declaró que el conflicto subyacente surgió a raíz de los esfuerzos de Haití por disolver el tratado comercial de 1875.

From Tuesday to Thursday there were 28 deaths from cholera, and 48 fresh cases of the disease at [illegible], Egypt. A special from the city of Santo Domingo says that on August 4, about 800 Haytian troops stationed near the frontier of the Dominican republic invaded the territory of this country. Dominican troops were at once despatched to the scene and repulsed the enemy, killing 23 men and wounding a number of others. President Heureaux says the real cause of the trouble is the design of Hayti to terminate the commercial treaty of 1875. The Novoe Vremya, commenting on the Berlin Post's suggestion that Russia should withdraw her troops from the German and Austrian frontiers, says that Russia massed her troops along the frontiers solely as a precaution after Germany and Austria had massed their forces on their frontiers, and that, therefore, those two powers should initiate the evacuation.
