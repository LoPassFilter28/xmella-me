The News reports that President Ulysses Heureaux has been assassinatedEl News reporta que el presidente Ulysses Heureaux ha sido asesinado

The News reports that President Ulysses Heureaux has been assassinated while attending a christening incognito, and gives a sketch of him: Gen. Ulysses Heureaux has been president of the republic of San Domingo for about ten years; he is a Haytian, about 58 years of age, and a fine type physically of the West Indian negro; while in office he has become immensely wealthy, and has several manufacturing monopolies, notably that of soap, the importation of which is practically impossible; several insurrections have taken place with the idea of overthrowing President Heureaux, but he has succeeded in putting them all down, shooting the leaders very often without trial; and in the early part of June last it was also reported that Gen. Heureaux had been assassinated during a revolution in San Domingo.El News reporta que el presidente Ulysses Heureaux ha sido asesinado mientras asistía de incógnito a un bautizo, y ofrece un esbozo de él: el general Ulysses Heureaux ha sido presidente de la república de Santo Domingo por unos diez años; es haitiano, de unos 58 años de edad, y un fino tipo físico del negro antillano; en el cargo se ha hecho inmensamente rico, y tiene varios monopolios de manufactura, notablemente el del jabón, cuya importación es prácticamente imposible; han tenido lugar varias insurrecciones con la idea de derrocar al presidente Heureaux, pero él ha logrado sofocarlas todas, fusilando a los líderes muy a menudo sin juicio; y a principios de junio pasado también se reportó que el general Heureaux había sido asesinado durante una revolución en Santo Domingo.

The Providence News. Providence, December 20, 1898. Page 1.
