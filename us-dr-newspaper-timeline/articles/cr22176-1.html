The Evening Star reported on diplomatic social receptions in Washington involving the Dominican Ambassador and Señora de Copello, who hosted guests alongside their daughters Adelina and Argentina at the embassy. The column noted that Flor de Oro Trujillo, recently appointed Minister Counselor of the Dominican Embassy after serving as First Secretary, would return to her official duties following her wedding trip. Señora de Copello also attended a luncheon hosted by the wife of the Cuban military attaché.

El Evening Star reseñó recepciones sociales del cuerpo diplomático en Washington ofrecidas por el embajador dominicano y la señora de Copello, quienes agasajaron a diversas personalidades en compañía de sus hijas Adelina y Argentina en los salones de la misión. La crónica hizo notar que Flor de Oro Trujillo, recientemente promovida a ministra consejera de la legación tras haberse desempeñado como primera secretaria, se reincorporaría a sus tareas consulares tras culminar su luna de miel. La señora de Copello tomó parte asimismo en un convite organizado por la esposa del agregado militar de Cuba.

Copello, who moved about in the drawing room and dining room, chatting with any of their own young friends as well as the numerous diplomats and officials, with their wives, whose acquaintance they have made since their arrival here. Also dispensing their customary hospitality were members of the Dominican Embassy staff. Senora de Copello, who stood with the Ambassador in the drawing room which is so exquisitely furnished, was gowned in black with a corsage of deep red roses at the neckline. The green frock of her daughter, Adelina also had a cluster of red flowers as its trimming, and Argentina, like her mother, was in black. Red and white was used as the color scheme in the dining room, where a large vase of red carnations and white stock graced the center of the table. Senorita Trujillo, who recently was appointed Minister Counselor of the Embassy, formerly was First Secretary at this post. Following her wedding trip she will return to her official duties here. Another party of yesterday, given by a hostess whose popularity is known far and near, was the luncheon given by Senora de Munilia, wife of the Military Attache of the Cuban Embassy. The buffet party was held in her apartment at Hotel Twenty-four Hundred, and was attended by many prominent in diplomatic and official society in the Capital.
