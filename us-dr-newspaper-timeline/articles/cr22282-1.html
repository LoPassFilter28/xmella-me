1847-04-24 · New-York [N.Y.]

FROM ST. DOMINGO.

SourceFuente: New-York daily tribune, New-York [N.Y.], 1847-04-24
https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn83030213/1847-04-24/ed-1/?sp=2
unclassified

FROM ST. DOMINGO.—By the arrival of the brig Henry Lee, Capt. Sandy, from the City of St. Domingo, we learn that American produce was in good demand. There had not been any late arrivals from the United States. Wm. F. Harrison, Esq. of New-York, has been acknowledged United States Consul at St. Domingo.
