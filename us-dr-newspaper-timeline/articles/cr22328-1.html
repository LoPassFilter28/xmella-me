LATE FROM ST. DOMINGO.

A letter, of what authority we know not, reported to have been received by the schooner Marietta Smith, Capt. Chase, from Port au Platte, on the 3d instant, says that a few days ago a Dominican war schooner arrived from St. Domingo city, the capital of the Dominican republic, and her officers reported the city in a state of revolution, in consequence of the treaty with Old Spain. The seventh article of the treaty favors all Spanish descendants and allows them to become Spanish citizens, and they, being the most numerous, wish to hoist the Spanish flag again. Citizens, soldiers, and all are applying to the Spanish Consuls for resisting. The day the schooner left the city 5,000 or more had registered their names, and many more would sign.
