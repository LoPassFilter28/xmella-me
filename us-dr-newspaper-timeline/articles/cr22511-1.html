A Washington news item dated February 24 reports, through a New York Herald special, that a Dominican leader has issued a proclamation calling upon all true Dominicans to rally to expel Baez. The proclamation speaks of the country being sold as a piece of merchandise.

Una noticia de Washington fechada el 24 de febrero informa, a través de un despacho especial del New York Herald, que un líder dominicano ha emitido una proclama llamando a todos los verdaderos dominicanos a unirse para expulsar a Báez. La proclama habla del país vendido como una pieza de mercancía.

NEWS FROM WASHINGTON. WASHINGTON, February 24. A New York Herald special says a Dominican leader has issued a proclamation calling upon all true Dominicans to rally to expel Baez. The proclamation speaks of the country being sold as a piece of merchandize. The House is considering the omnibus appropriation bill. It contains appropriations for public buildings in New Orleans, Charleston, Knoxville, St. Louis, Nashville and Montgomery. Miller, from Georgia, was seated in the Senate to-day, after taking the modified oath. The congratulations seemed general. The following nominations were made: Jos. S. Wilcher, collector for the third district of West Virginia; Robert Clark, assessor for the second district of Georgia. The judiciary committee made a report by five to one, that the decision of Attorney-General Akerman upon the right of the government to withhold the entire amount of money due for transportation over the Pacific Railroad is not in accordance with the letter and spirit of the law. The omnibus appropriation bill appropriates thirty-three and a half millions of dollars, including [illegible] for the freedmen's bureau. Vinnie Ream gets $5,000 more for Lincoln's statue.
