The Willmar Tribune reports that James A. Ostrand, formerly a judge of the Supreme Court of the Philippine Islands and recently appointed Chief Justice of the Dominican Republic, has been visiting his mother, Mrs. Carl O. Peterson, in Willmar during the past week and expects to arrive at his new post about July 1. A graduate of the Minnesota University Law School who spent about twenty years in the Philippines, Judge Ostrand was selected for the Dominican position because of his mastery of Spanish, while his son, James Ostrand, Jr., has entered the Willmar schools and will reside there with his grandmother.El Willmar Tribune informa que James A. Ostrand, exjuez de la Corte Suprema de las Islas Filipinas y recientemente nombrado presidente de la Suprema Corte de la República Dominicana, ha estado visitando a su madre, la Sra. de Carl O. Peterson, en Willmar durante la última semana y espera llegar a su nuevo cargo alrededor del 1 de julio. Graduado de la Facultad de Derecho de la Universidad de Minnesota y con unos veinte años de residencia en las Filipinas, el juez Ostrand fue elegido para el puesto dominicano debido a su dominio del idioma español, mientras que su hijo, James Ostrand, Jr., ha ingresado a las escuelas de Willmar y vivirá allí con su abuela.

Willmar Tribune. Willmar, Minn., May 12, 1920.
