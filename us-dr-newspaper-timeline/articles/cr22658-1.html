The Evening Star reported that Secretary of State Dean Acheson cautioned against resorting to war after Dominican President Rafael Trujillo requested congressional authority to declare war on nations harboring anti-Trujillo invasion forces. Acheson stressed that established inter-American procedures existed to resolve disputes peacefully without military conflict. Trujillo's war-power request stemmed from allegations that Cuba and Guatemala were facilitating renewed revolutionary plots against his government.

El Evening Star informó que el secretario de Estado Dean Acheson advirtió contra el recurso a la guerra después de que el presidente dominicano Rafael Trujillo solicitara autorización al Congreso para declarar la guerra a las naciones que albergaran fuerzas de invasión antitrujillistas. Acheson recalcó que existían procedimientos interamericanos establecidos para resolver disputas de manera pacífica y sin conflictos militares. La solicitud de poderes de guerra de Trujillo se derivaba de acusaciones de que Cuba y Guatemala facilitaban nuevos complots revolucionarios contra su gobierno.

Mr. Acheson insisted that proper procedures exist for dealing with situations which threaten the peace among the American republics without resort to “war.” Mr. Acheson noted that “there has been no evidence that any intervention or other interference by any foreign government was brought to bear in effecting recent changes in the government of Panama.” He said recognition of the Arias government follows assurances that it will fulfill Panama's international obligations and determination that it is actually in control of Panama and is generally accepted by the populace. These criteria are of general validity with regard to recognition of foreign government changes, Mr. Acheson said. Coup Disapproved. Emphasizing that recognition of the Arias government does not constitute approval of the manner in which it came to power, he pointed out that “we have in fact publicly deplored the means by which the political changes in Panama since November 19 were effected.” With regard to the Dominican Republic, Mr. Acheson said it was reported that President Trujillo charged this week that a new plan to invade his country is being organized abroad, and asked the Dominican congress for authority to declare war against any country which tolerates military forces in its territory for invasion of the Dominican Republic.
