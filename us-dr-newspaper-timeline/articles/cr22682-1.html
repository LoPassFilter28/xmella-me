'Dominican revolt causes concern': rebels defied the American officers at the Comendador custom house and forced 50,000 rounds of ammunition across from Haiti; a rebel general threatens the Dajabón custom house; President Bordas shells Puerto Plata and is cautioned by Captain Eberle for endangering non-combatants.

«La revuelta dominicana causa preocupación»: los rebeldes desafiaron a los oficiales americanos en la aduana de Comendador e introdujeron a la fuerza 50,000 cartuchos desde Haití; un general rebelde amenaza la aduana de Dajabón; el presidente Bordas bombardea Puerto Plata y es amonestado por el capitán Eberle por poner en peligro a los no combatientes.

the gunboat Wheeling, at Santo Domingo City, reported that the rebels had defied American officers in charge of a custom house near Comendador, and forced 50,000 rounds of ammunition across the border from Haiti. At Dajabon, also on the Haitian line, the commander reported General Dorcilien, with 300 or 100 men, has threatened to attack the custom house and the situation there is considered acute. While this is going on in the north President Bordas continues his attack upon the rebel stronghold, Puerta Plata, and small revolutionary bands are operating in the vicinity of Santo Domingo City itself. Captain Eberle of the United States cruiser Washington has been obliged to caution President Bordas to cease reckless firing into Puerta Plata where the lives of Americans and other noncombatants have been endangered. Dr. Hurtado, a Dominican by birth, but a naturalized American, was wounded yesterday in Puerta Plata by a stray bullet fired on the outskirts of the town. CONSTITUTIONALISTS CLASH WITH GERMANS. Commander of Ypiranga Reaches for Pistol, But Is Calmed by Governor. Vera Cruz, May 17. — The first clash between the constitutionalists and foreigners at Tampico occurred Friday night when 140 refugees of various nationalities were compelled to seek passports permitting their departure from the port after they had boarded the German steamer Ypiranga.
