Rumors of Another War. New York, April [illegible] — A dispatch received in this city says that rumors of war between Hayti and San Domingo are current. The old dispute, the boundary line question, is the cause of the strained relations of the two countries. The Dominican representative at Port-au-Prince has been recalled and diplomatic intercourse has ceased
