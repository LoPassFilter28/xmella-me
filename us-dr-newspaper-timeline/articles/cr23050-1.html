Evening star — 1940-01-31

The *Evening Star* reported that the Dominican government and the Dominican Republic Settlement Association signed a contract to admit approximately 500 European political refugee families immediately, aiming ultimately to settle 100,000 refugees over time. While the agreement did not restrict nationalities, most members of the initial contingent were German-Jewish refugees. The settlement association agreed to finance their installation, expecting eventual reimbursement from agricultural yields once the settlers achieved financial stability.

El *Evening Star* informó que el gobierno dominicano y la Dominican Republic Settlement Association firmaron un convenio para recibir de forma inmediata a cerca de 500 familias de refugiados políticos europeos, con la meta de asentar gradualmente a 100.000 inmigrantes. Si bien el acuerdo no estipulaba restricciones de nacionalidad, la mayor parte del contingente inicial correspondía a refugiados germanojudíos. La entidad colonizadora convino en sufragar su instalación, previendo reembolsos futuros derivados de los rendimientos agropecuarios una vez lograda la autosuficiencia de los colonos.
