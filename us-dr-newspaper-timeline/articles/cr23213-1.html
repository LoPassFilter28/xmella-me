the houses. MOVING ON DOMINGO. Efforts in Jamaica to Aid the Insurrection.

Kingston, Jamaica, Nov. 5.—A revolutionary Santo Domingo junta has established headquarters here, and this morning a sloop belonging to it took out clearance papers for Hayti. Her destination, however, is believed to be some point on the Dominican coast. On board the sloop are some of the principal revolutionary leaders. Attempts are being made here to charter a steamer in order to make a descent on Santo Domingo with a President Designate and his staff. ESTRADA DEFEATED. Zelaya's Forces Reported Moving on Rama.

Managua, Nicaragua, Nov. 4 (Delayed in transmission).—Government troops defeated the revolutionists in a sharp engagement to-day, many of General Estrada's followers being killed, wounded or captured. The government lost fifteen men, including General Castillo [Chamorro?]. President Zelaya's forces attacked the rebel general, Fornos Diaz, at Paso las Lajas, capturing that point, which had been defended by four hundred men. The rebels fled in the direction of Rama and were pursued. The above dispatch, which the correspondent was unable to get through yesterday, refers doubtless to the battle of Thursday, concerning which conflicting reports reached New Orleans and Washington. Sympathizers at New Orleans with the Nicaraguan rebels received advices saying that General Estrada was gaining ground and had taken Paso las Lajas.
