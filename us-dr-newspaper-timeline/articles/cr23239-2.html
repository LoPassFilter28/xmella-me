Haiti and Spanish Santo DomingoHaití y el Santo Domingo Español · 1804–1821

Following Haitian independence under Dessalines, the eastern two-thirds of Hispaniola (Santo Domingo) oscillated between French occupation, British blockades, and restored Spanish colonial rule ('España Boba'). Coverage focused on trade, privateering, and slave revolts.Tras la independencia haitiana, los dos tercios orientales de La Española oscilaron entre ocupación francesa, bloqueos británicos y el dominio colonial español restaurado ('España Boba').

FROM THE KENTUCKY ARGUS.

In the former part of October, the Delaware

Indians passed through Kaskaskia, on their way

from White Water in the Indiana to the Arkan

sas river, where they are to settle on lands to be

appropriated to them by our government. They

are said to be about 800 strong. One of their

chiels was killed in Kaskaskia by the accidental

discharge oi a rifle barrel, which was heating in

a Smith’s fire.

DIED,

On the 2?d instant, at Cedarville, N. J. Capt. James

Buncu,'a Revolutionary Patriot.

At Aux Cajes, on the 26th ult. Duncan Mclntosh.

He may be styled the Hero of Humanity, the Philan

thropist, and the Christian During the revolutionary

troubles of ’93, in the island of St. Domingo, this man,

at the hazard of his life, saved a large number of the’

trembling inhabitants from the remorseless fury of the

blacks: he courted danger in every shape for the preser

vation of his fellow man—he preserved the lives of others,

at the imminent and hourly hazard of his own. What

shall be now said of the Alexanders, of the Ctesars, of

the Bonapartes. “Ye little stars, hide your diminished

heads.”

A crown immortal sparkles on his brow,

Where are your laurels, mighty heroes, now

His spirit soars beyond the starry dome,

And shouting angels hail the Conqueror home.
