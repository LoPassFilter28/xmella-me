From: The Customs ReceivershipDe: La receptoría de aduanas · 1905–1916

A regiment of approximately 800 marines under Colonel Franklin J. MosesUn regimiento de aproximadamente 800 infantes de marina, al mando del coronel Franklin J. Moses

A regiment of approximately 800 marines under Colonel Franklin J. Moses and Lieutenant Andrew B. Drum is being sent to Santo Domingo, The Evening Capital and Maryland Gazette reports, aboard the cruiser transport Prairie from the League Island navy yard. Detachments for the force have been assembled from naval stations at Philadelphia, Charleston, Norfolk, and other installations. The regiment's primary mission is to enforce the reopening of customhouses along the Haitian border that were recently closed by insurgent forces in the Dominican Republic.Un regimiento de aproximadamente 800 infantes de marina, al mando del coronel Franklin J. Moses y del teniente Andrew B. Drum, es enviado a Santo Domingo, informa el Evening Capital and Maryland Gazette, a bordo del crucero de transporte Prairie desde el astillero naval de League Island. Los destacamentos que integran el contingente fueron reunidos a partir de puestos navales en Filadelfia, Charleston, Norfolk y otras instalaciones. La misión principal del regimiento consiste en obligar a la reapertura de las aduanas a lo largo de la frontera haitiana que fueron clausuradas recientemente por fuerzas insurgentes en la República Dominicana.

Evening Capital and Maryland Gazette. Annapolis, Md., September 26, 1912. Page 1.
