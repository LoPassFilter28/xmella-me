SummaryResumen

Based on the provided excerpt, the newspaper reported that while the white and better class of people sympathized with the revolutionary (Moa) party, a plot by young men in Santo Domingo to seize the city was uncovered after a messenger boy was arrested and forced to confess under threat of execution. Consequently, the government arrested about sixty implicated young men from the country's best families who had been meeting at three different locations to plan an imminent rising. The article noted it was supposed only slight punishment or short imprisonment might be inflicted, as the prisons were already crowded to capacity with political prisoners.

A partir del extracto periodístico provisto, el diario dio cuenta de que, en tanto los elementos blancos y las clases acomodadas simpatizaban con el bando revolucionario (moyerista), se desbarató una conjura de jóvenes capitaleños para adueñarse de Santo Domingo tras el arresto de un ordenanza que confesó los planes bajo amenaza de muerte. En consecuencia, las autoridades aprehendieron a cerca de sesenta jóvenes comprometidos pertenecientes a las familias más distinguidas, quienes celebraban juntas en tres parajes para ultimar el levantamiento. Anotó el texto que se esperaba la imposición de penas leves o breves arrestos, toda vez que las cárceles se hallaban colmadas de reos políticos.
