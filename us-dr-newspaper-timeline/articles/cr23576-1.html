Passage summaryResumen del pasaje

*The Lusk Herald* reported imminent conflict between France and the Dominican Republic after French warships appeared off Santo Domingo City to take soundings. The French government delivered an ultimatum demanding the return of $60,000 seized from a French-backed bank along with a $20,000 indemnity, threatening bombardment if President Heureaux failed to pay. Heureaux reportedly responded that the Dominican treasury lacked the money to meet the demand.

*The Lusk Herald* informó sobre un conflicto inminente entre Francia y la República Dominicana luego de que buques de guerra franceses aparecieran frente a la ciudad de Santo Domingo para realizar sondeos marítimos. El gobierno francés presentó un ultimátum exigiendo la devolución de 60.000 dólares confiscados a un banco con respaldo francés, junto con una indemnización de 20.000 dólares, amenazando con un bombardeo si el presidente Heureaux no efectuaba el pago. Según se informó, Heureaux respondió que el tesoro dominicano carecía del dinero necesario para satisfacer la demanda.

MAY BOMBARD SAN DOMINGO.

France Demauds Indemnity for

Confiscation of a French Bank.

New York special: A dispatch to the

World, dated Puerto Plat, San Domingo,

January 18, says:

“Trouble seems imminent between

France and the Dominican Republic. A

French man of war appeared off San Do

mingo City a few days ago and began to

take soundings. Soon after she departed

another appeared. The latter was boarded

by a pilot, who was informed that his

services were not needed, and when tie

asked the commander his intention, he was

told it was none of his business.”

The San Domingo authorities had a dis

pute with a bank a few years ago, and

eventually took $60,000 from it. The bank

was backed by French capitalists, and the

bankers laid the case before the French

Government. France demanded that the

money be returned to the bank, but diplo

matic negotiations seem to have resulted in

nothing. The World correspondent learns

upon trustworthy authority that the

French ultimatum has already been sent

in and demands the restoration of the $60,-

000 and $20,000 as indemnity. President

Heuraux, it is said, has declared that he

has no money to pay it with.

Dispatches for the Britsh Admiral at Ja
