The Commissioners' report describes Samana, Azua and Manzanillo as great bays admitting vessels of the largest draft, with Samana alone of military importance, and presents Samana Bay as a powerful military and naval station where an important commercial city could grow. It also gives a financial statement of the Dominican Government, listing unpaid salaries, bonds, loans, obligations and pending claims, and a summary of receipts for 1870.

El informe de los Comisionados describe Samaná, Azua y Manzanillo como grandes bahías que admiten barcos del mayor calado, siendo solo Samaná de importancia militar, y presenta la Bahía de Samaná como una poderosa estación militar y naval donde podría crecer una importante ciudad comercial. También ofrece un estado financiero del Gobierno dominicano, con sueldos impagados, bonos, préstamos, obligaciones y reclamaciones pendientes, y un resumen de los ingresos de 1870.

The Commissioners state the advantages of the acquisition of Samana Bay to be, a powerful military and naval station, and doubtless estimate the growth of an important city, a portion of the water in front of which has already been required by the citizens of the United States, being but a small part of what would be occupied by the site of a great commercial city. Inland communication by water and rail would be easy, and the future of Samana Bay, under the authority of the United States, could not fail to be of national importance. FINANCIAL STATEMENT.
