The paper reports, citing the New York Post, a revived scheme attributed to Mr. Calhoun to get the Dominican Government under American control, with a view to annexing the whole island and putting down Soulouque's empire in Haiti. It says Mrs. Cazneau has been appointed special commissioner to inquire into its feasibility, while her husband is ostensibly the commissioner.

El periódico informa, citando al New York Post, de un plan revivido atribuido al Sr. Calhoun para poner al Gobierno dominicano bajo control estadounidense, con miras a anexar toda la isla y derribar el imperio de Soulouque en Haití. Dice que la Sra. Cazneau ha sido nombrada comisionada especial para investigar su viabilidad, mientras su esposo es nominalmente el comisionado.

ASOTHEA SCHEME of Annexation.—According to the'

New York Post, the old scheme of Mr. Calhoun to get

the Dominican Government under American control, with

a view to annex the whole island of Hayti and put down

the negro empire established by Soulouque, is revived un

der the present Administration. A special commissioner,

or commission-ess, Mrs. Cazaeau, formerly Mies Storms,

and known as a writer for the newspapers by the name of

“Cera Montgomery,” has been appointed to undertake

the task and inquire into its feasibility.

Her husband is

ostensibly the commissioner, but the lady has left him in

Texas and gone on the mission herself. Under such

auspices the scheme, if it be seriously intended, is likely

to flourish better than some of the recent missions from

the United States. Petticoat diplomacy has not yet been

tried in this country, and “Young America” is the pro

per individual to initiate the experiment.—Phila. Ledger.
