The Evening Star reviewed the history of Caribbean political conflicts, tracing how Venezuelan exiles and Romulo Betancourt helped form the Caribbean Legion to invade Rafael Trujillo's Dominican Republic. Following the collapse of those invasion attempts, legion remnants aided Jose Figueres in establishing power in Costa Rica during the 1948 revolution. Dictators Trujillo and Anastasio Somoza accused Figueres of harboring communist conspirators and reforming the legion to attack their regimes, charges Figueres dismissed as dictatorial paranoia.

El Evening Star repasó la historia de los conflictos políticos del Caribe, reseñando cómo los exiliados venezolanos y Rómulo Betancourt colaboraron en la formación de la Legión del Caribe para invadir la República Dominicana de Rafael Trujillo. Tras el fracaso de aquellas intentonas de invasión, los remanentes de la legión ayudaron a José Figueres a afianzarse en el poder en Costa Rica durante la revolución de 1948. Los dictadores Trujillo y Anastasio Somoza acusaron a Figueres de cobijar a conspiradores comunistas y reorganizar la legión para atacar a sus regímenes, señalamientos que Figueres desestimó considerándolos paranoia dictatorial.

There, under the tutelage of President Rafael Trujillo Molina, they plotted a counter-revolution. Learning about this, Betancourt and party helped organize an expedi tionary force of asylees from Carib bean totalitarian regimes to invade the Dominican Republic. This force, named the Caribbean Legion, trained in Cuba and failed at two attempts to invade Trujillo's land. It then disintegrated. Costa Rican Adventure Remnants of the legion figured in a revolution that broke out in Costa Rica late in 1948. A conserva tive invalidated the free election of a liberal president. Jose Figueres mobilized a group of young liberals and forced the Conservative-Com munist coalition out, set himself up as provisional president, and even tually turned the government over to the constitutionally elected presi dent. Betancourt is in Costa Rica, with his friend, Figueres. Trujillo and Anastasio Somoza, Nicaragua’s "strong man,” claim Figueres and Betancourt are reorganizing the Caribbean Legion to invade Nicara gua and the Dominican Republic. They further claim Figueres is harboring a nest of Communist conspirators, a name they generally use for any one disagreeing with them.
