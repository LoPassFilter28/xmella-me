Annexation Means War with Hayti. Another Washington correspondent telegraphs that it seems to be the general opinion of the Commissioners that annexation cannot take place without involving the United States in a war with Hayti. Baez admitted to the Commissioners that one thousand square miles of territory belonging to the Dominican republic, and lying contiguous to Hayti, are under control of Cabral and his followers. It is stated that Baez did not make any effort to allow the Commissioners to meet Cabral, but on the contrary assured them that he could not afford them any protection if they visited the part of the island that Cabral controlled. All speak in the highest terms of the island, its climate and productions, but appear to entertain a very poor opinion of the people, who are described as indolent, superstitious and exceedingly undesirable in their present condition as an addition to the population of the United States. Ben Wade Quite [illegible]. Washington, D. C., March 28. The Santo Domingo Commissioners, Wade, White and Howe, arrived here last night, accompanied by Secretary Burton, Gen. Sigel and Fred Douglass. Wade was taken sick Sunday night, on the cars, with pleurisy, and was unable to sit up yesterday, and upon arrival was conveyed to the residence of Vice-President Colfax. The attack, although not serious, is sufficient cause for great anxiety on the part of friends, who fear that Wade's age and full habits may prove serious obstacles to his recovery.
