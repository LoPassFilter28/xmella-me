Continuing an account of a legal complaint in the New-York TribuneAl continuar una crónica de una demanda legal en el New-York Tribune

Continuing an account of a legal complaint in the New-York Tribune, this passage states that sixty-five thousand dollars in stock was returned and $12,500 was later paid in cash before San Domingo company directors Weed, Brown, and Wells were voted out of office. Newly elected officers found $137,000 in San Domingo Government scrip certificates carrying $14,980 in unpaid interest arrears, and were informed by the Dominican government that the San Domingo Finance Company had already received funds to pay interest charges and faced cancellation of its contract over unpaid scrip interest. The bill further alleges that Weed, Brown, and Wells organized the San Dominican Railroad Company, broke an original construction contract with New York's Drake Stratton Construction Company for the Puerto Plata–Santiago railroad, caused the railroad company to confess a judgment dated two years back and secured by improvement and finance company assets, and intentionally formed corporations to deceive the public.Continuando la reseña de una demanda judicial en el New-York Tribune, este pasaje señala que se devolvieron sesenta y cinco mil dólares en acciones y luego se pagaron $12,500 en efectivo antes de que Weed, Brown y Wells, directores de las compañías de San Domingo, fueran destituidos por votación. Los nuevos directivos hallaron entre los activos $137,000 en certificados del gobierno de San Domingo con $14,980 en intereses atrasados impagos, y recibieron respuesta del gobierno dominicano de que la San Domingo Finance Company ya había recibido los fondos para cubrir los intereses y estaba a punto de ver cancelado su contrato por falta de pago de intereses sobre certificados. El escrito alega además que Weed, Brown y Wells organizaron la San Dominican Railroad Company, incumplieron el contrato original de construcción con la Drake Stratton Construction Company de Nueva York para el ferrocarril entre Puerto Plata y Santiago, hicieron que la empresa ferroviaria reconociera un fallo fechado dos años atrás y garantizado con activos de las compañías de mejoras y finanzas, y crearon corporaciones deliberadamente para engañar al público.

New-York Tribune. New York, April 5, 1901.
