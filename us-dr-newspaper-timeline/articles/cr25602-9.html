The company is entitled to emit bonds for railroad, canal, telegraph and highroad constructions, with the faculty of guaranteeing payment there-against, and further, shall have the right to hypothecate the bonds emitted for the purpose of raising funds to carry on the work, and shall further have the right to emit further stock for the completion of said work.

Neither:—Emigrants who shall purchase land of the company or the agents of said company, or merchants who shall be engaged in commercial pursuits between Samana and other points of the Dominican Republic shall be compelled to pay higher imposts than Dominican citizens. It is also further understood that no taxes shall be levied by the Dominican government upon works of public utility, or the banking establishments—and, in a word, the company shall be treated as the most favored of Dominican citizens.

The company shall have the privilege of authorizing the formation of other companies, and has the right of ceding land to them upon such terms and stipulations as may be considered most advantageous, and, by so doing, carry out the aims and objects for which the company was organized.

The aforesaid smaller companies shall have their names designated by the said Samana Bay Company, and shall be under the superintendence and inspection of the said company, which shall have the privilege of holding shares in the said smaller companies.

The privileges and concessions accorded to this company shall be without prejudice to privileges and concessions accorded to any other person or persons by the Dominican government, for, up to the present time, no such privileges or concessions have been granted similar to those now conceded to the Samana Bay Company, which is now empowered, for the space of thirty days, to accept or refuse said treaty.

ART. 10.—These concessions, rights, privileges and immunities shall take effect on the first day of January, in the year eighteen hundred and seventy-three, and will continue in force and will remain in force for ninety-nine years from the said date (with the exception of a certain time that is to be conceded to the company for the purpose of taking possession, on the condition that the said company, its successors or representatives, pay or cause to be paid, to the Dominican government the sum of $150,000, in American gold, in advance, to the Dominican government. The said sum to be charged in advance to the company on the 1st day of January of each year, and the payment thereagainst to be made in the capital city of St. Domingo on the same, or at any place that may be designated by the Dominican government.

The first of these annual advance payments shall be made on the 1st of January, in the year 1873, unless it has been made before. The company and its territories shall remain under the united protection of the Dominican Republic and the United States of America, and the company and its agents, when they deem it necessary, shall ask the protection and intervention of either of these nations or of both.

ART. 11.—For the purposes of ratifying this convention a vote will be taken on the subject by the Dominican nation and the Dominican Senate. The non-payment of the annual rent alluded to in article 10 or the breaking out of war or other circumstances of force majeure will cause the said convention to be considered null and void after thirty days' notice has been given by the Dominican government.
