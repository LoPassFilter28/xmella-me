A report says Soulouque, who had gathered a formidable force against the Dominicans, was on his return without having fired a shot, after two French men-of-war arrived and sealed dispatches were sent to the Emperor. It was rumored and generally believed that France would not permit the Haitians to invade or molest the Dominicans.

Un informe dice que Soulouque, que había reunido una fuerza formidable contra los dominicanos, estaba de regreso sin haber disparado un tiro, después de que llegaran dos buques de guerra franceses y se enviaran despachos sellados al emperador. Se rumoreaba y se creía generalmente que Francia no permitiría que los haitianos invadieran o molestaran a los dominicanos.
