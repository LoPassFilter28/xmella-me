Passage summaryResumen del pasaje

The Evening Star reported that President Truman nominated Ralph H. Ackerman, a career Foreign Service officer from California, to serve as Ambassador to the Dominican Republic. The President also appointed Ambassador Henry F. Grady to an additional post on the United Nations Economic Commission for Asia and the Far East. Domestically, Michigan state police were dispatched to suburban Detroit to control picket line violence during a Chrysler auto strike.

En materia doméstica, agentes policiales del estado de Míchigan fueron comisionados al área metropolitana de Detroit para sofocar disturbios acaecidos en los piquetes fabriles durante una huelga automotriz en Chrysler.
