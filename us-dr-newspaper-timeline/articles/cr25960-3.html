A Mexico City dispatch of April 23 reports that the Dominican government has decorated President Cardenas with the ribbon and gold plaque of the Order of Juan Pablo Duarte. The order will be bestowed by the Dominican minister in Mexico at a ceremony to be arranged later.

Un despacho de la ciudad de México del 23 de abril informa que el gobierno dominicano ha condecorado al presidente Cárdenas con la banda y la placa de oro de la Orden de Juan Pablo Duarte. La orden será impuesta por el ministro dominicano en México en una ceremonia que se organizará más adelante.

MEXICO CITY, April 23 — [illegible] Agency — The Dominican government has decorated President Cardenas with the ribbon and gold plaque of the order of Juan Pablo Duarte. The order will be bestowed by the Dominican Minister in Mexico at a ceremony to be arranged later. PERISCOPE. Mrs. Bruce Brannom out a-grocery shopping early this mawnin'. Rolly Buford, and in his line of business too, marching about [illegible] a coat on today. A. R. Hunt out looking at his show windows this noon. Carlos Richter having a big time kidding a lot of kids last night at the kid play at Christen school. Frank Hill proud as proud watching his little daughter perform at the same play. Byron Emerson free wheeling through the business section this mawnin'. Milton Fish taking the calendar at its word and defying the nawth winds with a fresh seersucker suit. Ed [Rollman?] always doing a lot to help the kids of Laredo.
