Under the headline "Wants to Be President of Dominica, but State Department Won't Let Him,"Bajo el titular «Wants to Be President of Dominica, but State Department Won't Let Him»

Under the headline "Wants to Be President of Dominica, but State Department Won't Let Him," The Washington Herald reports that Frederico Velasquez, a political exile from the Dominican Republic who sailed from New York for Jamaica on Sunday, appealed to the State Department for backing to make himself president of the island republic. Velasquez offered himself as a substitute for the unpopular Victoria government, asserting that he could become president at any time if supported by the United States. Department officials refused to consider his proposals and requested that he not repeat the suggestion. Velasquez previously served as Minister of Finance under President Ramon Caceres until Caceres was assassinated the previous winter, having since remained in Jamaica awaiting an opportune moment to return.Bajo el titular «Wants to Be President of Dominica, but State Department Won't Let Him» (Quiere ser presidente de Dominica, pero el Departamento de Estado no se lo permite), The Washington Herald informa que Frederico Velasquez, exiliado político de la República Dominicana que zarpó de Nueva York hacia Jamaica el domingo, apeló al Departamento de Estado en busca de respaldo para proclamarse presidente de la república insular. Velasquez se ofreció como sustituto del impopular gobierno de Victoria, asegurando que podría asumir la presidencia en cualquier momento si contaba con el apoyo de Estados Unidos. Los funcionarios del departamento se negaron a considerar sus propuestas y le solicitaron no repetir la sugerencia. Velasquez se desempeñó anteriormente como ministro de Hacienda bajo el presidente Ramón Cáceres hasta que este fue asesinado el invierno anterior, permaneciendo desde entonces en Jamaica a la espera de un momento propicio para regresar.

In this article, the newspaper calls the country 'Dominica.' It means the Dominican Republic, not the island of Dominica.En este artículo, el periódico llama al país «Dominica». Se refiere a la República Dominicana, no a la isla de Dominica.

The Washington Herald. Washington, D.C., October 15, 1912.
