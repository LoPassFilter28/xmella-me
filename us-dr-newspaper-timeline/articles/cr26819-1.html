Diario Las Américas reported that Cuban Senator Rivero Agüero warned that the Cuban Air Force would destroy Ciudad Trujillo if the Dominican Republic attempted an attack on Havana. Dismissing rumors of a Dominican-based invasion force, Rivero Agüero asserted that Cuba's military positions in Oriente Province were strategically positioned to repel any aggression.

Diario Las Américas informó que el senador cubano Rivero Agüero advirtió que la Fuerza Aérea de Cuba destruiría Ciudad Trujillo si la República Dominicana intentaba un ataque contra La Habana. Desestimando los rumores de una fuerza de invasión asentada en territorio dominicano, Rivero Agüero afirmó que las posiciones militares cubanas en la provincia de Oriente estaban ubicadas estratégicamente para repeler cualquier agresión.

Senate, affirmed that, if the Dom inican Republic would dare to at tack Havana, the Cuban Air Force would destroy Ciudad Trujillo. Rivero Aguero, who claims to be spokesman for President Batista’s party, made declarations to the Press at the Presidential Palace, after talking with the Chief Executive. The Senator said that his declarations were justified by the fact that, according to what the technicians of the Cuban Army pointed out to him, Havana is four hours flight from the Dominican Republic, while Ciudad Trujillo is situated only 36 minutes from Oriente Province, where Cuba has important military posts.
