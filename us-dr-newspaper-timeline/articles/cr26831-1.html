Passage summaryResumen del pasaje

The *Monitor* reported on a rally where Richard B. Moore of the American Negro Labor Congress condemned U.S. imperialist control over Haiti over a century after its independence from Napoleon. Moore invoked the historical legacy of Toussaint Louverture, who fought slavery and reaction across the island of Santo Domingo, while speakers protested against modern foreign military subjugation.

Moore, del American Negro Labor Congress, condenó el control imperialista de Estados Unidos sobre Haití a más de un siglo de su independencia de Napoleón.
