A DOMINICAN OFFICER'S REPLY TO GEN. TATE.

Col. G. X. Franco, a Dominican officer, writes to THE TRIBUNE in reply to to Gen. Tate, who, as the embassador of Salnave, he regards as the natural enemy of Gen. Cabral. Regarding the annexation vote he says: "That the vote in favor of annexation is legal is false. This is proved by the insurrection of the people at Puerto Plata, their manifesto against annexation, and the blood that flowed upon the occasion." Col. Franco further says the statement made by Gen. Tate that "Cabral has only a few hundred men" is false. "Cabral has thousands of the best men of the Republic in his ranks, and holds the whole of the Province of the South, which contains from 35,000 to 40,000 inhabitants. In that part of the Republic there are 14 different communities. In the North, Gens. Pimentel, Cabrera, Valerio, and others are operating, and now hold eight settlements. They have, likewise, a large number of soldiers. In the East there are many political refugees, who are, every now and then, fighting the Government troops. In the Homenage Fortress at San Domingo are imprisoned more than 235 persons. Does Gen. Tate think that a country can be legally annexed under such circumstances?"
