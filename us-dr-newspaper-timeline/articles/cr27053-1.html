The Evening Star reported the arrival in Washington of Dr. Ángel Morales, the newly appointed Dominican Minister to the United States, and his wife. Dr. Morales, who succeeded Dr. José del Carmen Ariza, had previously served as Dominican Minister to Paris, vice president of the League of Nations, and Dominican Secretary of the Interior. The newly appointed legation secretary, Máximo L. Vásquez, a nephew of the Dominican president, also arrived in the capital.

El Evening Star informó de la llegada a Washington del Dr. Ángel Morales, recién designado ministro de la República Dominicana ante Estados Unidos, y de su esposa. El Dr. Morales, quien sucedió al Dr. José del Carmen Ariza, se había desempeñado previamente como ministro dominicano en París, vicepresidente de la Liga de las Naciones y secretario de Interior dominicano. El nuevo secretario de legación, Máximo L. Vásquez, sobrino del presidente dominicano, también arribó a la capital.

Crown Princess Louise of Sweden, will not return to Washington until after they sail from San Francisco Thursday, August 6, for the [illegible] The Minister will then start East arriving here about the middle [of?] August. Mme. Bostrom is in their Swedish home for the Summer. The newly appointed Minister of the Dominican Republic, Dr. Angel Morales, accompanied by Senora de Morales, have arrived in Washington and taken an apartment at the Wardman Park Hotel. Dr. Morales, who succeeds Dr. Jose del Carmen Ariza, served as Dominican Minister to Paris for one year, and prior to that was one of the vice presidents of the League of Nations at Geneva. An attorney by profession, he has held several governmental posts in the Dominican Republic, serving as secretary of the Interior for one term. The newly appointed secretary of the Dominican legation, Senor Maximo L. Vasquez, also came to Washington last week and, accompanied by Senora de Vasquez, is established at the Roosevelt Hotel. Senor Vasquez is a nephew of President Vasquez of the Dominican Republic. The charge d'affaires of Panama and Senora de Chevalier returned Friday from Philadelphia, where they attended the Sesquicentennial and the opening there of the Panama exhibit. The counselor of the Czechoslovakian legation and Mme. Lipova are expected to arrive Friday aboard the Mauretania, which sailed from Southampton yesterday. M. and Mme. Lipova have been on vacation in Europe for some time, and will come at once to Washington on their arrival.
