SAN DOMINGO'S TROUBLES OVER.

Gen. Herreux Victorious and the Leaders of the Revolution Fugitives.

NEW YORK, Oct. 26.—A Sun special from San Domingo under date of Oct. 10 says: The San Domingo revolution is over, and Gen. Herreux, for the government, has again been victorious. The leaders of the revolution are all fugitives. Gens. Moya and Moncion, with some of the others, are in Port de Paix, Hayti, where they are held prisoners by the Haytian government, and Gen. Pablo Villeneuve is in the south trying to make some conditions for surrender.

Herreux was too strong for them. Besides being better supplied with arms, money and men, he had such a reputation as a fighter that it appears that when he personally was in the field it was impossible to make the revolutionists stand their ground. In one of the former revolutions which he suppressed he marched seven of the leaders who had been taken prisoners through the streets of the capital out to the cemetery and had them shot, superintending the execution himself for fear of any mistake.
