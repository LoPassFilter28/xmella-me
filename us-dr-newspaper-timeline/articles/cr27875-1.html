The Barre Daily Times reported that United States Minister Powell delivered a farewell address reaffirming the Monroe Doctrine and denying any American intention to annex the Dominican Republic. Powell warned that the nation's future sovereignty depended upon fulfilling financial obligations to foreign creditors and maintaining domestic peace. The dispatch noted the appointment of Thomas C. Dawson of Iowa to succeed Powell, while the warship Independencia moved troops to confront insurgents at Monte Cristi.

El Barre Daily Times informó que el ministro de los Estados Unidos, Powell, pronunció un discurso de despedida en el que reafirmó la Doctrina Monroe y negó cualquier intención estadounidense de anexionar la República Dominicana. Powell advirtió que la futura soberanía de la nación dependía del cumplimiento de las obligaciones financieras con los acreedores extranjeros y del mantenimiento de la paz interna. El despacho señaló el nombramiento de Thomas C. Dawson, de Iowa, para suceder a Powell, mientras el buque de guerra Independencia trasladaba tropas para enfrentar a los insurgentes en Monte Cristi.

They desired that Santo Domingo should remain a sovereign independent power, and in this the republic would have the support of the United States government. Powell Reasserts Monroe Doctrine. Mr. Powell said that his government would not consent to any portion of Dominican territory passing under the control of any foreign power. The future of the republic rested largely on the keeping of all obligations toward foreign creditors, on respecting the concessions the government made to foreigners, on developing the country's resources and on the passage of such laws as would bring prosperity. These considerations and the taking of the whole people into the confidence of the government would be the greatest foil and guard against revolution and anarchy. The minister assured the president that the American people would be at his side if he remained steadfast to the principles of a good republic. Mr. Powell's departure is regretted by all classes. The United States cruiser Detroit has returned from visiting northern ports. The Dominican warship Independencia has left here with troops for Monte Cristi, which is still In the hands of the revolutionists. Mr. Powell, who Is minister to Haiti as well as to Santo Domingo, is succeeded as minister to the latter country by Thomas C. Dawson of Iowa. Iron, Steel and Tin Men Meet. Cleveland, May 4.
