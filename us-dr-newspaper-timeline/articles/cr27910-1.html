The Evening Star reported that Dominican Ambassador Joaquín Salazar condemned a Life magazine article as "a tremendous libel." The magazine had alleged that Columbia University professor Jesús de Galíndez was kidnaped in New York and flown to the Dominican Republic by American pilot Gerald Murphy, with both men subsequently murdered.

El Evening Star informó que el embajador dominicano Joaquín Salazar condenó un artículo de la revista Life calificándolo de «una tremenda calumnia». La revista había sostenido que el profesor de la Universidad de Columbia Jesús de Galíndez fue secuestrado en Nueva York y trasladado por vía aérea a la República Dominicana por el piloto estadounidense Gerald Murphy, habiendo sido asesinados ambos posteriormente.

REPORTERS afterwards asked the Ambassador to comment on the recent story in Life Magazine which claimed it had turned up information that the missing Dr. Jesus Maria De Galindez, bitter foe of the Dominican Republic's strongman Rafael Trujillo, was kidnaped in New York last March and flown to the Dominican Republic. The magazine also contended that the pilot of the plane was a young American Gerald Murphy, who later disappeared in the republic. The story implies that both Dr. Galindez (a professor at Columbia University) and Murphy met with foul play at the hands of the Dominicans.
