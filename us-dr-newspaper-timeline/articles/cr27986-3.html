Newsmen Held in Town The lack of clear-cut news coverage of the current trouble is no fault of the press. American newsmen trying to cover the conflict from the Haitian side are hamstrung by telephone and cable censorship and by refusal of the government to let them past the city limits to see for themselves what is going on.
