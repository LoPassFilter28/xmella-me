The Evening Star reported on efforts to prepare Haiti for eventual American withdrawal by training native personnel across various government departments. Haitian nationals were increasingly staffing public works, sanitary services, customs administration, and agricultural engineering, while Haitians comprised about half the commissioned officers in the gendarmerie. Concurrently, the American military presence had been reduced to an 800-man marine force.

El Evening Star informó sobre los esfuerzos para preparar a Haití para una eventual retirada estadounidense mediante la capacitación de personal autóctono en diversos departamentos gubernamentales. Ciudadanos haitianos se incorporaban de forma creciente a las obras públicas, servicios sanitarios, administración de aduanas e ingeniería agrícola, al tiempo que conformaban cerca de la mitad de los oficiales en la gendarmería. En forma paralela, la presencia militar estadounidense se había reducido a un contingente de 800 infantes de marina.

The yearly reports made to the State Department are eloquent in the increasing number and sphere of Haitian participation in the various fields. The department of public works is each year training a growing number of Haitian engineers, architects, artisans, office executives, hydrographic aids, steam gauge readers, telephone and telegraph operators and inspectors. The sanitary service is turning out Haitian physicians, surgeons, nurses, dentists, pharmacists and sanitary inspectors. Practically all the force of the financial adviser and general receiver of customs is purely Haitian. The recently formed office of the chief agricultural engineer is enrolling and training a Haitian personnel. In the ranks of the Haitian gendarmerie there are now approximately 60 Haitians of the best type who are either commissioned, acting or aspirant officers, or practically one-half of its commissioned strength. All these departments are organized into departments with district and subdistrict divisions, so that throughout Haiti the native Haitian is working shoulder to shoulder with Americans in the rehabilitation of the country. Coincident with this sound expansion has gone the gradual reduction of armed American forces to a minimum.
