The Evening Star reported that the Dominican Chamber of Commerce in the United States defended General Rafael Trujillo against criticism from the Committee for Dominican Democracy regarding his White House visit with President Roosevelt. The chamber praised Trujillo's public welfare and development achievements in the Dominican Republic. Meanwhile, Trujillo visited troops at Fort George G. Meade, while a reception scheduled at the Pan American Union was canceled out of respect for the late Secretary of the Navy Claude A. Swanson.

El Evening Star informó que la Cámara de Comercio Dominicana en Estados Unidos salió en defensa del general Rafael Trujillo ante los cuestionamientos formulados por el Committee for Dominican Democracy con motivo de su visita a la Casa Blanca para reunirse con el presidente Roosevelt. Dicha corporación comercial ensalzó las obras de bienestar social y de fomento material alcanzadas por Trujillo en la República Dominicana. Entretanto, Trujillo visitó a las tropas en Fort George G. Meade, al tiempo que una recepción programada en la Unión Panamericana fue cancelada en señal de duelo por el fenecido secretario de Marina Claude A. Swanson.

"As Americans, we strongly resent any criticism of the President of the United States from such a source, insulting his distinguished guest." the chamber declared in a statement for the press. The general, who will be here until Tuesday, when he departs by yacht for New York, went to Fort George G. Meade. Md., today to see the troops there. A reception at the Pan American Union which had been planned for tonight in the general's honor by Minister Pastoriza of the Dominican Republic has been canceled out of respect to the late Secretary Swanson. Mrs. Elizabeth Applefeld Dies of Short Illness Mrs. Elizabeth Clara Applefeld. 39. of 320 Jefferson street N.W., wife of Harry Applefeld, died yesterday in Sibley Hospital after a week's illness.
