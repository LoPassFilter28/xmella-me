According to a dispatch from Havana dated July 7, the recent presidential election in San Domingo was conducted peacefully and resulted in a majority of votes for the Rev. Fernando Arturo Merino, whose nomination was expected by the "Blues." General Caman, the Haytian Commissioner, was present in Puerto Plata to arrange for the expulsion of Dominican refugees from the Haytian frontiers. Additionally, the report noted that a railroad from Santiago to Samana was scheduled to begin construction soon and that the republic was prospering.

A tenor de un despacho de La Habana del 7 de julio, las recientes elecciones presidenciales en Santo Domingo transcurrieron pacíficamente, consagrando el triunfo del presbítero Fernando Arturo de Meriño, cuya postulación auspiciaba el partido de los «azules». El general Caman, delegado haitiano, asistió a Puerto Plata para concertar la expulsión de asilados dominicanos de la línea divisoria. Informóse asimismo del próximo inicio de las obras del ferrocarril entre Santiago y Samaná en medio del florecimiento que experimenta el país.

A Peaceful Election in San Domingo.

Havana, Jufy 7.—News from Puerto Plata,

San Domingo, to June 22, has been received.

Order prevailed during the election for Presi

dent of San Domingo. The Kev. Fernando

Arturo Merino obtained a majority of the

votes cast. The party of the "Blues" expect

ed the realization of their political programme

by the nomination of the Kev. Mr. Merino.

Gen. Caman, the Havtian Commissioner,was

in Puerto Plata with the object of arranging

for the expulsion of Dominican refugees from

the Haytian frontiers,whence they continually

infest the territory of San Domingo. The

building of a railroad from Santiago to Sa

mana was to be begun at an early day. The

republic was prospering.

Bernhardt's Plans for America.

London, July 0.—Sarah Bernhardt and a

complete company will sail from Havre on the

Kith of October next for New York. She will

make her debut at Booth's Theatre on the 18th

of November in "Adrienne Lecouvreur."

Jeanne Bernhardt, her sister, is among the

members of the company, which is now form

ing, and the stage manager will probably be

M. Belvaux, who was Rachel's stage manager

during her American tour.
