The Evening Star reported that the Senate confirmed five diplomatic envoys, including Joseph P. McGurk of New Jersey as Ambassador to the Dominican Republic. Other confirmed appointments included Ely E. Palmer to Afghanistan, Edwin Jackson Kyle to Guatemala, R. Henry Norweb to Panama, and Herman B. Baruch to Portugal. The paper also noted the death of Lt. Col. Isadore William Toubman in an airplane accident in Florida.

El Evening Star informó que el Senado ratificó el nombramiento de cinco diplomáticos de carrera, entre ellos Joseph P. McGurk, de Nueva Jersey, como embajador en la República Dominicana. Las demás designaciones confirmadas correspondieron a Ely E. Palmer en Afganistán, Edwin Jackson Kyle en Guatemala, R. Henry Norweb en Panamá y Herman B. Baruch en Portugal. El rotativo consignó asimismo el fallecimiento del teniente coronel Isadore William Toubman en un percance de aviación acaecido en Florida.

The Senate yesterday confirmed these envoys: Ely E. Palmer of Rhode Island as Minister to Afghanistan; Joseph P. McGurk of New Jersey as Ambassador to the Dominican Republic; Edwin Jackson Kyle of Texas as Ambassador to Guatemala; R. Henry Norweb of Ohio as Ambassador to Panama, and Herman B. Baruch of New York as Minister to Portugal. Mr. Baruch is a brother of Bernard M. Baruch, New York financier and adviser to the Government. Lt. Col. Toubman Killed in Airplane Accident By the Associated Press. ELGIN FIELD. Fla Feb 9 —Lt. Col. Isadore William Toubman, chief of the fighter department of the aircraft section here, was killed yesterday in an airplane accident, officers announced. A veteran of service In New Guinea and Australia, he formerly lived in Wethersfield. Conn. Jail for Cigarette Profiteer NEW YORK, Feb. 9 (UP).—Henry Smith, operator of a Bronx candy store, was sentenced yesterday to five days in jail on his plea of guilty to charging 20 cents for a 17-cent package of cigarettes.
