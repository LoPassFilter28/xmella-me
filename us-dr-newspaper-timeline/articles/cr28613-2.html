The Evening Star profiled Flor de Oro Trujillo following her diplomatic appointment handling cultural relations under Dominican Ambassador Dr. Jesús María Troncoso. The report noted her interest in philosophy, jewelry, and writing articles about the Dominican Republic for a New York magazine. Señorita Minerva Bernardino, Dominican delegate and vice chairman of the Inter-American Commission of Women, praised the appointment, highlighting that the Dominican Republic had granted women full civil and political rights.

El Evening Star consagró una crónica a la señorita Flor de Oro Trujillo tras su asignación diplomática a cargo de los asuntos culturales bajo la jefatura del embajador dominicano Dr. Jesús María Troncoso. El reportaje aludía a sus aficiones por la filosofía, el coleccionismo de alhajas y la preparación de artículos descriptivos sobre la República Dominicana para un magacín de Nueva York. La señorita Minerva Bernardino, delegada dominicana y vicepresidenta de la Comisión Interamericana de Mujeres, ensalzó el nombramiento resaltando la vocación artística de Flor de Oro y su versatilidad para expresarse fluidamente en diversas lenguas europeas.

Her burgundy colored slippers matched the figure in the scarf. To top off her attire she wore her most cherished possession, a handsome set of jewelry featuring Brazilian topaz and diamonds set in yellow gold. Senora Trujillo's fondness for lovely clothes and jewels is evident, but she has many serious interests. with a study of philosophy probably in the lead, she said. She likes to write and is doing a series of articles on the Dominican Republic for a New York magazine. She toys with the idea of writing fiction, but with a serious theme. "I think all my stories will have the same title, 'Realities,' she remarked. The Dominican Ambassador, Dr. J. M. Troncoso. has assigned her to special duties dealing with cultural relations. Her diplomatic appointment Is hailed by the Inter-American Commission of Women, whose vice chairman is Senorita Minerva Bernardino, the Dominican Republic's delegate. Look Forward to Celebration. Senorita Bernardino pointed out that the purpose of the commission is to raise the position of women of the Americas and to work for their civil and political rights. The fact that the Dominican Republic has given its women full political and civil rights means the fulfillment of that objective, she said. Both Senora Trujillo and Senorita Bernardino already are looking forward to the next women's congress to be held in their republic in celebration of the anniversary of women's rights.
