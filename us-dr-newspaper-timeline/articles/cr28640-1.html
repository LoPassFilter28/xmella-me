An effort to remove William E. Pulliam as collector of customs in the Dominican Republic failed, and the State Department announced that Pulliam is returning to his job in Santo Domingo and is being retained in recognition of his merit and capable work. The decision is described as a rebuke to the regime of President Rafael Trujillo, after Dominican Minister Despradel and Counselor Espaillat had represented that Pulliam was persona non grata. Pulliam served as collector from 1907 to 1913 and from 1921 to the present, and his honesty is widely praised.
