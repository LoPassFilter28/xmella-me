Passage summaryResumen del pasaje

*The Kenosha Telegraph* reported in a brief dispatch that another revolution had occurred in the Dominican Republic. President Ignacio González surrendered his office and departed the country.

*The Kenosha Telegraph* dio cuenta en un breve parte de un nuevo estallido revolucionario en la República Dominicana. El presidente Ignacio González resignó el poder y abandonó el territorio nacional.
