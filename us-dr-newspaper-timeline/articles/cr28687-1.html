The Evening Star reported that Dominican Minister Pastoriza dismissed reports of border violence as exaggerated, claiming that political enemies of Presidents Vincent and Trujillo were seeking to create tension. In contrast, a spokesman for visiting Haitian Cabinet Minister Léger stated that Dominican police opened fire on a crowd of 1,300 to 1,400 Haitian refugees during unannounced expulsions from Dominican territory. Léger traveled to Washington to confer with President Roosevelt and Acting Secretary of State Welles, while his spokesman denied rumors of domestic unrest against President Vincent.

Mr. Pastoriza, in a prepared statement, charged that "political enemies" of President Vincent of Haiti and President Trujillo of the Dominican Republic were seeking to create tension between the two countries and embarrassment for their respective administrations. The Haitian cabinet minister, who arrived in Washington early today for a conference with President Roosevelt and Acting Secretary of State Welles, declared there has been no frontier Incident nor any clashes between armed forces. Expulsion Order Cited. A spokesman for Mr. Leger said the attacks began as the result of an order by the Dominican government for the expulsion of Haitians from Dominican territory, with no notice served to the Haitian government. The Dominican police, the spokesman said, fired into a crowd of from 1,300 to 1,400 refugees in carrying out the expulsion order. Rumors of possible outbreaks in Haiti against the Vincent government were unfounded, the spokesman said.
