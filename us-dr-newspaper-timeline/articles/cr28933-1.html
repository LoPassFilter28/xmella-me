THE HAYTIAN REBELS.

E. Nadale, an emissary of the Haytian Gov-

ernment, arrived in this city on Monday on a secret

mission. He said that the rebels under Dazelais and La

Forestre numbered nearly 1,000 and were composed

mostly of exiled Haytiaus, Cubans and Dominicans.

Bazelals 1s a very brave man and would fight to the last

before giving up the port of Miragoane. La Forestre,

who was Inspector of Prisons under the late Emperor

Soulougne, was also not wanting in courage. Oa one

occasion he caused all the State prisoners to be shot,

saying that it was cheaper to kill than to feed them. A

stubborn and prolong rage may be expected, but

M. Nadale seemed confident that the insurgents would

finally be overthrown by the Government troops.
