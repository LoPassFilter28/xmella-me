Passage summaryResumen del pasaje

On Monday, E. Nadale, an emissary of the Haytian Government, arrived on a secret mission and reported that Haytian rebels under Bazelais and La Forestre numbered nearly 1,000, consisting mostly of exiled Haytians, Cubans, and Dominicans. Nadale stated that Bazelais would fight to the last to hold the port of Miragoane, and characterized La Forestre as a courageous former Inspector of Prisons. While a stubborn and prolonged struggle was expected, Nadale expressed confidence that the insurgents would ultimately be defeated by government troops.

El lunes arribó en misión confidencial E. Nadale, comisionado del Gobierno haitiano, manifestando que las partidas rebeldes haitianas a las órdenes de Bazelais y La Forestre sumaban casi un millar de hombres, en su mayor parte emigrados haitianos, cubanos y dominicanos. Aseguró Nadale que Bazelais se batiría hasta el último extremo por conservar la plaza de Miragoâne y definió a La Forestre como un intrépido exinspector de prisiones. Aun previendo una contienda tenaz y prolongada, Nadale confió en que los destacamentos gubernamentales aplastarían finalmente a los facciosos.
