The Evening Star reported that ousted Cuban dictator Gerardo Machado was preparing to leave his refuge in the Dominican Republic after President Rafael Trujillo advised him to depart. Trujillo had previously granted Machado an extensive estate and extraterritorial status protected by 130 Cuban guards, but faced threats of retaliation from the Cuban government. Machado was reportedly seeking a new haven in Venezuela under President Gómez.

El Evening Star informó que el derrocado dictador cubano Gerardo Machado se preparaba para abandonar su refugio en la República Dominicana luego de que el presidente Rafael Trujillo le aconsejara marcharse. Trujillo le había otorgado previamente a Machado una extensa finca y estatus extraterritorial custodiado por 130 guardias cubanos, pero enfrentaba amenazas de represalias por parte del gobierno cubano. Según trascendió, Machado buscaba un nuevo asilo en Venezuela bajo el gobierno del presidente Gómez.

is In a hot spot again. After many peregrinations he finally found haven in the Dominican Republic. President Trujillo, a close friend of Machado, offered him a huge domain in the island and gave him extraterritorial privileges. Machado settled down with his family and got himself a guard of 130 Cuban uniformed henchmen to form his personal guard. Neither the Dominican police nor the army was allowed to trespass on Machado's property. But the Cuban government threatened Trujillo with reprisals if he refused to surrender his old friend. The President of San Domingo got scared and advised Machado to clear out as soon as possible. Machado is looking now toward Venezuela, where his friend Gen. Gomez rules with an iron hand. He expects to find a place somewhere near [Caracas?], where he can settle down in peace and enjoy the millions he has been able to salt away while he was Cuba's dictator. RESERVE AWARDS MADE Naval Station at Philadelphia Wins First Place, The Naval Reserve communications station at Philadelphia yesterday was awarded first place by the Navy in the annual competition among these units. Philadelphia received a merit rating of 89, followed by San Francisco with 88 and Salt Lake City with 87. For the third straight year the 12th Naval Reserve district, with headquarters at San Francisco, received top rating for districts. [illegible]
