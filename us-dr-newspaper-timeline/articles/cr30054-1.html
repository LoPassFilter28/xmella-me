Springfield Weekly Republican. Springfield, Mass., November 20, 1903. Page 7. View the originalVer el original

The Republican prints a San Juan item and two Washington items: the St. Simon arrived at San Juan, Porto Rico, Wednesday from Hayti and San Domingo and reports that all of the Dominican republic, except the capital, San Domingo, was in the hands of the revolutionists, that General Jiminez, leader of the Dominican revolutionists, left Hayti the 16th and is due to arrive today before San Domingo and assume direction of the siege of that city, and that General Caceres was assisting in the military operations of the revolutionists. Associated Press dispatches from San Domingo Tuesday said that, yielding to the continued vigorous action of Minister Powell, the Dominican government had appointed Minister Galvan and Judge George Gray of Delaware to arbitrate the difficulties between that government and the San Domingo Improvement Company, an American concern, that Minister Galvan was to leave immediately for the United States, and that on his departure the insurgents might make an effort to capture him; the Improvement Company was operating under several concessions obtained from the Dominican government, when an attempt was made to withdraw the privileges granted under these concessions. Under the head 'Recognition Refused Revolutionists by United States,' the paper reports that the revolutionists of San Domingo Tuesday applied to the state department for recognition by the United States, the application being presented by J. H. Giordan, who represented himself as the provisional agent of the revolutionary government, of which General Jiminez is the head.El Republican publica una nota de San Juan y dos notas de Washington: el St. Simon llegó a San Juan, Puerto Rico, el miércoles procedente de Haití y Santo Domingo e informa que toda la república dominicana, excepto la capital, San Domingo, estaba en manos de los revolucionarios, que el general Jiménez, líder de los revolucionarios dominicanos, salió de Haití el 16 y debe llegar hoy frente a San Domingo y asumir la dirección del sitio de esa ciudad, y que el general Cáceres ayudaba en las operaciones militares de los revolucionarios. Despachos de Associated Press procedentes de San Domingo dijeron el martes que, cediendo a la continua y vigorosa acción del ministro Powell, el gobierno dominicano había nombrado al ministro Galván y al juez George Gray, de Delaware, para arbitrar las dificultades entre ese gobierno y la San Domingo Improvement Company, una empresa estadounidense, que el ministro Galván debía partir inmediatamente hacia los Estados Unidos, y que a su partida los insurgentes podrían hacer un esfuerzo por capturarlo; la Improvement Company operaba bajo varias concesiones obtenidas del gobierno dominicano, cuando se intentó retirar los privilegios concedidos bajo esas concesiones. Bajo el titular «Recognition Refused Revolutionists by United States», el periódico informa que los revolucionarios de San Domingo solicitaron el martes al Departamento de Estado el reconocimiento por los Estados Unidos, siendo presentada la solicitud por J. H. Giordan, quien se presentó como agente provisional del gobierno revolucionario, del cual el general Jiménez es el jefe.
