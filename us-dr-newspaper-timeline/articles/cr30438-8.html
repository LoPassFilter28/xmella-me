In Santo Domingo the troops of the province of Cibao entered Azua, while on the march towards the South, for the purpose of joining their forces with those of a neighboring State that had also declared in favor of the insurgents. Although inferior in numbers, they maintained their position until the arrival of reinforcements, when they drove back the St. Domingo troops with some loss.
