"I want?" demanded Senator Pomerene in opening today's hearing, of former American Minister Francisco Peynado. "Our independence with the assurance of American friendship," he replied. "That's all we want." Peynado testified that under the present election laws, which do not require registration, corrupt elections were certain, but that the frequent revolutions were largely harmless, involving but a few hundred people at the most. One American was killed in a revolution in 1885 by a stray shot, he declared. The Dominican government paid his heirs $33,000. Demand that the American government cease treating Dominicans "like negroes of the Congo" was made by Pedro Perez, former governor of Seibo Province, when he was called before the commission.
