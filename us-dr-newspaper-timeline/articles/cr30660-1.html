Washington, Nov. 13.—The extraordinary preparations now going on in the navy yards are not in expectation of war with Spain about Cuba, but simply to keep guard over the annexation of the Dominican Republic to the United States. The conditions of annexation have been fully settled between Gen. Babcock, confidential officer of Grant's staff, and the authorities of San Domingo. The treaty is similar to that for the annexation of Texas, and will be laid before the Senate at the coming session.
