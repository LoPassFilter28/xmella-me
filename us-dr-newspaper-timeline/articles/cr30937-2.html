An Associated Press dispatch from Puerto Plata reports that extensive repairs have begun on the Dominican Central railroad between Puerto Plata and Santiago, in line with the wishes of the United States military government, including new deviations that will reduce the grade from twelve percent to a maximum of three percent. Tons of tobacco, coffee and cacao have accumulated unmoved at Santiago, and other work described includes a new wharf and customs house at Puerto Plata, a hydro-electric project to serve Santiago, Moca, La Vega and Puerto Plata, rebuilding the bridges on the Santiago to Monte Cristi road that revolutionists burned last spring to check the advance of United States Marines, and a macadam road from Macoris to Santo Domingo City.

Un despacho de Associated Press desde Puerto Plata informa que han comenzado extensas reparaciones en el ferrocarril Central Dominicano entre Puerto Plata y Santiago, de acuerdo con los deseos del gobierno militar de los Estados Unidos, incluidas nuevas desviaciones que reducirán la pendiente del doce por ciento a un máximo del tres por ciento. Toneladas de tabaco, café y cacao se han acumulado sin mover en Santiago, y otras obras descritas incluyen un nuevo muelle y una aduana en Puerto Plata, un proyecto hidroeléctrico para servir a Santiago, Moca, La Vega y Puerto Plata, la reconstrucción de los puentes del camino de Santiago a Monte Cristi que los revolucionarios quemaron la primavera pasada para frenar el avance de los marines de los Estados Unidos, y un camino de macadán de Macorís a la ciudad de Santo Domingo.

Engineers are at work on the development of a hydro-electric project [illegible] which when completed will furnish electricity for Santiago, [Moca?], La Vega and Puerto Plata, the four principal cities of the Cibao and north coast. With the exception of San Pedro de Macoris and Santo Domingo City, Santiago and Puerto Plata are the only cities in the Republic now having electric lighting service. Other public improvements that are to be immediately undertaken include the [rebuilding?] of bridges on the road from Santiago to Monte Cristi. Seven bridges on this highway were burned by revolutionists last spring to check the advance of United States Marines.
