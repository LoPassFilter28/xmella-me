SCHOONER SEQUIN CLEARS FOR PORT IN SANTO DOMINGO The American schooner Sequin left Pensacola yesterday with a light load headed for St. Andrews up the gulf. At that point a big cargo of lumber is to be taken aboard, and the ship's papers indicated that this timber would be carried to the port of Macoris, Dominican Republic. Although several ships have been expected in the port for the past few days no arrivals were reported at the customs office yesterday. "Y" SECRETARIES AT LOCAL STATION OFF ON FURLOUGH The Y. M. C. A. secretaries stationed at the Naval Air Station, are taking a furlough, along with the service men during the holidays. Three of the men, R. B. Patton, Building secretary, W. W. Jones, business secretary, and J. D. McCord, education secretary, are to spend Christmas home. Secretary Patton was called away earlier than expected to his home in Livingstone, Ala., by a telegram from relatives in regard to his brother, who is very low. Secretary McCord will spend his holiday in Atlanta, Ga., his home, with his family, while Secretary Jones will go to Chipley, Florida, his home, where he ceased publication of his paper to enter the war work.
