Letters received in Boston from Cape Haytien and Port-au-Prince to July 16 make no mention of any disastrous battle having been fought on July 13, as reported. Letters from a member of Soulouque's cabinet state that the collision in May between the Haitians and Dominicans was accidental, and that the Emperor will firmly maintain the truce.
