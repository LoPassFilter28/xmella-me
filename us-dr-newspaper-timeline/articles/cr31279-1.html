The Evening Star profiled General Rafael Trujillo, noting that despite being denounced as a tyrant and accused of the massacre of thousands of Haitians two years earlier, he was recognized for administrative energy and public works. Having taken power in 1930 under the slogan "God and Trujillo," he oversaw road construction, school expansion, and the rebuilding and renaming of the capital before handing the presidency to Dr. Jacinto Bienvenido Peynado. The article noted that Trujillo continued to direct Dominican governance while being celebrated by sympathetic biographers as an inspired leader.

El Evening Star perfiló al general Rafael Trujillo, señalando que, a pesar de haber sido denunciado como un tirano y acusado de la matanza de miles de haitianos dos años antes, se le reconocía por su empuje administrativo y sus planes de obras públicas. Habiendo tomado el poder en 1930 bajo el lema «Dios y Trujillo», dirigió la construcción de caminos, la expansión de escuelas y la reedificación y cambio de nombre de la capital antes de traspasar la presidencia formalmente al Dr. Jacinto Bienvenido Peynado. El artículo consignaba que Trujillo seguía conduciendo las riendas del gobierno dominicano mientras biógrafos afines lo ensalzaban como un líder providencial.

Even if he is denounced as a tyrant and accused of the massacre of thousands of Haitians two years ago, during a border struggle along the boundary between the Dominican Republic and Haiti, there is no doubt that he is a forceful leader with a certain genius in public finance. He has an extraordinary concept of himself as a child of destiny When he was still a boy, he resolved to be the president of the Dominican Republic, where storm and strife in politics and insecurity of tenure for presidents was the classical political situation. He won the post in 1930, when he was 39, after a campaign whose slogan was "God and Trujillo." He relinquished it last August to a devoted follower. Dr. Jacinto Bienvenido [Peynado?], who still follows his wishes devotedly. Gen. Trujillo's biographer suggests that in his governing he is guided by an "electric force" from Heaven. He has built roads and pursued other public works, increased the number of public schools, reconstructed the ancient capital of the republic, Santo Domingo, after the 1930 hurricane (and renamed it in his own honor) and enhanced the lot of those persons in his country who are willing to accept his leadership. He is energetic, industrious and a born leader. An inspired biography of him published as a panegyric is revealing for all its encomiums. Progress Through Discipline.
