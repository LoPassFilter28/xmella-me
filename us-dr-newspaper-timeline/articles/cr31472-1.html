The Manning Times reported escalating conflict at Puerto Plata, where the gunboat Machias exchanged fire with shore combatants and used its main guns to silence President Bordas's artillery after non-combatant safety was violated. The revenue cutter Algonquin evacuated 42 American and Puerto Rican refugees from the besieged city amid heavy crossfire between federal forces and Legalista rebels. Meanwhile, a U.S. naval squadron patrolled Dominican coastal ports while Washington sought a mediator to restore peace.

The Manning Times informó sobre la agravación del conflicto en Puerto Plata, donde el cañonero Machias cruzó disparos con combatientes apostados en tierra y usó su artillería mayor para acallar los cañones del presidente Bordas tras quebrantarse la seguridad de la población civil. El guardacostas Algonquin evacuó a 42 refugiados estadounidenses y puertorriqueños de la ciudad sitiada en medio del recio fuego cruzado entre tropas gubernamentales y rebeldes legalistas. Mientras tanto, una escuadra naval de los EE. UU. patrullaba los puertos de la costa dominicana mientras Washington procuraba un mediador para restablecer la paz.

Meanwhile the navy is maintaining a considerable fleet in Dominican waters, including the South Carolina and Machias at Puerto Plata, the Marietta at Sanchez and the Castine at Azua. Other vessels are on the Haitien end of the island. Reckless firing. Dominican rebels sent several shots against the hull of the American gunboat Machias in Puerto Plata harbor late Tuesday night and drew upon themselves a warning volley from the Machias' three-pounders and automatic rifles. The shooting from shore stopped immediately. A few days ago the gunboat used her main battery of four-inch guns to silence President Bordas' batteries, which were bombarding the rebel garrison holding the city, in violation of the agreement that the lives of foreigners and other non-combatants would not be endangered by artillery fire. Capt. Russell, of the battleship South Carolina, in his report Wednesday night said considerable scattering rifle fire from the rebels passed over the Machias and some shots struck her. Later the dispatch said the "junta commission of Legalistas", representing the revolutionists, boarded the Machias and gave assurances of their desire to avoid endangering the safety of Americans.
