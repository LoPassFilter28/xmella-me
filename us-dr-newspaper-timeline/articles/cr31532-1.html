Passage summaryResumen del pasaje

The *Springfield Weekly Republican* reported on Charles Lindbergh's official reception in Santo Domingo, where he was entertained by Dominican President Vásquez, the American minister, and Customs Receiver-General W. E. Pullman. Lindbergh laid a wreath at the Columbus monument, met the archbishop, and reviewed a large military parade before immense crowds prior to flying to Haiti.
