The Evening Star reported that diplomatic representatives across the Americas accepted invitations to the Pan-American Aviation Day ball at the Mayflower Hotel. A special invitation was extended to General Rafael Trujillo of the Dominican Republic, who was staying in New York. The event featured decorations provided by Latin American embassies and participation from Cuban aviators.

El Evening Star informó que representantes del cuerpo diplomático de las Américas acogieron las invitaciones al baile del Día de la Aviación Panamericana en el Mayflower Hotel. Se cursó una invitación de honor al general Rafael Trujillo, de la República Dominicana, quien residía temporalmente en Nueva York. La velada contó con ornamentaciones suministradas por embajadas hispanoamericanas y con la presencia de aviadores cubanos.

Senor Dr. Gabriel Turbay and the Minister of Costa Rica and Senora de Fernandez are among the latest Pan-American diplomats to accept invitations to the Pan-American Aviation Day ball, to be held tomorrow night at the Mayflower Hotel. A special invitation has been sent to Gen. Rafael Trujillo of the Dominican Republic. Gen. Trujillo is now in New York. Members of the "Escuadrilla de Aviacion Interamericana de Cuba" also have been invited. This group is scheduled to take part in the Havana celebration of Pan-American Aviation Day and the plan is to have them fly here in time to be honor guests at the Washington celebration. Embassies and Legations of the Latin American republics are to supply decorations emblematic of their countries for use at the celebration here. A general Pan-American motif of decoration will be used. Manor House Unit Of D. A. R. to Meet Tuesday Night Manor House Chapter. Daughters of the American Revolution, will hold its December meeting in the historic Stephen Boomer Balsh house in Georgetown tomorrow at 8:30 p.m. The house, which is now the home of Miss Helen Montgomery, has been marked by the D. A. R. A skit, re-enacting a Christmas Eve in 1830, will be presented, following a business meeting. Dr. Clifton P. Clark will impersonate the Rev. Mr. Balsh. A mitten shower for one of the D. A. R. schools also will be a feature. Honor guests will include Mrs. Charles Carroll Haig. Mrs. Harry C. Oberholser and Mrs. Geoffrey Creyke. Miss Minne Hunt is the chapter regent.
