Passage summaryResumen del pasaje

The Evening Star carried an advertisement by the Atlantic Gulf and West Indies Steamship Lines promoting passenger cruise accommodations and freight services connecting Cuba, Mexico, Puerto Rico, and the Dominican Republic. The announcement highlighted modern amenities including air-conditioned staterooms, outdoor swimming pools, and shore excursions. It noted that regular freight service was currently operating while winter passenger cruise schedules were being determined.

El *Evening Star* publicó un aviso comercial de la compañía Atlantic Gulf and West Indies Steamship Lines promocionando servicios de pasajes turísticos de recreo y transporte de fletes entre Cuba, México, Puerto Rico y la República Dominicana. El reclamo publicitario realzaba las modernas comodidades a bordo, tales como camarotes con aire acondicionado, piletas exteriores y excursiones costeras organizadas, advirtiendo que los cargueros mercantes navegaban con regularidad mientras se ultimaban los itinerarios de cruceros invernales de pasajeros.
