The Most Reverend Adolfo Nouel, Catholic archbishop of Santo Domingo, has been elected provisional president of the republic for a term of two years by the Dominican Congress.

El reverendísimo Adolfo Nouel, arzobispo católico de Santo Domingo, ha sido elegido presidente provisional de la república por un período de dos años por el Congreso dominicano.

Seventeen persons were struck by lightning while standing around a cave at Germiston in Rhodesia, South Africa, Tuesday. Five were instantly killed and five others were perhaps fatally injured. Most Rev. Adolfo Nouel, Catholic archbishop of Santo Domingo, has been elected provisional president of the republic for a term of two years, by the Dominican congress. The schooner Wm. P. Frye arrived at Philadelphia Tuesday with a cargo of sugar from Hawaii, after a voyage of five months. During the whole trip after leaving Kahuliu, the schooner was not once in sight of land, nor did it sight another vessel during the trip. Six persons were killed, eight seriously injured and two score or more painfully bruised in a head-on collision on the Pennsylvania railroad near Dresden, Ohio, Tuesday. Senor McCumber of North Dakota, has introduced a bill to repeal what he characterized as the "obnoxious provisions" of the newspaper publicity law. Dr. Peter F. Bohnson, state veterinarian of Georgia, estimates that the cattle tick costs the people of the United States not less than $100,000,000 a year. Business failures throughout the United States during November totaled 1,175, with liabilities of $15,266.7[illegible]
