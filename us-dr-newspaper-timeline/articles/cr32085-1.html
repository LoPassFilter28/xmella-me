Beginning to see Their Danger.

SourceFuente: New-York dispatch, New York [N.Y.], 1856-08-17
https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn83030364/1856-08-17/ed-1/?sp=8
diplomacy_treatiessugar_trade_economyrevolution_unrest

—The people of Europe are beginning to see more and more clearly the evils and dangers of large standing armies. Even the press is beginning to speak out. The recent coup d'etat in Spain for the overthrow of constitutional government is alarming, as it shows what may be done at any moment in England and in other countries. It is not certain, but the latest accounts, through government sources, make it appear that the despots of Spain have triumphed, over-throwing the constitutionalists and trampling upon this news there was a row by the people. Col. Munoz had fought a duel with a young creole. Some of the political exiles were expected home soon from Spain. Trade and freights were dull. A letter, from Port au Platt, dated on the 3d instant, states that there has been exciting and revolutionary occurrences in St. Domingo city, a man-of-war at Port au Platt reports the capital of the Dominican republic in a state of revolution, in consequence of a treaty with Spain, on the part of the President, which was not approved of by the people.
