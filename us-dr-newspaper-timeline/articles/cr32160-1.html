The Evening Star reported that Cuban Foreign Minister Raúl Roa accused three U.S. senators of collaborating to assist Dominican dictator Rafael Trujillo and other opponents of Fidel Castro. Roa alleged that Senators Ellender, Eastland, and Capehart sought to undermine Cuba by attempting to reduce its sugar quota in the American market.

El Evening Star informó que el canciller cubano Raúl Roa acusó a tres senadores estadounidenses de confabularse para ayudar al dictador dominicano Rafael Trujillo y a otros adversarios de Fidel Castro. Roa afirmó que los senadores Ellender, Eastland y Capehart intentaban socavar a Cuba procurando reducir su cuota azucarera en el mercado estadounidense.

Mr. Roa spoke a few hours after returning from Washington, where he represented Cuba before the Council of the Organization of American States. He cleared the United States State Department of any link with the alleged plot. He named as a ringleader Emilio Nunez Portuondo, Cuban Ambassador to the United Nations during the regime of Dictator Fulgencio Batista. Mr. Roa also told a television audience he believes that three United States Senators—Ellender, Democrat of Louisiana; Eastland, Democrat of Mississippi and Capehart, Republican of Indiana—are working together to help Dictator Rafael L. Trujillo of the Dominican Republic and other foes of Mr. Castro by reducing the quota of sugar Cuba can sell in the United States.
