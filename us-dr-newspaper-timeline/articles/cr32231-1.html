MARINES SAIL FOR SANTA DOMINGO. Will Compel the Re-opening of Custom Houses Closed by Revolutionists Along Border of Hayti. Associated Press. Washington, D. C., Sept. 24. — A force of 750 American marines will sail Friday from Philadelphia aboard the transport Prairie for Santa Domingo, to compel the reopening of Dominican custom houses along the border of Hayti, which were closed by the revolutionists recently. The authority for this step was given in an order issued by the president today. The marines are expected to arrive on October 3
