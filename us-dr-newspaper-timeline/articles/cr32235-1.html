1858-06-29 · Evansville, Ia. [i.e. Ind.] · 1844-1861 Independence, recognition, Samana schemes

FOREIGN NEWS.

The steamship Moses Taylor, has arrived from California, with one million eight hundred thousand dollars in specie.

The Moses Taylor left at Aspinwal, the U. S. steamer Colorado, which arrived on the 17th from San Domingo.

The Powhatan was at Hong Kong. It is rumored that the Chinese intend to destroy all the European dwellings on the Hong Kong river.

The Emperor of Russia has invited the French Ambassador to accompany him on a tour through the Southern Provinces.

The Colorado brought intelligence that President Baez, of the Dominican Republic, had capitulated to Santa Anna, and was to leave San Domingo on the 12th for Curacoa. The most of his adherents left the day previous.

Santa Anna had given Commodore McIntosh the strongest assurances of the protection of American citizens and desired him to express to his government his wish to maintain the most amicable relations. It is said that the Commodore succeeded in obtaining from President Baez all the documents for which he was sent.

The Colorado was to sail on the 21st for Havana.

The U. S. steamer Saranac and the Vandalia, were lying at Panama.

The Decatur sailed on the 13th, and the Merrimack on the 19th.

The excitement in California in relation to the Frazier river gold mines continues. Three thousand persons have already left San Francisco for that region.

The California mines are yielding largely and accounts from every section are highly favorable. The agricultural prospects were never brighter, and a full harvest is confidently anticipated.

The California papers contain more than the usual amount of murders, assassinations, casualties, etc.

The advices from Oregon are to the 24th of May, they announce a general Indian outbreak there. Col. Stevens' command on Snake river was attacked on the 16th of May and forced to retreat with the loss of fifty privates, three officers, two howitzers, baggage wagons, and nearly all the animals.

Three companies of dragoons and one of infantry were engaged with one thousand five hundred Indians. The names of the officers killed are Capt. Winder and Lieut. Gaxen, the name of the third is not given.

A fire in Nevada on the 23d of May, destroyed nearly all the business portion of the city.

The town of San Andreas, Calveras county was entirely destroyed by fire on the 2d of June.

The U. S. surveying steamer, Shubrick, arrived from Philadelphia, via Panama, at San Francisco, May 27th.
