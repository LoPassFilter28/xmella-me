Evening star — 1939-07-14

The *Evening Star* published a guest list for a social gathering honoring General Rafael Trujillo, which included prominent figures such as Dr. and Mrs. Nicholas Murray Butler, General and Mrs. Walter C. Short, and Dominican officials. Following a luncheon, guests traveled to "The Chimneys," the Oyster Bay residence of Mr. and Mrs. Howard C. Brokaw, where an afternoon tea was held in Trujillo's honor.

El *Evening Star* publicó la nómina de concurrentes a una tertulia social ofrecida en homenaje al general Rafael Trujillo, en la cual figuraban personalidades destacadas como el Dr. Nicholas Murray Butler y señora, el general Walter C. Short y señora, y funcionarios diplomáticos dominicanos. Concluido un almuerzo, los agasajados se trasladaron a «The Chimneys», la residencia en Oyster Bay del matrimonio Howard C. Brokaw, donde se sirvió el té de la tarde en agasajo a Trujillo.
