The Waterbury Evening Democrat reported on the clandestine expedition of the steamer Fanita, which picked up exiled General Villanueva and pilot Chris Roberts at Great Inagua while concealing insurgent troops in its hold. Led by General Juan Isidro Jimenes, the expedition aimed to overthrow President Heureaux following extensive preparations. Although early observers expected success, the Dominican consul reported that the insurgent force had been defeated upon landing at Monte Cristi.

El Waterbury Evening Democrat informó sobre la expedición clandestina del vapor Fanita, que recogió en Gran Inagua al general exiliado Villanueva y al práctico Chris Roberts mientras ocultaba tropas insurgentes en su bodega. Encabezada por el general Juan Isidro Jimenes, la expedición tenía por objetivo derrocar al presidente Heureaux tras minuciosos preparativos. Aunque los primeros observadores auguraban el éxito, el cónsul dominicano informó que la fuerza insurgente había sido derrotada al desembarcar en Monte Cristi.

The captain of the Fanita said the portion of her cargo which was not landed consisted of coal, but there was good reason to suppose that it consisted of something very different from coal. The majority of the Dominicans, it appears, while here were shut up in the hold of the Fanita so as to escape attention. General Juan Isidro Jimenes and General Villanueva are believed to have been on board. It is noted that the former has long been in the public eye in Santo Domingo as a desirable man for the presidency. General Villanueva fought bravely against President Heureaux's alleged usurpation and was exiled. He has been living in Great Inagua for some time past, and the Fanita is said to have stopped there in order to take him on board. The preparations for the revolution are said to have been very elaborate, and there is a strong belief here that the revolutionists are sure of success. But, on the other hand, the Dominican consul here says he has a report that the revolutionists have been defeated at Monte Cristi. The report is not generally believed.
