Passage summaryResumen del pasaje

The *Evening Star* reported that a luncheon was held at the Statler Hotel honoring retiring Costa Rican Minister Dr. Luis Fernández and Dominican congressman Wenceslao Troncoso. Troncoso was visiting Washington as the guest of his brother, Dominican Minister Dr. Jesús María Troncoso, and sister-in-law. Attendees included diplomats from Panama and Honduras as well as officials from the State Department and Pan-American Union.

El *Evening Star* consignó un agasajo servido en el Statler Hotel en honor del saliente ministro costarricense Dr. Luis Fernández y del legislador dominicano Wenceslao Troncoso. Este último se hallaba de visita en Washington alojado por su hermano, el ministro dominicano Dr. Jesús María Troncoso, y su cuñada. Entre los convidados acudieron personeros de Panamá y Honduras, conjuntamente con delegados del Departamento de Estado y miembros directivos de la Unión Panamericana.
