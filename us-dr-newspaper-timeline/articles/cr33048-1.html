Passage summaryResumen del pasaje

Based on the provided excerpt, the American steamship *Tybee*, commanded by C. J. F. Kuhl, arrived in San Domingo on June 7th with passenger Gen. Pablo Villanueva, a former Minister of War under Báez and González who sought to retire on his estates. Although the local government demanded his surrender, Capt. Kuhl refused and claimed the protection of the American flag, while the U.S. Consul protested the forcible removal during an interview at the consulate. Ultimately, soldiers boarded the vessel, tied Villanueva to a chair with ropes, and handed him over the ship's side while Capt. Kuhl spread the Stars and Stripes on the deck, leading to current reports that a U.S. man-of-war has been sent to exact reparation.

Kuhl, arribó a Santo Domingo el 7 de junio con el pasajero general Pablo Villanueva, exministro de Guerra bajo Báez y González, quien pretendía retirarse a sus posesiones. UU. protestó por la extracción forzosa durante una entrevista en el consulado. Finalmente, los soldados abordaron la nave, ataron a Villanueva a una silla con sogas y lo descendieron por el costado del buque mientras el capitán Kuhl extendía las barras y estrellas en cubierta, motivando los despachos actuales según los cuales se ha enviado un buque de guerra estadounidense para exigir satisfacciones.
