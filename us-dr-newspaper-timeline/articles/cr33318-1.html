LATE FROM HAYTI.

The Dominican Government had recalled its Minister to Hayti, in consequence of an insurrectionary outbreak in the district of Cibao, in Santo Domingo, wnich the Dominican authorities believed had been instigated by the Haytians. The Haytian Government, in delivering his passports to the Minister, Gen. Valvende, had addressed a note to him, assuring him of the sincere desire of the Haytians for peace, and that whatever aid had been given the Dominican insurgents had been given by the Haytian rebels. Gen. Cabral, however, had notified the Haytian Government that relations between the two countries were suspended, and President Salnave had issued a proclamation reciting the facts of the dispute, and declaring that if the Dominicans made war, the Haytians were ready.
