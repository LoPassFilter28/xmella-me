'San Domingan revolution': by messenger from Cape Haytien - Mauricio Jiménez's revolutionists attacked Guayubín, killed the government commander of the town, cut the Monte Cristi-Santiago telegraph, and have been joined by the partisans of ex-President Morales.

«Revolución dominicana»: por mensajero desde Cabo Haitiano - los revolucionarios de Mauricio Jiménez atacaron Guayubín, mataron al comandante gubernamental del pueblo, cortaron el telégrafo Monte Cristi-Santiago y han sido reforzados por los partidarios del expresidente Morales.

SAN DOMINGAN REVOLUTION. [illegible] News has reached here by messenger, to the effect that the Santo Dominican revolutionists, commanded by Mauricio Jiminez, have attacked Guayubin, in Santo Domingo, and killed General [illegible], the commander of the town, and then retired immediately into the interior. Guayubin Captured and General [illegible] Killed. Telegraphic communication between Monte Christi and Santiago de los Caballeros, Santo Domingo, has been cut by the revolutionists, who have taken up arms [illegible] behalf of General [illegible] Jiminez, the former president of Santo Domingo. They have been joined by the partisans of General Morales, the predecessor of General Caceres as president of Santo [illegible]
