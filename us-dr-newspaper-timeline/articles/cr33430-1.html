The Key West Citizen reported on testimony by recalled U.S. Ambassador John Bartlow Martin regarding the recent military coup that deposed Dominican President Juan Bosch. Martin revealed to senators that top Dominican military officers tipped their hand by boycotting an embassy reception for an American admiral, prompting questions from Senator Wayne Morse about whether a U.S. naval show of force could have deterred the takeover.

By HAL BOYLE NEW YORK (AP)—Ever afterward the girl would remember this moment as the turning point in her life. She was tall and slender and 17 years old. She had green-blue eyes and reddish gold hair, and the freckles on her fair skin shone like tiny doubloons scattered by wastrel elves. She walked hesitantly into the room where a number of men were drinking cocktails and smoking. “Say the Lord's Prayer," brusquely commanded producer Gabriel Pascal. "Our Father, who art in heaven,” began the girl. For a moment, frightened, she paused then went on. "Hallowed be thy name.
