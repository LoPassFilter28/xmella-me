A Santo Domingo dispatch says President Baez emphatically denies reports that he intended to leave the country, and that because of reported troubles at Azua the United States steamer Nantasket would probably sail for that port. The commission had a long interview with Baez and his cabinet, and Baez offered guides, interpreters and, if needed, a guard, and agreed to grant safe conduct to the city even to Cabral or any of his partisans whom the commission wished to consult.

Un despacho de Santo Domingo dice que el presidente Báez niega enfáticamente los informes de que pensaba abandonar el país, y que, a causa de los disturbios reportados en Azua, el vapor estadounidense Nantasket probablemente zarparía hacia ese puerto. La comisión tuvo una larga entrevista con Báez y su gabinete, y Báez ofreció guías, intérpretes y, si fuera necesario, una guardia, y aceptó conceder salvoconducto hasta la ciudad incluso a Cabral o a cualquiera de sus partidarios a quienes la comisión deseara consultar.

This he emphatically denies, THE LATEST PROCEEDINGS—REACHING THE OPPOSITION, [illegible] 3.—In consequence of the reported troubles at Azua, it now seems probable that the United States steamer Nantasket will sail for that port to-day, for what purpose does not clearly appear, President Baez's brother, the Governor of Azua, has arrived here, and placed the Government in possession of all the facts, The whole thing is regarded here with suspicion, The Commission last evening had a protracted interview with President Baez and his Cabinet at the President's residence, during which the general purposes of the Commission were discussed. The manner in which information is to be obtained from all parts of the island was particularly debated, and certain arrangements made by which the enemies of Baez and the annexation scheme may be reached, The Commission having asked the President's aid in the matter, the latter said he would cheerfully furnish guides, interpreters, and, if thought necessary, would supply a guard to protect the Commission or their agents through the island, He also consented to grant safe conduct to this city to all persons whom the Commission desired to consult. He even agreed to grant safe conduct to and from this city to Cabral or any of his partisans, The Commission has not yet accepted his offers, but will probably do so.
