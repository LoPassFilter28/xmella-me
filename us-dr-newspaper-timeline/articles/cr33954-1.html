Passage summaryResumen del pasaje

The Daily Kennebec Journal noted that an American financial official in Dominican service ceased receiving U.S. compensation in August 1910 but continued serving Santo Domingo until its new bonds were fully disposed of. Condensed dispatches also reported a fire at Paul Revere's Massachusetts homestead, the purchase of an armed former Italian cruiser by the Haitian government, and an escalating rupture between church and state in Portugal.

The Daily Kennebec Journal señaló que un funcionario financiero estadounidense al servicio dominicano dejó de percibir sueldo de los EE. Breves despachos informaron también sobre un incendio en la histórica hacienda de Paul Revere en Massachusetts, la compra de un antiguo crucero armado italiano por el gobierno de Haití y la creciente ruptura entre la Iglesia y el Estado en Portugal.
