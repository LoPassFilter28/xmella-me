The Daily Kennebec Journal noted that an American financial official in Dominican service ceased receiving U.S. compensation in August 1910 but continued serving Santo Domingo until its new bonds were fully disposed of. Condensed dispatches also reported a fire at Paul Revere's Massachusetts homestead, the purchase of an armed former Italian cruiser by the Haitian government, and an escalating rupture between church and state in Portugal.

CONDENSED DISPATCHES. The homestead of Paul Revere at Sharon. Mass., was badly damaged and an old powder mill built in Revolutionary days was destroyed in a $30,000 fire Wednesday night. In addition two factory buildings occupied by the Plymouth Rubber Co. were destroyed and several houses slightly damaged. The fire started in the main building of the rubber works from spontaneous combustion and naphtha spread the fire about. It transpires that the cruiser [Groos Luc?], formerly the Italian cruiser [Umbria?], whose arrival at Las Palmas, Canary Islands, Tuesday aroused the curiosity of the inhabitants there, has been purchased by the Haitian government. The vessel carries [illegible] guns, is fitted out with modern armament and has a cargo of ammunition. She left Spezia May [illegible] in charge of a German crew. The long-looked for rupture between the church and the government of Portugal is now an accomplished fact, owing to the protest issued Tuesday by the bishops against the separation law.
