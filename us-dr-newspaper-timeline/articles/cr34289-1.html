The Key West Citizen reported that Haiti's application for an Export-Import Bank loan remained pending amid growing criticism from local Communists over past American occupation debts. Concurrently, Haitian President Dumarsais Estime moved toward closer ties with Argentina, with President Juan Domingo Peron reportedly extending credit and diplomatic overtures. The report also referenced the neighboring Dominican Republic under President Rafael Trujillo sharing the island of Hispaniola.

El Key West Citizen reportó que la petición formulada por Haití para un empréstito ante el Export-Import Bank permanecía irresuelta, en medio del creciente clamor opositor comunista derivado de las deudas financieras acarreadas de la pasada ocupación militar estadounidense. A la par, el gobernante haitiano Dumarsais Estimé procuraba estrechar lazos con Argentina, país cuyo mandatario Juan Domingo Perón le había extendido ofertas crediticias y diplomáticas. La nota aludió además a la condición de vecindad que unía a Haití con la República Dominicana de Rafael Trujillo en el suelo compartido de La Española.

An aggressive Communist front which could not muster enough strength to take command after the revolution, is supporting President Dumarsais Estime because he is “a Democrat and a man of the people.” United States delay in offering a loan is but one of the arguments upon which the Communists base denunciation of the Good Neighbor policy as “the most cruel policy" in the world. In 1922, during the American occupation. Haiti borrowed 22 million dollars from private United States sources at 6 per cent interest. Communists as well as men in the government, assert that although Haiti has repaid 38 million dollars in principal and interest, she still owes seven million dollars on that account. Haiti also owes the U. S. more than seven million on later loans totaling ten million. Argentina Moving In President Estime says Argentina and Haiti are to exchange ambassadors shortly. That indicates that President Juan Domingo Peron of Argentina is interested. Other well-informed individuals say that Argentina has offered a loan in furtherance of the commercial policy of that nation. An Argentine cruiser visited Port au Prince, the Haitian capital, recently. Even the sailors who thronged shops, their hands filled with U.S. dollar bills, proved good ambassadors.
