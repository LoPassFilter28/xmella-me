FOREIGN. — Advices from Puerto Plata said that San Domingo had been captured by the insurgents, and that Wos y Gil and his ministers had taken refuge on a German warship; the news of the surrender was confirmed by Minister Powell. The coming election in Colombia has caused serious party dissensions. The Panama "Star and Herald" advises the repudiation of the claims of holders of Colombian bonds. The Duke of Devonshire spoke at a meeting of the Free Food League in London in strong opposition to Mr. Chamberlain's scheme of tariff revision. A lunatic attempted to kill Kenneth Grahame in the Bank of England; the author, who is the bank's secretary, was unhurt. Eighteen lives were lost by the stranding of the Norwegian steamer Victoria.
