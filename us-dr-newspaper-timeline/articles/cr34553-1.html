The Indianapolis Journal reported that Dominican President Ulises Heureaux moved ruthlessly to crush a suspected conspiracy by ordering the summary arrest and execution of prominent political opponents in the capital. In one raid, government troops surrounded the residence of Dr. Theick, fatally shooting his wife and servants, while Theick narrowly escaped through a window and fled abroad to Jamaica.

The Indianapolis Journal informó que el presidente dominicano Ulises Heureaux actuó de manera despiadada para aplastar una supuesta conspiración al ordenar el arresto sumario y la ejecución de prominentes opositores políticos en la capital. En una redada, tropas gubernamentales rodearon la residencia del Dr. Theick, matando a tiros a su esposa y a sus sirvientes, mientras que Theick escapó milagrosamente por una ventana y huyó al extranjero rumbo a Jamaica.

A few days ago President Heureaux discovered what he announced was a conspiracy against him, with which many leading men of the Dominican capital were alleged to be connected. President Heureaux ordered that all the leaders of the conspiracy be arrested and shot. Soldiers were sent to surround the residences of all the suspected persons. Dr. Theick's house was one of the first visited. When the soldiers appeared he knew their visit meant death to himself, and he hastened preparations to escape. The soldiers opened fire and riddled Mrs. Theick and the servants with bullets. The Doctor leaped from a window, mounted a horse and fled. He made his way to Cape Hayti, and from there sailed to Kingston, reaching here in safety. His own departure was so hurried that he did not learn the fate of the other men whom Heureaux had sentenced to death. Generals Arnaux and Momplasier, exiles from Hayti, have been arrested here by order of the government of Jamaica. They are accused of violating international law by fitting out and arming a vessel in August, 1893, to be employed in ousting Hippolyte from the presidency of the black republic.
