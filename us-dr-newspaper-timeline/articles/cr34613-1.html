The Montgomery Advertiser prints an item: Secretary Bryan today declined to comment on a published report that information had been laid before the State Department regarding the conduct of James M. Sullivan of New York, the new minister to the Dominican republic. Charles B. Curtis, former secretary of the legation there, was in the capital recently and was said to have talked with officials about certain negotiations for concessions in Santo Domingo. Assistant Secretary Osborne of the State Department, who has charge of the personnel, said today that no charges had been laid before the department and was inclined to discredit statements regarding Minister Sullivan's affairs. Secretary Bryan, however, saying he had not seen all the published reports, refused flatly to discuss the subject. Various reports, however, are being discussed in congressional and diplomatic circles.The Montgomery Advertiser publica una nota: hoy se negó Bryan, el secretario, a comentar un informe publicado de que se había presentado información ante el Departamento de Estado sobre la conducta de James M. Sullivan, de Nueva York, el nuevo ministro en la república dominicana. Charles B. Curtis, ex secretario de la legación allí, estuvo recientemente en la capital y se dice que habló con funcionarios sobre ciertas negociaciones para concesiones en Santo Domingo. El subsecretario Osborne, del Departamento de Estado, que tiene a su cargo el personal, dijo hoy que no se habían presentado cargos ante el departamento y se inclinaba a desacreditar las declaraciones sobre los asuntos del ministro Sullivan. El secretario Bryan, sin embargo, diciendo que no había visto todos los informes publicados, se negó rotundamente a discutir el tema. Varios informes, sin embargo, están siendo discutidos en círculos congresionales y diplomáticos.

The Montgomery Advertiser. Montgomery, Ala., December 10, 1913.
