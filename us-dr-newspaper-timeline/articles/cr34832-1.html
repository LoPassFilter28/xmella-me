The Evening journal reported on testimony by Horace G. Knowles before the McCormick Senate committee condemning the $2,500,000 loan arranged by the military administration without Dominican approval at high interest rates. Knowles accused Military Governor Admiral Samuel S. Robison of insulting him, barring communications with Senator McCormick, and threatening him with force. He added that the loan was handled by New York banks with federal guarantees while financial director Lieutenant Commander Arthur H. Mayo operated with unexamined accounts.

Mr. Knowles said he would prefer charges against Admiral Robinson as soon as he reached Washington. Mr. Knowles said the financial affairs of the republic were in the hands of Lieutenant Commander Arthur H. Mayo, of the Navy Supply Corps. He asserted that an order had been recently issued that the personal accounts of Commander Mayo should be passed without question.
