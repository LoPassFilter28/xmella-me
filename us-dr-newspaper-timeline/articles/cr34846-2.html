A Santo Domingo City dispatch to The Sun reports that Archbishop Nouel resigned as provisional president of the Dominican Republic after having been in office a little more than thirteen days, though he subsequently suspended the action. The move caused little surprise, as recent advices indicated that the archbishop had been exasperated by the struggle for office waged by Horatio Vasquez and other leaders of the recent revolution. Nouel had accepted the compromise presidency expecting that constitutional revision and general elections would follow, an arrangement demanded by revolutionists and negotiated by special United States commissioners Brigadier General Frank McIntyre and W. T. S. La decisión causó escasa sorpresa, ya que despachos recientes indicaban que el prelado se hallaba exasperado por la disputa por cargos públicos librada por Horacio Vásquez y otros líderes de la reciente revolución. Nouel había asumido la presidencia de compromiso con la expectativa de que se revisaría la constitución y se celebrarían elecciones generales, acuerdo exigido por los revolucionarios y concertado por los comisionados especiales de los Estados Unidos, el general de brigada Frank McIntyre y W. T. S. Doyle, del Departamento de Estado.

The Sun. New York, December 17, 1912.
