The commissioners made an excursion to the plantation of Mrs. Dichmain, about two miles from Samana, where they drank fresh coconut milk and saw a primitive sugar mill turned by two bulls, with the juice boiled nearby into coarse brown sugar. Mrs. Dichmain, who has lived 42 years in Santo Domingo and has 15 children and 50 grandchildren, farms 100 acres producing bananas, pineapples, sugar, coffee and chocolate, and she told the visitors she was most decidedly in favor of annexation, longing intensely for quiet and order.

Los comisionados hicieron una excursión a la plantación de la señora Dichmain, a unas dos millas de Samaná, donde bebieron leche fresca de coco y vieron un primitivo molino de azúcar movido por dos bueyes, con el jugo hervido cerca de allí hasta convertirlo en azúcar moreno grueso. La señora Dichmain, que lleva 42 años viviendo en Santo Domingo y tiene 15 hijos y 50 nietos, cultiva 100 acres que producen plátanos, piñas, azúcar, café y chocolate, y dijo a los visitantes que estaba decididamente a favor de la anexión, pues anhelaba intensamente la tranquilidad y el orden.

Among the last excursions the Commissioners made was to the plantation of Mrs, Dichmain, about two miles from Samana. It was a bright, lovely day, with a mild sea-breeze tempering the hot sun, warm, but infinitely more agreeable and exhilarating than the close, sultry, Summer days at Long Branch, or other sea-side resorts, The curved shore of the little bay where we landed was fringed with cocoa-palms, laden with the tempting-looking ripe fruit. It was but the work of a moment for a native to climb a tree and throw down a number of smooth, shiny, green nuts, The [peon?] took his machete—a long knife worn at the side in a wooden scabbard, and used to peel an orange or clear a jungle—and cut off the end of one of the nuts which was quite soft and white, and filled with a deliciously cool sweetish liquid which afterward hardens, and thickens into milk. As we saw it, it was almost colorless and very refreshing, The [illegible] were [illegible] with a delicate pulp which could be [illegible] with a spoon. A doleful squeaking noise attracted our attention, and we soon found that it proceeded from a primitive sugar-mill, consisting of three wooden rollers in a frame surmounted by a palm thatch.
