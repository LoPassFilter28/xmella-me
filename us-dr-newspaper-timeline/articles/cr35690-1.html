Reporting from Santo Domingo, The Tabor Independent announcesAl informar desde Santo Domingo, The Tabor Independent anuncia

Reporting from Santo Domingo, The Tabor Independent announces the arrival of the United States auxiliary steamer Panther carrying marines. The new troops were sent to reinforce marine detachments previously landed in response to disturbances following the recent revolutionary outbreak.Informando desde Santo Domingo, The Tabor Independent anuncia la llegada del buque auxiliar de los Estados Unidos Panther con infantes de marina a bordo. Las nuevas tropas fueron enviadas para reforzar los destacamentos de infantes de marina desembarcados previamente en respuesta a los disturbios tras el reciente estallido revolucionario.
