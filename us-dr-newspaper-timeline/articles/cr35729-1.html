A UPI report from Santo Domingo indicates that armed leftist groups were prepared to stage an uprising against the Dominican Republic's civilian junta as soon as their leaders issued orders. Speaking at a clandestine meeting on the outskirts of Santo Domingo, an insurgent spokesman stated that authorities had failed to arrest a single member of their group and confirmed they possessed weapons. The representative explained that fighters held back on the first day of the coup that ousted former president Juan Bosch (printed 'Besch') to avoid needless casualties, adding that Communists had urged an immediate strike against the generals who deposed the president and established the three-man regime.Un despacho de UPI desde Santo Domingo indica que grupos armados de izquierda estaban preparados para sublevarse contra la junta civil de la República Dominicana tan pronto como sus líderes dieran la orden. En una reunión clandestina en las afueras de Santo Domingo, un portavoz insurgente afirmó que las autoridades no habían logrado detener a ningún miembro de su organización y confirmó que contaban con armamento. El representante explicó que los combatientes no entraron en acción el primer día del golpe que derrocó al expresidente Juan Bosch (impreso 'Besch') para evitar bajas innecesarias, agregando que los comunistas habían instado a asestar un golpe inmediato contra los generales que depusieron al mandatario e instalaron el régimen de tres hombres.

The Chronicle. Pascagoula, Miss., October 4, 1963. Page 14.
