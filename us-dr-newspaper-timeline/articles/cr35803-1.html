The Abbeville Press and Banner reported that Captain Stubbs of the American schooner Henry Crosby anchored near Old Azua after losing his bearings and dispatched a longboat to determine their location. Dominican soldiers on the beach opened fire without warning, striking a sailor in the back of the head and wounding the chief officer. Captain Stubbs proceeded to Azua and lodged a formal complaint with the American consul for transmission to Washington.

The Abbeville Press and Banner informó que el capitán Stubbs, de la goleta estadounidense Henry Crosby, fondeó cerca de Azua Vieja tras perder el rumbo y envió un bote para determinar su ubicación. Soldados dominicanos apostados en la playa abrieron fuego sin previo aviso, alcanzando a un marinero en la parte posterior de la cabeza e hiriendo al primer oficial. El capitán Stubbs se dirigió a Azua y presentó una queja formal ante el cónsul estadounidense para su transmisión a Washington.

December 11, but the populace of San Domingo City, which is eighty-four miles from Azua, were still talking of the affair when the Clyde came away, two days later. Old Azua harbor is ten miles away from Azua, and in approaching the first named Captain Stubbs, of the Crosby, lost his bearings. His schooner dropped anchor and the captain ordered four of his sailors in the long boat in charge of the chief mate to ascertain the exact position of the craft. As the boat drew near the beach a dozen soldiers in Dominican uniform were seen running toward the surf. Without warning of any kind the men leveled their carbines at the occupants of the long boat and fired. A bullet from the first volley hit one of the rowers in the back of the head, and a second shot from another round laid low the chief officer. The uninjured men then hurried back to the Crosby with their mates. All were thoroughly frightened, but Captain Stubbs was beside himself in anger. He was for going ashore and dealing vengeance then and there. But he weighed anchor instead and went to Azua, where he sent a complaint to the American Consul at St. Domingo City.
