The Sun reported that American commissioners investigating Santo Domingo found widespread corruption and treaty violations under the unpopular Victoria administration. The commissioners reported that public funds had been misappropriated, illicit debts incurred, and government employees left unpaid while border custom houses were forced shut. The United States demanded an immediate administrative cleanup and the reopening of frontier customs posts, holding marines in readiness to provide protection.

The Sun informó que los comisionados estadounidenses que investigaban en Santo Domingo constataron una corrupción generalizada y violaciones de los tratados bajo la impopular administración de Victoria. Los comisionados denunciaron que se habían malversado caudales públicos, contraído deudas ilícitas y dejado sin salario a los empleados públicos, al tiempo que se clausuraban a la fuerza las aduanas de la frontera. Los Estados Unidos exigieron un saneamiento administrativo inmediato y la reapertura de las receptorías fronterizas, manteniendo a los infantes de marina en alerta para brindar protección.

Not only Is there a revolution which has forced the closing of the customhouses on the Dominican-Haytian frontier, but Haytian aggressiveness and an ancient boundary dispute have led to filibustering and complications along the border. The Victoria Government of the Dominican republic is unpopular and the commissioners have found that the Dominican Administration has been wilfully violating its obligations under the treaty of 1907 between the Dominican republic and the United States. Public funds have been diverted from their proper use and, contrary to her treaty obligations, the Government has secretly assumed financial obligations other than those covered in the treaty. Dishonesty and graft are said to permeate the Government. It is understood that the soldiers and civilian employees have not been paid, the money having been misappropriated. The United States commissioners are understood to be Insisting that the Government clean house promptly and thoroughly. They are demanding that the custom houses be reestablished along the frontier. The American marines are being held In readiness to assist in this task should guards be necessary. The commissioners are also urging the Dominican Government to adopt other measures which will result In the restoration of the Integrity and stability of the administration as well as to observe strictly all their obligations under the treaty.
