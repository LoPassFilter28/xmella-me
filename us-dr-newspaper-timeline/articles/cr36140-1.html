A dispatch from Port-au-Prince reports that the revolutionary forces of Santo Domingo occupied Puerto Plata and Santiago, that a provisional government had been established at Santiago, and that the arrival of Jimenez was expected momentarily.

Un despacho desde Puerto Príncipe informa que las fuerzas revolucionarias de Santo Domingo ocuparon Puerto Plata y Santiago, que se había establecido un gobierno provisional en Santiago y que la llegada de Jiménez se esperaba de un momento a otro.

Board of Trustees.

To the Victors Belong the Spoils Is to Be

the New Policy in Relation to the

Library--Even the Janitor and

Engineer Removed.

last evening, and contrary to the usual

order of things, it was a rather lively

and Interesting session. An action was

taken which makes it pretty certain

that Librarian Davies will not much

longer be at the head of the institution.

resolution adopted at the time the new
