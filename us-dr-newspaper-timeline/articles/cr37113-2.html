Passage summaryResumen del pasaje

The newspaper reported that negotiations for a treaty of commercial reciprocity with the Dominican Republic were successfully concluded and would soon be submitted to the Senate. Additionally, the excerpt noted ongoing diplomatic matters involving the Spanish-American Claims Commission, the Ottoman Empire, and Venezuela.

El diario dio cuenta de la satisfactoria culminación de las negociaciones de un tratado de reciprocidad comercial con la República Dominicana, que en breve pasaría a examen del Senado. Consignó además gestiones diplomáticas en curso relativas a la Comisión de Reclamaciones Hispano-Estadounidense, el Imperio Otomano y Venezuela.

THE CARACAS AWARDS.

The final disposition of pending questions

with Venezuela has not yet been reached, but I

have good reason to expect an early settlement

which will provide for the means of re-examin
