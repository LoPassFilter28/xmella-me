HAVANA, April 27, 1868.

The Spanish steamer Francisco de Assis arrived here yesterday from St. Domingo. She brings news that a commission from the Baezist party has left for the United States to negotiate for the sale of Samana. The government of Baez has published a decree relative to the nature and value of the gold and silver mines and of the debts secured by guaranties of such mines. The general banker, Jesurun, and Colonel Fabens have been charged with the negotiation of a loan to be secured by the revenues from customs of the Dominican republic.

The revolutionary party is in the field. Cabral is at the head of 1,500 men. Luperon is at Puerto Plata with 500 men. The Baezists, on the other hand, claim that the revolution is at an end.

HAYTI.

The news from Port-au-Prince is to the 18th inst. Salnave was still in the capital. The revolutionary party was gaining ground. The Cacos had taken the town of Trou. The town of Gonaives was also in the hands of the revolutionists. The revolutionary party had also captured the steamer 22d of December.
