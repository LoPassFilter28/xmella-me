Hostilities in Santo Domingo, the outgrowth of an attempt to overthrow President Jimenez in which several persons were killed, were suspended as the diplomatic corps endeavored to settle the trouble, and the republic outside the capital was quiet. In a separate item, Haitian senators who persisted in meeting despite Admiral Caperton's warning were dispersed by a detachment of native troops.

Las hostilidades en Santo Domingo, resultado de un intento de derrocar al presidente Jiménez en el que murieron varias personas, fueron suspendidas mientras el cuerpo diplomático procuraba resolver el conflicto, y la república fuera de la capital estaba tranquila. En una noticia separada, los senadores haitianos que persistieron en reunirse pese a la advertencia del almirante Caperton fueron dispersados por un destacamento de tropas nativas.
