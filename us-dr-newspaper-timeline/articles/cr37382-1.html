The Yorkville Enquirer reported that Dominican authorities arrested former Admiral Catrain as he attempted to sneak aboard a German steamer in Santo Domingo to join an insurrection led by deposed President Morales. Catrain, who had previously commandeered the gunboat Independencia during Morales's 1906 ouster before receiving amnesty, was suspected of returning to command a revolutionary vessel. Dominican officials discounted Morales's assertions to his followers that the United States supported his return to power.

Catrain, quien anteriormente había tomado el mando del cañonero Independencia durante la caída de Morales en 1906 antes de acogerse a una amnistía, era sospechoso de regresar para capitanear un barco revolucionario. It is stated that Morales assured his followers that the United States government favors his return to power and will display no undue activity in balking his plans, but this is believed to have been said with a view of raising the courage of such of his supporters as were timorous of success. A fine steam yacht is said to form part of the revolutionary equipment and that a former San Domingan admiral now in Santo Domingo is to assume command. That the Santo Domingan administration gives credence to this statement is obvious from the recent arrest of ex-Admiral Catrain, on attempting to secretly leave Santo Domingo. Catrain, who is a prominent man in Santo Domingan politics and a famous leader, was admiral of the fleet during Morales's presidential term. When the latter was deposed in 1906 Catrain seized the gunboat Independencia and joining Morales's supporters at Monte Cristi attacked Puerto Plata, Samana and Sanchez, but was forced after a brief struggle to surrender. He accompanied Morales into exile, where he remained until an amnesty permitted him to return to Santo Domingo. On January 16 he went on board the German steamer lying in the port of Santo Domingo city and is said to have secreted himself with a view of escaping from the country.
