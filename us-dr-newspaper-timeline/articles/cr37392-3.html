The Indianapolis Journal reported that the United States State Department requested an urgent report from Consul General Maxwell regarding the Dominican government's reported termination of the San Domingo Improvement Company's contract. Meanwhile, dispatches from Santo Domingo indicated that the Dominican Congress had voted compensation for the American firm while resuming direct customs administration. Customs receipts were placed under the supervision of a joint commission representing Dutch, Belgian, French, American, and Dominican officials.

El Indianapolis Journal informó que el Departamento de Estado de los Estados Unidos solicitó un informe urgente al cónsul general Maxwell respecto a la presunta rescisión del contrato de la San Domingo Improvement Company por parte del gobierno dominicano. Mientras tanto, despachos procedentes de Santo Domingo indicaban que el Congreso dominicano había votado una indemnización para la firma estadounidense al tiempo que reasumía la administración aduanera directa. La recaudación aduanera quedó bajo la supervisión de una comisión conjunta en representación de funcionarios holandeses, belgas, franceses, estadounidenses y dominicanos.

Holls, Abbott Ward, of New York, representing the Santo Domingo Improving Company, have held lengthy conferences with Acting Secretary of State Hill and Solicitor Penfield, of the State Department, concerning the reported repudiation of the company's grant by Santo Domingo. It is understood that a line of action is being formulated and that a cable message of Inquiry has been sent to our representative in Santo Domingo. The government dispatch of Inquiry was sent to Consul General Maxwell at Santo Domingo City and asked him to advise the department if the press reports of a repudiation of the company's rights were correct, and also to forward a general statement of the situation. Although it is not so stated officially, it is probable an intimation has been conveyed to Santo Domingo not to take precipitate action until the matter can receive due consideration here. Santo Domingo Will Pay. SAN DOMINGO, Republic of Santo Domingo, Jan. 12. Congress has voted a credit for the government to compensate the San Domingo Improvement Company, of New York. Control of the customs will continue with the government, which will pay salaries monthly. A commission composed of the Dutch and Belgium consuls, the American and French vice consuls and one Dominican, will receive the customs duties. General satisfaction is felt throughout the country.
