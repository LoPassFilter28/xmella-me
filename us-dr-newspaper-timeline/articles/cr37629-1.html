1844-08-28 · Washington City [D.C.] · 1844-1861 Independence, recognition, Samana schemes

FROM ST. DOMINGO.

SourceFuente: Daily national intelligencer, Washington City [D.C.], 1844-08-28

An arrival from Hayti at Philadelphia brings intelligence to the 9th instant. In political affairs all was quiet. The Constituent Assembly had been dissolved. The project of dividing the Island into States was abandoned, and the Government is now in all respects as it was prior to the late revolution, with the exception that there are no municipalities, Port Republican (or Port au Prince) being the capital of the North, South, and West. At Port au Platt they still sustain the Dominican flag, and the authorities there have arranged the basis of a treaty with the French, by which the latter are to have a patent for the mines at Samana for a term of years, but it is thought it will not go into effect, as the majority of the population are opposed to it; meaning the partisans of Baez, who went to France to obtain a foothold on the Island. President Guerrier and suite were on a tour through the Island, and were daily expected at Cape Haytien.

FROM MOROCCO.

An arrival at New York from Gibraltar reports

that on the 20th July two Spanish and five French

ships of war sailed from Algesiras forTangiers, for

the purpose of enforcing the demands of the two

nations, which were to be agreed to by the 21st

upon the alternative of war. The vessel sailed on
