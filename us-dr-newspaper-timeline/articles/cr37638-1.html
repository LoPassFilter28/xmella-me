New York, November 16.—More trouble seems to be in store for the steamer Cherokee which arrived here last week from Dominican ports after several thrilling experiences. The agents of the Clyde line steamers In this city declared today that the Cherokee will sail next Wednesday on her regular schedule and will pursue her regular itinerary, stopping at Samana Bay, Monte Christi, Sanchez, Puerto Plata and Macoris. Manuel De J. Galvan, consul general from San Domingo to New York, said today that the Cherokee will certainly be sunk if she attempts to enter the five harbors named. He says that the entire San Domingoan navy the gunboats El Presidente and Independence are on guard to maintain the blockade of those ports, now in the hands of the rebels under Jiminez. “If the Cherokee attempts to enter any of those ports the gunboats will not parley with her this time,” said Mr. Galvan.
