A letter from Don Apolinar de Castro quotes the latest advices from the capital, Santo Domingo, dated the 14th, confirming the arrival of Cabral on the northern frontier, where he was received with enthusiasm. The advices report the firm position of Generals Adon and the Ogandos, who will soon march on Azua, a deepening division in Baez's party since Hungria's withdrawal from the ministry, and an irreparable lack of means to carry on the government.

Una carta de don Apolinar de Castro cita las últimas noticias de la capital, Santo Domingo, fechadas el día 14, que confirman la llegada de Cabral a la frontera del norte, donde fue recibido con entusiasmo. Las noticias informan de la firme posición de los generales Adón y los Ogando, que pronto marcharán sobre Azua, de una división cada vez mayor en el partido de Báez desde la retirada de Hungría del ministerio, y de una falta irreparable de medios para sostener el gobierno.

Much gratification has been caused by the knowledge that so many capitalists and respectable gentlemen are associated in the Company, and we now hope that this immense enterprise will shortly be commenced.
