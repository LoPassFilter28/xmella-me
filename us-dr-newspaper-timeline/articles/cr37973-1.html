Among deaths of noted people, the paper reports that General Carlos F. Morales, former president of the Dominican Republic, has died in Paris. He had lived there for some time as Dominican minister to France.

Entre las muertes de personas notables, el periódico informa que el general Carlos F. Morales, antiguo presidente de la República Dominicana, ha muerto en París. Había vivido allí durante algún tiempo como ministro dominicano en Francia.

Caleb Van Husen Whitbeck, editor of the Hackensack (N. J.) Evening Record, died of pneumonia at New York. He was thirty-five years old and was born in Rochester, N. Y. The death of General Carlos F. Morales, former president of the Dominican republic, occurred in Paris. He had lived there for some time as Dominican minister to France. Frederick Borman, eighty-one, died in Bayonne, N. J., where he was formerly postmaster during the administration of Benjamin Harrison. He was a veteran of the civil war. Miss Cornelia S. Baird, well known as an educator and for thirty-two years head of a seminary for young women at Norwalk, Conn., is dead. The Rev. Gaudentius Hayden, retreat master of the Blessed Gabriel monastery, at Brighton, N. J., died in Boston. Judge James S. Young of the United States court for the western district of Pennsylvania died at his home in Pittsburgh. Thomas Wilbur Cridler, former third assistant secretary of state of the United States, died suddenly at New York. Otto I. Leisy, fifty, millionaire brewer, nationally known for his benefactions, died in Cleveland. John [illegible], city solicitor of Pittsburgh and a widely known lawyer, is dead. NEWS OF OUR NEIGHBORS. Some Things That Are Happening in Other American Countries. At the cost of eleven lives the revolutionists in the Guayaquil (Ecuador) penitentiary were prevented by government troops from escaping.
