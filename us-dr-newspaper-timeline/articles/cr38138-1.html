Based on the provided newspaper excerpt from the Paris correspondent of the *Times*, the Dominican Minister requested a statement that his Government does not intend to cede Port Samana to the United States. Additionally, the Dominican Government stated it does not intend to recognize the debt to St. Domingo.

A tenor del extracto facilitado del corresponsal de *The Times* en París, el representante dominicano solicitó hacer público que su Gobierno no abriga el designio de enajenar el puerto de Samaná a los Estados Unidos. Subrayó asimismo que la administración dominicana rehúsa reconocer la deuda exigida a Santo Domingo.
