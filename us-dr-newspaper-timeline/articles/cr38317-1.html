GUELLITO SURRENDERS. Monte Christi Now in Possession of Dominican Revolutionists. CAPE HAYTIEN, Haiti, September 6. General Guellito, the Heureaux commander of Monte Christi, has surrendered, and Generals Pacheco and Roca are now occupying the city and its environs. General Caceres' command, which started to march against Monte Christi, has been ordered to return to Santiago de los Caballeros. All the country is rejoicing at the arrival of Juan Isidro Jiminez
