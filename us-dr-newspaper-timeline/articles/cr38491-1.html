From: The Customs ReceivershipDe: La receptoría de aduanas · 1905–1916

The Tulsa Daily World prints an item: Mitchell took the standThe Tulsa Daily World publica una nota: Mitchell subió al estrado

The Tulsa Daily World prints an item: Mitchell took the stand today at the investigation of the fitness of James M. Sullivan to retain his post as American minister to the Dominican republic. He was asked if he intended to accuse W. W. Russell, Sullivan's predecessor, with dishonesty, when he said in his testimony Friday that had there been an honest American minister at Santo Domingo before Sullivan took the post the Banco Nacional would have received without asking the deposit of customs receipts. Mitchell said he did not mean to imply 'absolute dishonesty,' but that Russell had been unfair to the institution. He said he had based his statement on 'the fact I had been told that Minister Russell had advocated that the Dominican government's loan be given to the National City Bank of New York because it would better suit the Washington government.'The Tulsa Daily World publica una nota: Mitchell subió al estrado hoy en la investigación sobre la aptitud de James M. Sullivan para retener su puesto como ministro americano en la República Dominicana. Se le preguntó si intentaba acusar de deshonestidad a W. W. Russell, predecesor de Sullivan, cuando dijo en su testimonio del viernes que si hubiera habido un ministro americano honesto en Santo Domingo antes de que Sullivan tomara el puesto, el Banco Nacional habría recibido sin pedirlo el depósito de las rentas aduaneras. Mitchell dijo que no quiso implicar una «deshonestidad absoluta», sino que Russell había sido injusto con la institución. Dijo que había basado su declaración en «el hecho de que se me había dicho que el ministro Russell había abogado porque el préstamo del gobierno dominicano fuera dado al National City Bank de Nueva York porque convendría mejor al gobierno de Washington».

Tulsa Daily World. Tulsa, Indian Territory [Okla.], January 26, 1915.
