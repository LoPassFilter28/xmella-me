The paper sets out the Washington program for former President Rafael L. Trujillo, now commander in chief of the Dominican army, who arrives Thursday: luncheon at the Senate on Friday, visits that afternoon to the Navy Yard and the Marine Barracks, and dinner that night as honor guest of Marine Corps Commandant Thomas Holcomb. On Saturday he will visit Fort Meade, lunch at the Army Navy Club and attend a reception at the Pan-American Union, on Sunday the Haitian Minister, Elie Lescot, will give a luncheon in his honor, and on Monday he will review troops at Quantico and attend a dinner by Chief of Staff George C. Marshall before leaving the capital Tuesday.

El periódico presenta el programa en Washington para el expresidente Rafael L. Trujillo, ahora comandante en jefe del ejército dominicano, que llega el jueves: almuerzo en el Senado el viernes, visitas esa tarde al Navy Yard y al Cuartel de la Infantería de Marina, y cena esa noche como invitado de honor del comandante del Cuerpo de Marines, Thomas Holcomb. El sábado visitará Fort Meade, almorzará en el Army Navy Club y asistirá a una recepción en la Unión Panamericana, el domingo el ministro haitiano, Elie Lescot, ofrecerá un almuerzo en su honor, y el lunes pasará revista a las tropas en Quantico y asistirá a una cena del jefe de Estado Mayor, George C. Marshall, antes de salir de la capital el martes.

Society Again Gets Breath

With Departure of Royalty

And Summer Season Here

Arrival of Gen. Rafael Trujillo,

However, Will Revive Interests;

Harriet Dawley Makes Debut

By MARGARET HART,

Society Editor.

Washington society is settling down to normalcy again after weeks

marked with excitement and brilliant festivities for royal visitors. And,

may it be said that, the National's Capital liked royalty and for many

years to come stories will be retold of the pleasant informality of these

persons of royal birth. To those who had never met royalty before, the

natural and wholesome manner in the royal visitors’ conversations and

the sincere interest displayed in all with whom they came in contact,

was a revelation.

The visits of their Britannic Majesties, King George VI and Queen

Elizabeth; the Crown Prince Frederik and Crown Princess Ingrid of

Denmark and Iceland and the Crown Prince Olav and Crown Princess

Martha of Norway all added indelibly to the pages of history of society

In the Capital of our country, as well as furthering the bonds between

this Nation and the kingdoms across the sea.
