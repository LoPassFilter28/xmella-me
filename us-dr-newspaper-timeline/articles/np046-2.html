The same Kingston dispatch reports a planned descent on Monte Cristi or Puerto Plata to overthrow President Heureaux: the sloop Olivia is cruising the northern coast of Haiti picking up Dominican revolutionaries and has taken on munitions of war, while revolutionary emissaries are at work to enlist prominent men in the movement.

El mismo despacho de Kingston informa de un descenso planeado sobre Monte Cristi o Puerto Plata para derrocar al presidente Heureaux: la balandra Olivia recorre la costa norte de Haití recogiendo revolucionarios dominicanos y ha cargado municiones de guerra, mientras emisarios revolucionarios trabajan para sumar hombres prominentes al movimiento.
