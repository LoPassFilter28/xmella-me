Grand Forks Daily Herald and the Evening Times

Grand Forks Daily Herald and the Evening Times prints: William E. Pulliam

July 11, 191411 de julio de 1914

Grand Forks Daily Herald and the Evening Times prints: William E. Pulliam, at that time general receiver of Dominican customs, a post which he occupied for six years, having been appointed by President Roosevelt, began one year ago the campaign for a splendid Pan-American tomb and memorial for Christopher Columbus in the city of Santo Domingo, Dominican republic, where his remains are declared now to rest. It is proposed that $500,000 be raised and, if more detailed study makes this seem feasible, to limit each subscription to 50 cents, so that the memorial may represent a great number of the sons and daughters of that New World found by the Genoese. During his residence in Santo Domingo Mr. Pulliam became greatly interested in the controversy which has been waged at various times over the resting place of the remains of Columbus. Both Santo Domingo and Spain claim to have the remains.

Grand Forks Daily Herald and the Evening Times publica: William E. Pulliam, en ese tiempo receptor general de las aduanas dominicanas, un puesto que ocupó por seis años, habiendo sido nombrado por el presidente Roosevelt, comenzó hace un año la campaña por una espléndida tumba panamericana y monumento a Cristóbal Colón en la ciudad de Santo Domingo, república dominicana, donde se declara que descansan ahora sus restos. Se propone recaudar $500,000 y, si un estudio más detallado hace esto parecer factible, limitar cada suscripción a 50 centavos, para que el monumento represente a un gran número de los hijos e hijas de ese Nuevo Mundo encontrado por el genovés. Durante su residencia en Santo Domingo el Sr. Pulliam se interesó enormemente en la controversia que se ha librado en varias ocasiones sobre el lugar de descanso de los restos de Colón.
