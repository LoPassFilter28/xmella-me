November 6, 19376 de noviembre de 1937

The U.S. Administration has ordered Norweb to leave the international radio conference in Havana, Cuba, and return immediately to his post, a move that lent credence to reports that soldiers of President General Trujillo had slaughtered 1000 Haitians near the Dominican border early in October. November 6, 19376 de noviembre de 1937

An order to return at once to the Dominican Republic on Sunday's first available plane lent credence to reports that soldiers of President General Trujillo had slaughtered 1,000 Haitians near the border early in October. In a July 9 Associated Press dispatch from the thirty-two-nation

In a July 9 Associated Press dispatch from the thirty-two-nation refugee conference at Evian-les-Bains, France, the Dominican Republic, Mexico, and Peru offered to open their doors to German and Austrian refugees after other countries stated they could not permit mass immigration. Following Mexico's pledge of asylum and work opportunities, the Dominican Republic and Peru followed suit, although Peru excluded refugee intellectuals. Tras la promesa mexicana de asilo y oportunidades de trabajo, la República Dominicana y el Perú siguieron el ejemplo, aunque este último excluyó a los intelectuales refugiados. El delegado dominicano Virgilio Trujillo Molina anunció que la República Dominicana aceptaría tanto a agricultores como a profesionales, afirmando que había grandes extensiones aptas para el cultivo y que la república proporcionaría semillas y asesoramiento técnico.
