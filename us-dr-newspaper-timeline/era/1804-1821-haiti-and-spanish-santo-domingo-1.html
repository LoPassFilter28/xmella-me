1804-1821 Haiti and Spanish Santo Domingo

1820-11-29 · Washington City [D.C.] · 1804-1821 Haiti and Spanish Santo Domingo

Daily national intelligencer

The newspaper reported that President Boyer had arrived at "the Gape." Additionally, it recorded the death of Duncan McIntosh at Aux Cayes on the 26th of the previous month, praising him for risking his life during the revolutionary troubles of 1793 in St. Domingo to save numerous inhabitants from the blacks.

El periódico informó que el presidente Boyer había llegado a «the Gape». Además, registró el fallecimiento de Duncan McIntosh en Aux Cayes el día 26 del mes precedente, elogiándolo por arriesgar su vida durante los disturbios revolucionarios de 1793 en Santo Domingo para salvar a numerosos habitantes de los negros.
