New-York tribune

SummaryResumen

According to the *New-York tribune* on February 1, 1883, the Government of Santo Domingo ordered that vessels sailing from countries with Dominican consuls must carry bills of health signed by those consuls in order to enter the ports of the Republic. Esta providencia entraría en vigor para los Estados Unidos treinta días después del 15 de diciembre.
