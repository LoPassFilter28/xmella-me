commission of five or seven mem

bers to go to Haiti and study the

relations between the little black

republic and the United States, the

sarcastic comments on his “mania”

matter, if it must be considered as

a cause for criticism. The Presi

commissions is to gather authentic

and unbiased information. It makes

precious little difference whether the

commission has one member or

seven members if its work is com

petent and satisfactory.

The point would not be raised in

Critical spirit if the President had

number of fact-finding commissions.

commission on the conservation of
