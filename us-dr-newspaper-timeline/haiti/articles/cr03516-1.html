PERSONAL AND GENERAL.

A congress of Austrian Socialists met

in Hainfeld on the 31st. The object of the

conference was to unite all labor parties

for common action.

Burglars entered a house at Bourne

mouth, England, on the 1st, at which

Count Karoly, the Austrian Ambassador,

and his wife were sojourning, and stole

jewelry valued at 3,000, belonging to the

Countess.

The health of the Archudke Louis

Victor, brother of Emperor Francis

Joseph of Austria, is improving.

Mrs. Mary Croxin, aged twenty-three,

was burned to death in her room at Ho.

242 West Tenth street, New York, on the

1st, her clothing having been set on fire
