Ambition and Success.

Questioned regarding his rules of

life and what led to his success, his

feeling towards his fellow men and

his life's ambitions. Representative

Madden said:

"I have always believed that my

success has been due to my determination

to know the thing I was doing

as well or better than anybody else,

not to think of hours, but the results

and the systematic disposal of everything

that was placed in my charge;

never to put off until tomorrow what

could be done today; to an economy

in my personal affairs that always

left a balance in my account, no matter

how small the Income; to a firm

determination to follow a course
