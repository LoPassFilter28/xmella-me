SCENES

AT ST. PAUL, MINNESOTA.

St. Paul, August 14, 1851.

Last Thursday was a lively day in Saint Paul; Indians all

over town with double eagles and Third street, especially,

was converted into an extempore horse bazaar. Probably

fifty horses were sold and swapped during the day.

Our red

neighbors will relieve the town of much crow-bait. Dogs are

also in demand, which will, to a certain extent, diminish the

demand for sage here in town.

On Friday every Indian who had been able to buy a horse

was anxions to try his speed.

Various contests of fleetness

were witnessed between old wheezing cart-horses, running

quarter races at the north end of Jackson street, in the sand

ankle deep. As this is a new wrinkle on the brow of civili

zation here, a very largo multitude turned out to see the

races.—Pioneer.
