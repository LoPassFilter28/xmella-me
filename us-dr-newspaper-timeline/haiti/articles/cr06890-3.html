THE WASHINGTON TIMES. WEDNESDAY. APRIL 18. 1934

The Secretary of the Interior,

Mr. Harold L. Ickes, will be the

guest of honor at an Informal

party to be given Saturday,

April 28, by the Press Sections

of the Department and the Ad

ministrations and Newspaper

men assigned to cover his ac

tivities.

The party will be held in the

Presidential suite and Cabinet

room of the Willard Hotel.

Mrs. Ickes will be at home

this afternoon from 4 until 6

o'clock, for the last time this

season. She will be assisted by

Mrs. Marshall Finnan and Mrs.

William Zimmerman.
