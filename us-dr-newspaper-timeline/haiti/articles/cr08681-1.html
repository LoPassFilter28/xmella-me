IMPORTANT FROM MEXICO.

_ New Orleans, July 21.

Dates from Mexico to the 7th inst. announce that the

British Minister lias advised the Mexican government

that decisive measnres would probably be taken at

once, if the claims of English creditors were not settled

before the next packet sailed. The French and Span

ish Mblisters said their governments must do likewise.

The Danish brig Proper, with the Prussian Minis

ter on board, was recently wrecked at Vera Cruz. All

the passengers were saved.

Revolutions were starting up in all quarters of Mexi
