Bark Vigo, Thurlow, for Buenos Ayres, cleared at X. York

27th inst.

Bark Mary F. Slade, Crowell, uncertain, was at Hong

Kong Oct. 13.

Bark Matagordia, Gardner, from Alexandria, arrived at

Providence, 26th inst.

Schr. Gertrude Horton, Myers, from Yew Bedford to Haiti

more, was at Newport, 25th inst.

Schr. Iram Smith. McGivern, for Alexandria, sailed from

Fall River. 24th inst.

Schr. Fair Dealer, for Baltimore, sailed from Cas

tine, 22d iyst.

Norfolk, Dec. 25.—The three-masted schooner Only

Daughter, Captain Bovener. from Yew York for Baltimore,

is ashore on the William K.ight Shoals. She is well up,

and the shoals dry at low water, it is thought she can be

gotten off.

EASTERN* PORTS.

YEW YORK, Dec. 28.—Arr. steamship Columbia, Charles

ton; harks B. Fountain, Y. Orleans; Dirogo, Glasgow; schrs.

Anthem, Lavaca; D.'Trowbridge, St Thomas. Chi. steam

ship Edinhurg, Glasgow; barks Octavio, Richmond: C. R.

Sutil. St. Thomas.

PHILADELPHIA, Dec. 28.—CPd steams'p Keystone State,

Charleston.

BOSTON 7 Dec. 27. —Noon. —Arr. brig Forest State, Pensa

cola; schrs. M. Tribon, Darien, Ga.; M. Burr, Georgetown,

D. C,: Joseph Turner and Henrietta, Norfolk: Neptune and

R. G. Whilden, Alexandria. Cl'd ship Queen of the Seas,
