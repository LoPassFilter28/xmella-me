AGAINST DICTATOR

Score U. S. Commissioner and Warn Investigators

Massacre Is In Sight.

Port-au-Prince, Haiti, March 3.—

Two prominent Haitian leaders, rep

resenting groups with a total mem

bership of 500,000, in testifying be

fore President Hoover’s investiga

tion commission today, urged the

immediate recall of Brig. Gen. John
