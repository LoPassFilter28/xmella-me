Boost Physician

For City Council

HARRISBURG. Pa (ANP) A

vigorous campaign is underway

here to boost Dr Joseph A Ran

dall. local physician, for nomination

for City Council

A leader for the past- 10 years.

Dr Randall has been putting up a

one-man fight for housing, health

facilities, political and civil rights.

He is an active worker in the

United Cerebral Palsy organization

of Dauphin. Cumberland and Perry

Counties and a member of both the

local and state board of directors

and the National Public Relations

Committee.

Dr. Randall was born in Jeffer

son, Pa., educated in the public

schools of Jefferson, Clairton and

Elizabeth and received both his

bachelor’s degree and medical edu

cation at Howard University.
