It was generally believed the day the Chilton sailed

that the Chamber had rejected the proposition, and

had also rejected a claim made for the detention of

the American brig Leanrler and the imprisonment of

her captain. In consequence of this, the Haytiens

were daily in expectation of tho arrival of the Ameri

can squadron. Five of the conspirators asrainst the

"Empire had been shot. One of them was a custom

house officer, who died with the declaration that there

were plenty more left behind who coincided with

him in the opinion that the Imperial government was

too expensive for so small an Empire. Sonlouqne

parades daily at the head of two or three thousand

Telegraphed for the Washington Union.

Baltimore, April 29 6, p. m. Jenny Lind, who

was advertised to sing this evening at the Front-street

Theatre, suddenly became so much indisposed to-day

as to render necessary a postponment of her first con

cert till Thursday evening next. Upon concluding

her engagement heee, she will proceed direct to Phil

adelphia, and from thence to New York, where she

will terminate her American tour.

On the 10th instant, by Dr. W. J. Blow,

Mr. J. L.
