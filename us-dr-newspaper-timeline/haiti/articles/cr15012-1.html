End or the Dominican Invasion .—Accounts

from Gonaives to February 1st, state that on the

morning of that day, a proclamation was pub

A Western Paper .—The editor of the Wis

consin Mirror, in his paper for Jan. 1, says,

We are doing what perhaps has never been

done in the Uuited States before we are prin

ting this paper in the woods, not a dwelling ex

cept our own within half a mile of us, and only

one within a mile. The forest oaks hang over

our office and dwelling, the deer and rabbits shy

around us, and the patridges and quails seek

our acquaintance by venturing near our doors.

But most of the romance is soon to be spoiled.

We expect a large village—yea, a city, to grow

up rapidly around us, and that is why we are

here and"printing in the woods."
