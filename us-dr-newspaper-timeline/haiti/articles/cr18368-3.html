ROAD BUILDING PLAN

PORT AU PRINCE, Haiti (UP)

President Francois Duvalier told a

group of 45 leading United States

newspapermen that his govern

ment is preparing large road-build

ing, slum clearance and housing

projects to alleviate Haiti’s serious

unemployment problem.

Leading executives of New
