Haytien Republic Homeward Bound.

NEW YORK, Jan. 3.—The Sun's special

dispatch from Santiago de Cuba says

that the United States steamer Galena is

towing the steamship Haytien Republic

to that port from Port au Prince. At

Santiago de Cuba the Haytien Republic

will await the arrival of a crew from

New York. She will then take aboard a

cargo of logwood and sail for Boston.

The Coal Output of Indiana.

INDIANAPOLIS, Jan. 7.—The report of

the Indiana mine inspector, which has

been filed with the governor, shows that

the output of coal in the state in 1888

was 3,140,979 tons, which was a decrease

from the previous year of 76,732 tons.

The decrease is attributed to the use of

crude oil for fuel in Chicago.
