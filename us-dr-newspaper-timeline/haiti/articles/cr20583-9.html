The Telegraph Operator.—As the telegraph

with its wonderful power stands separated from

all Other trades or professions, so the telegraph

operator occupies a place of peculiar interest and

difficulty as compared with other men. Of in

terest as possessing the power and skill to wield

the electric fluid, akin to the lightning of heaven,

at will and harmlessly, so that what is often an

instrument of death is converted in his hands in

to a mighty agency for good to humanity. He

is the possessor of the ’earliest tidings of life or

death, of wars and rumors of wars, of fire, flood

or tempest, and frequently of the weightiest se
