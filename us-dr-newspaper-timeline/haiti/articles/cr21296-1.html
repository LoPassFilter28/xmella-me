FILLMORE A FILIBUSTER.

The rich developments contained in the corres

pondence between this government and one Robert

Moyean Walsh, special agent, and we might add

1 Envoy Extraordinary to the government of Hayti,

as laid before the Senate in compliance with a reso

lution of that body, are worthy of the considera

tion of the American people. Coming forth upon

the heels of the Message, in which the President

so elaborately sets forth and enforces the orthodox

doctrine of non-intervention, and declaims in strong

school-boy eloquence against the heresies of Kos

suth and other champions of European freedom,

that have appealed to us, as one of the great family

of nations, to raise our voice in support of one of

the most wholesome and well-established laws regu

lating their intercourse with each other, and to

stretch forth our hand to the relief of the down-trod

den of their Father-land, we confess to no small

degree of amazement. The revelations contained

in this correspondence develops a hollow-hcarted

ness and hypocrisy in all this cant of the Executive

and his counsellors, about minding our own busi

ness, which we venture to say was never excel

led, and has been rarely equalled by the most cor

rupt of European courts.

The low flings of the Message at Kossuth, and

the false coloring given to his mission, are dignity

and fairness personified, compared with the position

of the President under this astounding disclosure.
