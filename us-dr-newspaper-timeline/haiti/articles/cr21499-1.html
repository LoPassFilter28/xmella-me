From Buenos Ayres. —Advices from Buenos

Another Failure. —Hurlbut failed to-day. His

liabilities are stated at $1,000,000. Protested bills

from Havre caused his failure.

Advices from Cape Haytien to the 30th contra

dict the recently published proclamation of the

Dominicans. Each party is bound to give two

months' notice before resorting to arms.

Buenos Ayrean letters say that the Brazilians

are threatening to blockade that port. There is

much excitement in consequence.
