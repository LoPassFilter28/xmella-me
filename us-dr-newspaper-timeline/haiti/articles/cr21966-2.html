Enquist's Case Taken Up.

St. Petersburg, Oct. 15.The com

mission which is investigating the

conduct of the Russian officers who

took part in the battle of the Sea of

Japan has taken up the case of Rear

Admiral Enquist, commander of the'

light cruiser squadron, who escaped

to Manila. Enquist will be heard on

Tuesday in justification of the with

drawal of his ships during the battle.'
