U. S. Help Needed

He is supposed to do no work

on weekends. But his concern

over Haiti’s misfortune led

him to invite me to the palace

Interview*. He said he urgently

“We are in dire circum

need help. We must get help

from the United States.”

Without assistance, he con

“We hope—and we think it

smallest and poorest country in

the Caribbean.

democracy In this country. And

we want to be of constructive

help to your country in its big

gest task, the light for a free

world.”
