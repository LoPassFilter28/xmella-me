The Committee of Ways and Means have done

nothing on the Tariff question, and the prospect

is that the whole subject will go over to the next

Congress.

The Hon. Robert Walsh will go to Hayti. His

object is to join the English and French agents,

in persuading the Dominicans to submit to the

government of Soulouque. It is thought that the

interests of all parties will be promoted by bring-

ing the whole island under his government.

I am informed that the statement that President

Fillmore has signified to Mr. Ewbank, Commis-

sioner of Patents, that he had better resign, is

true, and that Mr. Ewbank himself acknowledzed

it.

The Senate Committee on Naval Affairs have

this day agreed that if Secretary Grahan: has

made a verbal contract with Daken, Gilbert and

Secor, to erect a dry dock in California, the

agreement must be consummated. The House

Committee will report to the same effect on Taes-

Purchase of Bennett, the Fugitive Slave.

PHILADELPHIA, Saturday, Jan. 25.

Bennett, the Fugitive Slave, who was remand-

ed to the castody of his master, has been pur-

chased for about $350, and set at liberty.
