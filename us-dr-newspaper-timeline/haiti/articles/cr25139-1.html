FROM HAYIT.

Capt. Hoyt, of the barque Hecla, from Port au

Prince, whence she sailed 7lh Dec., informs

that the French Commercial agent residing at

the Cape, had been suspected of intriguing with

the Dominicans, and taken on board a Haytien

vessel and flogged, and had been released and

arrived at Port au Prince and made a complaint

to the French Consul, who had despatched a stea

mer to windward to the French Admiral and

who it was reported had arrived at the Cape just

previous to the sailing of Capt. Hoyt.—N* Y.

Courier._

"FROM BUENOS AYRES.

The bark Mason Barney, Capt. Scott, arrived

et New York in sixty-one days from Buenos Ay

res, five or six days later than our advices by

the brig Falconer, but she brings no additional

news of importance. The blockade was still

continued. U. S. birg Bainbridge sailed a few

days before the Mason Barney for Montevideo

the U. S. ship Boston wras still at Montevideo.
