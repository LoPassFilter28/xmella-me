HUGE DAIRY

FESTIVAL

MANASSAS, Va., Oct. 9.—With

last minute preparations under

way, a dress rehearsal will be held

here tomorrow morning for the

elaborate Fifth Annual Piedmont

Dairy Festival in Manassas Fri

day.

The festival will get underway

officially tomorrow night when

the opening festival ball will be

held in the Manassas gymnasium.

The second ball for the festival,

in honor of the queen, will be held

Friday night.

With several floats sent by

merchants from Washington,

D. C.. the number of float and

parade entries for the “Milky

Way” parade Is larger this year

than at any previous festival, it

is reported. Floral queen and

princess floats will lead more than

50 floats in the parade.

Approximately 200 Northern Vir

ginia boys will compete in the

dairy judging contests scheduled

to begin Friday morning.

James H. Price, lieutenant
