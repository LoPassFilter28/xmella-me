Baez started out upon the journey, from which he has

Just returned, accompanied only by Gautier, Delmonto,

Curiel, and a colonel of cavalry. He wenl unarmed—a

thing sald to be before unknown in his history. A few

days ago, the Commandanto Macoris became intoxicated,

abused his office and his people, was removed,

and undertook to eobel agalust his suc

cessor, He was captured and put in prison,

This was said to be a new revolution, and was reported

to Gen, Camencro as such, The General, with 60 men,

went to Macoris, but the man had been captured before

he reached there, and was lodged in the old castle In this

city, Gen. Camenero has just told me that tho only

foundation for the story was, that a mob was raised to

punish the man for abusing his wife. The German war

steamer Gazelle, Capt, Avent, was here some 10 days aco,

and remained several days, Thirty-five of the officers

tives, and prominen’ merchants of the place. He gave a

toast to the Dominican Government—the Governor being

present—and remarked that Le hoped they would be able
