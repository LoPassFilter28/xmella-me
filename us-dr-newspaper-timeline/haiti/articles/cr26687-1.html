SAN JUAN DE PORTO RICO, Au

gust 10.—A hurricane broke over the

south coast at 1 o'clock Tuesday morn

ing and swept northwest. There was

no abatement for nine hours, the great

est damage being done between tight

and ten o'clock a. m. The wires were

down and communication with the in

terior was for a time impossible. It is

now chiefly carried on by couriers. At

San Juan four natives were drowned in

the harbor; eighty houses were demol

ished and hundreds were unroofed. The

damage to property is estimated at

$500,000. Commissary stores to the

value of $50,000 were destroyed.

A despatch by cable from Ponce sent

at 10 o’clock this morning, says the

town was almost destroyed. Almost all

the frame buildings are down. The

bridge is swept away, and there is no

communication between, the port and
