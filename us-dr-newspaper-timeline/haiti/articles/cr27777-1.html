Buy, Display Defense Stamp

RT-ALi-PRINCE, Haiti. (ANP) The unsettled political

situation in the Republic of Haiti can best be pictured by noting

the recent passage by the national assembly of a law which

requires ail home owners to buy and display prominently a

national defense stamp.
