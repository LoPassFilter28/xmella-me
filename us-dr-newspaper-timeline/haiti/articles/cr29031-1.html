Being appointed Minister and

sailing for one’s new post all

in the space of a week is a man

sized job—and the Counselor of

the Dominican legation, Senor

Don Emilio Garcia Godoy, is

fast finding it out.

Senor Godoy was notified the

end of last week that he had

been appointed Minister to

Haiti. He left for New York

today and will sail for his new

station on Thursday.

Meanwhile Senora Godoy is

busy packing and getting the

two children. Aura and Hector,

ready to sail for Haiti. Senora

Godoy’s mother and sister,

Senora and Senorita de Caceres,

are stopping with her at present.
