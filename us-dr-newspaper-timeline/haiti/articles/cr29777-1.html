Boston, October 28.

Advices from Port au Prince to October 1st state that the

French and British Consuls had left for Gonaives, attended

by three vessels-of-war, to compel SOLOUQUE to make peace

with the Dominicans. They intended to give him forty-eight

hours to decide, and thought be would submit.
