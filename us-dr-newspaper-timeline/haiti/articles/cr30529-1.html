Pan-American Union

Q. How many republics constitute

the Pan-American Union?

A. Twenty-one: U. S. A., Brazil,

Argentina, Peru, Chile, Mexico, Bo

livia, Colombia, Ecuador, Paraguay,

Uruguay, Venezuela, Panama, El

Salvador, Costa Rica, Haiti, Nicara

gua, Guatemala, Dominican Repub

lic, and Honduras.

Shakespeare

Q. Who was the author of the

quotation, “And all our yesterdays

have lighted fools the way to dusty

death”?

A. It is from Shakespeare’s Mac

beth. Act 5, Scene 5.

The verse

reads:

“Tomorrow, and tomorrow and to

morrow,

Creeps in this petty pace from day

to day

To the last syllable of recorded

time.
