Secretaij Swanson Expects En

tire Force to Be Out of Haiti

by August 10.

By the Associated Press.

Secretary Swanson yesterday ordered

evacuation of the majority of the

Marines in Haiti in the first week of

August.

The entire force is expected to be

out of the island by August 10 at the

latest, although August 15 is the dead

line set by President Roosevelt in an

executive order.

Swanson said the Army transport

Chateau Thierry would leave Port Au

Prince July 29 and arrive at New York

August 3 with 129 dependents of com

missioned and non-commissioned offi

cers.

The Marines, in Haiti since 1915,

will be returned to Quantico, Va.. by

the naval store ship Bridge, now at

the Brooklyn Navy Yard.

The Bridge will make two trips

starting about August 1. The first

load will Include 25 officers and 290

enlisted men. The remaining 500 will

be taken on the second trip imme

diately afterward.

MARCH ON’CONSULATE

Communists Demonstrate Before

German Representative.

LOS ANGELES. July 17 (A5).—More

than 20 alleged Communists marched

on the German consulate here yes

terday, displaying banners and

placards demanding the release of

Ernest Thalniann, German Commu

nist leader, now awaiting trial in

Germany.

Max Grah, in charge of the con

sular office, was presented a petition

urging the release of Thalmann.

Grah said the radicals made no at

tempt at violence. Police were called

by the consul to disperse the group.
