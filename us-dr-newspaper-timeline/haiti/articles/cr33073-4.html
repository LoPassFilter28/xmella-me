The case is clear. We did not treat Hayti as our

To extenuate this plain outrage I have heard

it said, that, in relations with Hayti, we are not

bound by the same rules of conduct applicable

to other nations. So I have heard, and this, in

deed is the only possible defense for the outrage.

As in other days it was proclaimed that a black

had no rights which a white man was bound to

respect, so this defense assumes the same thing

of the black republic. But at last the black man

has obtained equal rights, and so, I insist, has

the black republic. As well deny the one as the

other. By an act of Congress, drawn by myself

and approved by Abraham Lincoln in the session
