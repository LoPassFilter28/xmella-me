Momdr. Keleher, S. N.,

On Extended Cruise.

Comdr. Timothy J. Keleher, U. S. N.,

has sailed from Philadelphia on the

U. S. S. Whitney for a cruise to Panama

and South American waters, and he

will return to Washington in May.

Mrs. Keleher will sail from New

York on the new' Amertca-Italy liner

Augustua. January 4, and will visit

Italy and Switzerland and later go to

the south of France, where she will

study for some time, meeting Comdr.

Keleher in this country on his return

in May.

Mr. and Mrs. Frederick Taylor and

their daughters, Miss Nancy Taylor and

their apartment at the Gladstone, 1423
