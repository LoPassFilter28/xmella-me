SOULOUQUE.

L. DuFHENE, Secretary General.

How Truk !—In the language of an eminent writer, man

courts happiness in a thousand shapes, and the faster he fol

lows it the swifter it flies from him. Almost every thing

promiseth happiness to us at a distance, such a pitch of es

tate, such a fortune, or match for a child, but when we come

nearer to it, either we fall short of it or it falls short of our

expectation and it is hard to say which of these is the greater

disappointment. Our hopes are usually bigger than enjoy

ment can satisfy; and an evil long feared, besides that it may

never come, is many times more painful and troublesome than

the evil itself when it comes,—Cherokee Advocate.
