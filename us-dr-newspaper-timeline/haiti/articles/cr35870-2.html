THE SMALL POX IN MASSACHUSETTS.

In default of information from the state board

of health, which ought to have put itself long

ago in communication with the local boards in

every city and town, gathered up the facts about

the spread of the small-pox, and published them

broadcast over the state, we can do no better

than to collect and publish such facts as come to

us. This we have been doing for the last six

weeks and more; but every day discloses facts

that are new and important. Most important of

all would be a list of the towns and cities where

it exists to-day, with the number of cases in

each. Snch a complete list we cannot give, and

probably nobody can, for it appears in some new.
