It is announced that the second and last volume

of Col. Benton’s Thirty Years’ Review has been

put to press by the Messrs. Appleton, of this city,

and will very soon be published.

Advance copies of the report of the Directors of

the New York and Harlem Railroad Company have

found their way into the street. The directors

state that the over-issue of stock in their road grow

ing out of the Schuyler and Kyle frauds has been

adjusted and settled, many of the claims arising

therefrom arranged, and the company relieved in a

great measure from the" embarrassed condition in

which it was found when the present direction as

sumed the management, in May, 1855.

The members of the New York Bar and Bench

assembled in large numbers this morning in the

United States Court-room to pay a tribute of respect

to the memory of their late distinguished associate,

Ogden Hoffman. Resolutions highly eulogistic

of the deceased were adopted, and many feeling

speeches were made by leading members of the legal

profession.
