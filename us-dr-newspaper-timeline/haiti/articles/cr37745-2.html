The Rev. Dyck pioneered a new

mission field along the Dominican

Haitian frontier in 1945. Since

then he has completed three terms

of missionary service there, serv

ing as field chairman for several

years.

Besides coordinating field ac

tivities he gave much time to

evangelism, building missionary

residences, schools and chapels.

He also organized a Bible school

to train native pastors and

workers.

Both of his lectures will be il

lustrated with on the spot pic

tures taken during his service.

Mrs. Brown, who speaks Sun

day morning, represented the

Missionary church in Haiti. She

promoted schools for girls and

directed a program for child

evangelism.

The public is invited to attend

all the services, the Rev. Robert

Dye Sr., pastor of the church,

said.

Mennonite Relief

Group Seeking

Used Clothing

The semi-annual collection of

used clothing for overseas has

been started by the Mennonite

area relief committee. The proj

ect will be continued through all

of October under the direction of

Mrs. Hiram Kohli.

Clean, mended clothing with at

least six months’ wear is desired.

Clothes may be taken to any of

the five area Mennonite churches.

Pickups can be arranged by cal

ling relief committee members.

Cooperating with Mrs. Kohli

in the project are Mrs. Reuel

Bixel of Lima, Elmer Burry of

Pandora and Alvin Augsburger

and Mrs. Herman Hilty of Bluff

ton.

The September beef canning

project netted 1,241 cans of meat

for overseas shipment by the

Mennonite Central Committee.
