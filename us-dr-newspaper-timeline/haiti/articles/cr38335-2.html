REFINERY

Port-Au-Prince (ANP) —The Valentine Petroleum and Chemical

Corporation of New York recently signed agreements with the Haitian

government for the construction of the first petroleum refinery in the

country. The refinery, which will cost between three and four million

dollars when it is completed within the next 18 months, will be owned

and operated by the Haitian Petroleum and Refining company of Haiti,

8. A., a recently formed subsidiary.

MALAGASY DICTIONARY

Tananarive (ANP) —Beginning with its first issue in January,

1963, the Madagascar Bulletin will publish the Grand Dictionary of

Madagascar, which was compiled by Barthelmy Huet of Froberville in

1826. The dictionary, still in manuscript form, contains historical,

geographical, commercial and linguistic information concerning the

country up to an including the beginning of the 19th century.

NON-RESIDENT BARRISTERS BILL

Lagos (ANP) —A new bill has been published by the Government

to enable an Act of Parliament to prohibit all non-Nigerian barristers,

not residents in the country, from practising in any part of the Federa

tion. Called the Legal Practitioners' bill, it has won the approval of the

Nigerian Bar association.
