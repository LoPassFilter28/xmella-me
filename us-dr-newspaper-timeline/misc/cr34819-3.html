SUNDAY, JAN. 1, 1922

Notice to Owners, Agents said

Ship Masters.

The Herald publishes daily

reports of the positions of merchant

vessels received through

the United States Naval Com.

munication Service. Shin masters

of vessels with radio apparatus

may, without charge, report their

(TR) noon positions through the

following naval radio stations in

the United States proper: Bar

Harbor, Me. (Ottercliffes); Amigansett,

L. I.; Cape May, N. J.;

Norfolk, Vs.; Cape Hatteras, N.

C.; Jupiter, Fla.; Key West, Fla.|

New Orleans, La. These position

(TR) reports must be addressed

only to the radio station called,

and they will be forwarded to

New York. It is important that

these reports should be sent

promptly at noon each day in

order that they will be received

in time for publication.
