The United States and the Dominican Republic resumed diplomatic

January 6, 19626 de enero de 1962

The United States and the Dominican Republic resumed diplomatic relations following internal political reforms in the Caribbean nation. Formal ties had been severed after Western Hemisphere foreign ministers voted in August, 1960 to impose sanctions over an assassination attempt against President Romulo (printed 'Rornulo') Betancourt of Venezuela, measures that were subsequently tightened. The sanctions remained in effect for months after the assassination of Generalissimo Rafael Trujillo, whose death initiated a period of political reform. Diplomatic restoration occurred when an American representative presented credentials to the Dominican foreign office.

Estados Unidos y la República Dominicana reanudaron relaciones diplomáticas tras las reformas políticas internas emprendidas en la nación caribeña. Los lazos formales se habían roto después de que los cancilleres del hemisferio occidental votaran en agosto de 1960 a favor de imponer sanciones tras un intento de asesinato contra el presidente Rómulo (impreso 'Rornulo') Betancourt de Venezuela, medidas que posteriormente fueron endurecidas. Las sanciones continuaron vigentes durante meses después del asesinato del generalísimo Rafael Trujillo, cuya muerte inició un período de reforma política. La restauración diplomática tuvo lugar cuando un representante estadounidense presentó credenciales ante la cancillería dominicana.

January 8, 19628 de enero de 1962

Teodoro Moscoso, Latin American director for the United States Agency for International Development, arrived in the Dominican Republic with technical staff only four days after diplomatic relations were resumed following the lifting of Organization of American States sanctions against the defunct Trujillo dictatorship.
