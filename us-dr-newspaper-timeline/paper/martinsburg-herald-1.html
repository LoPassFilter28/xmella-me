Martinsburg Herald

Federal authorities took former President Morales and former Vice President Jiminez of Santo Domingo

April 29, 191129 de abril de 1911

Federal authorities took former President Morales and former Vice President Jiminez of Santo Domingo into custody, along with Zenon Torrible, a dispatch from San Juan in the Martinsburg Herald reports, on charges of organizing a military expedition against the Dominican Republic. Hotel papers and documents seized by officials prompted the detentions. All three men were placed under $7500 bond each.

Las autoridades federales detuvieron al expresidente Morales y al exvicepresidente Jiminez de Santo Domingo, junto con Zenon Torrible, informa un despacho de San Juan en el Martinsburg Herald, bajo acusaciones de organizar una expedición militar contra la República Dominicana. Papeles y documentos de hotel incautados por los funcionarios motivaron las detenciones. Los tres hombres quedaron sujetos a una fianza de $7500 cada uno.

The paper reports that, under U.S.

December 7, 19127 de diciembre de 1912

The paper reports that, under U.S. treaty relations with the Dominican Republic threatened by suspension of American-administered customhouses on the Haitian frontier, special commissioners were dispatched to re-establish the customhouses with a guard. Their efforts reportedly restored normal conditions and helped bring contending parties together toward an adjustment.

El periódico informa que, bajo las relaciones de tratado de Estados Unidos con la República Dominicana amenazadas por la suspensión de las aduanas administradas por estadounidenses en la frontera haitiana, se enviaron comisionados especiales para restablecer las aduanas con una guardia. Sus esfuerzos, según se informó, restablecieron las condiciones normales y ayudaron a acercar a las partes en conflicto hacia un ajuste.
