New Orleans Daily Crescent

Under the heading "From San Domingo," the New Orleans Daily Crescent reports

July 27, 185927 de julio de 1859

Under the heading "From San Domingo," the New Orleans Daily Crescent reports that late advices from the Dominican Republic (referred to as "Dominica") indicate strong hopes for a peaceful settlement of the difficulties between the government of the Republic and that of Denmark, the latter having received the Dominican plenipotentiary very favorably. The report also notes that the issuance of irredeemable paper money by Báez during his administration had caused considerable trouble with foreign government representatives residing at San Domingo.

Bajo el titular «Desde Santo Domingo», el New Orleans Daily Crescent informa que las últimas noticias de la República Dominicana (denominada «Dominica») indican grandes esperanzas de una solución pacífica a las dificultades existentes entre el gobierno de la República y el de Dinamarca, habiendo recibido este último muy favorablemente al plenipotenciario dominicano. El informe señala además que la emisión de papel moneda irredimible por Báez durante su administración había provocado considerables problemas con los representantes de gobiernos extranjeros residentes en Santo Domingo.

A Washington dispatch reports that a San Domingo letter received

February 5, 18665 de febrero de 1866

A Washington dispatch reports that a San Domingo letter received there gives the substance of the conversation at the national palace, on the 14th of January, between Secretary Seward and President Baez, in reference to the recognition of the Dominican Republic.

Un despacho de Washington informa que una carta de Santo Domingo recibida allí da cuenta de la conversación celebrada en el palacio nacional, el 14 de enero, entre el secretario Seward y el presidente Báez, en relación con el reconocimiento de la República Dominicana.

New Orleans Daily Crescent

From St. Domingo, the Crescent prints paragraphs from the Havana papers

March 28, 186628 de marzo de 1866

From St. Domingo, the Crescent prints paragraphs from the Havana papers on conditions in the republic. In Puerto Plata, commerce is reported pretty brisk; the whole island was prospering, perfect tranquility reigned throughout the country, Spaniards continued to be very well treated, and General Báez was generally well liked, from which it was hoped the government would very soon be consolidated under his presidency. Another paragraph gives an unconfirmed report that some of the interior provinces had sent communications to the President calling on him to declare war against the Haytians for the injuries they were committing on the frontier. An earthquake of great severity had thrown down several houses and badly damaged others.

Desde Santo Domingo, el Crescent publica párrafos de los periódicos de La Habana sobre la situación en la república. En Puerto Plata, el comercio se reportaba bastante activo; toda la isla prosperaba, reinaba perfecta tranquilidad en todo el país, los españoles seguían siendo muy bien tratados y el general Báez era en general muy apreciado, por lo que se esperaba que el gobierno se consolidara muy pronto bajo su presidencia. Otro párrafo da la noticia no confirmada de que algunas provincias del interior habían dirigido comunicaciones al Presidente llamándolo a declarar la guerra a los haitianos por los daños que cometían en la frontera. Un terremoto de gran severidad había derrumbado varias casas y dañado gravemente otras.
