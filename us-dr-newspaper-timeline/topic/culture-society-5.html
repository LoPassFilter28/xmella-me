September 25, 194025 de septiembre de 1940

The Daily Alaska Empire reported that several New York creditors, including Louis Sherry, Cartier, Annin Flag Company, and an art gallery, sought payment from General Rafael Trujillo for services and goods provided during the previous year's World's Fair reception. Although creditors hired a lawyer and dispatched a U.S. Marshal to his hotel, Trujillo was shielded from legal action because he held diplomatic immunity as an Ambassador Extraordinary. As a result, creditors were unable to collect the debts or seize his yacht Ramfis, docked at the Washington Navy Yard.

El Daily Alaska Empire informó que diversos acreedores neoyorquinos, entre ellos Louis Sherry, Cartier, la Annin Flag Company y una galería de arte, procuraban el cobro de deudas al general Rafael Trujillo por concepto de bienes y atenciones brindados durante la recepción de la Feria Mundial del año anterior. Si bien los acreedores contrataron a un letrado y comisionaron a un alguacil federal (U.S. Marshal) a su hotel, Trujillo quedó a salvo de acciones judiciales en virtud de su inmunidad diplomática como embajador extraordinario. Por consiguiente, los demandantes no pudieron cobrar los créditos ni embargar su yate Ramfis, atracado en el Astillero Naval de Washington.
