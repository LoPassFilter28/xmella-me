Escalating military and diplomatic crises in the republic are described in accounts from travellers arriving from Santo Domingo, published in the New-York Tribune. J. T. El general Ricardo Limardo permanece sitiado en la ciudad de Monte Cristi, resistiendo con continuos refuerzos del gobierno pero sin lograr ventaja alguna sobre los rebeldes. After spending two months in the United States as special commissioner adjusting his country's finances, Federico Velasquez, minister of finance and commerce of Santo Domingo, departed for home aboard the steamer Cherokee, a New York dispatch in the Abilene Weekly Reflector reports. Before leaving, Velasquez announced that the Dominican republic intends to settle its public debt directly with creditors on equitable terms within its resources, rather than asking the United States to supervise the adjustment. The republic will then request that the United States government administer Dominican customs to service a loan to liquidate established obligations. He stated that Kuhn, Loeb & Co. have engaged to purchase an issue of $20,000,000 of sinking fund gold bonds (printed with an interest rate of S per cent), with the Morton Trust Company also participating.

Antes de partir, Velásquez anunció que la República Dominicana pretende saldar su deuda pública directamente con los acreedores en condiciones equitativas y dentro de sus recursos, en lugar de solicitar a Estados Unidos que supervise el ajuste.
