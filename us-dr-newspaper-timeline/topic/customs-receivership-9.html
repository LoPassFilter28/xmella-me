In commemoration of the newly completed highway in the Dominican

In commemoration of the newly completed highway in the Dominican Republic, Episcopal missionaries under Right Reverend Charles B. Colmore, D.D., Episcopal Missionary Bishop of Porto Rico and the Dominican Republic, are establishing a church at Puerto Plata to link the two sections of the country. September 5, 19305 de septiembre de 1930

The hurricane, which was the first of any consequence to strike Santo Domingo in more than 50 years, lost as much force traveling over the mountainous island as it would have across the Florida Peninsula, potentially sparing Cuba and the United States coast, weather forecaster Mitchell stated. Meanwhile, the devastation in the Dominican Republic caused apprehension among American commercial interests, which had already grown increasingly concerned over the island market's declining buying power during the past six months. Entretanto, los daños causados en la República Dominicana despertaron inquietud entre los intereses comerciales estadounidenses, cuya preocupación por la situación económica allí había aumentado de forma constante durante los últimos seis meses debido a la continua caída del poder adquisitivo de ese mercado insular. Although the All American Cable company restored exterior communications, telegraph lines to the interior vanished, leaving roads impassable and the rest of the republic cut off.

Following 34 hours of storm and a 180-mile-an-hour north wind that unroofed the legation and destroyed many homes in Santo Domingo, conservative estimates placed the death toll at 1500 and the injured at 5000, with no casualties reported among continental Americans or Europeans. President Trujillo has taken personal charge with the city under martial law, good order prevailing, and the dead being burned. Governor Theodore Roosevelt of Porto Rico sent personal representatives by airplane Thursday and is dispatching food, shelter, clothing, and medical aid, while a Marine Corps plane arrived from Port au Prince, Haiti, with representatives of a Marine colonel and is returning immediately for medical officers and supplies. Shipping cannot enter Santo Domingo port because of swift currents and a dredge sunk in the channel, and although the old cathedral and colonial monuments were practically undamaged, disabled water and electric plants and wrecked bridges have isolated the capital on the land side.

Tras 34 horas de tormenta y un viento del norte de 180 millas por hora que destechó la legación y destruyó muchas casas en Santo Domingo, estimaciones conservadoras situaron el número de muertos en 1500 y el de heridos en 5000, sin que se registraran víctimas entre los estadounidenses continentales ni los europeos. El presidente Trujillo ha asumido personalmente el mando con la ciudad bajo ley marcial, donde reina el orden y se están incinerando los cadáveres. El gobernador Theodore Roosevelt de Puerto Rico envió representantes personales en avión el jueves y está despachando alimentos, abrigo, ropa y ayuda médica, mientras que un avión del Cuerpo de Infantes de Marina llegó desde Puerto Príncipe, Haití, con representantes de un coronel de la Infantería de Marina y regresa de inmediato para traer oficiales médicos y suministros.
