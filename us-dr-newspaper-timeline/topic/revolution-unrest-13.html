The Portland Daily Press publishes a series of dispatches from San Domingo chronicling the rapid collapse of the Dominican government in late April. April 27, 190327 de abril de 1903

A Cape Haytien cable of April 23 in The Savannah Morning News relays reports brought by the German cruiser Vineta regarding the battle for Santo Domingo on April 18.
