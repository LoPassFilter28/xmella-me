July 7, 19397 de julio de 1939

Generalissimo Trujillo's entourage in Washington includes Vazquez, former Undersecretary of State and son of the former president whom Trujillo succeeded; Colonel C. A. La comitiva del generalísimo Trujillo en Washington incluye a Vázquez, ex subsecretario de Estado e hijo del ex presidente a quien sucedió Trujillo; al coronel C. A. McLaughlin, ex infante de marina estadounidense que ahora se desempeña como instructor militar de la República Dominicana; y a García Godoy, ministro dominicano en Cuba. La comitiva de bienvenida incluyó al embajador ecuatoriano, capitán Colón Eloy Alfaro; al ministro haitiano Elie Lescot; al director general de la Unión Panamericana, Dr. Leo S. Rowe; al ministro de los Estados Unidos en Ciudad Trujillo, Henry Norweb; y a oficiales militares estadounidenses. La columna señala que en una próxima recepción en la Unión Panamericana ofrecida por los señores de Pastoriza, se espera que la orquesta interprete merengues, descritos como el baile favorito del general.

Fish is quoted as having said at a dinner to Trujillo

July 15, 193915 de julio de 1939

Columnist Raymond Clapper in the Washington Daily News criticized Representative Hamilton Fish for praising General Trujillo's regime as a "golden age" during a New York dinner. Clapper cited first-hand reporting by Quentin Reynolds of Collier's, who investigated the border atrocities and observed victims mutilated by Dominican forces. Clapper maintained that any prosperity Trujillo claimed was purchased through the murder of thousands of obscure Haitian peasants.

El columnista Raymond Clapper, en The Washington Daily News, censuró al congresista Hamilton Fish por alabar el régimen del general Trujillo calificándolo de «era dorada» durante un banquete en Nueva York. Clapper citó testimonios directos de Quentin Reynolds de Collier's, quien investigó las atrocidades en la frontera y constató las mutilaciones de víctimas infligidas por tropas dominicanas. Clapper aseveró que cualquier prosperidad exhibida por Trujillo se pagó con la sangre y el exterminio de miles de humildes campesinos haitianos.

July 17, 193917 de julio de 1939

During his visit to Washington, the Dictator of the Dominican Republic, General Rafael Trujillo, received extraordinary protection not provided by U.S. officials. Following his arrival, the Dominican Legation was transformed under strict suspicion, with detectives examining grocery deliveries and inspecting camera cases for hidden bombs. Tras su llegada, la legación dominicana se transformó bajo rigurosa sospecha, con detectives que examinaban las entregas de víveres e inspeccionaban estuches de cámaras en busca de bombas ocultas. Aunque Trujillo muestra modales apacibles y una sonrisa cautivadora en público, los exiliados dominicanos en Puerto Rico y Estados Unidos lo recuerdan de otra manera, y un detective contratado de la policía de Nueva York vigila la puerta de su dormitorio por la noche.

The Evening Star reports that Virgilio Trujillo, identified by the newspaper as former President of the Dominican Republic, was aboard an incoming transatlantic ship with his family alongside other prominent passengers, including conductor Vladimir Golschmann and cellist Gregor Piatigorsky. Passengers reported that the vessel likely took a northern route due to foggy, cold weather, and noted that Canadian airplanes flew over the ship on Thursday. The article details wartime precautions taken amidst the outbreak of the war in Europe as various British and French liners arrived in port or prepared for ongoing operation.
