The Butte Inter Mountain

The Inter Mountain prints a headed Associated Press item from Washington of December 4:

December 4, 19034 de diciembre de 1903

The Inter Mountain prints a headed Associated Press item from Washington of December 4: John G. Carlisle has been named as a representative of the United States in the arbitration of the claims of the San Domingo Improvement company against the Dominican republic, which was arranged for by former President Woss y Gil as one of the last of his official acts; the item appears under the head 'Carlisle Is an Arbitrator'.

El Inter Mountain publica una nota titulada de la Associated Press, de Washington, del 4 de diciembre: John G. Carlisle ha sido nombrado representante de los Estados Unidos en el arbitraje de las reclamaciones de la San Domingo Improvement Company contra la República Dominicana, que fue concertado por el expresidente Woss y Gil como uno de los últimos actos oficiales suyos; la nota aparece bajo el título «Carlisle Is an Arbitrator».
