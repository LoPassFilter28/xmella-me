The Irish Standard

The Irish Standard prints an article: A movement is on foot

August 29, 191429 de agosto de 1914

The Irish Standard prints an article: A movement is on foot to raise, by subscriptions limited to fifty cents each from the people of the United States, of the twenty other republics of North, Central and South America and of Canada, the sum of $500,000 for a splendid Pan-American tomb and memorial for Christopher Columbus in Santo Domingo, where his remains are declared to rest. A superb site is available on the coral coast of the Caribbean Sea, where the Dominican Republic about twenty years ago dedicated the Plaza Columbina for the location of a monument to the great discoverer. It is proposed to construct a massive tomb, somewhat similar to the Grant Tomb on the Hudson, for the base, and then to sweep into the air with a superstructure, on the top of which will be placed one of the most powerful lights in the world, to be known as Columbus Light. The plan further provides for an endowment fund for the perpetual maintenance of the light.

The Irish Standard publica un artículo: Está en marcha un movimiento para recaudar, mediante suscripciones limitadas a cincuenta centavos cada una de parte de los habitantes de los Estados Unidos, de las otras veinte repúblicas de la América del Norte, Central y del Sur y del Canadá, la suma de $500,000 para una espléndida tumba panamericana y un monumento a Cristóbal Colón en Santo Domingo, donde se declara que reposan sus restos. Hay disponible un sitio soberbio en la costa de coral del mar Caribe, donde la República Dominicana dedicó hace unos veinte años la Plaza Columbina para la ubicación de un monumento al gran descubridor. Se propone construir una tumba masiva, algo parecida a la Tumba de Grant en el Hudson, para la base, y luego elevarse en el aire con una superestructura, en cuya cima se colocará una de las luces más potentes del mundo, que se conocerá como la Luz de Colón. El plan dispone además un fondo de dotación para el mantenimiento perpetuo de la luz.

El texto destaca que Sumner representaba las tradiciones más elevadas del Partido Republicano frente a la búsqueda de poder de Grant, mientras que los editoriales diarios de Greeley movilizaron a miles de lectores rurales contra el gobierno.

U.S. officials and Dominicans who have their country's best

May 20, 192320 de mayo de 1923

U.S. officials and Dominicans who have their country's best interest at heart hope full government control can be returned to the Dominicans as soon as possible without undue delays, The Evening star comments. Calling the intervention in Santo Domingo a thankless job that has drawn criticism in Europe and Latin America as a form of "imperialism," the paper states that before withdrawing, the United States will ensure that Dominican independence, territorial integrity, public order, and the security of life and property are adequately safeguarded.
