New Ambassadors Are Feted; Other Parties Also Given

Two of the newly-appointed Ambassadors to Latin American republics are being entertained while in Washington enroute to their new posts. The United States Ambassador-designate to the Dominican Republic, Mr. Ellis O. Briggs, will be the guest of honor at a late-afternoon party which Mrs. Leigh Wade will give Tuesday in her home on Leroy place. Guests are invited from 5:30 to 7:30 o'clock. The Ambassador-designate shared honors at a luncheon given today by the Dominican Ambassador in this capital and Senora de Copello. The former United States Ambassador to Ciudad Trujillo, Mr. Avra M. Warren, who has been appointed Ambassador to Panama, and Mrs. Warren, shared honors with Mr. Briggs at the luncheon. The Panama Ambassador in Washington and Senora de Jimenez were in the company and others at the luncheon were Senorita Adelina Copello, daughter of the hosts; Mr. Leon Falk and Dr. Oscar Diez Albertini.

The Panama Ambassador and Senora de Jimenez will be hosts at dinner this evening, entertaining in their charming Embassy on Woodland drive. The party was planned rather hastily owing to the lack of time of the newly-appointed Ambassador and Mrs. Warren, who are expected to leave for Ciudad Trujillo within a very few days.

The Ambassador-designate, Mr. Briggs, formerly served as Counselor of the United States Embassy in Havana for some time and was succeeded at that post by Mr. Allan Dawson. The marriage of Mr. Dawson to Mrs. Carley Robinson Hawkins took place March 16 and after a brief wedding trip in this country they went to Cuba, where the former assumed his new duties at the Embassy.

Senor Ernesto B. Freites has arrived here from the Dominican Republic to attend the Inter-American Development Conference in New York which will open Tuesday. Senor Freites heads the delegation from his country and serving with him will be the Minister Counselor of the Embassy, Dr. J. R. Rodriguez, and the First Secretary, Senor J. M. Sanz.

Another dinner party this evening will be that of Associate Justice of the Court of Appeals for the District of Columbia and Mrs. Justin Miller, who will entertain in honor of the Chief Justice of the Supreme Court and Mrs. Harlan Fiske Stone. Earlier today Senora de Danis, wife of the Minister-Counselor of the Spanish Embassy, gave an informal buffet luncheon in the Burgundy Room at Wardman Park Hotel, her guests numbering 50.

Those in the company included Senora de Escalante, Senora de Michels, Senora de Concheso, Senora de Blanco, Mme. Wei, Senora de Recinos, Senora de Castro, Senora de Caceres, Mme. Jawdat, Mme. Chautemps, Senora de Garland, Mme. Kwapiszewska, Senora de Elguera, Mme. Garin, Mrs. Mahoney, Senora de Bascunan, Senora de Scilingo, Senora de Munilla, Senora de Campbell and Senora de Echegaray.

Tomorrow, the former United States Ambassador to the Soviet Union and Mrs. Joseph E. Davies will be hosts to a large number of guests at an informal garden tea at Tregaron, their charming home on Klingle road. The extensive gardens there are particularly lovely at this season of the year and while the Davies have owned the place—which they purchased from the estate of the late Mr. and Mrs. James Parmelee—they have done considerable landscaping without destroying any of the giant old trees. When Mr. and Mrs. Parmelee lived at Tregaron they called the estate The Causeway, as the entrance roadway crosses Klingle creek which runs through the grounds.
