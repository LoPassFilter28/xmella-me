New Britain Herald. New Britain, Conn., January 21, 1915. Page 5.New Britain Herald. New Britain, Conn., 21 de enero de 1915. Página 5. View the originalVer el original

It was reported that Desidario Arias, revolutionist leader, had recommended that it would 'be a good thing to shoot off three or four good Americans' in order to bring about American intervention. Mr. Glynn was recently mentioned in testimony as having given Beer a letter of introduction to Secretary Bryan. Mr. Fort went to Santo Domingo recently to investigate affairs there for this government. Commissioner Phelan is also trying to procure the attendance of Jack Rose, one of the witnesses at the trial of Police Lieutenant Becker for the murder of Herman Rosenthal. Sullivan acted as Rose's counsel in the case and was associated with him, according to testimony in the present investigation, in the promotion of prizefights in Waterbury, Conn. Albany, N. Y., Jan. 21. 'The statement of M. E. Davis in the investigation of James M. Sullivan, American minister to the Dominican republic, that I am interested in Santo Domingo matters, is an absolute falsehood,' said ex-Governor Martin H.
