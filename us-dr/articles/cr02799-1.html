A wireless report from Rear Admiral Caperton to the Navy Department announces that one American marine was killed and another wounded Tuesday during an engagement with revolutionists near Monte Cristi. The casualties were reported as Private John Acriment of the Twenty-seventh Company of Marines, killed, and Private Albert Vielbaum of the same company, wounded in the upper left arm. Navy officials noted that the name Acriment did not appear on the official Marine Corps roster, suggesting an error in transmission that prompted a records search to determine his correct identity. ONE MARINE KILLED IN SANTO DOMINGO BATTLEUN INFANTE DE MARINA MUERTO EN COMBATE EN SANTO DOMINGO

One man was killed and another wounded Tuesday in an engagement between American marines and a force of revolutionists near Monte Christi, Santo Domingo, according to a wireless report received at the Navy Department yesterday from Rear Admiral Caperton, commanding the American forces in the Dominican republic.Un hombre resultó muerto y otro herido el martes en un enfrentamiento entre infantes de marina estadounidenses y una fuerza de revolucionarios cerca de Monte Cristi, Santo Domingo, según un informe radiotelegráfico recibido ayer en el Departamento de Marina del contraalmirante Caperton, al mando de las fuerzas estadounidenses en la república Dominicana.

The American casualties were: Private John Acriment, of the Twenty-seventh Company of Marines, killed, and Private Albert Vielbaum, of the same company, wounded in the upper left arm. The name Acriment is not found in the official roster of the Marine Corps, and it is thought probable that an error in spelling occurred in transmission. The records are being searched by the Navy Department to get the proper name. Private Vielbaum is a native of Aberdeen, Wash., is 25 years of age and enlisted in the Marine Corps September 3, 1912.Las bajas estadounidenses fueron: el soldado raso John Acriment, de la Vigésima Séptima Compañía de Infantes de Marina, muerto, y el soldado raso Albert Vielbaum, de la misma compañía, herido en la parte superior del brazo izquierdo. El nombre Acriment no figura en la lista oficial del Cuerpo de Infantería de Marina, y se considera probable que haya ocurrido un error ortográfico en la transmisión. El Departamento de Marina está revisando los registros para obtener el nombre correcto. El soldado raso Vielbaum es natural de Aberdeen, Washington, tiene 25 años de edad y se alistó en el Cuerpo de Infantería de Marina el 3 de septiembre de 1912.
