Trujillo Move Scored

HAVANA, Nov. 27 (AP)

A Dominican exile group said yesterday Dominican Dictator Rafael Trujillo "only made himself look more ridiculous" in sentencing and fining Cuban Prime Minister Castro and Venezuelan President Romulo Betancourt in absentia.

Mr. Castro, his brother Raul and Mr. Betancourt were among 113 persons sentenced to 30 years at hard labor and collectively fined $100 million in the Dominican Republic on charges of participating in an attempted invasion of the Caribbean island last June. Only five defendants were in court.

The fine "simply represents the amount he (Trujillo) spent for arms to maintain his war machine since last June," the exiles said in a statement.
