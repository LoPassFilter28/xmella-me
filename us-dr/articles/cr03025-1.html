The Montgomery Advertiser prints a dispatch: the Washington government decided to send observers speaking the language of San Domingo to act as impartial witnesses, but not as referees or officials, so that in case of dispute it would have persons to turn to for impartial testimony. The United States had used its good offices in persuading the revolutionists to quit hostilities on the promise that everything possible within international right would be done by the United States to insure fair elections. Protest from San Domingo, it was explained at the White House today, was founded on the belief that the United States intended a supervision of the elections, but now that the notion has been dispelled the administration here is confident there will be no objection to the presence of the American observers. WILSON EXPLAINS DOMINICAN ACTION Commissioners Go to Be Peaceful Spectators and Not to Supervise the Elections Next MondayWILSON EXPLICA LA ACCIÓN DOMINICANA Los comisionados van para ser espectadores pacíficos y no para supervisar las elecciones del próximo lunes

WASHINGTON, Dec. 8.—The United States does not intend to supervise or interfere with approaching elections in the Dominican Republic. A special commission from Washington will merely observe the election as "friendly and impartial spectators."WASHINGTON, 8 de diciembre.—Los Estados Unidos no tienen la intención de supervisar ni de interferir en las próximas elecciones en la República Dominicana. Una comisión especial de Washington se limitará a observar los comicios como «espectadores amistosos e imparciales».

This was the way President Wilson today outlined informally the mission of State Department officials now on their way to San Domingo.De este modo esbozó hoy informalmente el presidente Wilson la misión de los funcionarios del Departamento de Estado que se encuentran ahora de camino a Santo Domingo.

President Wilson said the United States had used its good offices in persuading the revolutionists to quit hostilities on the promise that everything possible, within international right, would be done by the United States to insure fair elections. The Washington Government decided to send observers speaking the language of San Domingo, to act as impartial witnesses, but not as referees or officials, so that in case of dispute it would have persons to turn to for impartial testimony.El presidente Wilson declaró que los Estados Unidos habían interpuesto sus buenos oficios para persuadir a los revolucionarios de que cesaran las hostilidades bajo la promesa de que los Estados Unidos harían todo lo posible, dentro del derecho internacional, para garantizar unas elecciones limpias. El Gobierno de Washington decidió enviar observadores que hablaran el idioma de Santo Domingo para que actuaran como testigos imparciales, pero no como árbitros ni funcionarios, de modo que, en caso de disputa, dispusiera de personas a las que recurrir para obtener un testimonio imparcial.

Protest from San Domingo, it was explained at the White House today, was founded on the belief that the United States intended a supeervision of the elections but now that the notion has been dispelled the administration here is confident there will be no objection to the presence of the American observers.La protesta procedente de Santo Domingo, según se explicó hoy en la Casa Blanca, se fundaba en la creencia de que los Estados Unidos pretendían supervisar las elecciones, pero ahora que dicha idea ha sido disipada, la administración aquí confía en que no habrá objeción alguna a la presencia de los observadores estadounidenses.

Senor Francisco J. Peynado, Dominican Minister, called on Secretary Bryan to protest that the Dominican Government cannot see how representatives of the State Department can visit Santo Domingo for the purpose indicated and without official status.El señor Francisco J. Peynado, ministro dominicano, visitó al secretario Bryan para protestar expresando que el Gobierno dominicano no alcanza a comprender cómo representantes del Departamento de Estado pueden visitar Santo Domingo con el propósito indicado y sin carácter oficial.

Before the elections next Monday the United States will have four ships in Dominican waters.Antes de las elecciones del próximo lunes, los Estados Unidos tendrán cuatro buques en aguas dominicanas.

WOMEN AND MEN SPEAK.HABLAN MUJERES Y HOMBRES.

SANTO DOMINGO, Dec. 8.—Women spoke with men orators today at an immense mass meeting in favor of "free and legal elections" in the Dominican Republic. The meeting was under the auspices of the Independence Party. A protest against the supervision of the elections by American commissioneres was circulated.SANTO DOMINGO, 8 de diciembre.—Mujeres hablaron hoy junto a oradores hombres en un inmenso mitin multitudinario a favor de «elecciones libres y legales» en la República Dominicana. El mitin estuvo bajo los auspicios del Partido de la Independencia. Se hizo circular una protesta contra la supervisión de las elecciones por comisionados estadounidenses.
