WATCH SITUATION CLOSELY Washington Officials Ready to Intervene in San Domingo.

Washington, Sept. 15.—The state department has now decided to await further reports from Minister Russell, the commander of the gunboat Petrel and some of the American consuls in the Dominican republic regarding the revolution before deciding whether the United States will intervene.
