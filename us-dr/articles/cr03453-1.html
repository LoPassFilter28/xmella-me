The U.S. gunboat had lately used her main battery of four-inch guns to silence President Bordas's batteries, which were bombarding the rebel garrison holding the city in violation of the agreement that the lives of foreigners and other non-combatants would not be endangered by artillery fire. A junta commission of legalistas, representing the revolutionists, boarded the vessel and gave assurances of their desire to avoid endangering the safety of Americans, after scattering rebel rifle fire had passed over the ship and some shots struck her. A cable report said Santo Domingo City was still besieged by rebels and that other towns had joined the revolutionist cause, with four separate revolutions raging in the country. From Washington, the little republic was causing grave anxiety: the American minister was returning home after several months of fruitless efforts to restore peace, and the day's reports allowed no improvement in conditions. Bordas, obliged to maintain himself in office by extra-official decrees and partial elections, had been leading his troops in person against the rebellious generals in the northern part of the republic without being able to overcome them, while revolutions had also broken out at Azua, where the government navy found it necessary to destroy the customs house, along the railway between Sánchez and Samaná Bay, at Montecristi and at La Romana, where the agents of extensive American-owned plantations appeared at the State Department seeking protection for their property. Official opinion in Washington was slowly crystallizing into a conviction that only powerful outside influence, probably that of the United States, could terminate the chaotic conditions and set up a stable government: the revolutionists appeared unable to overcome the Bordas government, supported by the monthly proportion of customs receipts handed over by American collectors, but Bordas had not diminished the number of his enemies after a six months' campaign. Mr. Vick, the American collector of customs, had resigned, and Minister Sullivan was returning to the United States, leaving Secretary White in charge of the American legation. FOUR SEPARATE REVOLUTIONS LITTLE REPUBLIC OF SAN DOMINGO GIVING WASHINGTON OFFICIALS GRAVE CONCERN. PRESIDENT BORDAS UNABLE TO SUCCESSFULLY HANDLE SITUATION Washington Government Morally Bound to Maintain Order, Under Treaty, Is Loath to Step in, and in the Meantime the Property of the Americans Are Being Destroyed, Minister Sullivan Is Returning Home, While the American Collector of Customs Has Resigned—Navy Now in Dominican Waters.CUATRO REVOLUCIONES DISTINTAS LA PEQUEÑA REPÚBLICA DE SANTO DOMINGO CAUSA GRAVE PREOCUPACIÓN A LOS FUNCIONARIOS DE WASHINGTON. EL PRESIDENTE BORDAS ES INCAPAZ DE MANEJAR CON ÉXITO LA SITUACIÓN El Gobierno de Washington, obligado moralmente a mantener el orden en virtud de un tratado, se muestra reacio a intervenir y, mientras tanto, las propiedades de los estadounidenses están siendo destruidas, el ministro Sullivan regresa a casa, mientras que el recaudador de aduanas estadounidense ha dimitido; la Armada se encuentra ahora en aguas dominicanas.

BULLETIN.BOLETÍN.

WASHINGTON, July 9.—Reckless firing by Dominican rebels sent several shots against the hull of the American gunboat Machias in Puerta Plata harbor late last night and drew upon themselves a strong volley from the Machias' three pounders and automatic rifles. The shooting from shore stopped at once.WASHINGTON, 9 de julio.—Disparos temerarios de los rebeldes dominicanos enviaron anoche varios proyectiles contra el casco del cañonero estadounidense Machias en el puerto de Puerto Plata y provocaron una fuerte andanada de los cañones de tres libras y fusiles automáticos del Machias. El tiroteo desde la orilla cesó de inmediato.

Lately the gunboat used her main battery of four-inch guns to silence President Bordas' batteries, which were bombarding the rebel garrison holding the city in violation of the agreement that the lives of foreigners and other non-combatants would not be endangered by artillery fire.Recientemente, el cañonero utilizó su batería principal de cañones de cuatro pulgadas para silenciar las baterías del presidente Bordas, que estaban bombardeando a la guarnición rebelde que ocupaba la ciudad en violación del acuerdo de que las vidas de los extranjeros y otros no combatientes no se pondrían en peligro por el fuego de artillería.

Reporting the latest incident to the navy department tonight, Captain Russell of the battleship South Carolina said considerable scattering rifle fire fro mthe rebels passed over the Machias and some shots struck her. Later the dispatch said the "junta commission of legalistas," representing the revolutionists, boarded the Machias and gave assurances of their desire to avoid endangering the safety of Americans.Al informar esta noche sobre el último incidente al Departamento de Marina, el capitán Russell del acorazado South Carolina declaró que un considerable fuego disperso de fusilería de los rebeldes pasó por encima del Machias y que algunos disparos lo alcanzaron. Más tarde, el despacho indicó que la «comisión de la junta de legalistas», en representación de los revolucionarios, subió a bordo del Machias y dio seguridades de su deseo de evitar poner en peligro la seguridad de los estadounidenses.

Captain Russell informed the department that he had a cable report that Santo Domingo City still was besieged by rebels and that other towns had joined the revolutionist cause. Late today a force of about 60 rebels was defeated at Cafemba.El capitán Russell informó al departamento de que tenía un informe por cable de que la ciudad de Santo Domingo aún se encontraba sitiada por rebeldes y que otras localidades se habían sumado a la causa revolucionaria. A última hora de hoy, una fuerza de unos 60 rebeldes fue derrotada en Cafemba.

WASHINGTON, July 8.—With four separate revolutions raging simultaneously and the American minister to the country returning home after several months of fruitless efforts to restore peace, the little republic of San Domingo is causing grave anxiety on the part of the Washington government, which is morally bound to maintain order there under the semi-protectorate created by treaty.WASHINGTON, 8 de julio.—Con cuatro revoluciones distintas que se desarrollan simultáneamente y el ministro estadounidense ante el país regresando a casa tras varios meses de esfuerzos infructuosos por restablecer la paz, la pequeña república de Santo Domingo está causando una grave inquietud en el gobierno de Washington, el cual está obligado moralmente a mantener el orden allí en virtud del semiprotectorado creado por tratado.

Today's reports showed no improvement in conditions in the island. For many months President Bordas obliged to maintain himself in office by a series of extra official decrees and partial elections, has been leading in person his troops against the rebellious generals in the northern part of the republic without being able to overcome them. Meanwhile revolutions have broken out in other sections, at Azua, where the government navy found it necessary to destroy the customs house; along the line of the railway between Sanchez and Sammana bay, at Montechristo and La Romana in the southeast corner of the island. In this last neighborhood there are some extensive American-owned plantations, the agents for which appealed to the state department today looking for protection for their property from the unorganized bands of revolutionists.Los informes de hoy no mostraron mejoría en las condiciones de la isla. Durante muchos meses, el presidente Bordas, obligado a mantenerse en el cargo mediante una serie de decretos extraoficiales y elecciones parciales, ha estado dirigiendo en persona a sus tropas contra los generales rebeldes en el norte de la república sin poder vencerlos. Entretanto, han estallado revoluciones en otras secciones, en Azua, donde la marina del gobierno consideró necesario destruir la aduana; a lo largo de la línea del ferrocarril entre Sánchez y la bahía de Samaná, en Montecristi y en La Romana, en el extremo sureste de la isla. En esta última zona existen algunas extensas plantaciones de propiedad estadounidense, cuyos agentes apelaron hoy al Departamento de Estado en busca de protección para sus propiedades frente a las bandas desorganizadas de revolucionarios.

Official opinion here is slowly crystalizing into a conviction that only through powerful outside influence, and probably that of the United States, can these chaotic conditions be terminated and a stable government set up. The revolutionists appear to be unable to overcome the Bordas government, supported by the monthly proportion of customs receipts handed over by American collectors, but Bordas has not been able to diminish a number of his enemies, after a six months' campaign. Mr. Vick, the American collector of customs, has resigned and now Minister Sullivan is returning to the United States, leaving Secretary White in charge of the American legation, and without any definite assurance that he will ever resume his official duties.La opinión oficial aquí se está cristalizando lentamente en la convicción de que solo a través de una poderosa influencia exterior, y probablemente la de los Estados Unidos, se puede poner fin a estas condiciones caóticas y establecer un gobierno estable. Los revolucionarios parecen incapaces de derrotar al gobierno de Bordas, apoyado por la proporción mensual de los ingresos aduaneros entregada por los recaudadores estadounidenses, pero Bordas no ha logrado disminuir el número de sus enemigos tras una campaña de seis meses. El Sr. Vick, el recaudador de aduanas estadounidense, ha dimitido y ahora el ministro Sullivan regresa a los Estados Unidos, dejando al secretario White a cargo de la legación estadounidense, y sin ninguna seguridad definitiva de que vuelva a reasumir alguna vez sus funciones oficiales.

The state department is still reluctant to intervene forcibly in San Domingo at this time, however, and it is expected that an effort will be made to send some strong man to the republic who may secure the confidence of all elements and bring them together. Meanwhile the navy is maintaining a considerable fleet in Dominican waters, including the South Carolina at Puerto Plata, the Marietta at Sanchez and the Castine at Azua. Other vessels are on the Haitian end of the island in case of need.El Departamento de Estado sigue siendo reacio, no obstante, a intervenir por la fuerza en Santo Domingo en este momento, y se espera que se haga un esfuerzo para enviar a la república a algún hombre fuerte que pueda ganarse la confianza de todos los elementos y unirlos. Mientras tanto, la Armada mantiene una flota considerable en aguas dominicanas, incluidos el South Carolina en Puerto Plata, el Marietta en Sánchez y el Castine en Azua. Otros buques se encuentran en el extremo haitiano de la isla en caso de necesidad.
