22,000 Smallpox Cases in Dominican Republic

An epidemic of smallpox involving 22,000 cases is raging in Santiago, Moca and La Vega, Dominican Republic, according to a report last night to the national headquarters of the American Red Cross from S. A. Mitchell, field director of the organization in Santo Domingo. Last week there were 225 deaths from the disease. It was stated that United States marines stationed at Santo Domingo have not been affected by the epidemic.

Accompanying Mitchell's report was a request, concurred in by the military governor, for $3,000 for food, medicine and burial of the smallpox victims. The request was granted.

In addition, on orders from the Red Cross headquarters, disinfecting apparatus has been sent to the Dominican chapter of the Red Cross by the Haitian chapter at Port au Prince, and large quantities of vaccine has been supplied by the United States navy.
