Members of Diplomatic Corps Take Prominent Part in Social News Senor and Senora de Garland Being Feted at Farewell Parties; De Martins Entertain

Members of the diplomatic corps were prominent in the social news of yesterday. The departure in the near future of the former Counselor of the Peruvian Embassy and Senora de Garland have added zest to the parties. The Brazilian Ambassador and Senhora de Martins were hosts at a farewell dinner party last night for their guests, Comdr. and Senhora do Amaral Peixoto. The Peruvian Ambassador, Senor Manuel de Freyre y Santander, who is dean of the corps, also were hosts last night. They gave a dinner party for Senor Garland, who is the newly appointed Peruvian Minister to the Dominican Republic, and Senora de Garland.

Dinner Party Takes Place At Peruvian Embassy.

The Ambassador, Senor de Freyre y Santander, is remaining in the house on Scott Circle which has served as the Embassy since he came as Ambassador. Guests at the dinner included the Bolivian Minister and Senora de Guachalla, who will be hosts at a cocktail party this afternoon for the Garlands; Senora de Pastoriza, wife of the Minister of the Dominican Republic, who now is in Trujillo City; the First Secretary of the Spanish Embassy, Senor Don Louis de Silva; the Marquis of Zahara, Senora Teresa de Olavegoya of Peru, and from the Peruvian Embassy staff the new Counselor, Senor Juan Elguera, who succeeded Senor Garland, and Senora de Elguera; the Naval Attache and Senora de Labarthe, the Air Attache and Senora de Revoredo, the Military Attache and Senora de Sarmiento, the Commercial Counselor and Senor de Chavez and the Second Secretary and Senor de Mackehenie y de la Fuente.

Other Parties Planned For the De Garlands.

Senora de Gazitua, wife of the Counselor of the Chilean Embassy, was hostess at a bridge tea yesterday afternoon for Senora de Garland. Senor and Senora de Elguera will give a dinner party for them tomorrow evening. The Guatemalan Minister and Senora de Recinos and Senora de Pastoriza will give parties for Senor and Senora de Garland Friday evening.

Guests at De Martins' Dinner for Visitors.

The Ambassador and Senhora de Martins' guests at the party for Comdr. and Senhora do Amaral Peixoto included the Ambassador of Cuba and Senora de Conchesco, the Secretary of the Treasury and Mrs. Henry Morgenthau, jr.; Mr. Justice and Mrs. Felix Frankfurter, Senator and Mrs. Millard E. Tydings, Senator and Mrs. Arthur H. Vandenberg, Senator and Mrs. Warren R. Austin, Representative Edith Nourse Rogers, Representative and Mrs. Foster Stearns, the Assistant Secretary of State and Mrs. Breckinridge Long, Mr. and Mrs. Herbert Feis, Mr. and Mrs. Ernest G. Draper, Comdr. and Mrs. Lee of New York, Miss Kay Lee, the Minister Counselor of the Embassy and Senhora de Kondor, the First Secretary and Senhora de Alencastro-Guimaraes, the Naval Attache and Senhora do Valle, Mrs. Nathan Wyeth, Mr. Joseph Alsop, Mr. Berent Freele and Senhor Luis Sam Paio.

La Follettes Are Hosts At Buffet Supper.

Senator and Mrs. Robert M. La Follette, jr., gave a buffet supper last night following the opening of the sixth subscription play of the Theater Guild and the American Theater Society at the National. The play is "Hope for a Harvest," which already has been a successful presentation in Boston. Senator and Mrs. La Follette had guests with them for the play, going later to their home for the supper. The party was in honor of Mr. and Mrs. Frederic March, who are stars in the play. Among the other guests were a number from out of town who came for the opening of the play.

Mrs. Henry A. Wallace, wife of the Vice President, will be among those attending the performance this evening. Others include Mrs. Hamilton Fish, jr.; Maj. Gen. C. M. Wesson, Mrs. Douglas P. Birnie, Lady Julia Reckitt, Col. Sidney Mashbir, Col. John Green Barr, Col. Arthur D. Elliott and Dr. and Mrs. Dexter Bullard.

The Secretary of Agriculture and Mrs. Claude R. Wickard have made reservations for Friday evening as have the Major General Commandant of the Marine Corps and Mrs. Thomas Holcomb.

McNutts and Mr. Thibault Are Honored at Party.

Mr. and Mrs. A. Burks Summers also were hosts a buffet supper last night. The party followed the opening session of the American Red Cross convention. Their guests of honor were the Federal Security Administrator and Mrs. Paul V. McNutt, who shared honors with Mr. Conrad Thibault, who earlier sang at the convention.

The Summers' house at 2800 Brandywine street was gay with early spring blossoms and the buffet table had an unusual setting. The cloth was black with a pattern of silver cellophane woven in the center. A low silver vase was filled with white lilies, gladioluses and snapdragons. The supper was served on white china and silver dishes.

Other guests at the supper were Dr. and Mrs. Stanley K. Hornbeck, Brig. Gen. Albert Cox, Col. and Mrs. Harrison Brand, Mr. and Mrs. Marshall Diggs, Mr. and Mrs. Emil Hurja, Mr. and Mrs. Walker Buel, Mr. and Mrs. Edwin W. Gableman, Mr. and Mrs. James Moran, Mr. and Mrs. F. G. Awalt, Mr. and Mrs. Wilmer Waller, Dr. and Mrs. William Calhoun Sterling, Dr. and Mrs. William B. Sims, Mr. and Mrs. Hilleary Hoskinson and Mrs. Irene Caldwell.
