The Dominican Dictator Rumored En Route Here

By EVELYN PEYTON GORDON

Some weeks ago a popular New York shop received an order for smart luggage amounting to $7500. Even the New York shop was staggered! Today, with the luggage, will arrive in New York Senora de Trujillo, wife of the "strong man" of Santa Domingo. La Senora and her "suite," which includes her young son, Rafael, her 75-year-old mother, her stepdaughter, Senora Flor Trujillo de Brea, and her brother, Senor Francisco Martinez Alba, are aboard the liner Santa Paula.

Now all that is interesting enough. But what makes the event more interesting is the fact that there are rumors abroad that Gen. Trujillo himself, is on the Santa Paula with the party. Nobody, not even the Grace Line, has been able to confirm or deny the presence of the dictator of the Dominican Republic on that ship. The party, traveling incognito, embarked at Cape Haitien, and not in their own country.

But, just to cinch things if the General is along, Andres Pastoriza, Dominican Minister to Washington, and several consular officials from New York, will hustle out to Quarantine early this morning. Then, whether Gen. Trujillo is aboard or not, the fireworks of entertaining will start. Mayor LaGuardia will be on hand to welcome the party, and thence they will go to the Waldorf, where an entire floor of the Towers has been reserved for them.

EN ROUTE TO GERMANY?

The reason there is so much importance attached to this arrival, is because the word goes forth that Gen. Trujillo and his family are en route to Germany. Some time ago, the Dominican Legation went to great pains to announce that Gen. Trujillo "was not going to visit Hitler." In denying the report that the generalissimo would not pay a visit to Der Fuehrer, there was no denial that he was not going to Germany!

And why shouldn't he go to see Hitler? No reason except perhaps it would be better if the Reichschancellor didn't wind up a deal on that same Samana Bay of which we spoke the other day. President Roosevelt didn't do any negotiating for the use of Samana Bay as a marine base when he was in southern waters. At least we haven't heard that he did. But Herr Hitler has long thought Samana Bay would be an excellent submarine base for himself!

LENGTHY VISIT HERE?

Last autumn, when Gen. Trujillo finished his term as President of the Dominican Republic, it was reported that he was coming to this country for a tour. No more has been heard of that visit, and to date no immediate plans have been made for it. Perhaps on his return from Europe he will see fit to pay us a lengthier visit.

With Senora de Trujillo's party there are also Dr. Jose Roman, private doctor; Joaquin Montero Monteagudo and Juan Antonio Perrota, listed, without rank, as "officers of the Dominican Army"; Emilia Tapia, maid; Felipa Nunez, maid, and J. M. Bonetti Burgos, secretary to Gen. Trujillo. Note those military aides, the private physician and Senor Bonetti make us very sure that the General himself is on that boat. Today will tell.

Meantime plans have been hurriedly, but carefully, made for the entertainment of the ladies of the party anyhow. Secret Service men and a police detail have been placed at their disposal during the stay; a motorcycle escort will precede them to the hotel; the 12-room suite with servants' quarters has been filled with flowers; arrangements have been made for Rafael—he's about 12 years old—to be made an honorary captain in the famous Knickerbocker Greys (that group to which all aristocratic little New York boys aspire to belong).

ROUND OF PARTIES

This evening, Mr. and Mrs. Reginald Lanier, Mr. and Mrs. James Lenox Banks, Virginia French (sister of young Mrs. John Jacob Astor), and other members of society will give a reception for the visitors. Later this week, Miss French will give a large luncheon for the General's daughter, Senora de Brea. Tomorrow Senora de Brea will be entertained by Mrs. Lanier and members of the New York Junior League, following a visit to Girl Scout headquarters, where she will be greeted by Mrs. Lyman Delano. International Girl Scout chairman. Senora de Brea is head of the organization in her own country.

Now, this visit isn't supposedly an official visit. Some years ago, another Senora de Trujillo, first wife of the General, officially visited the Capital and was entertained at the White House as the wife of a foreign president. But aside from the fact that it is not announced as such, the entire visit seems to have all the earmarks of a very official mission. We'll see!
