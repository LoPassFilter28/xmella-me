The Republic · February 18, 185118 de febrero de 1851

The barque Lysander, at Holmes's Hole, brings dates from Cape Haytien to the 24th of January inclusive. The country was tranquil. Coffee scarce.El país estaba tranquilo. Café escaso.

The Lysander brings a new ordinance, published at the Cape, in the name of the Emperor, on the 22d of January, relative to important amendments of the law of 1838, on customs.El Lysander trae una nueva ordenanza, publicada en el Cabo, en nombre del Emperador, el 22 de enero, relativa a importantes enmiendas de la ley de 1838, sobre aduanas.

The fixed duties on customs shall be paid in Spanish money, at the rate of five per cent. on the gross proceeds on sales, without prejudice to the manifest, measuring and weighing. If the manifest is produced without the certificate of the Haytien consular agent at the port of departure, the master of the vessel shall pay one Spanish dollar per ton additional. Copies of every manifest, for verification, must be sent to the Haytien consular agent at the port of departure.
