Evening Star · August 18, 193618 de agosto de 1936

Original in English.Original en inglés.

Japanese Naval Officers Will Be Honor Guests of Secretary of the Navy Festivities Arranged For Visitors From the Land of the Rising Sun Italian Embassy Staff Will Entertain at Farewell Dinner Party for Ambassador Augusto Rosso.

THE Secretary of the Navy, Mr. Claude A. Swanson, will entertain at a luncheon tomorrow at the Mayflower Hotel in honor of Vice Admiral Zengo Yoshida, commander of the Japanese training squadron. The secretary also will be host Thursday afternoon at a reception in honor of Vice Admiral Yoshida and the officers and midshipmen of the squadron. The secretary, who is spending some time at the Rapidan camp, is not expected to make the journey back to Washington, but will have a high ranking officer of the Navy Department act as host in his place.

The naval attache of the Japanese Embassy, Capt. Tamon Yamaguchi, has issued invitations to a reception tomorrow afternoon from 5:30 to 7 o'clock at the Mayflower Hotel in honor of the commander and officers of the Japanese training squadron. He will also give a private dinner tonight for the vice admiral and his party and will entertain at dinner tomorrow night in honor of the visiting admiral.

The training squadron arrived in Baltimore this morning and was greeted by the assistant naval attache of the Japanese Embassy, Comdr. Kanzo Miura, who went to Baltimore last night. The mayor of Baltimore, Mr. Howard Jackson, entertained in honor of Vice Admiral Yoshida at luncheon at the Southern Hotel today. Later in the afternoon Vice Admiral Yoshida and his staff and captains of the Iwate and Yakumo will leave Baltimore for Washington.

The Japanese Ambassador, Mr. Hirosi Saito, will entertain at dinner Thursday evening in honor of Vice Admiral Yoshida at the embassy. Friday evening the commander of the training squadron will be host at dinner aboard the Iwate at Baltimore and will be at home Saturday afternoon on board the Iwate.

The Royal Italian Ambassador, Signor Augusto Rosso, will be the guest in whose honor the members of the embassy staff will entertain at a farewell dinner this evening at the Mayflower Hotel. The Ambassador will sail Saturday from New York to assume his new duties as Italian Ambassador to Moscow.

The Dominican Minister and Mme. de Pastoriza entertained Sunday at their cottage in Sherwood Forest, where they are spending the Summer, to celebrate the anniversary of the independence of the Dominican Republic and also anniversary of President Trujillo's coming into office as President of the Dominican Republic.

Mrs. George V. Allen, wife of the United States Consul at Patras, Greece, with her infant son, George V. Allen, jr., will arrive in New York today on the Vulcania. Mrs. Allen will be met by her mother, Mrs. John T. Martin, and they will come to Washington, where Mrs. Allen and her son will visit Mrs. Martin for a month. Mrs. Martin returned to this country recently after visiting Mr. and Mrs. Allen in Patras.

Mrs. Allen will join Mr. Allen at his new post at Cairo, Egypt, where he has been transferred, leaving this country the latter part of September.

The second secretary of the British Embassy, Mr. J. M. K. Vyvyan, returned from England Friday on the Britannic after a visit in Great Britain.

Lieut. Rodney L. Youngkin, U. S. N., with Mrs. Youngkin and their three daughters, have returned to their home at 5818 Fourth street northwest after a fortnight's stay at Newport. Before returning they spent a short time at their cottage at Beverly Beach.
