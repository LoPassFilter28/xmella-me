Dominican Fete At Embassy

The Dominican Ambassador and Senora de Garcia Godoy were hosts yesterday afternoon, entertaining in honor of Maj. Gen. F. E. Watson, U. S. M. C., and Mrs. Watson. The hosts and their honor guests first met in Ciudad Trujillo when Gen. Watson was on duty there, and have renewed acquaintance several times in Washington and in the Dominican Republic. Gen. Watson recently came for station at Marine headquarters, after serving in the Pacific theater of the war, and the Ambassador is rather recently back from his native land. Guests yesterday included Latin American members of the corps and many fellow officers of Gen. Watson, not a few of whom have had service in the Dominican Republic.

The weather being perfect, tables were laid on the flagstone terrace and in the charming garden of the Embassy on Sixteenth street and were laden with tea-time delicacies as well as more substantial dishes, such as baked hams and roast fowl. Cooling liquids were dispensed on the terrace and in the garden. Small tables about the lawn were meeting places for groups of the guests who lingered through the early evening to talk and enjoy the fragrance of freshly cut grass and blossoms in the flower beds.
