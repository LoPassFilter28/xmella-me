A diplomatic affairs column reports that a new weekly bulletin from the Dominican embassy announces the upcoming celebration of Ciudad Trujillo's 450th anniversary next Sunday. The writer describes personal visits to the capital founded by the brother of Columbus in 1496, recalling its cathedral containing Christopher Columbus's bones and the 15th Century castle where his brother resided when Santo Domingo was among the earliest permanent Western settlements. Trujillo Sounds Trumpet for TrujilloTrujillo hace sonar la trompeta por Trujillo

By EVELYN PEYTON GORDONPor EVELYN PEYTON GORDON

PRESIDENT TRUJILLO of the Dominican Republic, who used to be heralded in the streets of Trujillo City as "El Benefactore," has recently been kicking up his heels about the "unfair public attitude" towards his little island republic. His complaints were registered in the form of a neatly printed pamphlet, pointing out, to all those who would read it, the injustices planted in the public mind to convince people that the Caribbean country wasn't in the right.El PRESIDENTE TRUJILLO de la República Dominicana, a quien solían aclamar en las calles de Ciudad Trujillo como «El Benefactor», ha estado protestando recientemente por la «actitud pública injusta» hacia su pequeña república insular. Sus quejas quedaron registradas en un folleto pulcramente impreso que señalaba, a todos los que quisieran leerlo, las injusticias sembradas en la opinión pública para convencer a la gente de que el país caribeño no tenía la razón.

Well, all those things resolve to a matter of opinion, but I was curious yesterday when another pamphlet arrived. This time I discovered no complaints, but a constructive effort to spread correct information about Trujillo City and its inhabitants. In other words, following the pattern set by the Chilean Embassy in Washington, the Dominican Republic has inaugurated a bulletin of the embassy.Bueno, todas esas cosas se reducen a una cuestión de opinión, pero ayer sentí curiosidad cuando llegó otro folleto. Esta vez no descubrí quejas, sino un esfuerzo constructivo por difundir información verídica sobre Ciudad Trujillo y sus habitantes. En otras palabras, siguiendo la pauta establecida por la Embajada de Chile en Washington, la República Dominicana ha inaugurado un boletín de la embajada.

Most interesting item included in the new weekly publication indicates that next Sunday Ciudad Trujillo will celebrate its 450th anniversary. The bright little capital was founded by the brother of Columbus in 1496 and I have stood in the ancient and beautiful little cathedral where they tell me the bones of Christopher Columbus himself are laid. I've seen the 15th Century castle where his brother resided in those early days when Santo Domingo was among the first permanent settlements of Western civilization.El artículo más interesante incluido en la nueva publicación semanal indica que el próximo domingo Ciudad Trujillo celebrará su 450.º aniversario. La pequeña y luminosa capital fue fundada por el hermano de Colón en 1496 y yo he estado en la pequeña, antigua y hermosa catedral donde me dicen que reposan los restos del propio Cristóbal Colón. He visto el castillo del siglo XV (15) donde residió su hermano en aquellos primeros tiempos en que Santo Domingo figuraba entre los primeros asentamientos permanentes de la civilización occidental.

Some of our most enterprising officials have gone to a good deal of trouble to black the eye of Rafael Trujillo on the grounds that he is a dictator. Politically I know not too much about the gentleman. But he claims to have worked great material good in his country and is set on making the Dominican Republic another Caribbean playground for weary winter travellers. And it's not a bad place.Algunos de nuestros funcionarios más emprendedores se han tomado muchas molestias para desprestigiar a Rafael Trujillo con el argumento de que es un dictador. En lo político no sé demasiado sobre el caballero. Pero él afirma haber obrado un gran bien material en su país y está decidido a convertir a la República Dominicana en otro lugar de recreo caribeño para los agotados viajeros de invierno. Y no es un mal lugar.
