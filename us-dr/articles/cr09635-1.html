Dominican Envoy Entertains Party Leaving for Republic

The Minister of the Dominican Republic and Senora de Pastoriza were hosts last evening at a buffet supper party, entertaining in compliment to members of Congress who left before dawn this morning for the Dominican Republic. The party is headed by Senator Theodore F. Green and Representative Hamilton Fish, members of the Foreign Relations and Foreign Affairs Committees of the Senate and House and the group of 16 is en route to Ciudad Trujillo, Dominican Republic, to inspect the site granted by the Dominican Republic for the proposed memorial to Christopher Columbus, which will symbolize the peace and unity of the 21 American nations. The party is making the trip by train to Miami, where a chartered clipper will take them by air to Ciudad Trujillo.

Other guests at the buffet supper last evening were Representative Matthew J. Merritt, Mrs. Hamilton Fish and Mr. Hamilton Fish, jr., Mr. Richard Chatfield-Taylor, son of the Assistant Secretary of the Treasury and Mrs. Wayne Chatfield-Taylor; the director general of the Pan-American Union, Dr. Leo S.
