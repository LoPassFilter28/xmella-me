New-York Tribune. New York [N.Y.], December 3, 1873. Page 6. View the originalVer el original

Buenaventura Baez writes to President Grant appealing for the Dominican people, saying the agreement with the Samana Bay Company has been followed by silence and indifference and that they cannot alone support the consequences of combined hatred against them. He solicits the establishment of an efficient United States protectorate to preserve them from unwarranted attacks aimed at their very existence.Buenaventura Báez escribe al presidente Grant apelando por el pueblo dominicano, diciendo que el acuerdo con la Compañía de la Bahía de Samaná ha sido seguido de silencio e indiferencia y que no pueden soportar solos las consecuencias del odio combinado contra ellos. Solicita el establecimiento de un protectorado eficaz de los Estados Unidos que los preserve de ataques injustificados dirigidos contra su propia existencia.
