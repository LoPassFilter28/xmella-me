Eight members of a committee supporting the Columbus memorial lighthouse gathered in Washington yesterday to receive insignia featuring gold with white and garnet enamel around a plaque of the navigator. Among the recipients was T. R. Armstrong, vice president of Standard Oil of New Jersey, who had met Generalissimo Rafael Trujillo the preceding summer, alongside prominent educators, scholars, and former diplomats. Newly appointed United States Minister to the Dominican Republic Robert Scotten and his wife attended the ceremony and displayed keen interest in the lighthouse project prior to their scheduled departure for the post on June 13.Ocho miembros de un comité promotor del faro en memoria de Cristóbal Colón se congregaron ayer en Washington para recibir una distinción de oro con esmalte blanco y granate alrededor de una placa conmemorativa del navegante. Entre los condecorados figuró T. R. Armstrong, vicepresidente de la Standard Oil of New Jersey, quien se había reunido con el generalísimo Rafael Trujillo el verano anterior, junto con destacados educadores, académicos y exdiplomáticos. El recién designado ministro de los Estados Unidos en la República Dominicana, Robert Scotten, y su esposa asistieron a la ceremonia y mostraron gran entusiasmo por el proyecto del faro antes de partir hacia su puesto el 13 de junio.

HOMAGE TO COLUMBUSHOMENAJE A COLÓN

In the very center of the Antilles, where routes of sky and seas cross, a lighthouse will soon be erected; a monument to the discoverer of this hemisphere, Christopher Columbus; a beacon to ships of sea and sky.En el mismísimo centro de las Antillas, donde se cruzan las rutas del cielo y de los mares, pronto se erigirá un faro; un monumento al descubridor de este hemisferio, Cristóbal Colón; una señal para las naves del mar y del cielo.

On a promentory outside the ancient capital of the Dominican Republic—Santo Domingo, as Columbus called it—the vast memorial with its practical value will soon be started. Yesterday, to commemorate the initial organization of this structure, the Dominican Minister and Mme. Pastoriza entertained at a small reception.En un promontorio en las afueras de la antigua capital de la República Dominicana —Santo Domingo, como la llamó Colón—, pronto se iniciará este vasto monumento conmemorativo con su valor práctico. Ayer, para conmemorar la organización inicial de esta estructura, el Ministro dominicano y la Sra. Pastoriza ofrecieron una pequeña recepción.

And on the members of the national committee for the Columbus Lighthouse and on the Director General of the Pan American Union, Dr. Leo S. Rowe, Mr. Pastoriza conferred the Heraldic Order of Cristobal Colon; a handsome decoration of gold with white and garnet enamel surrounding a plaque of the great navigator.Y a los miembros del comité nacional para el Faro de Colón y al Director General de la Unión Panamericana, el Dr. Leo S. Rowe, el Sr. Pastoriza les confirió la Orden Heráldica de Cristóbal Colón; una hermosa condecoración de oro con esmalte blanco y granate que rodea una placa del gran navegante.

That committee is large. It is composed of ditsinguished members. Yesterday eight of them were able to come to Washington to receive the honor. T. R. Armstrong, vice president of the Standard Oil of New Jersey, who had met Generalissimo Rafael Trujillo last summer; Robert Woods Bliss, former United States envoy to Argentina; Dr. Edwin Borchard, professor of law at Yale University, who testified so convincingly in the Senate hearing on "court packing" some years back; Dr. Ada Comstock, president of Radcliffe College—"I can't wait for commencement exercises to don my medallion"—Dr. Joseph Grace, Dr. John C. Merriam, Dr. James Brown Scott, secretary of the Carnegie Endowment for International Peace; Dr. Mary Wilhelmine Williams, of Goucher College, and Dr. Rowe.Dicho comité es amplio. Está compuesto por distinguidos miembros. Ayer, ocho de ellos pudieron acudir a Washington para recibir el honor. T. R. Armstrong, vicepresidente de la Standard Oil of New Jersey, quien se había reunido con el Generalísimo Rafael Trujillo el verano pasado; Robert Woods Bliss, exenviado de los Estados Unidos a la Argentina; el Dr. Edwin Borchard, profesor de derecho en la Universidad de Yale, quien testificó de manera tan convincente en la audiencia del Senado sobre la «reforma judicial» hace algunos años; la Dra. Mr. and Mrs. Robert Scotten were also present and much interested in the idea of the lighthouse. Mr. Scotten has recently been appointed United States Minister to the Dominican Republic, and with his wife will sail June 13, for the post.
