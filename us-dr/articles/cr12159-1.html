H. G. KNOWLES PROMOTED President Taft Names Him Minister to Bolivia

President Taft Tuesday announced the appointment of Horace G. Knowles, of Wilmington, to be envoy extraordinary and now a minister to the Dominican Republics, an advancement for Mr. Knowles, who is now minister to the Dominican Republics, and is stationed in San Domingo. Prior to that time he had been minister to Roumania and Servia. On his return from the European post he was appointed by the President to be minister to San Domingo and the allied Republics. He went there early this year and has handled an entangling situation there and straightened out some friction that threatened with the United States. Mr. Knowles' advancement to be minister to Bolivia, in South America, will be gratifying to his friends. It is expected that he will return to Wilmington for a vacation before taking up his South American diplomatic post. Bolivia has a population of 2,000,000 exclusive of the wild Indian population which lives in the mountains and on the plains.
