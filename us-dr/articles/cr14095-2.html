Christ To Host Speaker

The Reverend Neale Morgan, Vicar of the Church of the Epiphany in Santo Domingo, capital of the Dominican Republic, will be the guest of Christ Church Parish from Saturday, January 19, to Tuesday, January 22. He will then attend the sessions of the 178th Annual Convention of the Diocese of Delaware, to be held in Christ Church, Dover, on Tuesday and Wednesday, January 22 and 23, where he will be the keynote speaker. During his visit with the members of Christ Church. Mr. Morgan will speak at the regular 10 a.m. Worship Service on Sunday, January 20. His subject will concern his work in the Dominican Republic.

The Reverend Mr. Morgan was born on July 2, 1928 in Buffalo, the son of Dwight and Josephine Morgan. His high school days were spent in Kittaning, Pa., his college days at Washington and Jefferson; and his seminary days at Berkeley Divinity School in Connecticut. He was ordained to the priesthood by Bishop W. S. Thomas in December of 1953. His charges have included St. James, Penn Hills, Pa., where he was Priest-in-charge from 1953-57; Christ Church, Georgetown, Washington, D.C., as the Assistant Rector from 1957-60; and his present duty in Santo Domingo since 1960. He was married on November 29, 1958, to Mary Ann Neff. They have one child.

The sessions of the Annual Convention of the Diocese of Delaware begin at 3 p.m. with registration at the Parish House of Christ Church, Dover. This is followed by the First Session at 4:00 in the Parish House. The first day's activities terminate with the Convention Banquet and Bishop's Annual Address to be held at 7 in the Caesar Rodney Hotel.

Wednesday's program begins with Corporate Communion at 7:30 in Christ Church. The second General Session of the Convention starts at 10:30 in the Parish House. Luncheon follows at 1:15 p.m. in the Treadway Inn. The Convention's Final session will be initiated at 2:45 in the Parish House. Adjournment will take place at 5 p.m.

Representing the Parish of Christ Church, Milford, at this important gathering will be the Reverend E. John Dyer, Dr. John Baker, Mr. Robert Fischer, Mr. James Cresson, and Mr. Remsen Barnard.
