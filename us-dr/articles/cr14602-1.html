Acting Chief of Staff Brigadier General George Marshall hosted a dinner at the Mayflower Hotel honoring General Rafael Trujillo, Chief of Staff of the Dominican Army and former President of the Dominican Republic. Guests included Dominican Minister Andres Pastoriza, United States Minister to the Dominican Republic Henry Norweb, Lieutenant Charles McLaughlin, Dr. Francisco Benzo, and Mario Bonetti Burgos, along with numerous high-ranking American military officers.El jefe de Estado Mayor interino, general de brigada George Marshall, ofreció una cena en el hotel Mayflower en honor del general Rafael Trujillo, jefe de Estado Mayor del ejército dominicano y ex presidente de la República Dominicana.
