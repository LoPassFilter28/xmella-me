Trujillo's Crew Loses No Time In Covering Up "Zsa Zsa Slept Here"

SAN PEDRO, Calif., (AP) — People were pointing at Gen. Rafael Trujillo's yacht, the Angelita, and laughing. Crewman couldn't figure out why.

When a boatload of news photographers chugged up and began photographing the gleaming white yacht from the harbor side, a crewman leaned way out over the rail.

There, painted near the waterline—where the deck overhang hid it from above—were these words:

"Zsa Zsa slept here."

In a burst of energy not previously displayed by the yacht's 80-man crew, painters soon covered the words. Harbor officials said the sign was apparently applied from a small boat, probably with a spray can, during the night.

Zsa Zsa Gabor was the actress who introduced the son of the Dominican Republic's strongman to Hollywood society.
