A diplomatic dispatch in The Washington Times announces that Dominican Minister Andres Pastoriza has reached New York with his wife and small daughter en route to Washington. The family plans to spend a week or 10 days in New York before traveling to the capital. Meanwhile, the retiring minister, Senor Don Rafael Brache, and his family expect to sail from New York at the end of the week for their home in Santo Domingo, where they will stay before proceeding to a new diplomatic assignment that has not yet been designated.Un despacho diplomático en The Washington Times anuncia que el ministro dominicano Andrés Pastoriza ha llegado a Nueva York con su esposa y su pequeña hija en camino a Washington. La familia planea pasar una semana o 10 días en Nueva York antes de viajar a la capital. Mientras tanto, el ministro saliente, el señor Don Rafael Brache, y su familia tienen previsto zarpar de Nueva York al final de la semana hacia su hogar en Santo Domingo, donde permanecerán antes de partir hacia un nuevo destino diplomático que aún no ha sido designado.

They will remain there for a week or 10 days before coming to Washington.El recién nombrado ministro de la República Dominicana, el señor Andrés Pastoriza, junto con su esposa y su pequeña hija, ha llegado a Nueva York. The retiring minister, Senor Don Rafael Brache, and his family expect to sail the end of the week from New York for their home in Santo Domingo. They will spend some time there before going to their new station, which has not as yet been named.El ministro saliente, el señor don Rafael Brache, y su familia esperan zarpar a finales de semana desde Nueva York rumbo a su hogar en Santo Domingo.
