Evening Star · May 2, 19332 de mayo de 1933

Original in English.Original en inglés.

The director general of the Pan American Union, Dr. L. S. Rowe, entertained at luncheon today at the Pan-American annex, in honor of the minister of foreign affairs of the Dominican Republic, Senor Dr. Maximiliano Henriquez Urena. In addition to the guest of honor, those in attendance were: The Minister of the Dominican Republic, Senor Don Roberto Despradel; the Assistant Secretary of Commerce, Mr. John Dickinson; the counselor of the Dominican legation, Senor Don Ulises F. Espaillat; the first secretary of the legation, Senor Don Agustin Acevedo Feliu; Dr. Esteban Gil Borges, assistant director of the Pan American Union; Right Rev. Msgr. James H. Ryan, rector of the Catholic University of America; Rev. Dr. W. Coleman Nevils, S. J., president of Georgetown University; Dr. Cloyd Heck Marvin, president of George Washington University; Dr. James Brown Scott, secretary of the Carnegie Endowment for International Peace; Mr. Orme Wilson and Mr. Joseph F. McGurk of the Latin American division of the Department of State; Mr. George A. Finch, assistant secretary of the Carnegie Endowment for International Peace; Mr. Lester H. Woolsey, legal adviser to the Pan American Union; Mr. William A. Reid, foreign trade adviser, and Dr. William Manger, chief of the division of criminal information of the Pan American Union.
