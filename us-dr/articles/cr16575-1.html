An executive notice in The Free Lance reports that President Roosevelt has appointed William H. Gale of Virginia to serve as consul at Puerto Plata in the Dominican Republic. The designation places a Virginian at the head of the United States consular post in that Dominican port.Un aviso del poder ejecutivo en The Free Lance informa que el presidente Roosevelt designó a William H.
