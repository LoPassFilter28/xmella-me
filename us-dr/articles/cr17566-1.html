In order to make certain the re-establishment of communication with the arrival of the Des Moines, the Treasury Department was asked to dispatch the revenue cutter Algonquin from San Juan, Puerto Rico, to Mona Passage, a point on the coast midway between Puerto Plata and Santo Domingo City. REVOLT IN SAN DOMINGO. DOMINICAN GUNBOAT SHELL PUERTO PLATA. United States Sends Cruiser to Scene to Prevent Danger to American Lives and Property.REVOLUCIÓN EN SANTO DOMINGO. CAÑONERO DOMINICANO BOMBARDEA PUERTO PLATA. Estados Unidos envía crucero al lugar para evitar peligros a vidas y propiedades estadounidenses.

Washington, Sept. 8.—News that Dominion gunboats were shelling the city of Puerta Plata, endangering American lives and property, caused hurry orders to go from here today to the cruiser Des Moines, which had been preparing to sail from Guantanamo, Cuba, for Santo Domingo since reports of the latest revolution there were received several days ago. The cruiser was coaling today and was expected to sail tonight directly for Puerto Plata.Washington, 8 de septiembre.—Las noticias de que cañoneros dominicanos estaban bombardeando la ciudad de Puerto Plata, poniendo en peligro vidas y propiedades estadounidenses, provocaron que hoy salieran de aquí órdenes urgentes para el crucero Des Moines, que se había estado preparando para zarpar de Guantánamo, Cuba, con destino a Santo Domingo desde que se recibieron los informes de la última revolución allí hace varios días. El crucero estaba cargando carbón hoy y se esperaba que zarpara esta noche directamente hacia Puerto Plata.

In the meantime communication between Puerto Plata and the outside world has been broken and no word has come to the state department since the brief message early today announcing the bombardment. The cutter will reach there tomorrow and will serve as a wireless relay station between the Des Moines and the commercial station at Santo Domingo.Mientras tanto, la comunicación entre Puerto Plata y el mundo exterior ha quedado interrumpida y no ha llegado ninguna noticia al departamento de Estado desde el breve mensaje de hoy temprano que anunciaba el bombardeo. El guardacostas llegará allí mañana y servirá como estación de retransmisión inalámbrica entre el Des Moines y la estación comercial de Santo Domingo.

Long conferences were held today by President Wilson, Secretary Bryan and Assistant Secretary Osborne with James M. Sullivan, the newly appointed American minister to the Dominican republic.
