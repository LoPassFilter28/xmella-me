General Juan Isidro Jiménez, the former Dominican presidentEl general Juan Isidro Jiménez, expresidente dominicano

The Kansas City Sun. Kansas City, Mo., November 11, 1916. Page 6. View the originalVer el original

General Juan Isidro Jiménez, the former Dominican president, has traveled to the mountains near Wernersville, Pennsylvania, seeking quiet for his nerves, a feature in The Kansas City Sun reports. Jiménez had served intermittently for five years until his resignation on May 8 under pressure from the Dominican Congress. The piece reviews his turbulent career commanding forces, defying lawmakers, assuming office following an assassination, and resisting European demands. Seeking medical tranquility, the veteran general recently arrived by motor car accompanied by his wife and youthful daughter.El general Juan Isidro Jiménez, expresidente dominicano, viajó a las montañas cercanas a Wernersville, Pensilvania, en busca de tranquilidad para sus nervios, según una crónica en The Kansas City Sun. Jiménez había gobernado intermitentemente durante cinco años hasta su renuncia el 8 de mayo por presiones del Congreso dominicano. El artículo repasa su turbulenta trayectoria al mando de tropas, desafiando a legisladores, asumiendo el mando tras un magnicidio y resistiendo exigencias europeas. En busca de reposo médico, el veterano general llegó en automóvil acompañado por su esposa y su joven hija.
