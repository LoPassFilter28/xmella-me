Dr. Rodriguez Host at Merry Cuban Supper *** Bar Association of Havana Feted At Party by Charge d'Affaires Given at Embassy

By Carolyn Bell

"Visiting Firemen" are constantly turning up in this village of ours—like the proverbial bad penny. Unlike the penny, however, they are given the key to the city and told to "go to town"!

At 6:30 last evening the enormous Cuban Embassy on "Embassy Row" was ablaze with lights, for the Charge d'Affaires, Dr. Valdes Rodriguez, was host at a buffet supper in honor of the members of the Bar Association of Havana and their wives, who are here on a sightseeing tour.

For the moment, they shed their legal air to chatter delightfully and enjoy the lavish buffet set up in the dining hall. The embassy was in "party dress" for the occasion—gold and flame dahlias stood in huge urns, as did peach gladioli.

Senora de Carabia Assists

Senora de Carabia, sister of the Ambassador, Dr. Pedro Martinez Fraga, who is in Havana, received with Dr. Rodriguez. Wearing a pebble crepe plum frock, Senora Carabia made a pretty picture. Also receiving with her was her daughter, Senorita Ana Maria Carabia, who is destined to break many a heart this season! The senorita, who has blond hair and big, blue eyes, was gowned in a black frock shirred around the waist and held by brilliant clips. She also wore rhinestones in a cluster of curls at the top of her head.

Pink roses centered the banquet hall in the bright red-carpeted dining room. The table was laden with luscious lobster and vegetable salads, sliced pork and the inevitable arroz y frijoles—rice and beans to you!

MacCrackens Guests

Headed by the Associate Judge of the District Court Bolitha Laws, just about every legal "light" in town attended the party. There were Mr. and Mrs. William P. MacCracken—"Chic" looking chic as usual; Mr. and Mrs. Walter Bastian, he was a former president of the association; Mr. and Mrs. Harry I. Quinn, Mr. and Mrs. Wilbur Gray and Mr. and Mrs. Godfrey L. Munter.

The Mexican Ambassador—still one of the merriest of the diplomats despite the current "difficulties"—was enjoying himself enormously. The Minister of the Dominican Republic and Senora de Pastoriza were there. With a tricky tricorne perched mischievously on her silver locks, Mme. Pastoriza discussed her recent trip to Santo Domingo. Not far away were their two handsome sons, Andres and Tomas.

Sugar Baron Attends

Dr. Oscar Albertini—the Cuban sugar baron—was looking as suave and elegant as usual. Three pretty young things down from New York, where they are attending school, were the Senoritas Albertina O'Farrell (Cuban—imagine with a name like that!), Miguelina de la Campa and Graciela de la Campa, who are visiting Senor and Senora Rafael Nieto.

The Cuban Bar Association has spent a busy day today visiting the Secretary of State's office, the Department of Justice, the Supreme Court and the United States District Court.
