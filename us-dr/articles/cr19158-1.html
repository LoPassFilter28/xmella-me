Dr. Logrono, counselor at the Dominican Legation, has departedEl Dr. Logroño, consejero de la legación dominicana, ha partido

Evening Star. Washington, D.C., June 27, 1939. Page 2. View the originalVer el original

Dr. Logrono, counselor at the Dominican Legation, has departed Washington with his wife and two children to return to Trujillo, where he will serve as a judge of the Supreme Court. Prior to his diplomatic post, he served eight years as an appellate court judge in his native country and earned distinction as a noted lawyer possessing one of the finest libraries in Trujillo. The Logronos were widely known in Washington diplomatic society for their hospitality, with both hailing from prominent old families in the Dominican Republic.El Dr. Logroño, consejero de la legación dominicana, partió de Washington junto a su esposa y dos hijos para regresar a Trujillo, donde fungirá como juez de la Suprema Corte. Antes de su cargo diplomático, se desempeñó durante ocho años como juez del tribunal de apelaciones en su país natal y se distinguió como un notable abogado poseedor de una de las mejores bibliotecas de Trujillo. Los señores de Logroño eran ampliamente conocidos en la sociedad diplomática de Washington por sus agasajos, perteneciendo ambos a destacadas familias tradicionales de la República Dominicana.
