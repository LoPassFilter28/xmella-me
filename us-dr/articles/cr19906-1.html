U. S. SATISFIES SAN DOMINGANS No Election "Supervisors," Only "Friendly Spectators" to Be Sent by Wilson.

WASHINGTON, Dec. 8.—The United States does not intend to supervise or interfere with the approaching elections in the Dominican republic. A special commission from Washington will merely observe the election as "friendly and impartial spectators."

That was the way President Wilson outlined today informally the mission of state department officials now on their way to San Domingo.

President Wilson made it clear that the United States had used its good offices in persuading the revolutionists to lay down their arms on the promise that everything possible within international right would be done by the United States to insure fair elections.
