The article describes President Morales arriving at Puerto Plata with five hundred troops aboard his gunboat El Presidente, intending to proceed to Monte Cristi, whose authorities had signed a peace compact aboard the American ship Detroit. Those authorities told him by telegraph that he could not land his troops in their territory, and, finding them fully prepared and under arms to resist, he gave up the project and returned to the capital, while the American ship Bancroft sailed away to Porto Rico. SANTO DOMINGO IS TROUBLED. REVOLT AGAINST THE MORALES GOVERNMENT PROBABLE. Country Declared at Peace When There Was No Peace—Revolutionists Take to the Woods to Escape Assassination—Accusations Against the Executive.SANTO DOMINGO ESTÁ AGITADO. ES PROBABLE UNA REVUELTA CONTRA EL GOBIERNO DE MORALES. El país declarado en paz cuando no había paz—Los revolucionarios se echan al monte para escapar del asesinato—Acusaciones contra el Ejecutivo.

SANTO DOMINGO, Oct. 7.—Transpiring events demonstrate plainly that the country is on the verge of another revolution. Politicians continue to be arrested and shot; some are in hiding, and some are seeking safety in flight. Silent groups with anxious faces occupy the park benches where they consider themselves safer than in their own homes, for all houses are watched. Soldiers are being recruited and the President's two gunboats show an unusual activity in steaming in and out of the harbor and from one port to another.SANTO DOMINGO, 7 de octubre.—Los acontecimientos que trascienden demuestran con claridad que el país se encuentra al borde de otra revolución. Se sigue arrestando y fusilando a políticos; algunos están escondidos y otros buscan seguridad en la fuga. Grupos silenciosos con rostros angustiados ocupan los bancos del parque, donde se consideran más seguros que en sus propios hogares, pues todas las casas están vigiladas. Se están reclutando soldados y los dos cañoneros del Presidente muestran una actividad inusitada, entrando y saliendo a vapor del puerto y navegando de un puerto a otro.

Less than four months have passed, since President Morales took the oath of office and declared the country pacified. But the declaration was false, and such pacification as actually existed remained on the surface only. Morales knew, as every one else knew, that the country was not at peace, nor was it at peace when the elections were held, which he asserts, made him the Constitutional President.Han pasado menos de cuatro meses desde que el Presidente Morales prestó juramento del cargo y declaró pacificado el país. Pero la declaración era falsa, y la pacificación que realmente existía no pasó de ser superficial. Morales sabía, como todos sabían, que el país no estaba en paz, ni lo estaba cuando se celebraron las elecciones que, según él afirma, lo convirtieron en Presidente Constitucional.

According to the Dominican law no elections are constitutional as long as any of the people are in revolt against the Government. No one voted to speak of at these elections, for when they were held at least two-thirds of the voters were either under arms or in exile. The district of Monte Cristi, as well as the entire south and western part of the republic, was in open rebellion. There was actual fighting at La Vega, Moca and Esperanza, where at the latter place the Minister of War was killed and the Government troops routed, yet Morales asserted that the elections were held. He counted upon United States gunboats to aid him, and thought that if he could proclaim peace with their aid he could maintain it.Conforme a la ley dominicana, ninguna elección es constitucional mientras parte del pueblo se encuentre sublevado contra el Gobierno. Prácticamente nadie votó en estas elecciones, pues cuando se celebraron al menos dos tercios de los votantes estaban bajo las armas o en el exilio. El distrito de Monte Cristi, así como toda la parte sur y occidental de la república, se encontraba en abierta rebelión. Hubo combates reales en La Vega, Moca y Esperanza, muriendo en este último lugar el Ministro de Guerra y resultando desbandadas las tropas del Gobierno; no obstante, Morales afirmó que las elecciones se habían celebrado. Contaba con el auxilio de los cañoneros de los Estados Unidos y pensaba que si podía proclamar la paz con su ayuda, podría mantenerla.

Commander Dillingham of the United States steamship Detroit patched up a peace treaty for him with the rebels at Monte Cristi, by which they were allowed to govern that district independent of the central Government. Morales then sent one of his Ministers, Papi Sanchez, to treat with the rebels of the south, and a similar treaty was agreed to with them. Capt. Culver of the United States steamship Bancroft, on the behalf of Morales, left his ship and journeyed far into the interior to induce the rebel chief, Perico Lasala, to give up, but the old fellow informed the American Captain that he would only surrender on condition that the Captain would make him an American citizen, thus guaranteeing his life and liberty; but he subsequently allowed his men to go in, telling them that as he was born in the woods he would remain in the woods.El comandante Dillingham, del vapor de guerra estadounidense Detroit, le arregló un tratado de paz con los rebeldes de Monte Cristi, por el cual se les permitía gobernar ese distrito con independencia del Gobierno central. Morales envió entonces a uno de sus ministros, Papi Sánchez, a negociar con los rebeldes del sur, y se convino con ellos un tratado semejante. El capitán Culver, del vapor de guerra estadounidense Bancroft, en nombre de Morales, dejó su buque y viajó hacia el interior remoto para inducir a rendirse al jefe rebelde, Perico Lasala; pero el anciano informó al capitán estadounidense de que solo se entregaría con la condición de que el capitán lo convirtiera en ciudadano estadounidense, garantizándole así la vida y la libertad; no obstante, permitió posteriormente a sus hombres que entraran, diciéndoles que, habiendo nacido en el monte, en el monte se quedaría.

Morales's main object was to get the rebels to lay down their arms. He was willing to concede anything. Once that they were disarmed he could see no reason for keeping any of his promises or complying with the terms of any of his written compacts.El objetivo principal de Morales era lograr que los rebeldes depusieran las armas. Estaba dispuesto a conceder lo que fuese. Una vez desarmados, no halló razón alguna para mantener ninguna de sus promesas ni para cumplir con los términos de ninguno de sus pactos escritos.

With the surender of Perico Lasala's men the country was apparently pacified, yet the fires of the revolution were smouldering, not only throughout the country, but right here in the capital city itself. To get possession of Monte Cristi, Morales represented that these people had broken out in rebellion again, thus violating their part of the compact, and it is believed that he got Minister Dawson to cable to the Bancroft, then at Port-au-Prince, to proceed to that port and investigate matters.Con la rendición de los hombres de Perico Lasala, el país quedó al parecer pacificado; no obstante, los fuegos de la revolución ardían bajo las cenizas, no solo en todo el país, sino aquí mismo, en la propia capital. Para apoderarse de Monte Cristi, Morales adujo que esas gentes se habían sublevado de nuevo, violando así su parte del pacto, y se cree que logró que el ministro Dawson enviara un cable al Bancroft, que se hallaba entonces en Puerto Príncipe, para que se dirigiera a aquel puerto e investigara el asunto.

It is certain that the Bancroft turned up at Monte Cristi just about the same time that Morales, with five hundred troops, arrived at Puerto Plata on board his gunboat El Presidente, expressing his intention of proceeding also to Monte Christi. The governing authorities at Monte Christi, who had signed the peace compact on board the Detroit, aware that Morales's plan was to get them in his power, notified him at once by telegraph that they would be pleased to receive him, but they could not permit him to land his troops in their territory. Learning that these people were fully prepared and under arms to resist the landing of his troops he gave up the project and returned to the capital, and the Bancroft sailed away to Porto Rico.Es indudable que el Bancroft se presentó en Monte Cristi casi al mismo tiempo en que Morales, con quinientas tropas, llegaba a Puerto Plata a bordo de su cañonero El Presidente, manifestando su intención de proseguir también hacia Monte Cristi. Las autoridades gobernantes de Monte Cristi, que habían firmado el pacto de paz a bordo del Detroit, conscientes de que el plan de Morales consistía en tenerlos en su poder, le notificaron de inmediato por telégrafo que tendrían mucho gusto en recibirlo, pero que no podían permitirle desembarcar sus tropas en su territorio. Al enterarse de que estas personas estaban plenamente preparadas y sobre las armas para resistir el desembarco de sus tropas, desistió del proyecto y regresó a la capital, y el Bancroft zarpó rumbo a Puerto Rico.
