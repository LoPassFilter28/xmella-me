Original in English.Original en inglés.

Dominican Army and Insurgents in Fierce Battle Government Will Attempt to Re-take the Capital

CAPE HAYTIEN, Hayti, March 30.—A serious engagement took place on Saturday between the Dominican Government troops and the revolutionary forces at Juan Calvo. The losses on the Government side were twenty-seven killed and forty-three wounded, while the revolutionaries lost five killed and eleven wounded.

The telegraph line is interrupted beyond Cotui. It is rumored here that the Government troops have surrounded the capital, Santo Domingo, and that a battle is imminent.

SANTO DOMINGO, March 27.—The Commissioners who were sent to the towns of Bahia Honda, Asua Compola and San Pedro de Macoris to endeavor to obtain their admission to the revolutionary movement have returned here. Their mission was a failure. The situation is unchanged. The city is quiet, expecting news of events on the north side of the island. In the meantime business is at a standstill. It is difficult to foresee the final outcome.

NEW YORK, March 30.—The following dispatch was received to-day by the Consul of Santo Domingo at this port from Governor Caceres, at Santiago, in that republic:

"General Horacio Vasquez captured the cruiser Independencia at Macoris. All the chiefs of revolution on board captured and are imprisoned. Revolution now confined to Santo Domingo city, where they are beseiged by Government troops. Rest of country is with Government."

WASHINGTON, March 30.—The Atlanta left Cape Haytien yesterday for Santo Domingo City, where a battle is in sight.
