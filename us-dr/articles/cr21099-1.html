Morales Is Down and Out — Caceres Acting President of the Colored Republic.

Washington, Jan. 5.—Secretary of War Taft received the following cablegram today from Colonel Colton, controller and general receiver for the Dominican customs, dated Santo Domingo, January 2:

"Carlos F. Morales was legally impeached today by congress. Raymond Caceres is acting in accordance with the constitution pending a final action of the proceeding of the supreme court. This eliminates Carlos A. Morales from the situation and leaves without question the legality of the Raymond Caceres government. The government is acting with conservatism and adhering to the constitution. Carlos F. Morales is not yet heard from."

WAR AT END IN SANTO DOMINGO

Troops Are Dispersing to Their Homes—Country Quiet.

Washington, Jan. 5.—Cable advices received today by the state department from Santo Domingo announce that the revolution is practically at an end. The troops are dispersing to their homes and the country is becoming quiet.

PREPARES TO ATTACK SANTIAGO

General Gulleito Has Decided to Fight in His Own Behalf.

Cape Hayetin, Jan. 5.—After being decisively defeated the remainder of the followers of President Morales boarded the gunboat Independencia and sailed to Monte Christi.

General Guleito, the former governor of Monte Christi, who, with his troops, had declared in favor of Morales, on being informed of the death of General Rodriguez, decided to fight on his own behalf, and is preparing to attack Santiago.

General Jiminez, a former president of Santo Domingo, has gone to Monte Christi and with the aid of President Morales will seek the presidency, it is reported that a large part of the population of northern Santo Domingo favor the election of Jiminez.

Will Impeach President Morales.

Santo Domingo, Jan. 5.—A proclamation was issued this morning announcing the decision of congress to impeach President Morales, now a fugitive in his efforts to escape capture by the revolutionists.
