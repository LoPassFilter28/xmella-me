More Americans Flown Out Of Troubled Haiti

PORT AU PRINCE, Haiti (UPI) — The United States flew 158 more Americans to Miami and safety Thursday, winding up the first phase of Washington's evacuation of government dependents from troubled Haiti. Thursday's evacuees, flown out in two commercial airliners, together with Wednesday's total of 195 Americans brought the two-day total to 353. Of this number 220 were dependents.

The American Embassy said a third airlift of evacuees would be ordered if the some 1,200 American nationals who have so far resisted evacuation indicated a preference to leave Haiti.

Leaving on Thursday's shuttle flight to Miami were 32 Protestant missionaries of some 200 who have been working in Haiti. It was announced that Mrs. Raymond Thurston, wife of the U. S. ambassador, also will be leaving next Monday. She had sought to remain with her husband.

CITY IS CALM

Port au Prince was outwardly calm and some sources attributed it to unconfirmed rumors that Clement Barbot, arch-enemy of President Francois Duvalier and leader of the underground resistance movement, had fled for safety to a Latin American embassy here.

Barbot's wife and four children are known to be in diplomatic asylum in the Argentine Embassy here. However, his brother, Ernst, and Ernst's wife, Jeanine, are in the hands of authorities. If rumors of Barbot's choice of political asylum are confirmed, it would mean a substantial strengthening of the government's control over the internal situation. However, Duvalier, scheduled to make a speech Wednesday in Petionville, failed to appear.

The President's last public appearance was on May 1.

ATTACKS IN PROCESS

Although Haiti seemed willing to let such outside bodies as United Nations and the Organization of American States mediate its political problems with the neighboring Dominican Republic, the Haitian press and radio Thursday denounced Santo Domingo. Dominican President Juan Bosch was flayed as "an instrument" of the United States government, and Haiti was described as the target of what government organs called "an international conspiracy."
