New-York Tribune. New York [N.Y.], April 11, 1894. Page 4. View the originalVer el original

The Tribune quotes a just-returned traveler's high praise of President Heureaux. 'He is a fine man. He is a born leader and is just the man to lift his country to prosperity. He has a keen sense of moral responsibility. His word is as good as his bond in all contracts, public or private.' The party which has just returned from San Domingo, the item adds, consisted of Smith M. Weed, his son and daughter, W. Brown, Dr. Boyer, Professor J. Lawrence Laughlin, and Charles W. Wells.El Tribune cita los grandes elogios de un viajero recién regresado al presidente Heureaux: «Es un hombre excelente. Es un líder nato y es precisamente el hombre capaz de llevar a su país a la prosperidad. Tiene un agudo sentido de la responsabilidad moral. Su palabra vale tanto como un compromiso firmado en todos los contratos, públicos o privados». El grupo que acaba de regresar de San Domingo, añade la nota, estaba compuesto por Smith M. Weed, su hijo y su hija, W. Brown, el Dr. Boyer, el profesor J. Lawrence Laughlin y Charles W. Wells.
