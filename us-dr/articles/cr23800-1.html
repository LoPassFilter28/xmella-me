OFFICIAL SEEKS DUEL OR APOLOGY FROM ARGENTINE Dominican Claims He Was Insulted by Ambassador

The Consul General of the Dominican Republic wants the Argentine ambassador to Canada to meet him on the field of honor or apologize for what the Dominican insists is a double insult.

Julio Ricart, the Dominican, sent a challenge to Dr. Juan-Carlos Rodriguez, the Argentine, suggesting the duel be held in the Argentine Embassy, technically outside Canada, and urging the ambassador to choose his weapons.

The Argentine will send a reply—to Ciudad Trujillo, capital of the Dominican Republic.

Here is how it started according to Ricart: The Argentine Embassy recently was visited by the Humane Society after it was reported two dogs at the Embassy had been beaten to death. At an Embassy reception, Ricart said he made "some pleasantry" about dogs, not meaning to be nasty. The ambassador's wife "made what I thought was an offensive answer," Ricart said.
