Red Cross Medicines Rushed to Quake Zone

By the Associated Press

CUIDAD TRUJILLO, Dominican Republic, Aug. 13.—The American Red Cross rushed a planeload of medical supplies from San Juan, Puerto Rico, today to the earthquake and tidal wave disaster zone on the north coast of this Caribbean island where an outbreak of disease threatened the lives of thousands of homeless refugees.

H. E. Russell, Red Cross representative, said the medical supplies included hundreds of pounds of badly needed antitoxin, vaccine, tetanus toxoids, sulfa drugs and quinine.

More than 75 persons in a single camp of 500 refugees in the camp were reported ill with paratyphus, malaria or unidentified intestinal ailments.

The village of Matanzas was virtually wiped out when it was struck by two tidal waves in the wake of the August 4 earthquake. Flimsily constructed native huts were lifted by the inrushing tidal wave and hurled back 250 yards, to be splintered against the dense jungle growth.
