Evening Star · March 11, 193911 de marzo de 1939

Original in English.Original en inglés.

Social News of the Official And Diplomatic Sets In the Capital Secretary and Mrs. Hull Leave for Florida; Senor and Senora De Pastoriza Visit in New York

The Secretary of State and Mrs. Cordell Hull are en route to Miami, Fla., where they will spend several weeks. They had planned to leave early this week, but at the last minute their plans were changed.

The Minister of the Dominican Republic and Senora de Pastoriza will return today from New York, where they spent the week with Senora de Trujillo, wife of the former President of the Dominican Republic. Senora de Trujillo and her party sailed today for Europe to spend some time.

The Nicaraguan Minister and Senora de DeBayle returned yesterday from New York, where they have been for a short visit.

Dr. Keenleyside Guest Of Canadian Minister.

The Canadian Minister and Lady Marler have had as their guest this week, Dr. Hugh Keenleyside of the Department of the Exterior of Canada, who has been here for conferences with the State Department on the visit of their majesties, King George VI and Queen Elizabeth of Great Britain. Dr. Keenleyside will return to Canada today.

The Netherlands Minister and Mme. Loudon returned yesterday from Cuba, where the former also is accredited Minister. They have been absent several weeks and will be here only a fortnight before leaving Saturday, March 25, for California. They will visit the Grand Canyon en route west and will spend some time in Los Angeles and San Francisco, returning to Washington the latter part of April.

Representative and Mrs. John Taber of Auburn, N. Y., who make their home at the Wardman Park Hotel, have as their guests there Mr. and Mrs. George T. Purcell and their two daughters, Miss Jane Purcell and Miss June Purcell, of Auburn.

Fashion Luncheon Held At Shoreham Hotel.

Mrs. Harry R. Sheppard, wife of Representative Sheppard, and Mrs. C. Arthur Anderson, wife of Representative Anderson, were among the guests of Mrs. Martin Logan at the fashion luncheon at the Shoreham yesterday. Her other guests included Miss Ruth Hamilton, Mrs. Sam Jones, Mrs. Frank Weida, Mrs. Thomas Hardin and Mrs. Robert I. Berger.

Another hostess was Mrs. W. W. Pilkington, who had among her guests Mrs. T. L. Varnell, Mrs. Evan Lewis and her sister, Mrs. Henry Morrison of Constantine, Mich.

Mrs. Reese McDuffie and Mrs. H. L. Clapp were joint hostesses at another party. Their guests included Mrs. Albert Stabler, Mrs. Alexander S. Steuart, Mrs. William E. Lewis, Mrs. Walter Weaver, Mrs. George Winchester Stone, Mrs. J. Warnock Echol, Miss Ella Booream, Miss Annah Fletcher and Miss Mary Pond.

The Secretary of the Panama Legation and Senora de Briceno have returned to Washington after several days' visit in New York.
