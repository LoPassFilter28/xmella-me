In correspondence from Havana, The New York Herald reports that the rebellion in St. Domingo still continues and that Spanish troops are being dispatched to Puerto Plata. According to the dispatch, the screw corvette Santa Lucia left Santiago again on the 4th with troops, and the Ulloa sailed from Nuevitas on the 7th with a battery of artillery in company with the Isabel la Catolica, which had put in from stress of weather. The report further states that 216 soldiers of the Second Battalion San Marcial were found in Haiti by the Haitian government, disarmed, and delivered to the Spanish Consul at Port-au-Prince, who has sent to General Dulce for a vessel to take them away. HAVANA, Sept. 12, 1863.LA HABANA, 12 de septiembre de 1863.

The Rebellion in St. Domingo—Fires and Robberies in Havana—Arrival of an Anglo-Rebel Steamer.La rebelión en Santo Domingo—Incendios y robos en La Habana—Llegada de un vapor anglorrebelde.

Since my last we have had no news of importance. The rebellion in St. Domingo still continues, and troops are being despatched to Puerto Plata. The screw corvette Santa Lucia left Santiago again on the 6th with troops. The Ulloa sailed from Neuvitas on the 7th, with a battery of artillery, in company with the Isabella Catholica, which had put in from stress of weather.Desde mi última correspondencia no hemos tenido noticias de importancia. La rebelión en Santo Domingo aún continúa, y se están enviando tropas a Puerto Plata. La corbeta de hélice Santa Lucía salió de nuevo de Santiago el 6 con tropas. El Ulloa zarpó de Nuevitas el 7, con una batería de artillería, en compañía de la Isabel la Católica, que había arribado por fuerza mayor a causa del mal tiempo.

Report says that 275 soldiers of the Second battalion San Marcial were found in Hayti by the Haytien government, and were disarmed and delivered to the Spanish Consul at Port au Prince, and that he has sent to General Dulce for a vessel to take them away. The Haytien steamer Esther arrived at Santiago on the 3d, it is supposed with the despatches of the Spanish Consul for General Dulce.Se dice que el gobierno haitiano encontró en Haití a 275 soldados del Segundo batallón San Marcial, quienes fueron desarmados y entregados al cónsul español en Puerto Príncipe, y que este ha solicitado al general Dulce un buque para llevárselos. El vapor haitiano Esther llegó a Santiago el 3, según se cree con los despachos del cónsul español para el general Dulce.

On Monday more troops are to leave, and it is supposed that the militia will be called out.El lunes partirán más tropas, y se supone que se llamará a la milicia.

There have been several murders, fires and robberies here lately. Mr. Moral, proprietor of the Revista Militar, was murdered on the 5th, and his body was discovered on the 9th in an unoccupied house. A debtor of his—a bookdealer—has been arrested as the assassin, an investigation is going on, and sufficient proof has been found to convict him.Últimamente ha habido aquí varios asesinatos, incendios y robos. El Sr. Moral, propietario de la Revista Militar, fue asesinado el 5, y su cadáver fue hallado el 9 en una casa desocupada. Un deudor suyo —un librero— ha sido arrestado como el asesino; se está llevando a cabo una investigación y se han encontrado pruebas suficientes para condenarlo.

The house of the Count of the Reunion was set on fire and is still burning. Loss, $300,000. There was another incendiary fire last night.La casa del conde de la Reunión fue incendiada y todavía sigue ardiendo. Pérdidas, $300.000. Anoche hubo otro incendio provocado.

The Anglo-rebel steamer Laura, of 163 tons, came in this morning in ballast, one day from Nassau. Her captain says two steamers had arrived at Nassau from Charleston, and one from Wilmington.El vapor anglorrebelde Laura, de 163 toneladas, entró esta mañana en lastre, a un día de navegación desde Nasáu. Su capitán afirma que dos vapores habían llegado a Nasáu procedentes de Charleston, y uno de Wilmington.

Gen. Cortes, Governor of Sonora, Mexico, goes to Washington in the Eagle.El general Cortés, gobernador de Sonora, México, va a Washington en el Eagle.

The schooner Amaranth arrived, five days from New Orleans, on the 16th, with 14 passengers.La goleta Amaranth llegó el 16, a cinco días de Nueva Orleans, con 14 pasajeros.
