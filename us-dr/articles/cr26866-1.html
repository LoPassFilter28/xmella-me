The Express completes its assassination dispatch and its Washington item of July 27: it is probable that he will soon be captured; President-General Wenceslao Figuereo, immediately upon the announcement of the president's death, assumed the direction of affairs; at present calmness prevails in the republic; and the remains of President Heureaux will probably be taken to Santo Domingo for the funeral services. The Washington item says that no information was received last night from the consular representatives of the United States in San Domingo regarding the assassination of President Heureaux, and that, pending official advices of the assassination, no formal action will be taken by this government: William F. Powell, the minister to Hayti, is also charge d'affaires to San Domingo, while this government is directly represented in the republic in the person of Campbell T. Maxwell, who is consul general, and John A. Read, who is vice consul; officials here recall that attempts have heretofore been made on the life of President Heureaux; Secretary Hay paid a brief tribute to the work of the deceased president, saying he understood that he had given the country a good administration; and Secretary Long said he could not recall that there were any ships of the United States now in Santo Domingo waters, and that, should the developments of the next few days show a feeling of unrest and uncertainty, a United States man-of-war will be dispatched to protect American interests.El Express completa su despacho del asesinato y su nota de Washington del 27 de julio: es probable que pronto sea capturado; el presidente general Wenceslao Figuereo, inmediatamente después del anuncio de la muerte del presidente, asumió la dirección de los asuntos; al presente prevalece la calma en la república; y los restos del presidente Heureaux serán probablemente llevados a Santo Domingo para los servicios fúnebres. GENERAL HEUREAUX SLAIN The President of San Domingo Assassinated —No Disturbance of the People—Iowa Cattle Going Blind.EL GENERAL HEUREAUX ASESINADO El Presidente de Santo Domingo asesinado —Ningún disturbio entre el pueblo— El ganado de Iowa se queda ciego.

FORT DE FRANCE, Martinique, July 27.—General Ulysses Heureaux, president of the Dominican Republic, was assassinated at Moca, San Domingo, at half-past 4 o'clock yesterday afternoon. Ramon Caceros, the murderer, succeeded in making his escape, but an energetic pursuit was at once begun and it is probable that he will soon be captured. President-General Wencelao Eiguereon immediately upon the announcement of the president's death, assumed the direction of affairs.FORT-DE-FRANCE, Martinica, 27 de julio.— El general Ulysses Heureaux, presidente de la República Dominicana, fue asesinado en Moca, Santo Domingo, a las 4 y media de la tarde de ayer. Ramón Caceros, el asesino, logró darse a la fuga, pero de inmediato se inició una enérgica persecución y es probable que sea capturado pronto. El presidente general Wencelao Eiguereon, inmediatamente tras el anuncio de la muerte del presidente, asumió la dirección de los asuntos.

At present calmness prevails in the republic. The remains of President Heureaux will probably be taken to Santo Domingo for the funeral services.Al presente reina la calma en la república. Los restos del presidente Heureaux probablemente serán trasladados a Santo Domingo para los servicios fúnebres.

WASHINGTON, July 27.—No information was received last night from the consular representatives of the United States in San Domingo regarding the assassination of President Heureux. Pending official advices of the assassination no formal action will be taken by this government. William F. Powell, the minister to Hayti, is also charge d'affaires to San Domingo, while this government is directly represented in the republic in the person of Campbell T. Maxwell, who is consul general, and John A. Read, who is vice consul.WASHINGTON, 27 de julio.— Anoche no se recibió información de los representantes consulares de los Estados Unidos en Santo Domingo sobre el asesinato del presidente Heureux. En espera de avisos oficiales del asesinato, este gobierno no tomará ninguna medida formal. William F. Powell, ministro en Haití, es también encargado de negocios en Santo Domingo, mientras que este gobierno está directamente representado en la república en la persona de Campbell T. Maxwell, quien es cónsul general, y John A. Read, quien es vicecónsul.

Officials here recall that attempts have heretofore been made on the life of President Heureux. Secretary Hay paid a brief tribute to the work of the deceased president, saying he understood that he had given the country a good administration.Los funcionarios aquí recuerdan que hasta ahora se han realizado atentados contra la vida del presidente Heureux. El secretario Hay rindió un breve tributo a la labor del difunto presidente, diciendo que entendía que había dado al país una buena administración.

Secretary Long said he could not recall that there were any ships of the United States now in San Domingo waters. Should the developments of the next few days show a feeling of unrest and uncertainty a United States man-of-war will be dispatched to protect American interests.El secretario Long dijo que no recordaba que hubiera buques de los Estados Unidos en aguas de Santo Domingo en este momento. En caso de que los acontecimientos de los próximos días muestren un sentimiento de inquietud e incertidumbre, se enviará un buque de guerra de los Estados Unidos para proteger los intereses estadounidenses.
