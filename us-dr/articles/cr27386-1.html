Va., 20 de enero de 1915. Página 1. Publicado por Intelligencer Pub. Co. Periódico diario, 1903–1961. View the originalVer el original

Alleged political activities of Congressman James A. Hamill, of New Jersey, W. Beer of New York, and John O. Gray, of Wilmington, Del., in procuring the appointment of James M. Sullivan as minister to the Dominican republic were described today at the investigation which is being conducted by Senator-elect Phelan, of California, into Sullivan's fitness to hold office. W. G. McGunness, of Washington, who described himself as a personal friend of President McKinley and a 'Republican politician,' testified that Beer and Sullivan had promised him the position of director of posts in Santo Domingo in return for his assistance in promoting Sullivan's candidacy. The position was to be a 'sinecure,' said the witness. He described meetings which he said were held between Beer, Hamill and Sullivan in Washington, previous to Sullivan's appointment, the subject of which was ways and means to get the ear of Secretary Bryan. Beer, it has been brought out in previous testimony, was attorney for the Banco Nacional interests, Santo Domingo, who are charged with schemes to exploit public works contracts with the aid of Sullivan.El puesto iba a ser una «sinecura», dijo el testigo. Se describió a sí mismo como amigo personal del presidente McKinley y un «político republicano». Las supuestas actividades políticas del congresista James A. Hamill, de Nueva Jersey, W. Beer de Nueva York, y John O. Gray, de Wilmington, Delaware, para procurar el nombramiento de James M. Sullivan como ministro en la República Dominicana fueron descritas hoy en la investigación que conduce el senador electo Phelan, de California, sobre la aptitud de Sullivan para ocupar el cargo. Describió reuniones que dijo se celebraron entre Beer, Hamill y Sullivan en Washington, antes del nombramiento de Sullivan, cuyo tema fue los medios para ganarse el oído del secretario Bryan. Beer, se ha revelado en testimonios anteriores, era abogado de los intereses del Banco Nacional de Santo Domingo, a quienes se acusa de maquinaciones para explotar contratos de obras públicas con la ayuda de Sullivan. Nueva York, 19 de enero.
