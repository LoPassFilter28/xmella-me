Original in English.Original en inglés.

Gen. Trujillo Is Here on Short Visit

Visiting Washington unofficially is the former president of the Dominican Republic, Gen. Rafael L. Trujillo, now Dominican delegate to the United Nations.

The Generalissimo arrived in the Capital Wednesday, en route to his homeland from Europe. After spending several days here at the Mayflower, he expects to leave for New York tomorrow.

He was accompanied by his son, Gen. Rafael Trujillo, jr., who is staying with his family at the Dominican Embassy.

The visiting official was the guest of honor yesterday at a stag luncheon given by Assistant Secretary of State Henry F. Holland at Blair House.

Guests included the Dominican Ambassador, Senor de Moya; the Minister, Dr. Horacio Vicioso; Plinio B. Pina Chevalier, Minister and Commercial Counselor of the Embassy, and Jose R. Rodriguez, Dominican Ambassador to the Organization of American States.

Members of the visitor's party included Gen. Trujillo, jr., and Gen. Anselmo Paulino, minister without portfolio.

Among others were Gen. Nathan F. Twining, Chief of Staff of the Air Force; Maj. Gen. Robert Webster, chairman of the United States delegation to the Inter-American Defense Board; Homer Cummings, Dr. William Morgan, Charles Burrows, director of the State Department's Office of Middle American Affairs, and William B. Connett, officer in charge of Dominican affairs.
