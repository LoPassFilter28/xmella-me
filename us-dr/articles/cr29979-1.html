Catholic World News In Review

DECEMBER

Pope John's Aeterna Dei Sapientia encyclical invited all separated Christian bodies back to unity, particularly the Eastern schismatic churches.

On a three-day visit to Washington where he was Apostolic Delegate 25 years, Cardinal Amleto Cicognani, Papal Secretary of State, paid a courtesy call on President Kennedy, addressed the National Press Club, was feted at a NCWC reception. Archbishop Vagnozzi, Apostolic Delegate to the U.S., told the CCD congress in Dallas, Tex., U.S. Catholics have produced the finest education system in the church's history.

GREGORIO PIETRO XV Cardinal Agagianian, Prefect of the Sacred Congregation for the Propogation of the Faith, addressed the national convention of the Society for the Propagation of the Faith in San Francisco. After a preliminary trip to Panama, New York's Cardinal Spellman left on a 14-day trip to Germany and France, his 11th consecutive Christmas visit to U.S. troops overseas. Cuba's Fidel Castro made a public admission that he is a communist.

MARGARET ANN SEFCIK, Corpus Christi, Tex., CYO leader, was named "Outstanding Catholic Youth of the Year." Archbishop Edward F. Hoban, Bishop of Cleveland, observed the 40th anniversary of his consecration.

DEATH CAME TO: Archbishop Francis P. Keough, 70, of Baltimore, from a heart attack in Washington and he was immediately succeeded by his coadjutor, Archbishop Lawrence J. Shehan; and Archbishop Ricardo Pittini, S.D.B., 85, of Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic, from a heart attack, succeeded by his coadjutor, Octavio A. Beras. Bishop Jaroslav Gabro of the new St. Nicholas of Chicago Ukrainian Greek Rite diocese was enthroned.

Msgr. Charles A. Salatka, vice chancellor, was named Auxiliary Bishop of Grand Rapids, Mich., and Msgr. Ernest L. Unterkoeffer, Chancellor, was named Auxiliary Bishop of Richmond, Va.

Archbishop Leo Binz of Dubuque was transferred to the Archdiocese of St. Paul. Missouri's Supreme Court upheld the state's 134-year-old law banning unnecessary Sunday business. Announcement by R. Sargent Shriver, Peace Corps director, that the corps will make no contracts with church-related groups evoked a protest from Bishop Edward E. Swanstrom, chairman of the American Council of Voluntary Agencies. Cardinal Cushing flew to Lima, Peru and presided at the funeral Mass for Father Thomas Duggan, 71, Irish missioner and longtime friend.
