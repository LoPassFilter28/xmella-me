An editorial hails the triumph of the Dominican Republic over SpainUn editorial celebra el triunfo de la República Dominicana sobre España

New Orleans Tribune. New Orleans [La.], June 30, 1865. Page 1.New Orleans Tribune. New Orleans [La.], 30 de junio de 1865. Página 1. View the originalVer el original

It describes the republic as occupying the eastern part of the island, some 17,500 square miles with a population put at between 120,000 and 200,000, recalls that Santana sold San Domingo to Spain, and notes that the Spaniards, confined since 1863 to a few seaport towns, have been compelled to withdraw and to recognize the independence of the country.Describe la república ocupando la parte oriental de la isla, unas 17,500 millas cuadradas con una población estimada entre 120,000 y 200,000 habitantes, recuerda que Santana vendió Santo Domingo a España y señala que los españoles, confinados desde 1863 a unos pocos puertos, se han visto obligados a retirarse y a reconocer la independencia del país.
