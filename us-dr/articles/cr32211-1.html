American International Corporation is to be Established in South

Santo Domingo City, Dominican Republic, March 5.—The American International Corporation, affiliated with the National City Bank, New York, soon is to become established here through the purchases of the banking interests of Santiago Michelena, head of the largest banking house here. No announcement has been made as to when the Michelena bank, which has branches in all of the principal cities, is to be taken over by the American International Corporation, but all details of the purchase have been agreed to, it is stated authoritatively, and formal announcement of the sale is expected to be made very shortly.
