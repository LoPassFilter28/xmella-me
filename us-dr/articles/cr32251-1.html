Gardners Entertain For Navy Chief

By RUTH DEAN

Hurricanes or no hurricanes, the United States Navy is always on time when it comes to keeping a schedule and yesterday's arrival of the chief of staff of the Dominican Republic Navy, Rear Admiral Rafael Richardson, for an official visit here was no exception.

It so happened that Hurricane Ione had long departed by 13 hours or so and the visiting admiral made it into National Airport at 3:30 p.m. yesterday on the dot, "but even if we'd had to go north as far as Pittsburgh and then in, we still were going to be on time," declared United States Naval Lt. August Schirmer at a party in the admiral's honor last night.

The party was a reception given by Deputy Chief of Naval Operations for Plans and Policy, Vice Admiral and Mrs. Matthias B. Gardner, at the Officers' Club of the National Naval Medical Center in Bethesda.

Inspection Visit

Lt. Schirmer, who is with the Navy's Pan American Affairs Division, has charge of arrangements for the admiral's visit which will take him on an inspection of naval installations throughout the country. He flew down to the Dominican Republic's capital, Ciudad Trujillo, to bring Admiral Richardson and his party here. "We were flying through overcast a good part of the time," said Lt. Schirmer, "but we were in constant contact with the Washington Airport."

Admiral Richardson was met at the airport by his country's Ambassador to the United States, Dr. Joaquin E. Salazar, and the Naval Attache, Comdr. Luis Lajara Burgos, both of whom later attended the reception in Bethesda.

Admiral Richardson stood in the receiving line with Vice Admiral and Mrs. Gardner to receive the more than 350 guests who attended the 6:30 to 8:30 o'clock affair.

Among those who had accepted invitations to attend were the Chief of Naval Operations, Admiral and Mrs. Arleigh Burke; the head of the Joint Chiefs of Staff, Admiral and Mrs. Arthur Radford, and the director of the Division of Pan American Affairs, Rear Admiral and Mrs. C. W. Wilkins.

Also on hand were the Marine Corps Chief of Staff, Lt. Gen. R. McC. Pate and Mrs. Pate; Rear Admiral and Mrs. Howard E. Orem, Rear Admiral and Mrs. William K. Mendenhall, Jr., Rear Admiral and Mrs. Harry Sanders, Rear Admiral and Mrs. Carl F. Espe, Rear Admiral and Mrs. Elton W. Grenfell, and Rear Admiral and Mrs. Albert G. Mumma.

Official Call

Admiral Richardson was to pay an official call on Admiral Burke today and then make visits to the Naval Gun Factory and the Navy Hydrographic Office.

Tomorrow morning he will place a wreath on the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier in Arlington and attend a special session of the Inter-American Defense Board, called in his honor. He will remain for luncheon with board members.

Fellow staff officers accompanying Admiral Richardson are Capt. Ramon Julio Burgos, superintendent of the Dominican Republic Naval Academy; Lt. Comdr. Victor Manuel Gomez Pierret, chief of the bureau of naval personnel, and Lt. Comdr. David Antonio Hart, chief of the bureau of naval ordnance.
