PAN-AMERICAN FLIGHT FOR GOOD WILL LISTED

Plans for a Pan-American good-will flight to promote the construction of a gigantic lighthouse at Punta Torrecilla, Dominican Republic, as a memorial to Christopher Columbus were announced yesterday by Dr. Pedro Martinez Fraga, Ambassador from Cuba, and Andres Pastoriza, Minister from the Dominican Republic.

The flight will be conducted by three Cuban planes and one Dominican craft. The Cuban planes will leave Havana October 10 for Trujillo City, to be joined there by a Dominican plane to form the "Columbine Squad," which will depart from the Dominican capital on October 12, date of the discovery of America 455 years ago, for the tour outlined in a resolution passed at the Buenos Aires Peace Conference.

The good-will squad will be in Washington December 5-6 and New York December 6-7.
