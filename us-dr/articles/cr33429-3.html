South Bend News-Times · January 1, 19311 de enero de 1931

Original in English.Original en inglés.

DISASTERS

Jan. 2—Ten men killed when movie company's two stunt planes collided near Santa Monica, Calif.

Jan. 10—Many thousands reported dead in China of cold and famine.

Jan. 12—Disastrous floods in Mississippi and Ohio river valleys.

Jan. 13—Many killed in furious storm that swept over England and northern Europe.

Jan. 19—Sixteen killed in airplane crash at Oceanside, Calif.

Jan. 21—Large part of Canea, capital of Crete, destroyed by fire.

Feb. 7—Twenty men killed by mine explosion at Standardville, Utah.

Feb. 11—North German Lloyd liner Muenchen destroyed by fire and explosion at New York dock.

Feb. 23—Eleven killed and 11 hurt in interurban train wreck at Kenosha, Wis.

March 2—Three million dollar fire on New Orleans waterfront.

March 4—Floods in southwestern France took 400 lives.

March 18—Seventy-six killed in movie film explosion at Kirin, Manchuria.

March 26—Twelve miners killed in gas explosion at Arnettsville, W. Va.

March 29—Sixteen miners entombed and killed by explosion at Kettle Island, Ky.

April 2—One hundred drowned when Japanese ferryboat capsized.

April 3—Explosion in fireworks plant at Devon, Pa., killed 30.

April 6—Seventeen killed by explosion on Japanese train.

April 11—Train struck a bus at Isleta, N. M., killing 19.

April 12—Seventeen men killed by mine explosion at Carbonado, Wash.

April 18—Fire in church at Choesti, Rumania, killed 144.

April 21—Incendiary fire in Ohio state penitentiary at Columbus killed 318 convicts.

May 1—Violent storms in midwest states killed 18.

May 3—One hundred and fifty Japanese fishermen perish in typhoon.

May 4—Conflagration in Nashua, N. H., rendered 1,000 persons homeless.

May 5—Earthquake at Pegu, Burma, killed 600 and destroyed the city.

May 6—Sixty-nine persons killed by tornado in Texas.

May 7—Earthquake at Urumiya, Persia, killed about 2,000.

May 8—Twenty killed by explosion in packing plant at South St. Joseph, Mo.

May 18—Twenty-one killed by storms in Arkansas, Texas and Tennessee.

May 21—Two hundred Mohammedan pilgrims killed when French steamer Asia burned at Jeddah.

May 30—Sixteen lives lost when excursion boat upset at Santa Monica, Calif.

June 10—Forty-eight lives lost when steamer Fairfax collided with and sank tanker Pinthis in Massachusetts bay.

June 25—Million dollar fire in Chicago municipal airport.

June 26—Thirty-one killed when boat struck dynamite scow in St. Lawrence river.

June 28—Thousands killed by earthquake in China.

July 9—Explosion in coal mine near Breslau, Germany, killed 162.

July 18—Typhoon swept southern Japan and part of Corea, killing hundreds and doing vast damage.

July 22—Thirty-eight killed when bridge at Coblenz, Germany, collapsed.

Earthquake killed 1,472 persons in central southern Italy and ruined many cities and villages.

July 27—Adrianople, Turkey, largely ruined by tornado; 20 killed.

July 29—Fifteen men drowned when sandboat capsized in Lake Erie.

Aug. 7—Twenty drowned in cloudburst at Nogales, Texas, and Nogales, Mexico.

Aug. 18—British steamer Tahiti sank in mid-Pacific, passengers and crew rescued by Matson liner Ventura.

Aug. 23—Santo Domingo, capital of Dominican republic, destroyed by hurricane; 4,000 killed, 5,000 injured.

Sept. 24—Earthquake in Soviet republic of Tadjikistan killed 175.

Oct. 5—British dirigible R-101 crashed and exploded near Beauvais, France.; 48 killed, including Lord Thomson, minister of air.

Oct. 6—Sixty lives lost in river flood at Pachuca, Mexico.

Oct. 20—Conflagration on waterfront of Wuchow, China, killed 650.

Oct. 21—Explosion in coal mine at Alsdorf, Germany, killed 243.

Oct. 25—Coal mine explosion near Friedrichsthal, Germany, killed 107.

Oct. 27—Thirty men killed by mine explosion at McAlester, Okla.

Oct. 30—Earthquake on eastern coast of Italy killed 50.

Nov. 5—Eighty-three killed by mine explosion at Millfield, Ohio.

Nov. 13—Landslides killed about 100 in a suburb of Lyon, France.

Nov. 19—Twenty killed by tornado at Bethany, Okla.

Nov. 22—Terrific storms swept France, Germany, Belgium and Austria.

Nov. 26—Earthquake in central Japan killed several hundred persons.

Nov. 29—Thirteen miners killed by gas blast at Lutie, Okla.
