Party to Confer On Lost Flyer

The father of Gerald L. Murphy, American flyer who disappeared mysteriously in the Dominican Republic a year ago, is expected to meet tomorrow with a group interested in the case.

Mr. Murphy's disappearance has been linked with that of Jesus de Galindez, a Columbia University professor and outspoken political enemy of Generalissimo Trujillo, Dominican dictator. The professor vanished one night in March, 1956, after leaving his classroom.

Tomorrow's meeting has been arranged by Representative Porter, Democrat of Oregon. Mr. Murphy's father, Lester G. Murphy of Eugene, Oreg., will be present.

The meeting will be held at the Congressional Hotel. Others expected to attend are Prof. Robert Alexander of Rutgers University; Carlton Beals, author of books on Latin America; Serafino Romualdi, Inter-American representative of the AFL-CIO; Miss Frances R. Grant, secretary-general of the Inter-American Association for Democracy and Freedom, and Miss Louise Crane, publisher of the Latin American magazine Iberica.

Dominican Republic officials have stated that the 23-year-old Mr. Murphy was thrown from a cliff into the sea by another flyer who later hanged himself in a jail cell. The State Department has called this explanation unsatisfactory.

Mr. Porter said tomorrow's meeting will discuss whether rewards should be offered for information, investigators hired and whether Government agencies are pressing the investigation vigorously enough.
