SHOT DOWN FOR CONSPIRACY. Two Sons of an Ex-Priest of San Domingo, among the Victims of Huereaux.

PANAMA, March 12.—The Star and Herald of March 2d says: A story from San Domingo, coming from good authority, says that the two sons of ex-Priest Baez had been shot by order of President Heureaux. According to the story, the elder son had previously been sentenced to imprisonment for life for plotting to overthrow the existing government, and when the younger was also discovered to be implicated, Heureaux determined that both should die.

Soon after the younger's arrest the President sent word to the Baez family that the two brothers had need of two broadcloth suits. This was on February 2d. The clothes were sent and the next morning the youths were told to put the black suits on. When they had complied, they were told that the freedom of the jail-yard was given them. In the yard was a squad of soldiers, whose bullets speedily put an end to their lives.

This, it is said, makes twelve martial executions in the Dominican Republic in sixty days. Among the twelve was that of Francis Pildado, an alleged dynamiter, brother of the Spanish Consul to San Domingo. The consul himself was ejected from the island without recourse to any of the diplomatic formalities.
