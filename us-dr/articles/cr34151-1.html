Caribair Gets Safety Award

For the thirteenth consecutive year, the National Safety Council, a non-profit organization dedicated to accident prevention with headquarters in Chicago, named Caribbean Atlantic Airlines, better known as Caribair, Puerto Rico's only local airline, winner of the Aviation Safety Award. Caribair has completed 18 years of service without a single passenger or crew fatality.

This annual award goes only to domestic, territorial and overseas air carriers which fly scheduled passenger runs. Besides Caribair, 39 other U.S. scheduled airlines were named winners of the National Safety Council's Aviation Safety Award.

At the end of 1955, Caribair planes had flown a total of 77,937,000 passenger miles with a perfect safety record. Caribair's daily scheduled flights are to and from San Juan, Ponce, and Mayaguez in Puerto Rico; St. Thomas and St. Croix in the Virgin Islands, and Ciudad Trujillo in the Dominican Republic. Dionisio Trigo is president of the airline.

All Council awards were made on the basis of official records of the Civil Aeronautics Administration, and total miles are those accumulated in scheduled passenger carrying operations only. Members of the Council's Advisory Committee are Lt. Gen. James H. Doolittle and Harry F. Guggenheim.
