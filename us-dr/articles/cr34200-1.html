The Washington Times · January 10, 193510 de enero de 1935

Original in English.Original en inglés.

Town Topics

The new Minister of the Dominican Republic, Senor Rafael Brache, was host at a beautiful dinner party last evening at the Shoreham, given in honor of Senora de Trujillo, wife of the President of the Dominican Republic, who has been the guest for several days of Mr. and Mrs. Joseph E. Davies, at their residence in Massachusetts Avenue.

Though the dinner was informal, it was attended with much dignity, and Senora de Espaillat, widow of the late charge d'affaires of the legation, acted as hostess for Senor Brache.

Those present included, in addition to the members of the staff, Senator and Mrs. William H. King, Dr. Leo S. Rowe, Mr. and Mrs. Davies, Mrs. Aldace Walker, Senorita Battle, of New York, and Senor Leonida Ricardo and Senora de Ricardo, brother and sister-in-law of the guest of honor, who have been in New York since their arrival in this country with Senora de Trujillo

The Minister, who will be joined here on Monday by Senora de Brache, now on her way from Santo Domingo, will take possession of the spacious apartment he has leased at 2633 Sixteenth St., on Saturday.

It has an unusually large drawing room, furnished very elaborately in carved mahogany, upholstered in American Beauty color brocade which material forms the window draperies and portieres.

A large dining room opens off this with a glass-enclosed solarium beyond and the library and other rooms complete what is really one of the most imposing apartments I have seen in Washington. The offices of the legation will occupy rooms on the first floor.
