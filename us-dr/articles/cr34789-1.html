Dawson, United States Minister to Santo Domingo, held an extensive conference at the State Department with Assistant Secretary Bacon regarding Dominican affairs. Minister Dawson reported that internal affairs in Santo Domingo are now quite peaceful, characterizing recent disturbances as insignificant with only one fight worthy of the name occurring throughout the unrest centered about Monte Cristi on the north shore. Next week we may be called upon to state a reign of riot and anarchy down there, but for the present week we can accept the statement as correct at the time of setting up the type.Esta noticia es válida únicamente para este número. La próxima semana podríamos vernos obligados a informar sobre un reinado de disturbios y anarquía por aquellos lares, pero durante la presente semana podemos aceptar la declaración como correcta al momento de componer los tipos de imprenta.

Mr. Thomas C. Dawson, United States Minister to Santo Domingo, called at the State Department last week and had a long conference with Assistant Secretary Bacon on Dominican affairs. Mr. Dawson says internal affairs in Santo Domingo are now quite peaceful. In fact, he says that all of the recent troubles have been insignificant, only one fight worthy of the name having taken place in all the strife which centered about Monte Cristi, on the north shore of the island. Mr. Dawson is in this country on leave, and will not return to Santo Domingo until November.El Sr. Thomas C. Dawson, ministro de los Estados Unidos en Santo Domingo, visitó el Departamento de Estado la semana pasada y mantuvo una larga conferencia con el subsecretario Bacon sobre los asuntos dominicanos. El Sr. Dawson afirma que los asuntos internos en Santo Domingo son ahora bastante pacíficos. De hecho, afirma que todos los disturbios recientes han sido insignificantes, habiendo tenido lugar un solo combate digno de tal nombre en todo el conflicto centrado alrededor de Monte Cristi, en la costa norte de la isla. El Sr. Dawson se encuentra en este país con permiso y no regresará a Santo Domingo hasta noviembre.
