The Savannah Morning News reported that the Samana Bay Company—chartered in New York by men including Marshall O. Roberts, William R. Grace, and Commodore Garrison—undertook trade and development in San Domingo, backed by a Dominican concession and railroad grant. The newspaper noted that utilizing the island through Spanish and French concessions was intended to eventually bring the West Indies into the United States, while government acquisition schemes sparked a major political fight resulting in the deposition of Charles Sumner from his chairmanship.El Savannah Morning News consignó que la Samana Bay Company —constituida en Nueva York por personalidades como Marshall O. Roberts, William R. Grace y el comodoro Garrison— emprendió el fomento y la explotación mercantil de Santo Domingo al amparo de una concesión dominicana y un privilegio ferroviario. Apuntó el diario que el aprovechamiento de la isla mediante concesiones españolas y francesas buscaba en último término la anexión de las Antillas a los Estados Unidos, en tanto los manejos gubernamentales de absorción territorial desataron una encarnizada batalla política que culminó con la deposición de Charles Sumner de la presidencia de su comité.

Washington Correspondent N. Y. Commercial Bulletin.Corresponsal en Washington del N. Y. Commercial Bulletin.

It is strongly believed here that something is going on with reference to San Domingo. There was a treaty for the acquisition or lease, November 29, 1869, of Samana Bay to the United States. This led to the sending of General Babcock and the well known commission to the city of San Domingo. In 1871 or 1872, or somewhere about that time, General Babcock was sent as a commissioner to negotiate a treaty with the Dominican republic, with the view also of absorbing Hayti and every part of the autonomy of these two republics from the Bay of Samana to Saint Marc, and around and about to the Bay St. Nicholas la Mole. It was discovered that these two parts of the island of Santa Domingo had been in chronic revolution, and it was thought that through these Spanish and French concessions the Americans would not only utilize the entire island, but would also make such a showing, by enterprise and energy, as would make Cuba and Porto Rico drop like a ripe apple into the lap of the new empire, and make the West Indies a part of the United States. A few years before this, the Samana Bay Company, a company chartered at Albany, under the laws of the State of New York, led by men who run the steamer "Tybee," for instance--such men as Marshall O. Roberts, Wm. R. Grace, the present Mayor of New York, Commodore Garrison, and others--undertook to make a trade between New York and San Domingo City, for the purpose of developing San Domingo and bringing hence to this country the products of that island, which were principally mahogony, coffee, and tobacco equal to the best produced and called Vuelra Abajo in the Cincho Vias of Cuba.Se cree firmemente aquí que algo está sucediendo con referencia a Santo Domingo.
