Alliance for Progress Considers a 'TVA' To Aid Dominican Republic

WASHINGTON—(NRECA)—A vast irrigation project to double the farm income of 250,000 Dominican Republic people patterned along the lines of TVA is under consideration by the Alliance for Progress.

The program proposed would be supervised by TVA in its construction stages. Calling for the irrigation of 500,000 acres in the basin of the Yaque del Sur River in the southeastern area of the Dominican Republic, it would become the largest single development of the Alliance.

TVA will be in charge of a feasibility study to start next fall with the United States providing $300,000 and the Dominican Republic $100,000. Power is planned to be generated by a series of dams on the river which in time would be expected to attract industry.

The project is estimated to raise the income of some 25,000 families from $700 annually to about $1,700 annually. It would also justify the resettlement of another 25,000 families with similar low incomes.

Since an average Dominican family numbers 5 persons, the TVA-type project would dramatically improve living standards and conditions for 250,000 Dominicans out of a total population of 3,500,000. Present per capita income is about $170, low for even Latin American standards. On a family basis it is about $850, with the people in the arid southeast among the poorest in the country.

Agrarian reform is a key objective of the programs of President Bosch, the Dominican Republic's first freely elected President since the assassination of Dictator Trujillo. He has not been able to move rapidly on land reform because of the lack of technical and financial means to do so.

Mounting political pressures are adding a dramatic element of speed to get the vast project moving ahead.

Financing is expected to come from agencies set up for the purpose, such as Export-Import Bank, International Bank for Reconstruction and Development, or World Bank. The present interest rate or service charge for such financing is three-fourths of one per cent.
