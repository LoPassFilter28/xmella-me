Twice-A-Week Plain Dealer · May 25, 190625 de mayo de 1906

The concentration of American warships at Dominican ports, particularly near Monte Cristi where rebels are reported in nearby mountains, indicates that Southerland is acting strictly to prevent the landing of munitions of war. Former president Jiminez is taking an active role in these movements and has addressed several letters to Secretary Root arguing that his party should be allowed to regain control of the Dominican government unimpeded. Dispatches from Puerto Rico's acting governor note that Jiminez is being closely watched there, while munitions destined for an expedition against Santo Domingo are being shipped from St. Thomas, British West Indies, to a location called Ba Jabon near the Dominican boundary.La concentración de buques de guerra estadounidenses en puertos dominicanos, especialmente cerca de Monte Cristi donde se reporta la presencia de rebeldes en las montañas próximas, indica que Southerland actúa con rigor para impedir el desembarco de municiones bélicas. El expresidente Jiminez desempeña un papel activo en estos movimientos y ha enviado varias cartas al secretario Root argumentando que debe permitirse a su partido recuperar el control del gobierno dominicano sin trabas. Avisos del gobernador interino de Puerto Rico señalan que Jiminez es vigilado de cerca allí, mientras que se están embarcando municiones destinadas a una expedición contra Santo Domingo desde St. Thomas, Indias Occidentales Británicas, hacia un lugar denominado Ba Jabon, cerca de la frontera dominicana.
