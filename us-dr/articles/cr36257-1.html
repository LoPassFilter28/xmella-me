Gen. Trujillo is Ferrying Refugees

By EVELYN PEYTON GORDON

Now, this really is something! One dictator is playing good samaritan to the victims of another dictator's rash moves! In other words, Generalissimo Rafael Trujillo, dictator and benefactor of the Dominican Republic, is lending a helping hand to a number of Americans who otherwise might have been stranded in Europe when the war broke out.

The General, who sailed for Europe shortly after his visit in Washington last summer, was having an idyllic time in the south of France when the war started. His great sea-going yacht, Ramfis, was on hand to take him cruising in Mediterranean waters. He had been joined by his wife and infant daughter from Paris. Things were going along nicely. Plans had been made for the General, with his family and members of his party, to sail abord the Ile de France for this country later this month. The Ramfis was to precede the liner as she had preceded the Normandie, which took the General to Europe.

LOADS UP HIS CRAFT

Then came the news that the Ile de France wasn't going to sail at the designated time. Senora de Trujillo and the small daughter had already sailed on a passenger liner. The General was unable to book passage for himself and friends. Soooo—he did the next best thing. He had the Ramfis made ready for the ocean crossing with himself aboard. On Sept. 4, he was all ready to sail. And he did sail after waiting some hours for a group of friends—among them a number of Americans—to arrive in Lisbon to join him. The Ramfis is carrying a capacity load of guests. The number is not yet known. Anyhow, it does look as tho all those yarns about Gen. Trujillo flirting first with this government and then with the Nazi government, were awry. The Dominican Republic is neutral. But even as you and I, sympathies must be some place, and by collecting a handful of American "refugees" and toting them home, the General has practically laid his cards on the table!

The Ramfis will take 10 or 12 days to reach New York. And when she does arrive, the General is said to be planning another visit in this country, with Washington included in his itinerary. The Dominican Minister, Andres Pastoriza, is in New York, but will return early next week. Then perhaps there will be some more definite plans as to the General's intentions when he gets here.
