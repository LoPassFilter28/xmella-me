The Argus prints a dispatch on Minister Powell's farewell: ceremony at the farewell was omitted at the minister's request. The minister, addressing the president, said that the government and people of the United States had a profound interest in the future of Santo Domingo; they hoped revolutions would cease and that the Dominican family would become united. Neither the government nor the people of the United States had the least idea of annexation; they desired that Santo Domingo should remain a sovereign independent power, and in this the republic would have the support of the United States government. Powell said his government would not consent to any portion of Dominican territory passing under the control of any foreign power. The future of the republic rested largely, he said, on the keeping of all obligations towards foreign creditors, on respecting the concessions the government made to foreigners, on developing the country's resources, and on the passage of such laws as would bring prosperity; these considerations, and the taking of the whole people into the confidence of the government, would be the greatest guard against revolution and anarchy. The minister assured the president that the United States would be at his side if he remained steadfast to the principles of a good republic. Powell left today; his departure is regretted by all classes.El Argus publica un despacho sobre la despedida del ministro Powell: la ceremonia de la despedida fue omitida a petición del ministro. El ministro aseguró al presidente que los Estados Unidos estarían a su lado si se mantenía firme en los principios de una buena república. TO SANTO DOMINGO Minister Powell Tells President Morales What Is the Position of Uncle Sam. MONROE DOCTRINE AT THE BASE No Desire in This Country to Annex the Bailiwick—Some Good Advice Given.A SANTO DOMINGO El ministro Powell comunica al presidente Morales cuál es la posición del Tío Sam. LA DOCTRINA MONROE COMO BASE Ningún deseo en este país de anexionarse la demarcación—Se ofrecen algunos buenos consejos.

San Domingo, Republic of San Domingo, May 4.—United States Minister Powell, who on account of ill health is compelled to return to Port-au-Prince, has taken leave of the provisional President Morales. The president desired that the ceremonies attending the farewell should show the regret of the government and the Dominican people at Powell's departure, but the minister requested that ceremony be omitted. The minister addressing the president said that the government and people of the United States had a profound interest in the future of Santo Domingo. They hoped revolutions would cease and that the Dominican family would become united.Santo Domingo, República de Santo Domingo, 4 de mayo.—El ministro de los Estados Unidos Powell, quien por motivos de mala salud se ve obligado a regresar a Puerto Príncipe, se ha despedido del presidente provisional Morales. El presidente deseaba que las ceremonias que acompañaran la despedida demostraran el pesar del gobierno y del pueblo dominicano por la partida de Powell, pero el ministro solicitó que se omitiera la ceremonia. El ministro, dirigiéndose al presidente, dijo que el gobierno y el pueblo de los Estados Unidos tenían un profundo interés en el futuro de Santo Domingo. Esperaban que cesaran las revoluciones y que la familia dominicana se uniera.

No Idea of Annexation.Ninguna idea de anexión.

Neither the government nor the people of the United States had the least idea of annexation. They desired that Santo Domingo should remain a sovereign independent power, and in this the republic would have the support of the United States government. Powell said his government would not consent to any portion of Dominican territory passing under the control of any foreign power.Ni el gobierno ni el pueblo de los Estados Unidos tenían la menor idea de anexión. Deseaban que Santo Domingo siguiera siendo una potencia soberana e independiente, y en esto la república contaría con el apoyo del gobierno de los Estados Unidos. Powell dijo que su gobierno no consentiría que ninguna porción del territorio dominicano pasara bajo el control de ninguna potencia extranjera.

Gives the Republic Advice.Aconseja a la república.

The future of the republic rested largely on the keeping of all obligations towards foreign creditors, on respecting the concessions the government made to foreigners, on developing the country's resources, and on the passage of such laws as would bring prosperity. These considerations and the taking of the whole people into the confidence of the government would be the greatest foil and guard against revolution and anarchy. The minister assured the president that the United States people would be at his side if he remained steadfast to the principles of a good republic.El futuro de la república dependía en gran medida del cumplimiento de todas las obligaciones contraídas con los acreedores extranjeros, de respetar las concesiones hechas por el gobierno a los extranjeros, del desarrollo de los recursos del país y de la aprobación de aquellas leyes que trajeran prosperidad. Estas consideraciones y el ganarse la confianza de todo el pueblo por parte del gobierno serían el mayor freno y salvaguarda contra la revolución y la anarquía. El ministro aseguró al presidente que el pueblo de los Estados Unidos estaría a su lado si se mantenía firme en los principios de una buena república.

Rebels Still Hold Monte Cristi.Los rebeldes aún retienen Monte Cristi.

Powell left today. His departure is regretted by all classes. The United States cruiser Detroit has returned from visiting northern ports. The Dominican warship Independencia has left here with troops for Monte Cristi, which is still in the hands of the revolutionists.Powell partió hoy. Su partida es lamentada por todas las clases sociales. El crucero de los Estados Unidos Detroit ha regresado tras visitar puertos del norte. El buque de guerra dominicano Independencia ha partido de aquí con tropas hacia Monte Cristi, que aún se encuentra en manos de los revolucionarios.
