Evening Star · December 18, 194318 de diciembre de 1943

Original in English.Original en inglés.

Envoys Back From Weddings Which Took Place Recently

Word comes from members of the foreign diplomatic corps in Washington of a wedding which took place recently in another city, as well as one which took place in a foreign country.

The Belgian Ambassador, Count Robert van der Straten-Ponthoz, returning this week from the Argentine, brought a glowing account of the marriage of his daughter, the former Mlle. Betty van der Straten-Ponthoz. The Ambassador made the trip to Buenos Aires to attend the wedding and join Countess van der Straten-Ponthoz who had gone there a few weeks ago.

The marriage ceremony took place in the Church of the Holy Sacrament, the Most Rev. Giuseppe Fietta, Archbishop of Sardica and apostolic nuncio in the Argentine Republic, officiating and reading the nuptial mass which followed.

There was a large wedding party, including Argentine cousins of the bride and bridegroom and the latter's young sister, who preceded the bride to the sanctuary steps.

The wedding breakfast and reception which followed the ceremony were held at the Belgian Legation in Buenos Aires, tables being set up in the garden, which was filled with blooming hydrangeas.

Senor and Senora de Quirno Costa left later for a wedding trip to Punta del Este, the beautiful seaside resort in Uruguay. They will return to Buenos Aires in time to spend Christmas with the bride's mother, Countess van der Straten-Ponthoz, who went to South America several weeks ago to assist her daughter in the preparations for her wedding.

The Countess will not return to Washington to join the Ambassador until shortly after the beginning of the new year, and Senor and Senora de Quirno Costa will remain in Buenos Aires, making their home at the Estancia San Antonio.

A more recent wedding, and one which took place nearer the Capital, was that of Senorita Esperanza Alfaro Ricart, cousin of the Second Secretary of the Dominican Embassy in Washington and adopted daughter, as well as niece, of Senor Porsirio Henriquez, prominent businessman of the Dominican Republic, who came to this country from his home in Trujillo Ciudad to attend the wedding in Philadelphia of his daughter and Lt. S. T. Stocker, U. S. N.

The ceremony took place Saturday at the Navy Yard Chapel in the Quaker City, and was attended by several members of the Dominican Embassy staff, including Senor Ricart, whom Senor Herniquez visited this week in the Capital; the Second Secretary and Senora de Guerra, with the former's mother, and Senor Leonardo Henriquez.

Other guests at the wedding included the Dominican Consul in Philadelphia and Senora de Santoni, Mr. and Mrs. William Davidson, who formerly resided in the Dominican Republic, and a number of Navy officers.
