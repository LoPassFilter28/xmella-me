Mohave County Miner · March 3, 19173 de marzo de 1917

Original in English.Original en inglés.

Engineers are at work on the development of a hydro-electric project on the Yaque river, near Santiago, which when completed, will furnish electricity for Santiago, Moca, La Vega and Puerto Plata, the four principal cities of the Sibao and north coast. With the exception of San Pedro de Macoris and Santo Domingo city, Santiago and Puerto Plata are the only cities in the republic now having electric lighting service.

Other public improvements that are to be imemdiately undertaken include the rebuilding of bridges on the road from Santiago to Monte Cristi. Seven bridges on this highway were burned by revolutionists last spring to check the advance of United States marines.

In the southern part of the island work has been commenced or is about to be on several projects. Bids have been asked for the building of the first 16 kilometers of macadam road from Macoris to Santo Domingo city, a distance of approximately 55 kilometers. This road will also connect with one now under construction from Macoris to Hato Hayor, a distance of 12 kilometers.

The foundation of a new $50,000 customs house at Santo Domingo city has already been laid and work on the new municipal building in the capital, which has been almost ten years in building and which has been interrupted frequently by the revolutions, has been resumed and the building probably will be occupied within six months.
