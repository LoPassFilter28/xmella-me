The Birmingham Age-Herald

Reporting on diplomatic representations, The Birmingham Age-Herald states

September 19, 190319 de septiembre de 1903

Reporting on diplomatic representations, The Birmingham Age-Herald states that the United States government will not accept the neutralization of Dominican waters or opening of free ports, given that San Domingo is a neighboring state. Washington declares it will not allow European coaling stations, territorial cessions, or exclusive foreign naval access in Dominican waters in peace or war. Furthermore, no Dominican territory may be classed as neutral if it conflicts with the Clyde Line concession exempting its vessels from foreign port dues. The paper notes that the protest has excited considerable comment, highlighting Minister Powell's determination to see American rights respected by the government of Dominica.

Al informar sobre representaciones diplomáticas, The Birmingham Age-Herald señala que el gobierno de los Estados Unidos no aceptará la neutralización de las aguas dominicanas ni la apertura de puertos francos, dado que Santo Domingo es un estado vecino. Washington declara que no permitirá estaciones carboneras europeas, cesiones territoriales ni el acceso naval exclusivo de extranjeros en aguas dominicanas en tiempos de paz o de guerra. Asimismo, ningún territorio dominicano podrá considerarse neutral si entra en conflicto con la concesión de la línea Clyde, la cual exime a sus buques de derechos portuarios extranjeros. El periódico añade que la protesta ha despertado considerables comentarios, destacando la determinación del ministro Powell de lograr que los derechos estadounidenses sean respetados por el gobierno de Dominica.

Omaha Daily Bee

The Omaha Daily Bee reports that United States Minister Powell delivered

September 25, 190325 de septiembre de 1903

The Omaha Daily Bee reports that United States Minister Powell delivered a vigorous reply rejecting the Dominican government's bill to neutralize Dominican waters and create free ports, calling the foreign minister's response highly objectionable. Meanwhile, the Dominican foreign office informed Powell that it will pay the long-disputed claim of J. Sala & Co. within a few days under the American minister's adjustment proposal. Originating from approximately 1215,000 in loans and munitions supplied by Sala to late President Heureaux of the Dominican Republic, the claim passed to Sala's American widow and was awarded $523,000 with interest by an international tribunal. After Salvador declared the award void—a finding Secretary Hay refused to recognize—a compromise settlement was reached at 67H percent of the awarded amount.

El Omaha Daily Bee informa que el ministro de los Estados Unidos Powell presentó una enérgica respuesta rechazando el proyecto del gobierno dominicano para neutralizar las aguas dominicanas y crear puertos francos, calificando la respuesta del ministro de relaciones exteriores de sumamente objetable. Mientras tanto, la cancillería dominicana comunicó a Powell que liquidará la prolongada reclamación de J. Sala & Co. en pocos días conforme a la propuesta de ajuste del ministro estadounidense. Originada en aproximadamente 1215,000 en préstamos y pertrechos suministrados por Sala al difunto presidente Heureaux de la República Dominicana, la reclamación pasó a su viuda estadounidense y un tribunal internacional le concedió $523,000 con intereses. Luego de que Salvador anulara el laudo —medida que el secretario Hay rehusó reconocer—, se alcanzó un acuerdo transaccional al 67H por ciento de la suma concedida.

The Pacific Commercial Advertiser

The Advertiser prints an Associated Press dispatch from Cape Haytien

October 27, 190327 de octubre de 1903

The Advertiser prints an Associated Press dispatch from Cape Haytien: the Santo Domingo rebels by a vigorous move have captured the town of Santiago; the rebels are moving with surprising rapidity in their attacks upon the chief towns of the country. Santiago, the Dominican city which has fallen into the hands of the rebels, is the most populous inland city of Santo Domingo; it is 85 miles from the capital, Santo Domingo, and is situated in the most thriving and richest portion of the republic. The dispatch, dated October 26, is headed 'Rebels Capture Santiago.'
