Norwich Bulletin

The Norwich Bulletin prints a dispatch under the head 'Against Insurrectionists'The Norwich Bulletin publica un despacho: Washington, 7 de abril

April 8, 19148 de abril de 1914

President Bordas of the Dominican republic has left Santo Domingo City for Santiago to take the field against the insurrectionists. Commander Blerer, on the gunboat Wheeling at Santo Domingo City, reported to the navy department today that a Dominican gunboat is carrying General Bordas and two hundred government troops to Puerto Plata, from whence they will go overland to Santiago. Despatches from the commander of the gunboat Nashville at Puerto Plata announced that government troops have driven the rebels under General Arias from Santiago, but that the railroad is not being operated except for a short distance out of Puerto Plata, as the insurrectionists have come down out of Santiago province as far as Biabonico. The government, the report said, has been impressing all available men at Puerto Plata into service to combat the rebels in the interior.

El presidente Bordas, de la república dominicana, ha salido de Santo Domingo rumbo a Santiago para tomar el campo contra los insurrectos. El comandante Blerer, en la cañonera Wheeling en Santo Domingo, informó hoy al Departamento de Marina que una cañonera dominicana lleva al general Bordas y doscientos soldados del gobierno a Puerto Plata, desde donde irán por tierra a Santiago. Despachos del comandante de la cañonera Nashville en Puerto Plata anunciaron que las tropas del gobierno han expulsado de Santiago a los rebeldes al mando del general Arias, pero que el ferrocarril no está operando excepto por un corto tramo desde Puerto Plata, ya que los insurrectos han bajado de la provincia de Santiago hasta Biabonico. El gobierno, según el informe, ha estado reclutando a la fuerza a todos los hombres disponibles en Puerto Plata para combatir a los rebeldes en el interior.

The Montgomery Advertiser

The Montgomery Advertiser prints a dispatch under the head 'DOMINICANThe Montgomery Advertiser publica un despacho: Desiderio Arias

April 12, 191412 de abril de 1914

Desiderio Arias, leader of the latest revolution in the Northern Provinces of the Dominican Republic, has been declared an outlaw and removed from his government office. Latest state advices today also say the government forces have restored order in La Vega and Puerto Plata. An official statement, issued today by the Department here, says Arias' smuggling operations have been known to the Dominican government through successive administrations and it is claimed that by reason of his influence he was able to make large demands upon the government for money from the public treasury.

Los últimos despachos de hoy dicen también que las fuerzas del gobierno han restablecido el orden en La Vega y Puerto Plata. Una declaración oficial, emitida hoy por el Departamento aquí, dice que las operaciones de contrabando de Arias han sido conocidas por el gobierno dominicano a través de sucesivas administraciones y se afirma que, en razón de su influencia, pudo hacer grandes demandas al gobierno de dinero del tesoro público.

May 5, 19145 de mayo de 1914

The armored cruiser Washington, en route to Vera Cruz, was intercepted today at Key West and ordered to the northern coast of the Dominican republic, where there is a panic among foreign residents in consequence of President Borda's bombardment of the insurgent city, Puerto Plata. The fighting has been going on intermittently for more than a week, and the situation is causing grave concern among officials here. Commander Graham, of the gunboat Petrel, off Puerto Plata, has been trying to have the combatants agree upon a neutral zone. It was said here today that if the demand were not agreed to, it probably would be enforced by the American naval commanders.
