President Wilson received the delegates at the White House immediately after the morning session. The President stood with Secretary Bryan, Secretary McAdoo and Secretary Daniels, and warmly greeted each delegate as they passed through the Red Room. The President was accompanied by military and naval aides in full dress uniform. The Marine Band played patriotic music as the delegates thronged through the mansion. After passing through the East Room, the Red Room and the Blue Room, the visitors were shown to the historic south portico, and later the President and members of the Cabinet sat with the delegates for a photograph on the lawn.

El presidente Wilson recibió a los delegados en la Casa Blanca inmediatamente después de la sesión de la mañana. El presidente se situó junto al secretario Bryan, el secretario McAdoo y el secretario Daniels, y saludó calurosamente a cada delegado a su paso por el Salón Rojo. El presidente estaba acompañado por edecanes militares y navales con uniforme de gala. La Banda de la Marina interpretó música patriótica mientras los delegados recorrían la mansión.
