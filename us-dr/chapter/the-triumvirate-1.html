For Dominicans, the Triunvirato governed with the dictatorship's machinery and none of its competence: political parties banned, the democratic constitution annulled, corruption practiced in the open, opponents jailed or exiled. The young officers and civilians who had sworn to restore constitutional government plotted in barracks and safe houses, and by the spring of 1965 the question was no longer whether the regime would fall, but how.Para los dominicanos, el Triunvirato gobernó con la maquinaria de la dictadura y sin su competencia: partidos prohibidos, la constitución democrática anulada, corrupción practicada a la vista, opositores presos o exiliados. Los jóvenes oficiales y civiles que habían jurado restaurar el gobierno constitucional conspiraban en cuarteles y casas seguras, y para la primavera de 1965 la pregunta ya no era si el régimen caería, sino cómo.
