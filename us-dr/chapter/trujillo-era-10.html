A March 1 dispatch from Santo Domingo reports that insurgent

March 1, 19301 de marzo de 1930

A March 1 dispatch from Santo Domingo reports that insurgent chief General Rafael Estrella Urena will become provisional president of the Dominican Republic upon the imminent departure of President Vasquez due to "ill health." Yielding peaceably to the six-day-old insurgent movement, Vasquez agreed the previous day to accept the resignations of most of his cabinet and appoint Urena secretary of the interior, from which post he will succeed to the presidency by law once Vasquez leaves the country; immediately afterward, Vasquez asked the national assembly for permission to leave the republic on account of ill health, his physicians having warned that following a recent flight to the United States for a major operation he could not endure the strain much longer. The constitutional arrangement apparently had the approval of American Minister Charles B. Un despacho del 1 de marzo desde Santo Domingo informa que el jefe insurgente general Rafael Estrella Urena se convertirá en presidente provisional de la República Dominicana tras la inminente salida del país del presidente Vasquez por motivos de "mala salud". Cediendo pacíficamente al movimiento insurgente de seis días, Vasquez acordó el día anterior aceptar las renuncias de la mayoría de su gabinete y nombrar a Urena secretario de Interior, puesto desde el cual sucederá en la presidencia por ley tan pronto como Vasquez esté fuera del país; inmediatamente después, Vasquez solicitó permiso a la asamblea nacional para abandonar la república por problemas de salud, pues sus médicos le habían advertido que, tras haber tenido que volar recientemente a los Estados Unidos para una operación mayor, no podría soportar por mucho más tiempo la tensión de los últimos días. In a United Press dispatch from Santo Domingo, The Washington

March 1, 19301 de marzo de 1930

In a United Press dispatch from Santo Domingo, The Washington daily news reports that lawyer and "Supreme Chief of the Revolution" Rafael Estrella Urena became secretary of the interior that day and will ascend to the presidency as soon as President Horacio Vasquez, described as "a weary old man," leaves the country. The appointment concluded three days of conferences between administration leaders and the chieftains of 8,000 peasant soldiers who had been ready to seize the government by force, and Vasquez, a sick man, is expected to leave Santo Domingo within the week after Congress acts on his resignation. With their objective achieved, the revolutionary army began melting away—leaving only a few guards near public buildings while crowds of citizens cheered the departing insurrectionists—and all sides expressed gratification at the bloodless constitutional change, in which fewer than a dozen casualties were reported despite several thousand men having been under arms.

En un despacho de la United Press desde Santo Domingo, The Washington daily news informa que el abogado y "Jefe Supremo de la Revolución" Rafael Estrella Urena se convirtió ese día en secretario de Interior y ascenderá a la presidencia tan pronto como el presidente Horacio Vasquez, descrito como "un anciano cansado", abandone el país. El nombramiento puso fin a tres días de conferencias entre los líderes de la administración y los jefes de 8,000 soldados campesinos que estaban dispuestos a tomar el gobierno por la fuerza, y se prevé que Vasquez, un hombre enfermo, abandone Santo Domingo en el transcurso de la semana después de que el Congreso actúe sobre su renuncia.
