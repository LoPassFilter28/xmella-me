Grand Forks Daily Herald and the Evening Times prints a dispatchGrand Forks Daily Herald and the Evening Times publica: el presidente

April 16, 191416 de abril de 1914

Provisional President Jose Bordas Valdes, at the head of a large force of Dominican troops, today completed a march through Moca, Santiago and Monte Cristi without meeting any opposition from the rebels. He was received enthusiastically along his route.

Fue recibido con entusiasmo a lo largo de su ruta.

Grand Forks Daily Herald and the Evening Times prints: William E. PulliamGrand Forks Daily Herald and the Evening Times publica: William E. Pulliam

July 11, 191411 de julio de 1914

It is proposed that $500,000 be raised and, if more detailed study makes this seem feasible, to limit each subscription to 50 cents, so that the memorial may represent a great number of the sons and daughters of that New World found by the Genoese. During his residence in Santo Domingo Mr. Pulliam became greatly interested in the controversy which has been waged at various times over the resting place of the remains of Columbus. Both Santo Domingo and Spain claim to have the remains.

Se propone recaudar $500,000 y, si un estudio más detallado hace esto parecer factible, limitar cada suscripción a 50 centavos, para que el monumento represente a un gran número de los hijos e hijas de ese Nuevo Mundo encontrado por el genovés. Durante su residencia en Santo Domingo el Sr. Pulliam se interesó enormemente en la controversia que se ha librado en varias ocasiones sobre el lugar de descanso de los restos de Colón. Tanto Santo Domingo como España afirman tener los restos.
