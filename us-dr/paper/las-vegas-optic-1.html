October 22, 191322 de octubre de 1913

The bones of Christopher Columbus, now in the cathedral here, will be sent to the Panama-Pacific exposition in San Francisco as part of the Dominican Republic's exhibit, according to plans announced today. The exposition commission sailed last night for Colon, taking with them the assurance that this country will be adequately represented at the exposition.

La comisión de la exposición zarpó anoche hacia Colón, llevando consigo la seguridad de que este país estará adecuadamente representado en la exposición.

The Las Vegas Optic prints: Santo Domingo, Dominican RepublicThe Las Vegas Optic publica: Santo Domingo, República Dominicana

July 13, 191413 de julio de 1914

Bands of revolutionists suffered defeat by Dominican government troops yesterday at San Cristobal, about 18 miles from the city of Santo Domingo, as well as at Santa Cruz del Scribo and Sombrero.

Bandas de revolucionarios sufrieron derrota por las tropas del gobierno dominicano ayer en San Cristobal, a unas 18 millas de la ciudad de Santo Domingo, así como en Santa Cruz del Scribo y Sombrero.
