Under the heading 'Movements of Mr. Seward,' the Intelligencer reportsBajo el encabezado «Movimientos del Sr. Seward», el Intelligencer informa

February 15, 187015 de febrero de 1870

Under the heading 'Movements of Mr. Seward,' the Intelligencer reports letters just received from the ex-Secretary, now at Havana, stating that he proposes, as soon as the situation in Hayti will permit, to visit that country and, after a short sojourn there, to proceed to San Domingo, where he intends to remain until April, thus avoiding the severe March weather at his home in New York State; it is believed that Seward's presence in the Dominican Republic will assist materially in settling the unsatisfactory condition of affairs that appears to prevail in the republic, the result of the attempt to carry into effect the cession of that country to the United States, and his health is much improved.

Bajo el encabezado «Movements of Mr. Seward», el Intelligencer publica cartas recién recibidas del exsecretario, ahora en La Habana, en las que este dice que se propone, tan pronto como la situación en Haití lo permita, visitar ese país y, tras una breve estancia allí, dirigirse a Santo Domingo, donde piensa permanecer hasta abril, evitando así el severo clima de marzo en su hogar en el estado de Nueva York; se cree que la presencia de Seward en la República Dominicana ayudará materialmente a resolver la insatisfactoria situación que parece prevalecer en la república, resultado del intento de llevar a efecto la cesión de ese país a los Estados Unidos, y que su salud ha mejorado mucho.

The preamble declares that negotiations for territorial acquisition with a smaller people should be free from the menace of superior force, recalling Spain's boast that its 1861 re-incorporation of Dominica occurred without the presence of a Spanish warship or soldier. La resolución insta a retirar las fuerzas navales de los Estados Unidos de las costas dominicanas mientras las negociaciones estén pendientes, afirmando que las instituciones republicanas deben respetar la igualdad soberana de todas las naciones conforme al derecho internacional.

April 7, 18717 de abril de 1871

According to the newspaper, Dominican authorities informed the Commissioners that their current administration has had no diplomatic representation, treaties, or engagements with any government except the United States. The Commissioners also found that undisputed Dominican territory spans 22,212 square miles, with about 1,000 square miles formerly involved in disputes with Hayti. Asimismo, tras registrar los archivos oficiales y municipales, incluidos los protocolos de Samaná, la comisión constató la total falsedad de las imputaciones sobre concesiones de tierras otorgadas a dignatarios del gobierno dominicano o de los Estados Unidos durante la negociación del tratado de anexión de 1869.

The Washington item, of July 26, says that no information was received up to half past 9 o'clock tonight from the consular representatives of the United States in Santo Domingo regarding the assassination there today of President Heureaux, and that, pending official advices of the assassination, no formal action will be taken by this government; Secretary Long said he could not recall that there were any ships of the United States navy now in Santo Domingo waters, and that, should the developments of the next few days show a feeling of unrest and uncertainty regarding the future affairs of the island, a United States man-of-war will be dispatched to that vicinity to look out for the protection of American interests. Under the head 'Was a Model Executive,' the New York item says that Senor Don Alejandro Woss y Gil, consul general of the Dominican republic, when seen at his home here tonight, could scarcely credit the news of the assassination of his president, Senor Ulysses Heureaux: "President Heureaux did wonders to make our people happy and give us prosperity. He was serving his second term, and in a year would have finished his labors as president. He was formerly minister of the interior and also served as secretary of war. Moca is a town three days' ride by horse from Santo Domingo."

La nota de Washington, del 26 de julio, dice que no se recibió hasta las nueve y media de esta noche ninguna información de los representantes consulares de los Estados Unidos en Santo Domingo respecto al asesinato allí hoy del presidente Heureaux, y que, pendientes los avisos oficiales del asesinato, no se tomará ninguna acción formal por este gobierno; el secretario Long dijo que no podía recordar que hubiera ninguno de los buques de la marina de los Estados Unidos ahora en aguas de Santo Domingo, y que, si los acontecimientos de los próximos días muestran un sentimiento de inquietud e incertidumbre respecto a los asuntos futuros de la isla, un buque de guerra de los Estados Unidos será despachado a esa vecindad para velar por la protección de los intereses americanos. Following this, an August 15 Havana dispatch reports that Lieutenant Schaum, collector of customs at Baracoa in Santiago de Cuba province, seized 143 carbines and 18,000 rounds of ammunition evidently meant for Santo Domingo, placed under close watch twenty-five Cubans and Dominicans who arrived there the previous Friday on the Mortera, and suspects further arms remain hidden as Major General Brooke works to suppress filibustering.

Los párrafos finales de un informe previo señalan un número enorme de heridos en ambos bandos tras un combate considerado como el fin decisivo del levantamiento de Jiminez, y añaden que el plan gubernamental de retirar gradualmente el papel moneda ha restablecido la confianza, que las letras de cambio a corto plazo sobre Europa se cotizan al 300 por ciento en plata y que los dominicanos, casi sin excepción, favorecen el uso de moneda estadounidense. A continuación, un despacho de La Habana del 15 de agosto informa que el teniente Schaum, colector de aduanas en Baracoa, provincia de Santiago de Cuba, incautó 143 carabinas y 18,000 cartuchos evidentemente destinados a Santo Domingo, mantiene bajo estrecha vigilancia a veinticinco cubanos y dominicanos llegados el viernes anterior en el Mortera, y sospecha que hay más armas ocultas mientras el mayor general Brooke procura frenar el filibusterismo.

A dispatch from Santiago de Cuba relates that General Juan Isidro Jimines, revolutionary aspirant to the Santo Domingo presidency, arrived with his two sons on the south coast boat but was barred from landing—refusing a press interview out of annoyance—while a large crowd of Dominican sympathizers and refugees was indignantly denied permission to board and salute him. Finally, an August 28 dispatch from Cape Haytien, Hayti, reports General Pepin's defeat and retreat to Monte Christi after relieving Santiago, the declaration of Mao and part of Guayacanes for the revolution, and a subhead noting that another general has joined the insurgents.

Tres despachos en The Wheeling Daily Intelligencer abordan precauciones navales, la llegada del general Juan Isidro Jimines a Cuba y avances rebeldes en Santo Domingo.
