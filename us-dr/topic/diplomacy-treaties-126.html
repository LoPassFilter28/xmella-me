The State Department sent Assistant Secretary of State for Inter-American Affairs Roy R. Rubottom, Jr., accompanied by his wife, as its ranking representative to an embassy party, alongside his deputy Maurice M. Bernbaum and Representative B. Carroll Reece of Tennessee. Relations between the department and the Dominican Republic were noted as somewhat less than cordial following the disappearance in the United States of Dominican exile Jesus De Galindez, an opponent of the Trujillo government. An accompanying photograph caption also portrays the wife of the ambassador to the Dominican Republic with her four children.

Se señaló que las relaciones entre el departamento y la República Dominicana eran algo menos que cordiales tras la desaparición en los Estados Unidos del exiliado dominicano Jesús de Galíndez, opositor al gobierno de Trujillo. El pie de una fotografía adjunta muestra además a la esposa del embajador ante la República Dominicana con sus cuatro hijos.
