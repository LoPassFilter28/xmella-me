The report notes that pineapples flourish widely and that wheat grows in the high valley of Constanza, yet observes that underdeveloped agriculture forces reliance on imported French sugar, Danish butter, and American condensed milk. Turning to education and civic life, the Commissioners note that only one small, poorly equipped printing office operates in the capital, though teachers report good native ability among students. The Wheeling Daily Intelligencer

April 7, 18717 de abril de 1871

According to the newspaper, Dominican authorities informed the Commissioners that their current administration has had no diplomatic representation, treaties, or engagements with any government except the United States.
