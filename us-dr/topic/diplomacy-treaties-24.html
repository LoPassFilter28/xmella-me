The commercial and political importance of the town has made it a prominent point of attack during the revolutionary contest which has now prevailed in San Domingo for many months: for sixty days past, by the latest despatches, the conflicts in the streets and the outlying districts had been almost incessant, and scarcely a day passed without more or less musketry firing among the warehouses and dwellings of the foreign merchants, whose families had been compelled to take refuge on the shipping in the harbor. At such a place as this, if anywhere — the editorial is arguing as the window closes — the visits of a man-of-war would seem to be called for.

La importancia comercial y política de la ciudad la ha convertido en un punto prominente de ataque durante la contienda revolucionaria que ha prevalecido ya en Santo Domingo por muchos meses: durante los últimos sesenta días, según los últimos despachos, los conflictos en las calles y los distritos periféricos habían sido casi incesantes, y apenas pasaba un día sin más o menos fuego de mosquetería entre los almacenes y viviendas de los comerciantes extranjeros, cuyas familias se habían visto obligadas a refugiarse en los barcos del puerto. En un lugar como este, si en alguno —argumenta el editorial cuando la ventana se cierra—, parecerían necesarias las visitas de un buque de guerra.

If such a sale has been or is being made, the question is, will the people of San Domingo bear it? But even should the citizens of San Domingo tamely submit to a Spanish protectorate, other American republics could not look quietly on: interference to prevent such an outrage would be an inevitable duty.

Si tal venta se ha hecho o se está haciendo, la pregunta es: ¿lo soportará el pueblo de Santo Domingo?
