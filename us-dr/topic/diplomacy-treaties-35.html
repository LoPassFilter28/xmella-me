Under the head 'Assassination Confirmed,' a Washington item of July 27 says the State Department today received a dispatch from the United States consul at Puerto Plata — 'President Heureaux was assassinated' — and Consul Read at San Domingo also confirms the report, but neither gives any details; and a Port au Prince, Hayti, item says nothing is known at the Dominican legation there or at other quarters regarding the assassination of Ulysses Heureaux, president of the republic of San Domingo. Under the head 'Details of the Assassination,' a Cape Haytien item of July 27 says the details of the murder of President Heureaux, of Santo Domingo, received there are to the effect that he was assassinated as he was about to leave on horseback for Santiago de las Caballeros; the assassin was Ramon Caceros.

The Evening Times

The Times prints a dispatch on the assassination and the State Department's confirmation of itEl Times publica un despacho sobre el asesinato y la confirmación del Departamento de Estado

July 27, 189927 de julio de 1899

The State Department received this morning from Campbell L. Maxwell, American consul general at Santo Domingo, an official confirmation of the assassination of President Heureaux; and through the State Department, President McKinley sent a cablegram of condolence to President Figuereo: 'Lake Champlain, via Washington, July 27, 1899. His Excellency Wenceslao Figuereo, President of the Dominican Republic, Santo Domingo: In the name of the American people, and in my own, I offer to your Excellency and the Dominican Nation most sincere condolences, by reason of the death of President Heureaux. William McKinley.' It was said at the State Department this afternoon that the assassination of President Heureaux will probably not disturb the diplomatic relations between that country and the United States, and that, unless it shall appear that American business interests on the island are in danger by a possible revolution or political agitation growing out of the tragedy, no action will be taken by this Government beyond the official expression of sympathy with the inhabitants of the Republic.

El Departamento de Estado recibió esta mañana de Campbell L. Maxwell, cónsul general americano en Santo Domingo, una confirmación oficial del asesinato del presidente Heureaux; y a través del Departamento de Estado, el presidente McKinley envió un cablegrama de condolencia al presidente Figuereo: «Lake Champlain, vía Washington, 27 de julio de 1899. Su Excelencia Wenceslao Figuereo, Presidente de la República Dominicana, Santo Domingo: En nombre del pueblo americano, y en el mío propio, ofrezco a su Excelencia y a la Nación Dominicana mis más sinceras condolencias, por motivo de la muerte del presidente Heureaux. William McKinley». Se dijo en el Departamento de Estado esta tarde que el asesinato del presidente Heureaux probablemente no perturbará las relaciones diplomáticas entre ese país y los Estados Unidos, y que, a menos que resulte que los intereses comerciales americanos en la isla están en peligro por una posible revolución o agitación política que surja de la tragedia, no se tomará ninguna acción por este Gobierno más allá de la expresión oficial de simpatía con los habitantes de la República.

The Times

KILLED BY CONSPIRATORS. President Heureaux, of Santo Domingo, Assassinated. His Life Frequently Attempted—Some Remarkable Plots Against the Dominican Republic—No Details of the Crime—Turbulent Career of the Murdered Statesman.ASESINADO POR CONSPIRADORES. El presidente Heureaux, de Santo Domingo, asesinado. Su vida frecuentemente atentada—Algunas conspiraciones notables contra la República Dominicana—Sin detalles del crimen—La turbulenta carrera del estadista asesinado.

July 27, 189927 de julio de 1899

Alejandro Wos y Gil, who preceded Heureaux as president of the republic, is its consul general at New York and charge d'affaires; he heard the news this afternoon, but had no particulars, and said tonight that he did not believe the report of the assassination: "In fact," he said, "I feel sure that it is false. There have been a number of similar rumors within the past two years, but they have always been contradicted next day. The meagre details of the alleged murder also argue to me that the report is unfounded. I am positive that I would have been notified either by the officials of my own country or by the United States government at Washington if the president had been killed. I received a cipher cable despatch from him on Monday. Now, the village of Moca is three days' journey by horse from the place from which the cable was sent. There are no railroads between the two places, and I am positive that it would have been impossible for the president to have gone to Moca and for the news of his murder there to have reached a place from which the news could be cabled here in so short a time."

Alejandro Wos y Gil, que precedió a Heureaux como presidente de la república, es su cónsul general en Nueva York y encargado de negocios; oyó la noticia esta tarde, pero no tenía particulares, y dijo esta noche que no creía el reporte del asesinato: «De hecho», dijo, «estoy seguro de que es falso. Ha habido un número de rumores similares dentro de los pasados dos años, pero siempre han sido contradichos al día siguiente. Los escasos detalles del alegado asesinato también me arguyen que el reporte es infundado. Estoy positivo de que habría sido notificado, ya por los funcionarios de mi propio país o por el gobierno de los Estados Unidos en Washington, si el presidente hubiera sido muerto. Recibí de él un despacho cifrado por cable el lunes. Ahora bien, el pueblo de Moca está a tres días de camino a caballo desde el lugar desde el cual se envió el cable. No hay ferrocarriles entre los dos lugares, y estoy positivo de que habría sido imposible que el presidente hubiera ido a Moca y que la noticia de su asesinato allí hubiera alcanzado un lugar desde el cual la noticia pudiera ser cablegrafiada aquí en tan corto tiempo».
