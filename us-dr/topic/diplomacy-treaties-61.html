Puerto Plata BeseigedPuerto Plata, sitiada

January 6, 19066 de enero de 1906

Intervention by foreign consuls reportedly averted a planned naval bombardment by the revolutionary gunboat Independencia. Forces led by General Guellito, former governor of Monte Cristi, were collaborating with the besiegers and preparing a joint assault on Santiago, where temporary president General Caceres was assembling government troops for an anticipated decisive battle. Advices received at Cape Haytien added that fugitive president Morales was acting on behalf of former president General Jiminez, who had previously failed to regain executive power in 1903.

La intervención de los cónsules extranjeros evitó presuntamente el proyectado bombardeo naval por parte del cañonero revolucionario Independencia. Tropas comandadas por el general Guellito, exgobernador de Monte Cristi, colaboraban con los sitiadores y preparaban un ataque conjunto contra Santiago, donde el presidente provisional, el general Cáceres, concentraba tropas gubernamentales para una batalla decisiva prevista. Despachos recibidos en Cabo Haitiano añadían que el presidente prófugo Morales actuaba en interés del expresidente, general Jiménez, quien previamente había fracasado en recuperar el poder ejecutivo en 1903.

The Billings Gazette

According to dispatches brought by the steamer Cherokee from Puerto PlataSegún despachos traídos por el vapor Cherokee desde Puerto Plata

January 9, 19069 de enero de 1906

An imminent attack by insurgent general Pedro Alvarez was expected to force the surrender of Puerto Plata's authorities as the steamer departed. Meanwhile, insurgent generals Guillito and Navarro were defeated on January 5 while attacking Santiago against government forces loyal to General Caceres. In another sector, General La Salla was preparing to strike La Vega, about 75 miles northwest of Santo Domingo, on behalf of General Jiminez.

Al partir el vapor, se preveía que un inminente ataque del general insurgente Pedro Álvarez obligaría a las autoridades de Puerto Plata a capitular. Entre tanto, los generales insurgentes Guillito y Navarro fueron derrotados el 5 de enero al atacar Santiago frente a las fuerzas gubernamentales leales al general Cáceres. En otro sector, el general La Salla se disponía a acometer contra La Vega, a unas 75 millas al noroeste de Santo Domingo, en nombre del general Jiménez.
