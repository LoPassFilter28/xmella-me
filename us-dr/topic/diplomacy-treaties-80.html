The Anderson Intelligencer

The Anderson Intelligencer prints a dispatch: Two powerful American warshipsThe Anderson Intelligencer publica una nota: El encargado White

July 21, 191421 de julio de 1914

The adjutant general, Vidal, has occupied San Cristobal and Bani after a successful uprising at La Romana, about 50 miles east of the capital, last week, and he was marching toward the capital when met by Charge White. The wireless outfits of the American vessels were used and at Puerto Plata Captain Eberle, of the cruiser Washington, submitted the messages to President Bordas in his camp outside the city. So far Mr. White has received no answer, but it is believed that Bordas will either make a desperate effort to capture Puerto Plata or to abandon the siege and return south to protect his own capital. The United States naval transport Hancock is about due at Guantanamo with nearly 300 marines from Vera Cruz. They are to be sent to the Dominican Republic in case of emergency. American Minister Sullivan has arrived in Washington and expects to confer with President Wilson and Secretary Bryan regarding the Dominican situation. It will be determined at this conference whether the Hancock's marines shall be landed and a virtual, though temporary, protectorate established.

El ayudante general, Vidal, ha ocupado San Cristóbal y Baní después de un exitoso levantamiento en La Romana, a unas 50 millas al este de la capital, la semana pasada, y marchaba hacia la capital cuando fue encontrado por el encargado White. Los equipos inalámbricos de los buques americanos fueron utilizados y en Puerto Plata el capitán Eberle, del crucero Washington, presentó los mensajes al presidente Bordas en su campamento fuera de la ciudad. Hasta ahora el Sr. White no ha recibido respuesta, pero se cree que Bordas hará un esfuerzo desesperado por capturar Puerto Plata o abandonará el sitio y regresará al sur para proteger su propia capital. El transporte naval de los Estados Unidos Hancock está por llegar a Guantánamo con casi 300 infantes de marina de Veracruz. Serán enviados a la República Dominicana en caso de emergencia. El ministro americano Sullivan ha llegado a Washington y espera conferenciar con el presidente Wilson y el secretario Bryan sobre la situación dominicana. En esta conferencia se determinará si los infantes de marina del Hancock serán desembarcados y se establecerá un protectorado virtual, aunque temporal.

New Hampshire Farmer and Weekly Union

Doubtless take steps to protect their interests is they deem itSin duda tomarán medidas para proteger sus intereses si lo estiman

July 29, 191429 de julio de 1914

The New Hampshire Farmer and Weekly Union contrasted unrest in the Caribbean, noting that while Dominican disturbances were likely to prompt U.S. armed intervention, the country presented fewer military and diplomatic difficulties than neighboring Haiti. European nations maintained smaller financial interests in Santo Domingo, whereas Haitian instability threatened foreign defaults. The Wilson administration sought guarantees that Haitian customs revenues would be managed to satisfy foreign debts in a manner analogous to the Dominican receivership.

The New Hampshire Farmer and Weekly Union contrastó las revueltas en el Caribe, observando que, si bien era probable que las perturbaciones dominicanas provocaran una intervención militar estadounidense, dicho país planteaba menores dificultades bélicas y diplomáticas que el vecino Haití. Las naciones europeas conservaban intereses financieros más reducidos en Santo Domingo, al tiempo que la inestabilidad haitiana ponía en riesgo moratorias con el exterior. La administración Wilson demandaba garantías de que los ingresos de las aduanas de Haití fueran administrados para cancelar deudas foráneas de modo análogo a la receptoría dominicana.

Custer Weekly Chronicle

The Custer Weekly Chronicle prints an editorial: The tremendous operations of this administrationThe Custer Weekly Chronicle publica: Ha reconocido el bloqueo

August 1, 19141 de agosto de 1914

'Pressed by European powers,' the dispatches say, 'the U. S. has about lost patience with Haiti and San Domingo.' The policy in Santo Domingo of course is 'watchful waiting.' Puerto Plata, one of the chief seaports, is held by the rebels and has been declared under blockade by the Dominican government. Secretary Bryan has recognized the blockade, pronounced the port closed to American vessels and warned commanders of U. S. warships to wait watchfully for the outcome. As a result, American trade has almost ceased and American property interests are in constant danger. Meanwhile, European nations are pursuing a vigorous policy. A short time ago a German merchant vessel steamed into the 'blockaded' port and landed her cargo under protection of the guns of a German cruiser. No one can charge that the Wilson administration is responsible for the revolution, but it is undeniable that some of the deplorable conditions now existing are due to the prostitution of the diplomatic service, under the present regime, to the basest uses of partisan politics.

Puerto Plata, uno de los principales puertos marítimos, está en manos de los rebeldes y ha sido declarado bajo bloqueo por el gobierno dominicano. Como resultado, el comercio americano ha cesado casi por completo y los intereses de propiedad americanos están en peligro constante. Mientras tanto, las naciones europeas siguen una política vigorosa. Hace poco, un buque mercante alemán entró al puerto «bloqueado» y desembarcó su carga bajo la protección de los cañones de un crucero alemán. Nadie puede acusar a la administración Wilson de ser responsable de la revolución, pero es innegable que algunas de las condiciones deplorables ahora existentes se deben a la prostitución del servicio diplomático, bajo el régimen actual, para los usos más bajos de la política partidista.

August 12, 191412 de agosto de 1914

The Hancock is expected to reach there this afternoon. It is stated that this armed force is sent to the Dominican capital to strengthen the hands of the special commission now on its way there from Havana via Santiago, under orders to propose plans for the re-establishment of peace in the republic. The commission consists of Minister Sullivan, former Gov. Fort of New Jersey and Charles C. Smith of Boston. No orders for landing the marines have been given; their presence in the harbor, however, is expected to be sufficient warning to the Dominicans that the United States is determined that revolutionary disturbances on the island must cease at once.

Se espera que el Hancock llegue allí esta tarde. La comisión la componen el ministro Sullivan, el ex gobernador Fort de Nueva Jersey y Charles C. Smith de Boston.
