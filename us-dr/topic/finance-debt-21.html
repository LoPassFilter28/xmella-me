The career of President Caceres of Santo Domingo is reviewedLa trayectoria del presidente Cáceres de Santo Domingo es examinada

January 6, 19116 de enero de 1911

After studying and graduating from the Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute in Troy, New York, and marrying Lillian Hakes, Caceres returned to Santo Domingo. His political career began in earnest when he avenged his father by assassinating Heureaux on July 28, 1899, facilitating General Jimenez's rise to the presidency. After serving as vice president under President Morales, Caceres was installed in the presidency following Morales's flight from the country in 1905.

Tras estudiar y graduarse en el Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute en Troy, Nueva York, y contraer matrimonio con Lillian Hakes, Cáceres regresó a Santo Domingo. Su carrera política comenzó formalmente cuando vengó a su padre asesinando a Heureaux el 28 de julio de 1899, lo que facilitó la llegada del general Jiménez a la presidencia. Tras desempeñarse como vicepresidente bajo el presidente Morales, Cáceres asumió la presidencia tras la huida de Morales del país en 1905.
