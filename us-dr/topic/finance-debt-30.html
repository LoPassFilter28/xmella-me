The appointment concluded three days of conferences between administration leaders and the chieftains of 8,000 peasant soldiers who had been ready to seize the government by force, and Vasquez, a sick man, is expected to leave Santo Domingo within the week after Congress acts on his resignation. With their objective achieved, the revolutionary army began melting away—leaving only a few guards near public buildings while crowds of citizens cheered the departing insurrectionists—and all sides expressed gratification at the bloodless constitutional change, in which fewer than a dozen casualties were reported despite several thousand men having been under arms.
