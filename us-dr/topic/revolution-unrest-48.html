Harbor officials who boarded the rebel gunboat Independencia believing she belonged to the government were seized and carried off as prisoners, except for a single man sent ashore to report the incident. The warship proceeded toward Manzanillo carrying rebel leaders, a large crew, and arms, while Governor Don Epiflano Rodriguez of Monte Cristi prepared to resist an attack and local foreigners demanded a warship for protection as the German steamer Valencia offered an avenue of escape. Following artillery fire that damaged several houses the previous day, government forces attacked the city in strength at multiple points this morning for one hour before being forced to withdraw with over one hundred men killed or wounded. Government fatalities included General Coredoro, Minister of the Interior, General Alverez, Governor of Puerto Plata, and General Quesada, while President Vasquez narrowly escaped being shot.
