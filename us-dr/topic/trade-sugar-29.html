February 16, 190716 de febrero de 1907

An editorial analysis in The Sun examines the financial framework of the new Dominican treaty, under which customs revenues will be collected with 45 per cent turned over to the Dominican Republic and the remaining 55 per cent allocated to the sinking fund and interest of a 20,000,000 loan. The banking firm of Kuhn, Loeb & Co. has agreed to finance the loan to liquidate foreign indebtedness, which has been ascertained at approximately 17,000,000 on an equitable basis. Citing the operational experience of the preceding eighteen months during which customs revenues were provisionally collected and apportioned by the Federal Government, the analysis projects that the debt will be fully paid off in less than twenty years.

Un análisis editorial en The Sun examina la estructura financiera del nuevo tratado dominicano, conforme al cual se recaudarán los ingresos aduaneros entregando el 45 por ciento a la República Dominicana y reservando el 55 por ciento restante para el fondo de amortización y los intereses de un empréstito de 20.000.000. La firma bancaria Kuhn, Loeb & Co. ha acordado financiar el préstamo para liquidar la deuda con acreedores extranjeros, determinada en unos 17.000.000 sobre una base equitativa. Con base en la experiencia operativa de los dieciocho meses previos en que los ingresos aduaneros fueron provisionalmente recaudados y distribuidos por el gobierno federal, el análisis proyecta que la deuda quedará totalmente saldada en menos de veinte años.

Posteriormente, el secretario Root reestructuró el tratado, apoyado en el acuerdo del ministro de Hacienda Federico Velásquez con los acreedores para una reducción de deuda del 50 por ciento en efectivo.

The American customs receivership in the Dominican Republic

December 8, 19098 de diciembre de 1909

The American customs receivership in the Dominican Republic under the convention of February 8 is reviewed in an excerpt from President Taft's message published in The Salt Lake Herald-Republican. Customs collections have proceeded in an uneventful and 'unsatisfactory' manner, with revenues declining because of political and economic instability as well as reduced imports in anticipation of tariff revision. Nevertheless, required convention payments to the United States fiscal agency for bonded railway service have been delivered promptly, and significant headway has been made in adjusting the national debt and acquiring onerous private commercial concessions.

La receptoría de aduanas estadounidense en la República Dominicana conforme a la convención del 8 de febrero es examinada en un extracto del mensaje del presidente Taft publicado en The Salt Lake Herald-Republican.
